Install:

Unzip the archive and put the STREETFIGHTER-EX-PLUS folder in the mugen data folder

Open mugen.cfg and copy/paste either motif line

Full intro: 

motif = data/STREETFIGHTER-EX-PLUS/SFEX-SYSTEMEX.def

Intro minus ken and ryu fighting:

motif = data/STREETFIGHTER-EX-PLUS/SFEX-SYSTEM.def
