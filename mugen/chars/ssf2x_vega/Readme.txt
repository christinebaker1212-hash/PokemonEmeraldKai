To change the current palette in game,
press Start before the round begins to
bring up the color select menu. Change
it by pressing left or right. Pressing
up or down changes between SF2-X version,
or the SF2'CE version of Vega.

Alternately, you can use vega-sf2ce.def
instead if you only want the CE version.

==============
SF2'CE Version
==============

Special Moves
-------------
Back flip:		Back, Back,

Rolling Claw:		Hold Back, then Forward, Punch

Wall Climb/Jump:	Hold Down, then Up, Kick
	Claw Dive:	Press Punch when coming down.
	Izuna Drop:	Hold Back or Forward, then

			press Punch when close to opponent.
==============
SSF2X Version
==============

Special Moves
-------------
Back flip:		Press any 2P, or all 3P simultaneously.
Short Back flip:	Press any 2K, or all 3K simultaneously.
Scarlet Terror:		Hold Down-Back, then Forward, Kick.
Sky High Claw:		Hold Down, then Up, Punch


Super Moves
-----------
Super Claw Impact:	Hold Back, then Forward, Back, Forward, Punch
			Claw must be attached to work.
Scarlet Mirage:		Hold Back, then Forward, Back, Forward, Kick
Super Izuna Drop:	Hold Down-Back, then Forward, Back, Up, Kick.
