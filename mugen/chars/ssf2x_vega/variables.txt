Vega (Balrog)
SSFIIX + SFII'CE
-------------------
Notes on Variables
-------------------

Character Variables
-------------------
var(0)	-	Randomizer: Used for various things.
Var(1)	-	Sp/hyp Move + weak/strong/fierce checker.
var(2)	-	Super Izuna Drop activater.
var(3)	-	Attack Animation Checker.

var(5)	-	Round Over indicator. Forces in to stand state.
var(6)	-	Opponent checker. Checks if target animations exists.
var(7)	-	Dizziness hit counter.
var(8)	-	Hits requierd before dizziness.
var(9)	-	Actually dizzy falling indicator.
var(10) -	Fireball hit sound indicator.
var(11) -	Fireball guard sound indicator.

var(15) -	If Target's animation exists, activate Win Quote system.

var(20) - 	Claw is Attached/Detached.
var(21) - 	Random # of hits needed until claw is fallen off.
var(22) - 	# of actual hits done until claw is fallen off.

var(30) -	Random AI Checker.

var(35) -	Amount of Power to save.

var(40) -	Rounds existed/checked once a round is over.
var(41) -	Win indicator.
var(42) -	Lose indicator.

var(50) -	Type of Sound Set to use.
var(51) -	SSF2X/SF2'CE Mode option.
var(52) -	Sets sound effects once depending on palette/color.

var(55) -	Palette/Color Selector.
var(56) -	KO Scream indicator.

var(58) -	Stage interaction activated with stage.var code.
var(59) -	Pause Menu can be activated only once.
