MOEMON VMU PET - KANTO EDITION
==============================

A Dreamcast VMU virtual pet: raise one MOEMON from an Egg, then bring it into Moemon Emerald Kai at level 50
through a VM2 (a VMU with USB), or send Kai's Eggs to it.

The files
- PET.VMS + PET.VMI       the pet program (96 blocks: room for later versions)
- PICTURES.VMS + .VMI     the MOEMON's portraits for its colour file icon (81 blocks; the pet works without them)
- pet.bin                 a whole VMU image holding the pet
- pet_vm2.bin             the same, marked as a VM2's (its LINK menu shows): for the VM2's SD card
- pet_pictures.bin        a whole VMU image with the pet and its PICTURES

Getting it on
- VM2: put pet_vm2.bin (or PET.VMS + .VMI with the VM2's own software) on it. Connecting the VM2 to Kai once
  (PC > VM2) also marks it as a VM2.
- A VMU: the VMU's own menu won't copy a game, so the first copy needs a Dreamcast (copy it from the VM2 in the
  Dreamcast's memory card screen). After that, the VM2 sends every new version: join them top to top, then on the
  VM2: MENU > LINK > UPDATE (a VMU answers by itself).

On the VM2, MENU > LINK (with a VMU joined on top):
- SWAP     the two MOEMON change places (a pulled cable loses neither)
- UPDATE   this version of the pet to the VMU
- BATTLE   a battle between the two

In Kai: plug the VM2 into the PC (GUI-mode). A PC in a POKEMON CENTER then has VM2: take the pet's Egg or its
grown MOEMON out of the VM2 box, or put an Egg there (KANTO's MOEMON, #1-151).
