Moemon Music Studio 1.3 "Blast Processing!": Sega Genesis songs sound like the Genesis: every drum, the square waves and the noise, at the right levels.

## Sega Genesis songs
- **The square waves and the noise play.** Before, they were too quiet for the GBA's Game Boy channels and
  came out silent. Now they're their own instruments, at the same level as on the Genesis.
- **Every drum hit plays.** A drum that followed another one without the song jumping in its sample memory
  (as in Sonic 3's Angel Island Zone) was skipped. Now the drums are read from what the sound chip outputs.
- **The FM instruments keep their attack and decay in every octave.** Each instrument is recorded in each
  octave it's played in, so a bass no longer rings on four times too long and high notes no longer cut off
  too soon.
- **The drums, the FM and the square waves are balanced as on the Genesis.** The drums were too quiet next
  to the FM instruments.
- A channel the song never pans no longer goes silent.

## Download
Windows: unzip **MoemonMusicStudio-windows.zip** and run MoemonMusicStudio.exe.
Linux: unpack **MoemonMusicStudio-linux.tar.gz** and run moemon_music_studio (needs SDL2: `sudo apt install libsdl2-2.0-0`).
