Moemon Music Studio 1.5 "8-Bit Encore!": any song as NES or Game Boy music, NES music in, and a GREAT BALL icon.

## CHIPTUNE
- **Chiptune...** (top bar) turns the open song into NES or Game Boy music, whatever it came from: a MIDI, a project,
  or any rip the studio plays (PlayStation 1 and 2, N64, Game Boy, NES, DS, GBA, Super Nintendo, Genesis).
- **Arranged like a person would.** The studio works out what each part of the song is: the melody, its harmony, the
  bass, the drums, ostinatos and pads, and which parts are only echoes or doublings of another. The melody plays on
  square 1, the harmony on square 2, the bass on the triangle (NES) or wave channel (Game Boy), the drums on the noise.
  It reads how the notes behave, not which instrument plays them, so it works for any song and any instrument.
- **Harmonies kept.** Square 2 picks the note that harmonizes with the melody (a third or sixth first), and keeps it
  for its whole length.
- **Steady octaves.** Each part keeps its octave. Only a part too low or high for its channel moves, all of it together.
- **Sweeps and slides.** Pitch bends, slides and vibrato follow the original exactly, and a melody that slides into its
  notes slides on the chip too.
- **Real NES and Game Boy files.** It saves an `.nsf` or a `.gbs` with its own little sound driver, so it plays in any
  NSF / GBS player (and on a flash cart), keeps its loop, and opens in the studio again like any other NES or Game Boy
  song: hear it on the console's own chip (Original), export it for Moemon, or make it chiptune for the other console.
- **Chord arpeggio** (off unless you turn it on): a chord in one part goes round its notes, the classic chiptune way.

## NES music
- **Open NSF and NSFe files** (and NEZplug .m3u playlists), every song in them one by one, in the playlist too.
- The NES's squares, triangle and noise play on the GBA's own Game Boy channels; DMC samples come in as samples.
  **Convert NES > GBA** makes them GBA instruments, as for Game Boy songs.
- The cartridges' extra sound chips are read too: Konami's VRC6 and VRC7, Namco 163, Sunsoft 5B, the Famicom Disk System
  and MMC5.

## And
- The studio has an icon: a GREAT BALL, on the window and on the Windows exe.

## Download
Windows: unzip **MoemonMusicStudio-windows.zip** and run MoemonMusicStudio.exe.
Linux: unpack **MoemonMusicStudio-linux.tar.gz** and run moemon_music_studio (needs SDL2: `sudo apt install libsdl2-2.0-0`).
