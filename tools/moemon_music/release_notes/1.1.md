Moemon Music Studio 1.1 "Press Play!": a player for game music, SNES and Genesis songs, truer N64 songs and loops you can set yourself.

## Two ways to use it
- **LISTEN** is a music player. It shows the song, its game, composer and console, how long it is and where it loops. **GBA / Original** (or Tab) switches between Moemon's version and the console's own sound at the same moment of the song.
- The open song's folder is your playlist: Prev / Next (or Page Up / Down), **Open folder...**, a Game Boy file's songs one by one, and shuffle. After a song: repeat it, go on to the next one after 1-9 times through its loop (with a fade), or stop.
- **CONVERT** is the editor, with the steps to a song for the game along the top: the song, its instruments, its loop, where it plays, whether it fits the game, and export. Click a step to go there.

## New consoles
- **Super Nintendo** songs (.spc), with the console's own samples and echo.
- **Sega Genesis** songs (.vgm / .vgz): FM instruments, drum samples and the square-wave chip.

## Better sound
- **Nintendo 64** songs play far closer to the original: notes start on time, each instrument triggers when it should, and no more stuttering.
- **Game Boy songs as GBA instruments**: fast arpeggios become real chords, buzzing retriggered notes become one held note, and notes that change their tone halfway stay one note, so they sound right on real instruments.
- Reverb follows the original: SNES and PlayStation songs get as much as the console used, instead of one fixed amount.

## Loops
- Loops are found by listening to where the original song actually repeats, exact to the sample, instead of rounding to the nearest beat. Songs that don't clearly repeat don't loop.
- **custom** lets you set the loop yourself: nudge either end by a frame or a second, or click **here** to use the spot that's playing. **Hear the loop** plays the jump.

## Download
Windows: unzip **MoemonMusicStudio-windows.zip** and run MoemonMusicStudio.exe.
Linux: unpack **MoemonMusicStudio-linux.tar.gz** and run moemon_music_studio (needs SDL2: `sudo apt install libsdl2-2.0-0`).
