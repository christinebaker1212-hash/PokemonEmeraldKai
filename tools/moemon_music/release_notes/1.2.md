Moemon Music Studio 1.2 "Konami Code!": Yu-Gi-Oh! Worldwide Edition's music and Bomberman Hero's songs open and play.

## More games
- **Game Boy Advance games with Konami's own sound engine** (Yu-Gi-Oh! Worldwide Edition, and other games on the same engine) now open, where before they said the game didn't use the game's sound engine. Their notes and instruments are read as the song plays: the sampled instruments with their loops, the square waves, the wave-channel bass and noise, each on time with the original.
- **Nintendo 64 songs on the older audio code** (Bomberman Hero and other games using it) now open, where before they said the rip played nothing. Every note and instrument comes through, at the original's loudness.

## Like every other rip
- These songs work like any other console's: **GBA / Original** to compare, **All original / All GM**, changing instruments, finding and setting the loop, and exporting a song for the game.

## Download
Windows: unzip **MoemonMusicStudio-windows.zip** and run MoemonMusicStudio.exe.
Linux: unpack **MoemonMusicStudio-linux.tar.gz** and run moemon_music_studio (needs SDL2: `sudo apt install libsdl2-2.0-0`).
