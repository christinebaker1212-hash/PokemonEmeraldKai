Moemon Music Studio 1.4 "Sound Archive!": play a Nintendo DS game's own music straight from its sound archive, no rip needed.

## Nintendo DS sound archives
- **Open a DS game's SDAT file and play any song in it.** The studio reads the game's own sequences, instrument banks and
  waves and plays them on its own DS sequencer, with the game's tempo, loops, envelopes, pitch bends and vibrato. No
  emulator or 2SF rip is needed.
- **Pick a song by its number.** Opening an `.sdat` file asks which song to play. From the command line, name it after a
  `#`: `sound_data.sdat#SEQ_TOWN01` or `sound_data.sdat#1001`.
- **Songs that loop are recorded through their intro and their loop twice,** so the loop is found the same way as in
  any other rip. Songs that end are recorded to their last note.
- Everything else works as it does for a 2SF rip: the instruments, tuning, and exporting the song for the game.

## Download
Windows: unzip **MoemonMusicStudio-windows.zip** and run MoemonMusicStudio.exe.
Linux: unpack **MoemonMusicStudio-linux.tar.gz** and run moemon_music_studio (needs SDL2: `sudo apt install libsdl2-2.0-0`).
