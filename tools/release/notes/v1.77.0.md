Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.77.0 "Every Box at Once!": sort your PC on your phone across all your boxes, and a fresh look for the app.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are. Update MOEMON
COMPANION too (it offers the update when you open it).

## MOEMON COMPANION
- **ALL BOXES:** tap ALL BOXES on BOXES to see every box at once, with your party at the top. Hold a POKéMON and drag
  it into any box (the list scrolls when you reach the edge), or tap SELECT, pick several, and tap a box's name to send
  them all there. Tap a box's name to open it on its own.
- **A bigger box:** a single box now fills the screen, so every POKéMON is easier to tap and drag.
- **ON OFFER:** a POKéMON on offer in a trade by mail has a small orange tag in your boxes and on its card.
- **New tab bar:** each page has its own little icon, in the style of the DS's touch screen menus.
