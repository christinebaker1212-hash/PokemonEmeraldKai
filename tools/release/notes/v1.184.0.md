Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.184.0 "Spread the Spark!": moves that hit everyone around hit everyone again, and JOHTO loses the last of its extras.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## Battles
- **Moves that hit every POKéMON around** (DISCHARGE, LAVA PLUME, SURF from later games, SLUDGE WAVE, BOOMBURST,
  PARABOLIC CHARGE and the rest) now hit both foes and your partner in Double and Triple Battles. Before, they played
  the whole animation but only one POKéMON took the hit.

## JOHTO
- **The TOWN MAP** no longer shows the road and places west of CIANWOOD that were never part of JOHTO, and the POKéDEX's
  AREA page no longer points there.
- **TOHJO FALLS** no longer has a passage to a room that isn't JOHTO's.
- **LEGENDARY BEASTS** is earned by catching RAIKOU, ENTEI and SUICUNE, wherever you find them.
- **VOICE OF THE FOREST** is earned by catching CELEBI at ILEX FOREST's shrine.

## KANTO
- The ARCEUS trials' achievements are gone, along with the trials themselves.
