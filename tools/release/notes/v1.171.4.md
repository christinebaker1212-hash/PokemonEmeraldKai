Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.171.4 "Steady Frames!": choose how often the picture is shown, 60, 120 or uncapped.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
1.171.4 plays online with friends on 1.171.3 too.

## New
- OPTIONS > DISPLAY > FRAME RATE: 60 (the game's own rate, and the default), 120, or UNCAPPED. It sets how often the
  picture is shown; the game itself always runs at 60 frames a second, so its speed never changes. The card games,
  fights, the DANCE machine and other full-screen games used to draw as fast as the PC could when VSYNC was off, which
  could make the picture tear; they now keep to the frame rate you choose. If the picture still tears, turn VSYNC on.
