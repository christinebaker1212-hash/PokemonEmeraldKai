Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.186.1 "Shipshape!": fixes for the first voyage to a region.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## The first voyage
- The sailor on the pier names the right professor: PROF. ELM in JOHTO and PROF. OAK in KANTO.
- While your partner is at the professor's lab, the menu no longer offers POKéMON, which opened an empty screen.
- An empty slot on the POKéMON screen can no longer be chosen. Choosing one and opening its summary closed the game.

## ORRE
