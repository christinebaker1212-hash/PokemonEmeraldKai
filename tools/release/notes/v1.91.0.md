Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.91.0 "Johto, As Crystal Had It!": JOHTO's people keep Crystal's promises, and its berry trees look like trees again.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## FIXED
- **JOHTO's berry trees** no longer show a second, Emerald-style berry tree standing over them when the berries are
  ripe. The tree looks the same ripe or not, as in Crystal; talk to it to see what's there.

## PEOPLE WHO DO THEIR JOBS AGAIN
- **BLACKTHORN's MOVE DELETER** is home again and makes POKéMON forget moves.
- **ECRUTEAK:** the treasure hunter gives you the ITEMFINDER.
- **CIANWOOD's MANIA** lends you SHUCKIE, his last POKéMON, and asks for it back from the next day. If SHUCKIE has
  come to love you, he lets you keep it.
- **LAKE OF RAGE:** the FISHING GURU measures your MAGIKARP again and gives an ELIXIR for a new record.
- **ROUTE 27:** the woman checks how much your lead POKéMON trusts you before she gives TM37.
- **DRAGON SHRINE:** the DRATINI you're given is Lv. 15, and knows EXTREMESPEED if you answered every question
  right.
- **GOLDENROD's HAPPINESS RATER** rates how much your lead POKéMON likes you.
- **KURT** gives a LURE BALL first, with his own words.
- **DJ MARY** thanks you with a SILK SCARF.

## JOHTO AS IT WAS
- **ROUTE 32:** the man by VIOLET CITY lets you pass once you have the ZEPHYRBADGE.
- **ROUTE 43:** the ROCKETS at the gate charge 1,000 yen (or all the money you have) while they hold it.
- **MAHOGANY:** the RAGECANDYBAR seller is sold out once the ROCKETS' hideout is cleared.
- **TOHJO FALLS** has its MOON STONE back, and no one hands out a SUPER ROD there (ROUTE 12's FISHING GURU in KANTO
  still does).
- SAGE LI, the GYM leaders' words, the GYM statues and guides, and more of JOHTO's lines are Crystal's again.
