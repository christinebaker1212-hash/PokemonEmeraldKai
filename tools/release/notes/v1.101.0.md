Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.101.0 "All Aboard!": SINNOH's edges look right, the HMs of every region, faster contests you can concede, legendaries that come back, trips led by the host and a STREAMER MODE.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## SINNOH
- **The trees and cliffs around every town and route** sit at the right height and wear the same leaves as the map
  you're on: no more raised trees, black gaps or white ground under them at the map's edge.
- **FLOAROMA's arch** has grass and its shadow under it, not a black hole. Shadows and glass everywhere are drawn
  the way the DS draws them.
- **HM ROCK CLIMB and HM DEFOG** are SINNOH's own HMs, as in Platinum: rocky walls are climbed with ROCK CLIMB, and
  muddy slopes need the BICYCLE's speed.
- **Rocks you smash stay smashed** until you leave the area, and cave mouths take you in.
- **The Sprayduck waters BERRY trees** like the WAILMER PAIL.
- **The VALLEY WINDWORKS door** opens once you have its key.
- **The FASHION CASE's collector** walks over to you as he should.
- **Trainers who said "Message missing"** say their own lines again.
- Barry's line after a loss, Mom's OAK'S PARCEL and the special POKé BALLs' prices are right.

## JOHTO and KANTO
- **HM WHIRLPOOL** is JOHTO's own HM again, from the TEAM ROCKET hideout, for the whirlpools.

## Contests
- **The whole contest runs at your host's ANIM SPEED**: the appeals, the words and the results, like battles.
- **CONCEDE**: press START on the move list. Alone, the contest ends and you take last place. With friends, your
  POKéMON sits out the rest and takes last place on everyone's screen.
- **SUPER CONTESTS** in SINNOH: START on the ACTING round's move list concedes too; their words, acting and results
  run at the ANIM SPEED, while the dance and the dressing keep the music's time.

## Legendaries come back
- **A legendary you knocked out comes back a day later**: DEOXYS on BIRTH ISLAND, MEW on FARAWAY ISLAND, LUGIA and
  HO-OH on NAVEL ROCK, LATIAS or LATIOS, the REGIs, KYOGRE, GROUDON, RAYQUAZA, and every legendary in JOHTO and
  KANTO. One you knocked out before this update is already back.

## Travelling between regions
- **Online, only the room's host can lead the way to another region.** When the host sets sail, everyone in the
  room is told where to, picks the one POKéMON that comes along and boards the same boat.
- **Customs now lets you pick** the POKéMON that boards with you (before, it could pick one for you).
- **Your luggage is too heavy!** Only your KEY ITEMS board with you: each region's devices, bikes, tickets and the
  like. Everything else waits in the GLOBAL PC, and each region keeps its own item PC.
- **The GLOBAL PC hands your items over** when you open the item PC in a region whose POKéMON LEAGUE you've beaten.

## STREAMER MODE
- **OPTIONS > CO-OP > STREAMER MODE** hides real names on your screen. Your name and your friends' show as a hero
  from the games (GAME), a character from the anime (ANIME) or a common first name (ORIGINAL), picked by each
  trainer's ID and fitting their character. Nicknamed POKéMON show one of three pet names for their kind.

## Fixes
- **The summary screen** shows the No. of every POKéMON, also past 386, and a POKéMON's status no longer looks
  garbled there.
