Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.61.3 "Gotta Catch 'Em All, Really!": every POKéMON can be caught at your GENERATION, JOHTO and KANTO stop freezing, and DARKRAI, MEW and ARCEUS have their scenes.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
1.61.3 plays online with friends on 1.61.0 to 1.61.2 too.

## Fixed
- **New games in JOHTO or KANTO** had no POKéMON or POKéDEX in the START menu after getting a starter, and the
  RUNNING SHOES didn't work. Saves where this happened get them back when loaded.
- **STRENGTH couldn't move any boulder in JOHTO or KANTO**, and VICTORY ROAD's and the TANOBY KEY's switches never
  opened. Boulders move, and switches work when a boulder is pushed onto them.
- **The DANCE THEATER** in ECRUTEAK CITY froze the game on every visit after its first scene, and a few scenes in
  JOHTO and KANTO (BILL's teleporter, the ICE PATH, FIVE ISLAND) could freeze it too.
- **GOLDENROD's department store elevator** couldn't be entered. It runs between B1F and 6F now.
- **JOHTO's shop clerks** froze the game. Every POKé MART in JOHTO and KANTO sells again, more as you earn BADGES.
- **CUT and ROCK SMASH** froze the game on JOHTO's and KANTO's small trees and rocks.
- **KURT's shop** and the GOLDENROD underground's shops froze the game when you picked an item.
- **KANTO's POKéMON CENTERS** froze the game when the nurse healed your team.
- **In-game trades** in JOHTO and KANTO froze the game. Their people now trade the POKéMON they ask about.
- **Triple Triad card sellers** in JOHTO and KANTO froze the game.
- **The TRAINER TOWER's records** in KANTO left you unable to move. They show the tower's records now.
- **CERULEAN CITY's badge expert** kept you in his list after the first badge. B leaves it now.
- **ARCEUS stood in PROF. ELM's lab**, and other people and POKéMON meant to show up later in the story stood
  around from the start of JOHTO and KANTO. They wait for their moment now.
- **ZARUDE, MAGEARNA, ZYGARDE and HOOPA** stood on JOHTO's and KANTO's maps but did nothing when you talked to them.
  They can be battled and caught from their GENERATION on, and so can the second ARTICUNO, ZAPDOS and MOLTRES
  (from GEN 8).
- **The MOéMON GARDEN** from JOHTO or KANTO took you back down into HOENN. Its escalator now leads back to where
  you came from.
- **The forests' and the SAFARI ZONE's edges** in KANTO repeat properly past the map's end.
- **LANCE's battle in the TEAM ROCKET HIDEOUT** didn't start, leaving you stuck. Pick your POKéMON and fight ARIANA
  and her grunt side by side with LANCE; your whole team is back with you afterwards.
- **The S.S. ANNE's departure** left you frozen at the end of VERMILION's pier. The ship sails and the story goes on.
- **Several story scenes in JOHTO and KANTO** could stop with you unable to move after a special moment (a boat
  ride, a ship leaving). They all carry on now.

## New
- **Every POKéMON can be caught.** At each GENERATION, every POKéMON up to it can be found in the wild, received,
  traded for or evolved somewhere in the three regions:
  - At GEN 3, HOENN has SURSKIT (ROUTE 102), ROSELIA (ROUTE 117), ZANGOOSE (ROUTE 114), LUNATONE (METEOR FALLS) and
    MEDITITE (MT. PYRE) again, as rare finds.
  - At GEN 9, the paradox POKéMON live in HOENN: GREAT TUSK on ROUTE 111, IRON HANDS in NEW MAUVILLE, ROARING MOON at
    the top of SKY PILLAR, and eleven more, each in a place of its own.
  - MANAPHY's eggs hatch PHIONE.
- **The NATIONAL DEX counts your GENERATION's POKéMON**: 386 at GEN 3, 493 at GEN 4, up to 1025 at GEN 9.
- **New ways to evolve:**
  - The LINKING CORD works like a trade on every POKéMON that evolves by trading (KADABRA, MACHOKE, GRAVELER,
    HAUNTER; ONIX, SCYTHER, SEADRA and others while holding their item). It's sold at every GENERATION.
  - EEVEE learns BABY-DOLL EYES and becomes SYLVEON with high friendship (from GEN 6).
  - MILCERY becomes ALCREMIE with any sweet, MELTAN becomes MELMETAL at level 40, FARFETCH'D holding a STICK becomes
    SIRFETCH'D, and MR. MIME holding NEVER-MELT ICE becomes MR. RIME.
- **More to find:** UP-GRADE and the SCROLL OF DARKNESS / WATERS at the department stores' evolution counters, DREAM,
  FAB and RETRO MAIL at LILYCOVE, RARE BONE, ODD KEYSTONE and GIMMIGHOUL COIN through PICKUP, and a wild SHUCKLE may
  hold BERRY JUICE.
- **A GAME CENTER in JOHTO and KANTO.** An attendant by the elevator in GOLDENROD CITY's and CELADON CITY's GAME
  CORNERS takes you up to the GAME CENTER's CARD SHOP (2F) and games floor (3F).
- **Card duels with JOHTO's and KANTO's GYM LEADERS, ELITE FOUR and more.** Bring your CARD CASE: FALKNER, BROCK
  and the rest duel you once you've won their BADGE (the ELITE FOUR once beaten, LANCE once you're CHAMPION; SILVER,
  BLUE, RED, PROF. ELM, PROF. OAK and BILL any time). Each one plays the deck of a HOENN counterpart and hands over
  the same promo card the first time you win, so every POKéMON promo card can be won in every region.
- **Achievements for JOHTO and KANTO**: two new tabs with each region's badges, CHAMPION and legendary POKéMON,
  plus WORLD TRAVELER (visit all three regions) and TRIPLE CROWN (become CHAMPION of all three).
- **HEADBUTT** shakes JOHTO's and KANTO's trees for the POKéMON living in them, and ROCK SMASH finds POKéMON in rocks.
- **DARKRAI's dreams.** Sleep in your bed at home in JOHTO, or in a cabin of the S.S. ANNE in KANTO, and a dream
  may lead you to DARKRAI. In PALLET TOWN, a vision of ARCEUS waits after KANTO's League.
- **MEW under the truck.** Near the S.S. ANNE, a level 100 NIDOKING that knows STRENGTH can push the old truck aside.
- **The MAGNET TRAIN** runs between SAFFRON CITY and GOLDENROD CITY after KANTO's League, from both ends.
- **Trips together.** When the room's host takes a ship or train to another region, friends in the room come along.
