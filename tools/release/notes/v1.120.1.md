Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.120.1 "Ball's in Your Court!": POKé BALL effects shine on their own again, and no more beams.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## Fixes
- BATTLE ANIMS > MODERN no longer adds its own light while a POKé BALL is thrown, sends a POKéMON out or calls one back: those moments look exactly as on CLASSIC, so your POKé BALL effects show as you set them.
- The same goes for items, SUBSTITUTE, CASTFORM's changes and the battle GIMMICKS: only moves, status conditions and weather get MODERN's touch.
- MODERN no longer draws its own beams: no lines from the attacker to the target in THUNDERBOLT, ICE BEAM, TAKE DOWN, DOUBLE-EDGE and the rest, no bolts from the sky, no speed lines. The move's own animation shows as it is, with MODERN's light where it lands.
