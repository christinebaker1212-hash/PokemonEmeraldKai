Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.200.5 "Caught in Every Style!": the caught mark in every UI STYLE, move swapping in co-op, and a WHITE BG switch for RED and CRYSTAL.

UI STYLE
- A wild POKéMON whose kind you have already caught shows its POKé BALL mark in every style, as each game draws it: FIRERED's and PLATINUM's beside the HP bar, BLACK's beside the name, CRYSTAL's sitting on its box's corner. RED never had one, so it shows none.
- The mark now shows in Double and Triple Battles too, KAI's own Triple Battle cards included.
- Swapping moves with SELECT shows which move you are holding in every style.
- New in OPTIONS > DISPLAY for RED and CRYSTAL: WHITE BG. Turn it off to see the battle through the boxes, without their white backing.
- THEME is locked while a UI STYLE other than KAI is on, as FRAME already was: each style brings its own colours.
- RED and CRYSTAL's POKéMON list has no CANCEL: the cursor goes round from the last POKéMON back to the first, and B closes it.
- The party screen's "Do what with this one?" and its list are drawn in the style's own letters and windows.
- The MOéDEX page after a catch no longer has a battle message box drawn over it.
- Closing START, a menu or a message no longer flashes the old window for a moment, and a message box stays put while the town's name slides away.
- The move list no longer shows the wrong type and PP for a moment after you pick a move.
- RED and CRYSTAL write "…" properly.

CO-OP
- You can swap your POKéMON's moves with SELECT in co-op and online battles, in Doubles and Triples too. The new order stays after the battle.
