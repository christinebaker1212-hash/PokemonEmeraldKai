Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.74.1 "True Colors!": nicknamed and shiny POKéMON keep their colors on the phone, and linking is quiet.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## Fixes
- **MOEMON COMPANION colors:** with OPTIONS > CREATURES set to POKéMON, a nicknamed or shiny POKéMON looked scrambled
  on the phone (on a walk, in its summary, everywhere). The phone now always gets the right colors. A POKéMON already
  out on a walk is fixed the next time the phone links.
- **No more link sound:** the game no longer rings each time the phone links to it.
