Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.28.0 "Shuffle Up!": card duels made over: a CARD CLUB, Gym Leaders with decks of their own, a full-window table, new menus and trades with friends.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, settings and cards stay as they are.
Online, everyone needs 1.28.0: it can't play with 1.27.0 or older.

## New
- The CARD CLUB: a club member waits in the POKéMON CENTERS of OLDALE, MAUVILLE and LILYCOVE. They hand you a CARD
  CASE, show three starter decks to pick from, and offer a teaching duel if you'd like one. Later they run the CARD
  COUNTER.
- Challenges work like Triple Triad: register the CARD CASE to a button like any key item, then press that button
  in front of someone to ask for a duel. Nobody in front of you? The CARD CASE opens. Talking with A is unchanged.
- Most people play: Trainers once you've beaten them, plenty of townsfolk, and they answer in their own words, with
  over 30 lines for youngsters, hikers, swimmers, ladies, sailors and the rest. Their decks fit who they are and get
  stronger as you earn badges.
- Special decks: the eight Gym Leaders (after you win their badge), the ELITE FOUR, WALLACE, STEVEN, your rival, WALLY,
  MAXIE, ARCHIE and the FRONTIER BRAINS each have a deck of their own, and many hand over a promo card the first time
  you beat them.
- Every opponent has their own table: a forest mat for BUG CATCHERS, the sea for SWIMMERS, each Gym's type for its
  Leader, darker mats for the ELITE FOUR and the teams.
- Rewards: every win still gives a booster pack, and once a day each opponent also lets you pick one card from their
  deck. Nothing comes from MoeGatcha: cards are won from people.
- The CARD COUNTER trades spare cards for CARD POINTS and sells booster packs, single cards, RARE CANDY, PP UP and
  MOEGATCHA TICKETS. Collecting most of a set earns a promo card; the whole collection earns something special from
  the CARD CLUB and a star on your TRAINER CARD.
- A CARDS page of achievements: first win, a hundred wins, all eight Gym Leaders, a flawless win, a one-type deck,
  opening fifty packs, a whole set and more.
- Friends: CO-OP > FRIENDS > a friend > CARD DUEL or CARD TRADE, or the CARD CASE button in front of them. They get the
  question on their screen and answer YES or NO; then you both pick a deck, or each offer a card and both confirm.
  The winner of a friendly duel gets a booster pack, and nobody loses anything.
- Friends watching from SPECTATE see your duel, and your nametag says CARD DUEL. The friend you're playing can't peek
  at your hand.
- OPTIONS > DISPLAY > CARD ART: the printed card art or the original Game Boy art.

## Changed
- The card table fills the window with the game's own scaling, just like the rest of the game.
- The POKé BALL on the table is round again, every zone lines up with its cards, BENCH shows once, and the PRIZES sit
  neatly in two stacks.
- The opponent's cards face them.
- B no longer gives up the duel: it closes the hand, then asks whether to end your turn. The END TURN button became
  CONCEDE, and it asks first.
- A Prize card you take is shown in the middle of the table before it joins your hand.
- Cards like PROFESSOR OAK and BILL slide your hand into view so you can see what changed.
- After a Knock Out, the cursor goes to your Bench to pick the next Pokémon.
- Every card menu is new, in the style of the BAG: the CARD CASE, a deck builder with the cards in a grid and your deck
  beside them, the binder, booster packs and the challenge window with your opponent's picture.
- Short tips during your first duels.
- The CARD CASE is handed over in conversation, with no extra pop-up.

## Not yet
- 20 attacks (METRONOME, MIRROR MOVE, AMNESIA and a few more) and every POKéMON POWER show as not ready.
