Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.29.0 "Fair Play!": card players fit their decks to yours, and every POKéMON POWER works.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, settings and cards stay as they are.
Online card duels need everyone on 1.29.0.

## New
- CARD SCALING (OPTIONS > GAME, ON at first): card players pick the deck of theirs that best fits yours, so a
  starter deck meets starter-sized decks and a stronger deck meets stronger ones. They learn from how your duels
  go, too: win a lot and they bring their best, lose a lot and they ease off. Gym Leaders and the other trainers
  with decks of their own play a little above you, with their own deck or a version of it. Badges still cap how
  strong a regular card player can get. OFF: decks get stronger with your badges, as before.
- A challenge now says how the MATCH looks: EASY, EVEN, TOUGH or VERY TOUGH.
- Every POKéMON POWER works, all 26, as its card says. Use one with PKMN POWER on the Pokémon's menu, or SELECT >
  PKMN POWER. When one can't be used, the menu says why. RAIN DANCE, ENERGY TRANS, DAMAGE SWAP, CURSE and the
  others ask which Pokémon; SHIFT asks for a type and PEEK for a card to look at, which only you see. The others
  work on their own: TOXIC GAS, INVISIBLE WALL, STRIKES BACK, RETREAT AID, FIREGIVER and the rest. The power's
  name shows over the card when it works.
- With OMANYTE in play (CLAIRVOYANCE), A on your opponent's hand shows it to you.
- A CLEFAIRY DOLL or MYSTERIOUS FOSSIL in play can be discarded from its menu, as the card says.

## Fixed
- After KANGASKHAN's FETCH, both players seemed to draw a card. Only the attacker draws: the other card was the
  next turn's draw, which now comes after the turn banner.
- Card effects checked against the cards' text and the original rules:
  - ZUBAT's and GOLBAT's LEECH LIFE heal as much damage as they did, not 10.
  - FURY ATTACK flips 2 coins, not more.
  - PSYCHIC and PSYWAVE count Energy cards: a DOUBLE COLORLESS ENERGY counts once.
  - WATER GUN and HYDRO PUMP count only the Water Energy that wasn't needed to pay for the attack (up to 20 more).
  - ZAPDOS's BIG THUNDER hits each of the other Pokémon in play as likely as another.
  - GROWL, POUNCE, SNIVEL, LEER and TAIL WAG only work while the Pokémon that used them stays Active.
  - RHYDON's RAM switches the Defending Pokémon even when RHYDON is Knocked Out (and not when the Defending
    Pokémon is).
  - FULL HEAL cures Asleep, Confused, Paralyzed and Poisoned only; a Pokémon's WITHDRAW, BARRIER or AGILITY
    stays.
  - ENERGY RETRIEVAL and SUPER ENERGY RETRIEVAL can't discard a card they bring back.
  - A Pokémon that DEVOLUTION SPRAY was used on can evolve again the same turn.
