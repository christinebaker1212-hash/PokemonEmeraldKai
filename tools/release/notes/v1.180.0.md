Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.180.0 "PHENAC Unblocked!": RUI can't be left in her sack, the MAYOR's door opens, and ORRE's people stand where they should.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## ORRE
- **RUI's sack:** you can no longer walk out of PHENAC CITY before freeing her. If you already did, coming back plays
  the kidnappers' scene again so you can free her and carry on.
- **The MAYOR's house** opens when you walk up into any part of its door after the strange man has been.
- **Out of the PRE GYM** you are drawn at once, not hidden in the plaza until you walk a few steps.
- **In scenes:** people behind handrails and walls stay behind them, close-ups show faces instead of feet (RUI was
  hidden under the message box when she climbed out), and the "!" over someone sits over their head.
