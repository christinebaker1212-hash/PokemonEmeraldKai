Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.132.2 "Family Ties!": EVERSTONES and DESTINY KNOTS work for breeding as they should.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## Breeding
- A parent holding an EVERSTONE now always passes its Nature to the EGG, whichever parent holds it. If both hold one, the EGG takes one of the two Natures.
- EGGS now always get three different IVs from their parents. Before, the same stat could be picked twice, so an EGG often got only one or two.
- A parent holding a DESTINY KNOT makes the EGG take five IVs from its parents instead of three.
- This works the same for EGGS made with a friend's DAY CARE POKéMON.
