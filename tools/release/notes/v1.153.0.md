Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.153.0 "One For Each!": 11 more MAGIC cards that do something for each player.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- Spells that go round every player or every creature now work, one after the other: ACIDIC SOIL hurts each player for
  their own lands, BIORHYTHM sets each life to the creatures they control.
- Cards that set a life total, like BLESSED WIND, work too.
- 11 more cards can be played, among them ACIDIC SOIL, PRICE OF PROGRESS, BIORHYTHM, BLESSED WIND, STRONGHOLD
  DISCIPLINE, WING STORM and WAITING IN THE WEEDS. Every one has its printed art.
