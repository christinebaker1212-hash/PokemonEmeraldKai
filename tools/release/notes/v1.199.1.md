Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.199.1 "Point and Pick!": the CO-OP menu's cursor is the game's own again, and the room window comes and goes with your friends.

The CO-OP menu
- The row you're on is marked by the game's own cursor, as in the START menu, in every CO-OP screen.
- Playing alone, there's no room window: it only shows when someone else is here, and grows and shrinks as friends come and go.

Emotes and phrases
- The emote or phrase you're on is outlined in your window frame's color, with a small pointer beside the emote; nothing moves or grows as you pick.
