Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.79.0 "It's Time to Duel, Anywhere!": YU-GI-OH! on your phone, with your own cards and decks.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are. Update MOEMON
COMPANION too (it offers the update when you open it).

## MOEMON COMPANION: CARDS
- **YU-GI-OH! on the phone:** CARDS has a third game. Tap YU-GI-OH! at the top to duel with the game's own rules and
  every card's effects, laid out upright for your phone with the real cards. Your opponent's cards face them, across
  the table, as in the game.
- **Playing:** in your Main and Battle Phases the cards that can do something glow. Tap one to see it up close with
  its text and what it can do (SUMMON, SET, ACTIVATE...), and the phases are buttons at the bottom. Targets, Tributes,
  chains and every other choice open a picker or a list with the card's own words. Chances to chain while nothing is
  happening are passed for you; MENU > STOP AT EVERY CHANCE keeps them.
- **FREE DUEL:** take on a duelist with a deck from the Game Boy Advance games, about as strong as yours, as in the
  game. Win and a booster goes to your game: three a day. Every duel teaches the game how well you play.
- **VS FRIENDS:** challenge a friend to a YU-GI-OH! duel a move at a time, with your own deck. A notification says when
  it's your move.
- **BOOSTERS, DECKS and the BINDER:** open your game's boosters on the phone (NEW! on the cards you didn't have), build
  your eight decks with the Main and Fusion Decks and your Forbidden & Limited list, choose the one you duel with, and
  browse your BINDER set by set.
- You need your first YU-GI-OH! deck from the CARD CLUB in the game before you can play.
