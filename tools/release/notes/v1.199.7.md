Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.199.7 "Plugged Into UNOVA!": the C-GEAR and the ENTRALINK arrive, EXP keeps pace with where you are, and LEVEL SCALING works the same in every region.

UNOVA
- The C-GEAR: once you have it, START > C-GEAR opens it over the field. IR with a friend standing in front of you offers a TRADE or a BATTLE, WIRELESS opens the CO-OP menu, and ENTRALINK takes you to the ENTRALINK.
- In the ENTRALINK, the ENTREE lets you take missions with friends in the room. A SUPPORT mission is done by talking to the friend it names, who receives its PASS POWER; a BATTLE mission is done by battling them through the C-GEAR. Everyone in the mission earns Pass Orbs.
- PASS POWERS from Black: spend Pass Orbs at the ENTREE once your Black Level is high enough, then turn them on from the C-GEAR. They change encounters, EGG hatching, friendship, prices, EXP, prize money and catching, one of each kind at a time. SHARE turns one on for the whole room.
- CHECK STATUS shows your Black Level, the PASS POWERS you have and the missions you've completed.
- UNOVA's trainers now keep up with your team when LEVEL SCALING is on, as every other region's do, and its Gym Leaders, Elite Four, Champion and rivals get their extra levels as bosses.
- Field moves in UNOVA no longer ask for HOENN's badges.

EXP
- New with LEVEL SCALING: the EXP CURVE (OPTIONS > GAME > LEVEL SCALING, or GAME RULES when you host). Each area is measured by the levels of its own wild POKéMON and trainers. A POKéMON below the highest level you've reached in the region earns more EXP ("a boosted amount"), and one far above it earns less, so staying in one place to catch never leaves your team far ahead of the story. Going back to an earlier route costs nothing. GENTLE is the default, STRONG reduces more, OFF turns it off. It switches off once a region's LEAGUE is beaten.
- With the EXP CURVE on, a friend whose team is behind earns more EXP toward the rest of the room, up to the level of the area you're in.
- The LUCKY EGG now gives +50% EXP (it was +100%).
- MoeGatcha's POKéMON prizes come at the average level of your three strongest POKéMON.

Every region
- LEVEL SCALING's badge caps and "after the LEAGUE" now count the badges and LEAGUE of the region you're in, instead of always HOENN's. ORRE counts as fully earned.
- The MOéMON GARDEN and its BERRY farm grow with your best region's badges, so they never shrink when you sail somewhere new.
