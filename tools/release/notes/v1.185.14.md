Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.185.14 "Insert Coin, Clear Sound!": the GAME CENTER's arcade cabinets sound right on slower PCs.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## GAME CENTER
- The arcade cabinets (the CAPCOM games, PUZZLE BOBBLE and TETRIS THE GRAND MASTER 2 PLUS): on a PC that can't keep
  the game at a full 60 frames a second, the cabinet's sound ran out and crackled with gaps. Played alone, the cabinet
  now keeps pace with the sound by itself, so the music and effects play smoothly. Online VS play is unchanged.
- PUZZLE BOBBLE's music and effects sound cleaner: the high notes no longer turn into hiss.
- TETRIS THE GRAND MASTER 2 PLUS runs at its own arcade speed.
