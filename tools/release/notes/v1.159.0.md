Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.159.0 "No Takebacks!": 11 more MAGIC cards, among them spells that can't be countered.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- Spells that can't be countered now work: a COUNTERSPELL aimed at them does nothing, and the opponent won't waste one.
- GAEA'S HERALD and ROOT SLIVER keep creature and Sliver spells from being countered.
- UNCLE ISTVAN and ARGOTHIAN PIXIES take no damage from creatures (or artifact creatures).
- 11 more cards can be played, among them BLURRED MONGOOSE, OBLITERATE, NO MERCY, TEPHRADERM and WALKING DREAM.
  Every one has its printed art.
