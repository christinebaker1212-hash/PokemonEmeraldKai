Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.61.2 "Kanto Unlocked!": KANTO's story scenes play to the end, and the newer legendaries of JOHTO and KANTO appear at your GENERATION.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
1.61.2 plays online with friends on 1.61.0 and 1.61.1 too.

## Fixed
- **KANTO's scenes stopped halfway.** Many conversations in KANTO ended in the middle, before they gave what they
  should: the S.S. ANNE's CAPTAIN never handed over his gift and the ship couldn't sail, and scenes in PROF. OAK's
  LAB, PALLET TOWN, the POKéMON TOWER, SILPH CO., the GAME CORNERS, VIRIDIAN CITY's school and more were cut short.
  They all play to the end now.
- **VIRIDIAN CITY's old man** lies in the road again until he's had his coffee, and FUCHSIA CITY's pond shows its
  POKéMON. Copies of your rival stood there instead.
- **The ICE PATH's legendary** shows as itself.

## New
- **Newer legendaries in JOHTO and KANTO.** The POKéMON from the fifth generation on that stand waiting on the
  maps of JOHTO and KANTO (MELOETTA, ZEKROM, TERAPAGOS, REGIELEKI, REGIDRAGO, MAGEARNA and many more) appear once
  OPTIONS > GAME > GENERATION reaches their generation.
- **MELOETTA joins the CHANSEY Dance** in SEVAULT CANYON's house on SEVEN ISLAND, if your whole team dances: a
  CHANSEY leading, holding a LUCKY PUNCH and knowing SING, and five POKéMON of different families that each know a
  dance move (SWORDS DANCE, DRAGON DANCE, PETAL DANCE...). Afterwards it stays there for you to catch. From GEN 5.
