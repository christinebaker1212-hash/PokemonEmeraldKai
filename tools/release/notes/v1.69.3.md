Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.69.3 "Where's My Team?": the START menu's POKéMON right after the starter, and more tutors.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## SINNOH
- **GRANDMA WILMA** on ROUTE 210 teaches DRACO METEOR to a DRAGON-type POKéMON that fully trusts you, and the man on
  ROUTE 228 teaches the ultimate moves (BLAST BURN, HYDRO CANNON, FRENZY PLANT) to fully evolved starters, as in
  Platinum.

## Fixes
- **SINNOH:** after picking your starter from PROF. ROWAN's briefcase, the START menu shows POKéMON right away. Before,
  it only came back after loading the game again.
- **JOHTO, KANTO and SINNOH:** the START menu shows POKéMON whenever your party has one, however you got it.
