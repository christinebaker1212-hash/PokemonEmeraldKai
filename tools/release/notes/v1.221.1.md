Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.221.1 "All at Once!": your Graveyard any time in YU-GI-OH!, and SURF hits every target together for everyone.

YU-GI-OH!
- Press R at any moment of a duel to look through your Graveyard, and R again for your opponent's. B takes you back to what you were doing.
- On the duel table, A on a Graveyard opens it too.

CO-OP
- Moves that hit several POKéMON at once (SURF, EARTHQUAKE...) now flash, drain the HP bars and faint them all together on every player's screen, not only the room host's.
