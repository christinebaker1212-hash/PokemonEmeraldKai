Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.211.0 "Winter Is Coming!": almost 700 more MAGIC cards play, from Morph and Kicker to Blood Moon and Winter Orb.

MAGIC
- 686 more cards play, 3,593 of the 5,408 in all.
- Echo and Cumulative upkeep: pay each upkeep or the permanent goes.
- Kicker, Buyback and Flashback: the table offers each way to cast a card, and FLASHBACK from your graveyard.
- Morph: cast a card face down as a 2/2 for 3, then TURN FACE UP whenever you can pay. Only you see what your face-down cards are.
- X costs: choose how much X is as you cast, and spells that split damage among several targets (ARC LIGHTNING) let you divide it. Players can be among a spell's targets.
- Lands and creatures that change: BLOOD MOON, LIVING LANDS, KORMUS BELL, CONVERSION, DARKEST HOUR, THRAN LENS and the rest change what a card is and what color it is, and a land that becomes a Mountain makes only red mana.
- WINTER ORB, STATIC ORB, SMOKE and MUNGHA WURM: in your untap step you pick which of your lands or creatures untap.
- Protection from artifacts, from creatures and from creature types, and cards that give it (UNQUESTIONED AUTHORITY, TATTOO WARD, AKROMA'S BLESSING).
- Attack taxes (PROPAGANDA's kind), menace, can't be blocked by more than one (CHARGING RHINO), maximum hand size (SPELLBOOK, THOUGHT NIBBLER), OPHIDIAN, the walls that attack, STAFF OF THE AGES, UPWELLING, FLARING PAIN, and costs that sacrifice, discard, mill or remove counters.
- Mana you have floating shows as discs beside your life.
