Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.162.0 "Ouch!": 29 more MAGIC cards that react to damage and to what's on the table.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- Creatures that leave when something is missing now work: COVETOUS DRAGON without an artifact, EMPEROR CROCODILE
  alone, BARBARIAN OUTCAST without a Swamp, and the sea creatures that need an Island (SEA SERPENT, DANDAN, MANTA RAY,
  PIRATE SHIP...).
- Cards that react when they're dealt damage now work: JACKAL PUP and PHYREXIAN NEGATOR make you pay for it, FUNGUSAUR
  grows, WALL OF ESSENCE and WALL OF HOPE turn it into life.
- Auras like BINDING AGONY and DRUID'S CALL react when the enchanted creature is hurt.
- 29 more cards can be played. Every one has its printed art.
