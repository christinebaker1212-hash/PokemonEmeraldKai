Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.97.0 "Gone Fishin'!": fishing the way it was in RED and BLUE, no reflexes needed.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## FISHING
- **Just cast and wait:** use a ROD and, after a moment, it's either "Oh! A bite!" and the battle starts by itself,
  or "Not even a nibble...". No more watching the dots and pressing A in time.
- **Every bite is landed:** nothing gets away any more, and the GOOD and SUPER RODS no longer make you reel in round
  after round.
- What bites, and how often, is unchanged: each place keeps its own fishing POKéMON, and SUCTION CUPS or STICKY HOLD
  in the lead still bring more bites.
