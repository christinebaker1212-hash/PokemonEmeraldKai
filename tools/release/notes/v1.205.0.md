Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.205.0 "Cards Across the Regions!": the POKéMON card game reaches every region, with nine new booster sets, 67 special card trainers and the sets' own theme decks.

POKéMON CARDS
- Nine new booster sets: Gym Heroes and Gym Challenge in KANTO; Neo Genesis, Neo Discovery, Neo Revelation and Neo Destiny in JOHTO; Expedition, Aquapolis and Skyridge in HOENN, SINNOH and UNOVA. Their packs are sold at the CARD CLUB counters of their regions, and the card players there hand them out as prizes.
- New kinds of cards and rules: Stadiums, POKéMON Tools, Supporters (one a turn), Technical Machines, Darkness and Metal POKéMON and Energy, Baby POKéMON, Shining POKéMON, Light and Dark POKéMON, Crystal POKéMON, POKéMON-ex (worth two Prize cards), Poké-Powers and Poké-Bodies.
- The duel table shows the Stadium in play, each POKéMON's Tool and the turn's Supporter.
- Aquapolis and Skyridge cards have their own e-Card look, with the whole illustration shown.
- 67 special card trainers in JOHTO, KANTO, SINNOH and UNOVA: GYM LEADERS, the ELITE FOUR, CHAMPIONS, rivals and professors, each with their own deck and a Black Star promo card for your first win.
- The sets' own theme decks, card for card: their region's card players bring them, and its CARD CLUB counter sells them for 250 CARD POINTS each, ready to play.
- Beat one of JOHTO's, KANTO's or SINNOH's special card trainers before they had promo cards of their own? Someone from the CARD CLUB will find you and bring them.
- Whenever a card lets a player choose (which Benched POKéMON, which Energy, whether to use a power), the choice is that player's, on the duel table and in the phone app.
- Online card duels need both players on this version.
