Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.108.0 "Downhill All the Way!": CYCLING ROAD's slope, VIRIDIAN's catching lesson and two department stores back as FireRed and Crystal had them.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## KANTO
- **CYCLING ROAD's slope is back.** On your BICYCLE, ROUTE 17 carries you downhill on its own. Hold B to stop, or pedal back up slowly.
- **VIRIDIAN's old man shows you how to catch a POKéMON** once he's had his coffee, just like in FireRed.
- **CELADON DEPT. STORE is FireRed's again:** the TRAINER'S MARKET, TV GAME SHOP, WISE MAN GIFTS and DRUGSTORE, with
  their signs, shoppers and clerks. Each clerk has one list again, no more section menus. The newer items you could buy
  there are still on sale, now on the clerks' lists.

## JOHTO
- **GOLDENROD DEPT. STORE's directory and floor signs are Crystal's,** and its shoppers are back: the boy with the
  POKéGEAR tip, the gentleman, the impatient nerd, the X SPECIAL fan and the IRON fan.
- The SAFARI ZONE gate's clerks now sell what they say they do.
