Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.44.0 "Drop the Beat!": New music can play anywhere in the game, one song or one place at a time.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
Playing online needs everyone on 1.44.0.

## New
- Custom music: songs made with Moemon Music Studio (.kaisong files) play in place of the game's own. Put them in the
  music folder next to the game: mus_route101.kaisong changes that song everywhere, and a song can also change just
  one place, like mus_vs_gym_leader@rustboro_city_gym.kaisong for the battle with ROXANNE, or one scene: the
  BATTLE SIMULATOR, POKéMON TCG, YU-GI-OH! and TRIPLE TRIAD games, the title screen and more. A song changed for one
  place or scene stays the same everywhere else.
- New songs made for everyone come with updates, so all players hear them.
- music\SONG_TABLE.md lists every song in the game and every place it plays: maps, events, battles, the music when a
  trainer spots you, and screens.
- Songs keep everything they were made with: all of their tracks and instruments, even songs made with more than the
  GBA could play at once, their echo, and their loop.
- Walking from a place with its own version of a song to one without it fades over to the other version.
- A friend watching you over SPECTATE hears the song for the place you're in.

## Moemon Music Studio
- SONG > Replaces: pick any song the game plays as music and see every place and scene it plays in. Choose Everywhere,
  one map or one scene.
  Search finds songs by their name or by a place.
- Exporting no longer asks you to pick a song first. The file is named for the song and place you picked.
- Every export also writes SONG_TABLE.md next to it, the same list the game comes with, with your songs in it.
