Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.189.6 "A Black Aura!": ORRE's first Shadow Pokémon is back, RUI calls them out, and they can be snagged.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## ORRE
- PHENAC CITY: the scene in the Mayor's house plays. It was cut off the moment it began, so the Mayor, FOLLY's
  and TRUDLY's battles there and the first Shadow Pokémon of the story never appeared. If your save already
  went past it without seeing it, walk into the Mayor's house again (before PYRITE TOWN's story) and it plays.
- Scenes that begin as you walk into a room no longer get cut off as they start. The Mayor's house was one;
  any other room that opens with a scene is safe from it too.
- Shadow Pokémon of the story's trainers are Shadow Pokémon in battle. Until now they fought as ordinary
  Pokémon: no dark aura, and a ball thrown at them was blocked like any trainer's.
- RUI speaks up when an opponent sends out a Shadow Pokémon, as she does in Colosseum: her words about the
  black aura the first time, and "There's a SHADOW POKéMON!" later on.
- With the SNAG MACHINE, a ball thrown at a trainer's Shadow Pokémon snags it.
