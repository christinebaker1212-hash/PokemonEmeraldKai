Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.13.0 "All Set!": every setting in one OPTIONS screen, and a few new ones.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
You can play online with friends on 1.11.0 or later.

## New
- **OPTIONS in tabs.** START > OPTIONS (and OPTIONS on the title screen) now holds every setting, in the game's own
  options style, in four tabs: L / R switches between them, and a line at the bottom says what each row does.
  - GAME: the original options, TYPE ICONS, NAME CASE, CREATURES, and the new BIKE MUSIC and SURF MUSIC.
  - DISPLAY: fullscreen, window scale, scaling, filter, vsync (what START > SETTINGS had).
  - CONTROLS: controller, pad buttons, stick run, rumble (also from SETTINGS), and the new BUTTON ICONS.
  - CO-OP: friends' screens (with a page for each friend online), the new friends' size, autosave (now in the game,
    not only the settings file), the BATTLE FLAG's range, and the new MOENAV CALLS.
- **BIKE MUSIC / SURF MUSIC.** Turn them off to keep the map's own song on a bike or while surfing; diving then keeps
  the song of the sea above.
- **BUTTON ICONS.** The button pictures in help lines follow the keyboard or controller you used last (AUTO), or always
  show KEYBOARD, XBOX, PLAYSTATION or NINTENDO buttons.
- **MOENAV CALLS.** BATTLES: trainers only call you when they want a battle; no more chat calls. Story calls always
  come.

## Changed
- START no longer has SETTINGS, and the CO-OP menu no longer has SPECTATE: both are in OPTIONS now (nothing was
  removed).
