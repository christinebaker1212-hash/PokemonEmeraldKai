Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 0.4.0 "Grow Together!": co-op EXP, ball effects, and Red's and Crystal's POKé BALLS.

Test build of October 21.

## New
- Co-op EXP at a single battle's pace: everyone levels up and picks moves at the same time.
- Ball entrance and recall effects for every Moemon, MY PRESET 1-3 and APPLY TO PARTY.
- TEAM PADDING (host setting): trainers get one extra Moemon when you team up.
- ANIM SPEED (host setting): battle animations NORMAL, +25% or +30% faster.
- OPTIONS > TYPE ICONS beside each Moemon's name in battle.
- Graphics past the GBA's limits: more sprite colors, more sprites, more room for their art.
- Three more emote pages (MOODS, REACT, HYPE) and 8 pages of custom phrases.
- Walking with a follower makes it happier.
- MoeGatcha: free pulls pile up, and MOEGATCHA TICKETS.
- GENESIS and EPOCH BALLS: the POKé BALLS of Pokémon Red and Crystal, with their own catch rules, throws, art and sounds.

## Fixed
- Waiting for a third player for a Triple Battle no longer ends on a stray B press.
- A stray graphic in the top-left corner of some menus.
