Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.144.0 "Shields Up!": 42 more MAGIC cards that prevent damage.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- "Prevent the next 1 damage that would be dealt to any target this turn" now plays: a shield on a creature or a
  player that soaks up that much damage, used up first, gone at the end of the turn. The table says how much was
  prevented.
- 42 more cards can be played, among them CLERGY EN-VEC, FEMEREF HEALER, ALABASTER WALL, CONSERVATOR, BANDAGE,
  BATTLEFIELD MEDIC and AVEN REDEEMER. Every one has its printed art.
- Your opponents use their healers to save a creature that's about to die, or themselves when an attack could finish
  them.
