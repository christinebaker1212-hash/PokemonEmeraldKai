Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.66.0 "Pass It On!": one TRAINER CARD for every region, made yours with Battle Pass designs.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
Playing online needs everyone on 1.66.0.

## New
- **One TRAINER CARD for every region:** your card is now the same everywhere, with each region's stars on it: one for
  beating its CHAMPION, one for all eight badges, one for all eight shining. Turn the pages with L / R: your PROFILE,
  BADGES & STARS, RECORDS (with the time spent in each region) and TEAM & SIGNATURE. Tilt the card with the right stick
  or the mouse.
- **Battle Pass designs:** pick from 36 Battle Pass designs. The recolorable ones can be turned to any color.
- **MY CARD:** START on your card. Choose the design and its color, an accent color, a frame, what the words sit on
  (LIGHT, DARK, GLASS or none), a holo shine that follows the tilt, a POKéMON to show off big on the front, your title
  and your card's team. Sign your card on the signature pad with the stick, the D-Pad or the mouse. Your card no longer
  changes color by itself: it looks the way you made it.
- **Friends' cards:** a friend's card shows the way they made it, with a page of what the two of you have done together.
- **Polishing badges, by feel:** in the BADGE CASE, press A on a badge to make it chime: the cleaner it is, the higher
  it rings. Hold A and turn the stick round and round to polish it (the D-Pad and the mouse work too). Controllers
  rumble as you rub, and a badge spins when it shines up a level.
- **Spectating:** friends watching you can now see your TRAINER CARD and your BADGE CASE.
