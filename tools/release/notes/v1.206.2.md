Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.206.2 "Fair Shares!": in co-op battles with the EXP CURVE on, everyone's POKéMON now earn EXP at the same pace.

CO-OP
- With LEVEL SCALING's EXP CURVE on, a co-op battle now judges every POKéMON in it by the same level, the higher of the players'. Before, a friend could get a tenth of the room host's EXP in the same battle, even with the same badges and lower levels.
- The trainers you beat together now count toward how far along your own game thinks you are, not only the room host's.
