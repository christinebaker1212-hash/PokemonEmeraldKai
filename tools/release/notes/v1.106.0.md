Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.106.0 "TMs of Old!": RED / BLUE's and Crystal's TMs join the game as TM93-TM139, and JOHTO gives them out where Crystal did.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## New TMs
- **47 new TMs, TM93 to TM139:** every TM from RED / BLUE and Crystal whose move isn't on that number here, like
  MEGA PUNCH, HEADBUTT, CURSE, BODY SLAM, ICE PUNCH, DRAGONBREATH and NIGHTMARE. Every number keeps its old move;
  moves that are already a TM or HM here (SWORDS DANCE, ROCK SMASH, DREAM EATER...) don't get a second one.
- They sit after TM92 in the TMs & HMs POCKET with three-digit numbers, and like every TM here you keep them after use.
- **JOHTO gives them where Crystal did.** The GYM LEADERS hand out Crystal's TMs again: FALKNER's MUD-SLAP, BUGSY's FURY
  CUTTER, CHUCK's DYNAMICPUNCH, PRYCE's ICY WIND and CLAIR's DRAGONBREATH.
- ILEX FOREST's tree-shaker teaches you HEADBUTT again (no more second copy of him), and the teacher in ROUTE 34's
  gate gives SWEET SCENT.
- ROUTE 31's sleepy man gives NIGHTMARE for KENYA's MAIL.
- GOLDENROD DEPT. STORE 5F sells THUNDERPUNCH, FIRE PUNCH and ICE PUNCH, and HEADBUTT once you have it.
- Item balls hold ROLLOUT (ROUTE 35), SWIFT (UNION CAVE), DEFENSE CURL (MT. MORTAR), DETECT (LAKE OF RAGE) and SNORE
  (DARK CAVE). MT. MORTAR's other items are Crystal's again.
- **KANTO:** CELADON DEPT. STORE 2F sells all the RED / BLUE TMs, plus CURSE and ZAP CANNON.

## JOHTO
- **MOOMOO needs seven BERRIES, as in Crystal.** Heal her and the farm gives you TM SNORE. If you already healed her,
  she stays healthy.
- No more CHANSEY beside JOHTO's nurses.
- GOLDENROD's POKéMON CENTER lost the two "gold roof" people. Its old woman asks for EON MAIL, as in Crystal.
- ECRUTEAK's BILL talks about the TIME CAPSULE again.
- OLIVINE lost the SAFARI ZONE news and MARY's fan. Its sailor and boy say Crystal's lines.
- ROUTE 39 lost the HO-OH shadow scene and the "giant PIDGEY" man.
- ROUTE 38 has its TRAINER TIPS sign back.
- OLIVINE GYM lost its cheering crowd.
- BURNED TOWER lost the FIREBREATHER and the BURGLAR.
- OLIVINE CAFE is Crystal's again: its FISHING GURU and STRENGTH sailor say Crystal's words.
