Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.17.0 "A Place for Every Type!": a place in the GARDEN for every type.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
Everyone in a room needs 1.17.0 to play together (the GARDEN changed).

## New
- The MOEMON GARDEN is bigger, and every type has a place of its own there, built from the game's own scenery:
  a cozy nook (NORMAL), a sand training ground (FIGHTING), the open meadow (FLYING), a puddle (POISON), soft
  sand to dig in (GROUND), a plateau of boulders (ROCK) with a lookout over its ledge (DRAGON), tall grass (BUG),
  a dark hollow in the trees (GHOST, DARK), shiny stones and crystals (STEEL), LAVARIDGE's hot spring (FIRE: you
  can walk in too), the pond (WATER), ROUTE 117's flower bed (GRASS), the storm stone (ELECTRIC), a standing stone
  in a ring of flowers (PSYCHIC) and a shaded spring with frost crystals (ICE).
- MOEMON go to their type's place for what they love, and find their way there round the trees, rocks and water.
- Every badge your group holds adds a place: 1 a long grass field, 2 a marsh, 3 a boulder field, 4 a dig field,
  5 more shiny stones, 6 a perch grove, 7 a second flower bed, 8 a stone circle. With friends, the GARDEN shows
  the most badges among you, and grows while they're there.

## Changed
- Decorations you had out in the GARDEN go back to your collection once (the GARDEN's layout is new): put them
  out again wherever you like.

## Fixed
- With a friend in the GARDEN, one of their MOEMON sometimes skipped a turn on your screen (it turned to look at
  something just as it finished a step). Every turn now shows, in order.
