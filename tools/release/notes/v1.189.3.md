Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.189.3 "The Way Out!": ORRE's ways out of town open the map at once.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## ORRE
- PHENAC CITY's ways out open the world map as soon as you step onto them. Before, the west and east ones often
  did nothing until you took another step along them. The same goes for any other way out in ORRE that shares
  its ground with a spot where the story speaks up.
