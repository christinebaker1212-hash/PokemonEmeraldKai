Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.189.9 "On the Air!": ORRE's TVs show their programmes, its music is as loud as the rest of the game's, and a snag ball goes for the Shadow MOéMON.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## ORRE
- The TVs are on. The OUTSKIRT STAND's diner, the houses of PHENAC, PYRITE, AGATE and THE UNDER, and THE UNDER's
  big screens show the programme that is on: the newsreader, the kids' show, VENUS on her stage. Before, every
  screen was dark. The picture is a still of each programme.
- Double Battles: a POKé BALL thrown for a snag goes for the Shadow MOéMON still standing, whichever side it is
  on. Before, with its partner already beaten, the ball flew at the empty spot: RUI stopped you, and a second
  ball that turn could take the wrong one.
- Each of Colosseum's songs now plays as loud as the game's other music. Some were much quieter than the rest,
  some louder. The sounds of a place keep their own balance with its song. Quiet tracks made to be quiet,
  like the wind over the desert, stay quiet.

## Music
- Every custom song was measured again from nothing, to be sure of the levels. They came out the same as
  before, so nothing you hear has changed there.
