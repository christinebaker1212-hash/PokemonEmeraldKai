Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.199.14 "VS!": UNOVA's 3D cut-ins, the VS screens before its big battles, and more of Black's moments.

UNOVA
- Every badge you win is presented as in Black: the badge spins in, the BADGE CASE flies up and it is set into its place.
- Key items arrive with their own cut-in, the LIGHT STONE and DARK STONE among them.
- When your POKéMON clears the way with a field move, it flashes across the screen on Black's speed lines with its cry.
- A Shadow Triad member dashes away across the screen before vanishing.
- The VS screens are back: CHEREN, BIANCA, every Gym Leader, the ELITE FOUR, ALDER, N and GHETSIS face you with their portraits and names before the battle, and Team Plasma's grunts flash their emblem. The battle music starts under it and carries straight on into the battle.
- The ELITE FOUR's rooms make their sounds as their machinery moves.
- At the ABUNDANT SHRINE, LANDORUS can be seen flying in from the sky and landing before you.
- For a day after you become Champion, the gates' news boards welcome your HALL OF FAME team by name.
- Examining a gate's news board brings the view up close so you can read it; turn away to step back.
