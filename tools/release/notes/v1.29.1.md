Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.29.1 "Clean Slate!": taking cards out of a deck and deleting decks, made easy to find.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, settings and cards stay as they are.
1.29.1 plays online with friends on 1.29.0 too.

## New
- When building a deck, SELECT takes one of the card under the cursor out of the deck, right from the cards. You can
  still go RIGHT over to the deck's list and take cards out there with A.
- On the DECKS list, SELECT now says DELETE: it deletes the deck, and asks first. The cards stay in your collection.
- The type tabs show their L and R buttons.

## Fixed
- Deleting the deck you duel with left you with an empty one. The next deck you can play takes over, and the game
  tells you which.
