Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.190.6 "DATA Joins The Tabs!": the POKéDEX entry has a DATA tab beside SIZE, and AREA lists its places on A.

- An entry's tabs are now TEXT, AREA, CRY, SIZE, DATA and CANCEL. DATA takes the place of the R button's MORE: pick it and press A, then LEFT / RIGHT turn its pages and B closes it.
- DATA's pages: PROFILE (type, Ability, gender ratio, EGG groups, how many steps its EGG takes to hatch, catch rate), STATS, EVOLUTION, MOVES BY LEVEL and MOVES BY TM. UP / DOWN scroll the move lists.
- AREA shows the map as it always did. Press A on it for the places by name and how it is found there (grass, water, rod, rocks); A or B goes back to the map.
