Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.76.0 "Triple Triad to Go!": play TRIPLE TRIAD on your phone, with your own cards and decks.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are. Update MOEMON
COMPANION too (it offers the update when you open it).

## MOEMON COMPANION: CARDS
- **TRIPLE TRIAD on the phone:** the new CARDS page plays the game's own TRIPLE TRIAD, with the same rules, the same
  computer players and the same cards, laid out for your phone. Tap a card and then a square, or drag it there.
- **FREE DUEL:** take on a duelist from where you are, under that place's rules. Their hand is matched to yours as in
  the game. Win and a pack goes to your game: three a day (a tough or perfect win earns a SILVER pack). No cards change
  hands on the phone.
- **DAILY PUZZLE:** three cards left and only one or two plays that win. Find one for a SILVER pack, once a day. Everyone
  gets the same puzzle that day.
- **VS FRIENDS:** challenge a friend to a game a turn at a time. Each of you plays when you like, and a notification
  says when it's your turn. Choose the rules when you challenge them.
- **PACKS, DECKS and the BINDER:** open your game's unopened packs on the phone (the cards flip over one by one), build
  and rename decks with the game's deck rules, and browse every card you've found.
- You need your first hand from a card seller in the game before you can play.

## Co-op servers
- Friend duels on the phone use the same co-op server as trades by mail. Nothing changes for server owners.
