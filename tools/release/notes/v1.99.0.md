Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.99.0 "Smooth Moves!": MAGIC duels move your hand and cursor for you, and attacking is clearer.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- **Your hand comes up when you need it**: at the start of your turn, on a card you can play; after you play one, on
  the next (or down to your cards on the table when there's nothing left to play). On your opponent's turn it goes
  down, unless they cast a spell and you have an answer in your hand. A card you draw shows for a moment.
- **B** in your hand puts it away; backing out of choosing a target takes you back to the card.
- **Keywords under the name**: FLYING, HASTE, FIRST STRIKE and the rest show under a card's name on the table and in
  its view, including the ones it has just gained.
- **Choosing attackers is clearer**: creatures that can attack light up, the others dim. A chosen attacker steps
  forward with ATTACK over it and a line to your opponent, and the prompt says how much damage they would deal. A
  creature that can't attack says why. Blocking works the same way, and shows how much damage is still coming.
- The CARD SHOP's regulars go by their names only.
