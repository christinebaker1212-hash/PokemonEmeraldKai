Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.189.7 "Up the Stairs!": PHENAC's stadium can be reached again, ORRE's background sounds stop when they should, and lost lines are back.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## ORRE
- PHENAC CITY: the walk round the fountain is open all the way round, so you can go up the stairs to the
  stadium. An invisible wall cut it on both sides of the stairs, and nothing you did got you past it.
- The same fault is fixed everywhere else in ORRE where a raised walk runs beside a drop: a terrace over a
  basin, a bridge, a balcony.
- A room's background sounds (running water, machines, the wind) end when you leave the room, and when a
  battle begins. Before, they carried on into the next room and under the battle's music. They start again
  when you are back in the room, or the battle is over.
- Twelve lines of dialogue that were missing are spoken again: the songs of the man who cranks the gears in
  PYRITE's windmill, the KIDS GRID song, and a few more that end on a sung note.
- RUI's words when you throw a ball at a POKéMON that isn't a Shadow POKéMON now follow OPTIONS > CREATURES,
  like the rest of ORRE's dialogue. They always said POKéMON before.
