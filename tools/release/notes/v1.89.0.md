Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.89.0 "Old Friends Return!": more of FireRed's KANTO and Crystal's JOHTO come back.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## KANTO, AS IN FIRERED
- **Your rival's name:** in PROF. OAK's lab you name his grandson, as in FireRed, and KANTO's people call him by it.
  Online, everyone sees the host's rival name.
- **POKéMON TOWER:** with the SILPH SCOPE, the MAROWAK on 6F is a ghost again: it can't be caught, and a thrown
  POKé BALL is dodged.
- **FIVE ISLAND:** SELPHY's wish is back. She asks to see a certain POKéMON, and once you've walked with it for a
  while she thanks you with a gift.
- **ICEFALL CAVE:** the thin ice cracks under your feet, and cracked ice gives way, dropping you to the floor below.

## JOHTO, AS IN CRYSTAL
- **The GS BALL:** once you've beaten JOHTO's LEAGUE, GOLDENROD's POKéMON CENTER has a gift waiting for you at the
  door. KURT keeps the BALL for a day to look at it, and ILEX FOREST's shrine calls CELEBI only once.
- **Your rival after the LEAGUE:** he waits at the INDIGO PLATEAU on Mondays and Wednesdays for a battle, and
  trains in DRAGON'S DEN on Tuesdays and Thursdays.
- **LAKE OF RAGE:** the RED GYARADOS is red again.
- **MAHOGANY TOWN:** the old man talks about the MAGIKARP coming back only once the hideout is cleared.
- **No more camera pans:** the odd views on ROUTE 29 and in UNION CAVE that played every time you stepped on them
  are gone.
- The SAFARI ZONE warden's "SAFARI LEVEL" errand, which could never start, is gone.

## BETWEEN THE REGIONS
- **VICTORY ROAD gate:** you no longer walk your whole team from JOHTO into KANTO. The gate is customs, like the
  ports: once both LEAGUES are beaten, you pick the POKéMON who comes along, and the rest wait in the PC.
