Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.96.0 "A New Look at MAGIC!": MAGIC's cards, screens and table now look and play like the other card games'.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- **Every card has its picture now**, the art of its first printing from the game's classic years, in a frame
  colored by the card's color. A card's page names the artist, and A shows the picture big.
- **The CARD CASE's MAGIC screens work like the other card games' screens**: your deck's ACE CARD on the first page,
  DECKS with RENAME, ACE CARD, COPY and DELETE, DECK RECIPES to build the starter decks again from your cards, a
  BINDER of cards by color (SELECT shows every card), NEW on cards you just got, and a PRACTICE DUEL.
- **HOW TO PLAY**: press SELECT on MAGIC's first page for a short guide, page by page.
- **The duel table is laid out like the POKEMON card table**: a cloth mat, your hand on the tray at the bottom that
  slides up while you choose a card, your life and the cards' power and toughness on plates, your library and
  graveyard at the side. Your opponent's cards face them. Spells on the stack show in the middle with a line to
  what they aim at, and your opponent's spells show big as they're cast.
- **B opens the turn's menu** (PASS, END TURN). A on your library lets you concede; A on a graveyard shows its cards.
- **DUEL SPEED** works for MAGIC too.
- **A booster pack is opened card by card**, the rare ones with a shine.
- The regulars tell you what they think before a duel, and you pick which deck to duel with.

## THE CARD CASE
- **L and R go round the games in a fixed order**: POKEMON, YU-GI-OH!, MAGIC, then TRIPLE TRIAD, always last.
