Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.59.0 "Brains at the Table!": The Frontier Brains now play cards outside their facilities.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, cards and decks stay as they are.

## Card games
- Beat a Frontier Brain in their facility and win its silver symbol, and you'll find them outside its door with
  their deck:
  - ANABEL by the BATTLE TOWER;
  - TUCKER by the BATTLE DOME;
  - SPENSER by the BATTLE PALACE;
  - GRETA by the BATTLE ARENA;
  - NOLAND by the BATTLE FACTORY;
  - LUCY by the BATTLE PIKE.
- About half of the Brains you've beaten are out on any day, and the lineup changes every day.
- Press the CARD CASE's button in front of one to ask for a POKéMON card duel or a YU-GI-OH! duel.
