Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.132.0 "Pedal Power!": SINNOH's BICYCLE rides like Platinum's, and people walking away fade out.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## SINNOH's BICYCLE
- In SINNOH the BICYCLE has Platinum's two gears again: press B to switch between 3rd and 4th gear (a click tells you). 4th rides faster, 3rd is easier to handle, and the bike stops as soon as you let go.
- Muddy bike slopes can only be ridden up in 4th gear.
- The narrow bike bridges on ROUTES 210, 212, 227 and 228 are for bikes only: ride them straight along, never on foot, and you can't get off in the middle.
- ROUTE 227's ramps jump the bike over them.

## SINNOH
- People who walk away after a talk or a battle now fade out instead of vanishing while still in view. The same goes for people leaving the edge of the screen.
- ROUTE 211's high bridge can no longer be climbed onto from the river bank underneath it.
