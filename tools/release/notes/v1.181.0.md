Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.181.0 "ORRE Comes Alive!": ORRE's water flows, its machines turn, and SINNOH's JOURNAL lets you go.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## ORRE
- **Moving scenery:** everything Colosseum animates in its places now moves: PHENAC CITY's water flows, and wheels,
  fans, signs and machines across ORRE turn and slide as they did on the GameCube.

## SINNOH
- **The JOURNAL** shown when you continue no longer leaves you unable to move after you close it.
