Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.187.0 "ORRE Finds Its Voice!": ORRE has its music and sounds.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## ORRE
- ORRE is no longer silent. Every place plays its own song from Colosseum, with the wind over the OUTSKIRT STAND and
  the sound effects its scenes call for.
- The music goes through a GBA sound filter, so it sits with the rest of the game's songs, and it is as loud as a
  HOENN town.
- The song keeps playing through your menus, waits while you battle, and stops when you sail away.
