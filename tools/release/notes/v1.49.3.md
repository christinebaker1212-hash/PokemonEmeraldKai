Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.49.3 "Look Before You Take!": R2 shows a card up close on every card screen, and promo cards stay one of a kind.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, settings and cards stay as they are.

## Fixed
- R2 (RT, or D on the keyboard) shows a card up close on the screen where you pick a card from a beaten player's
  deck, in POKéMON cards and Yu-Gi-Oh!, and on the booster reveal, the Starter Deck choice and the CARD CLUB counter
  too, like START.
- POKéMON cards: a trainer's promo card can no longer be picked from their deck after a win. It comes only as their
  gift the first time you beat them, so it can't be taken twice.
