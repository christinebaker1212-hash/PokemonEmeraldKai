Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.71.0 "Tick Tock!": the POKéTCH on your phone, a new POKéDEX, and POKéMON across boxes.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MOEMON COMPANION
- **The POKéTCH on your phone:** the new DEVICE tab shows the game's own POKéTCH, live. Touch its screen to use the
  app, and press the button on its side (or swipe) for the next one: the watch, the calculator, the MEMO PAD, the
  PEDOMETER, the MOVE TESTER, the MARKING MAP, the DOWSING MACHINE and every other app you've unlocked, exactly as in
  the game, in its COLOR CHANGER colors. It shows once you have it, in SINNOH (everywhere once SINNOH's LEAGUE is
  beaten).
- **A new POKéDEX:** cards with each POKéMON's picture, number, name and type colors, a search bar (type a name or a
  number), and filters for ALL, SEEN, OWNED and MISSING. Each entry has a large picture, its category, height,
  weight and footprint, the entries from RUBY, SAPPHIRE, EMERALD and FIRERED, and its base stats.
- **WHERE TO FIND:** every entry lists the places it lives and how (grass, SURF, ROCK SMASH, each ROD) with its
  chance; tap a place to open its SPAWNS. Rare ones stay secret until you've met them there.
- **Across boxes:** carry a POKéMON to a box's arrow and the next box turns, as in the game's PC; MOVE also stays on
  while you swipe or pick another box.
