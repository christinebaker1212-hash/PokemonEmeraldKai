Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.199.0 "Lights Down, Friends Up!": a new CO-OP menu that keeps the field in view, quicker emotes and A-Life picks, and a DARK theme for every window.

The CO-OP menu
- The CO-OP menu opens over the field like the START menu: your room at the top left (who else is here, where they are, who's in a battle), the menu at the right, a line of help along the bottom.
- Its rows sit in three tabs, PEOPLE, PLAY and ME (L / R or left / right): FRIENDS, SOCIAL, TRADE, SHARED PC; TOURNAMENT, MOEGATCHA, DAILY, BERRY GAMES; LOOK, MOVE TUTOR, SERVER. Each row has its own item's picture and a line of news: friends online, pulls waiting, today's goals done, HEART SCALES on hand, your room's CODE.
- FRIENDS lists everyone in the room with their face, name and where they are; pick one for GO TO, TRAINER CARD, their SECRET BASE and card games. TRADE and PRANK ask who from the same list.
- SOCIAL, SHARED PC, TOURNAMENT and BERRY GAMES open beside the room, the field still in view; the longer pages (DAILY, the CHAT LOG, the SHARED PC's ITEMS) open wide.
- Every co-op screen has the same look: its title in the game's own large font, the picked row lit, and the buttons at the right of the help line.

Emotes and phrases
- Hold LT for emotes, RT for phrases, as before: now the four choices sit around you, one for each direction. Emotes show as their pictures; phrases as speech balloons.
- L / R turn the pages of what you opened (the four emote pages, or your phrases and the ready-made ones, shown as dots).

Your POKéMON
- Holding A in front of your POKéMON puts its actions around it: PET, PLAY, FEED A BERRY and TALK; PRAISE, COMFORT, GROOM and SIT WITH; WATCH QUIETLY and GIVE A KEEPSAKE.
- What it thinks of what you did shows on the place's own sign at the top of the screen, then slides away: no box to click through. It stays with you while the sign is up, and A again does something else.

The DARK theme
- New in OPTIONS > DISPLAY: THEME, DEFAULT or DARK.
- DARK turns every window dark: the START menu, messages, YES / NO, shops, the PC, OPTIONS, the BAG, the CO-OP screens, and the menus in battle.
- Each of the 29 window frames has its own dark version that keeps its look (its colors and pattern at dusk), the message box's edges stay quiet, and colored text stays bright and easy to read. Button pictures in the help lines turn light to stand out.

Fixes
- Your POKéMON's memories and moods could load as blank if the game was reloaded right after saving.
