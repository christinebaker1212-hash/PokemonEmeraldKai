Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.69.1 "Who's on Top?": JUBILIFE TV's rankings machines, filled by your friends.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## SINNOH
- **JUBILIFE TV's ranking rooms:** the six record machines on the third floor work as in Platinum: BATTLE TOWER
  streaks, POKéMON caught, defeated, hatched and fished, CONTEST wins and RIBBONS, with your place among everyone you
  have mixed records with. The GROUP room keeps to the friends in your CO-OP room.
- **Mixing records with friends** now brings their rankings along. You can delete anyone else's entry with SELECT.

## Fixes
- **SINNOH's SUPER CONTESTS** now count toward your contest wins, entries and ribbons in your records.
