Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.116.0 "Music Box!": SINNOH's last missing songs, and the VILLA's furniture comes to life.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## SINNOH's music
- The Underground has its own song now, as in Platinum, instead of OREBURGH MINE's.
- The VILLA's music box plays its own little tune when you look at the rack it sits on. Press B to stop it and the VILLA's song comes back.
- ROUTE 201's first scene and the lab play Platinum's opening song, and ROUTE 202's catching lesson plays its own song.

## The VILLA
- Every piece of furniture you buy now stands in the VILLA. Most pieces were bought but never showed up.
- Each piece says what it should when you look at it: the bed, the piano, the audio system, the clock and the rest.
