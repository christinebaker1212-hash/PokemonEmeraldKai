Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.48.2 "Shuffle Up!": Yu-Gi-Oh! Decks are shuffled again before every duel.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, settings and cards stay as they are.
Playing online works with 1.44.0 and later, but card games with friends need everyone on 1.48.2.

## Fixed
- Yu-Gi-Oh!: Decks weren't shuffled at all, so every duel opened with the same hand and drew the same cards. Every
  duel now shuffles both Decks, so your opening hand and draws are different each time.
