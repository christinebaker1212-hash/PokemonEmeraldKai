Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.4.1 "Seven Letters Strong!": full 7-letter names no longer crash the CO-OP menu.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## Fixed
- **Players with 7-letter names (like SATOSHI or TAKESHI) could crash the CO-OP menu or show garbled text.** A name
  that fills all 7 letters has no room for its end mark, and three places forgot to add one: the list of players at
  the bottom of the CO-OP menu, "from NAME" in the SHARED PC, and "CAUGHT BY NAME" in the POKéDEX. The text ran on
  into whatever came after it, which showed as junk or, with four players online, crashed the game. All three now
  end the name properly.
