Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.50.0 "Clean Hits!": the VS cabinet's fights look sharp and even, follow your display settings, and their music never drowns out the fighters.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## VS cabinet: the picture
- Fighters, stages and the menus are drawn with even pixels: no more lines drawn twice as thick as the rest, no
  more letters missing strokes, and no more pixels crawling across a fighter as the camera moves.
- Stage floors slide smoothly with the camera instead of breaking into steps.
- Spinning and turning moves are drawn smoothly too.
- A fight now follows OPTIONS > DISPLAY like the rest of the game: PIXEL PERFECT, SHARP FIT and STRETCH, and the
  scanlines and LCD grid filters.

## VS cabinet: the sound
- A stage's music always stays a little quieter than the fighters' voices, songs you add to the music folder
  included.
- The fight's sounds and songs now use the GBA's own sound quality, like the rest of the game, and the fight's
  files take a third less space.

## Fixed
- Chun-Li's and Dhalsim's endings play their music again.
