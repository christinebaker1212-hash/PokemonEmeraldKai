Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.53.0 "Learn the Ropes!": a HOW TO PLAY guide for every card game.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## Card games
- New: HOW TO PLAY. Press SELECT on the CARD CASE's first screen of any of the three card games for a short guide to
  its rules, with each rule acted out by the game's own cards: winning, setting up, a turn, attacking and damage,
  evolving, summoning, chains, capturing in TRIPLE TRIAD, SAME, PLUS, COMBO, the rules of HOENN and more.
- TRIPLE TRIAD: the light that picks who goes first now stops right on that player's name (it used to jump to it at
  the end), and its clicking slows down with it and stops when it does.
- The cursor in the CARD CASE's menu and on the Decks, Binder and Booster pages sits 1 pixel lower, centered on what
  it points at.
