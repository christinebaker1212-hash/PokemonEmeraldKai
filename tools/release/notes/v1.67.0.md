Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.67.0 "Platinum Proper!": SINNOH runs on Platinum's own story, words and events; KANTO and JOHTO get their HMs, tutors and water back.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## SINNOH
- **Platinum's own story:** SINNOH's people, cutscenes and events now play Platinum's own scripts and words, from the
  briefcase on ROUTE 201 and BARRY's first battle to the gym leaders, the tag battles and the walking partners who
  follow you from map to map. Platinum's own menus, music cues and camera moves come with them.
- **Super Contests:** the CONTEST HALL in HEARTHOME is open: dress-up, dance and acting rounds, the FASHION CASE and
  its accessories, and the POFFIN HOUSE. POKé BALL effects take the place of Seals.
- **SINNOH's TV:** all nine programs air on Platinum's schedule, with news about what you've been up to, the
  JUBILIFE TV dress-up studio, and the ROAMER NEWS FLASH.
- **The Underground:** dig for fossils, spheres and plates with the EXPLORER KIT on Platinum's own digging screen,
  and help bring SPIRITOMB to ROUTE 209's HALLOWED TOWER.
- **The roaming legendaries:** MESPRIT flies off from VERITY CAVERN, CRESSELIA leaves FULLMOON ISLAND, and PROF.
  OAK in ETERNA sets MOLTRES, ZAPDOS and ARTICUNO loose, all roaming SINNOH's routes as in Platinum. They keep the
  damage you deal, and beaten ones return after the HALL OF FAME.
- **BERRY patches:** all 118 of Platinum's soil patches grow BERRIES, starting with the ones Platinum plants there.
- **Getting around:** IRON ISLAND's and the POKéMON LEAGUE's platform lifts, the elevators and their attendants,
  and the GREAT MARSH's quick tram all run as in Platinum.
- **Puzzles and places:** ETERNA's clock gym, PASTORIA's water gym and SUNYSHORE's turning bridges, the REGI ruins'
  dots and the statue's quake, the OLD CHATEAU's ROTOM, the UNOWN letters in SOLACEON's ruins (in their own
  alphabet), ROTOM's room, JUBILIFE TV's lottery and the VEILSTONE DEPARTMENT STORE's regulars.
- DAWN and LUCAS have their own overworld sprites.

## KANTO and JOHTO
- **CERULEAN CITY:** the tree east of the city can be CUT, so ROUTE 9 and ROCK TUNNEL can be reached.
- **HMs:** KANTO's HM givers hand over their HMs as in FireRed (the S.S. ANNE's CAPTAIN, ROUTE 16's house, the
  SAFARI ZONE's secret house, the WARDEN, OAK's AIDE, the EMBER SPA, ICEFALL CAVE). JOHTO's do the same as in Crystal
  (ILEX FOREST, CIANWOOD, the DANCE THEATER, OLIVINE's cafe, SPROUT TOWER, ROUTE 36, ICE PATH).
- **No HM twice:** a giver whose HM you already carry from another region says so instead of handing over a copy.
- **Field moves:** CUT, ROCK SMASH, STRENGTH and WHIRLPOOL now need the region's own badge and a POKéMON that knows
  the move (or holds its HM), as in the original games. Trees could be cut at the very start of a new game before.
- **Move tutors:** KANTO's move tutors are back as in FireRed: MEGA PUNCH and MEGA KICK on ROUTE 4, and DOUBLE-EDGE,
  THUNDER WAVE, ROCK SLIDE, EXPLOSION, DREAM EATER, SOFTBOILED, SUBSTITUTE, SWORDS DANCE, SEISMIC TOSS, METRONOME and
  BODY SLAM in their places.
- **PEWTER CITY:** PROF. OAK's aide brings the RUNNING SHOES after you beat BROCK.
- **VERMILION CITY:** the market stalls selling MEGA STONES, Z-CRYSTALS and RARE CANDY whatever your GENERATION
  setting are gone (CELADON DEPT. STORE sells them when your settings allow).
- **Your rival in JOHTO:** as in Crystal, you name him when the officer at ELM's lab asks (left empty, he's SILVER);
  online, the room's host names him for everyone. He takes the starter strong against yours, and his teams bring it.
- **MOM's savings:** as in Crystal, MOM offers to save money when you set off from NEW BARK TOWN; while she does, a
  quarter of your winnings in JOHTO and KANTO goes to her, and at home you can GET or SAVE money with her.
- **Water and doors:** KANTO's and JOHTO's water, shorelines and flowers animate, and KANTO's doors open and close as
  in FireRed, one tile wide.
- **Ladders:** climbing down a ladder leaves you standing in front of the ladder, not on it.

## Every region
- **Morning, day and night:** as in SINNOH, the grass of JOHTO, KANTO and HOENN holds different POKéMON in the
  morning (4:00-9:59) and at night (20:00-3:59): JOHTO's and KANTO's as in Crystal, HOENN's own night owls and early birds.
- **Legendaries at home:** the legendary POKéMON JOHTO and KANTO borrowed from other regions are gone: each region's
  are found in their own region (the island events of the original games, NAVEL ROCK, BIRTH ISLAND and FARAWAY
  ISLAND, stay as they were). The plates are SINNOH's too.
- **BADGES:** the continue screen and the SAVE window count the badges of every region, not only HOENN's.
- **Gyms:** beating a KANTO, JOHTO or SINNOH gym leader counts the gym's other trainers as beaten, as in HOENN.
- **Each region's device:** the MOéNAV is on START in HOENN; once HOENN's ELITE FOUR is beaten it comes along to the
  other regions as a key item you can register to SELECT. The POKéTCH works outside SINNOH once SINNOH's ELITE FOUR
  is beaten.
- **RETURN TO MAIN MENU:** a new choice in OPTIONS > GAME. It offers to save first; if you'd rather not, it asks
  once more, then takes you back to the title screen.
- **Shadows:** fade out with the screen, so a fade to white or black no longer leaves them behind.
- **Followers:** a POKéMON walking behind you through tall grass is no longer drawn over you, its reflection in water
  no longer has a shadow of its own, and your shadow no longer goes missing after some map changes.
