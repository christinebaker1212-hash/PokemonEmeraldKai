Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.69.2 "Fair Trade!": SINNOH's trades, the move deleter, fossils and prizes, as in Platinum.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## SINNOH
- **In-game trades:** KAZZA the ABRA in OREBURGH, CHARAP the CHATOT in ETERNA, GASPAR the HAUNTER in SNOWPOINT and
  FOPPA the MAGIKARP at the MEISTER's house, each with Platinum's own stats, held item and Trainer.
- **CANALAVE's move deleter** makes your POKéMON forget a move, as in Platinum.
- **The SHARD move tutors** on ROUTE 212, in the SURVIVAL AREA and in SNOWPOINT CITY teach Platinum's 38 moves for
  RED, BLUE, YELLOW and GREEN SHARDS.
- **The OREBURGH MINING MUSEUM** revives your fossils at Lv. 20: pick one, come back later, give it a nickname.
- **VEILSTONE's PRIZE EXCHANGE:** all 19 of Platinum's prizes at Platinum's prices, in a list that scrolls and
  remembers where you were.
- **The GAME CORNER's clerk** sells COINS, and **FLOAROMA MEADOW's HONEY seller** sells HONEY one or ten at a time.
- **DR. FOOTSTEP**, the ribbon people and others who read your POKéMON's nature, moves or ribbons now answer as in
  Platinum. SINNOH's own ribbons are kept with your save.

## Changes
- **The COIN CASE holds 50,000 COINS** (it was 9,999), in every region. The slot machines and roulette show the
  extra digit.
