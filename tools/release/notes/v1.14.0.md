Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.14.0 "Dex Deep Dive!": the POKéDEX tells you everything four games had to say.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
You can play online with friends on 1.11.0 or later.

## New
- **POKéDEX entries from RUBY, SAPPHIRE, EMERALD and FIRERED.** A Moemon's page now holds every entry those games
  wrote about it, as one long entry (one that says exactly what an earlier game said is shown only once). The page
  shows the beginning, with a small arrow when there's more. The new TEXT button at the top (where the cursor starts)
  opens it: UP / DOWN scroll, B is done, and then UP / DOWN go to the next Moemon again. AREA, CRY and SIZE are one
  step to the right and work as before. The page that appears after a catch keeps EMERALD's entry.
