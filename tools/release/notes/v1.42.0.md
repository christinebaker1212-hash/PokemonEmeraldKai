Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.42.0 "Hop to It!": the DANCE machine gets a hop in its step.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
Playing online needs everyone on 1.42.0.

## New
- You hop up onto the DANCE machine's pad before a credit starts and hop back down after it, with the ledge sound.
  Waiting for a friend, you wait up on the pad; a friend who says YES hops up onto the other one.
- On the pad you're seen dancing: two arrows at once make a little hop, and you turn toward the arrows you step on
  (LEFT, RIGHT, UP). Friends watching see it too.
- The machine's screen switches on and off like an old TV, the same way the VS cabinet's does.
- After a credit you say how it went, from your grades: from "That was easy!" to "My legs feel like jelly…".

## Fixed
- The machine's rails now stand in front of you while you're up on the pad, instead of you standing on top of them.
