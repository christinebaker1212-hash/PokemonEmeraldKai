Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.31.1 "Out Of Sight!": the FRIEND FINDER, a quieter way to see where your friends are.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, settings and cards stay as they are.
1.31.1 plays online with friends on 1.31.0 too.

## New
- FRIEND FINDER: the arrows toward your friends stay out of the way until you want them. Click the right stick
  (G on the keyboard) to show them, and again to hide them. The game remembers which you picked. A friend's
  "look here!" ping always shows.
- The arrows are smaller, see-through and still. One stands out for a moment when its friend goes off somewhere,
  then settles back. A friend less than 15 steps away gets no arrow at all.
- A radar in the top right corner shows a dot in each friend's color: the closer the dot is to the middle, the
  closer they are.
- Everything fades while you walk and comes back when you stop.
- OPTIONS > CO-OP > FRIEND FINDER: BOTH, ARROWS or RADAR.
