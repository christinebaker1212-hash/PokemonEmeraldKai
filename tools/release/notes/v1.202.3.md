Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.202.3 "Hop to It!": RED and CRYSTAL's message boxes and party POKéMON, the way the Game Boy games did them.

UI STYLE
- RED and CRYSTAL: a message too long for the box's two lines no longer squeezes three lines together. The box keeps its usual two lines, waits with its arrow at the third, and A scrolls it up, as on the Game Boy.
- RED: in the POKéMON list only the one under the cursor moves, as in Red. VOLTORB-style and OMANYTE-style icons shake by a pixel, and the rest step through their own two pictures.
- CRYSTAL: the POKéMON under the cursor hops up and down, by 2 pixels with plenty of HP, 1 when it's low, and stays still in the red, as in Crystal. Every icon's two pictures sit exactly where Crystal draws them.
