Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.45.0 "Same Table, Three Games!": the POKéMON, Yu-Gi-Oh! and Triple Triad card games share their buttons, cursor and look.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, settings and cards stay as they are.
Playing online works with 1.44.0, 1.44.1 and 1.45.0.

## Changed
- One button looks at a card up close in all three card games: R2 on a controller (the right trigger), D on the
  keyboard. R only turns pages now, so it never does two things at once, like in the Triple Triad deck builder.
  The hints on screen show the right button.
- POKéMON and Yu-Gi-Oh! duels: CONCEDE is no longer in the B menu, so it can't be picked by mistake. Move onto your
  own deck and press A to concede. Hovering over your deck shows how many cards are left in it, in your hand and in
  your discard pile.
- The Yu-Gi-Oh! table uses the same cursor as the POKéMON table: white corners that step in and out, yellow while
  you're choosing a target. Numbered picks look the same in both.
- Challenge screens show the opponent's trainer picture from battles, framed in the window beside their card.
  People without one show the trainer they look most like. Friends show the picture of their look.
- Triple Triad: the deck's card on the CARD CASE's first screen floats like the other two games' cards, over a shadow.

## New
- POKéMON card duels: attacks land with a flash and a burst, a Knock Out breaks the card apart, and a Pokémon put into
  play rises in a ring of light, like Yu-Gi-Oh! monsters do. Duels don't take any longer.

## Fixed
- POKéMON card duels: the outlines of the spaces where cards go looked broken and blotchy. They're clean, even lines
  now, with each space set a little into the mat.
