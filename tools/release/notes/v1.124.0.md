Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.124.0 "Back From the Brink!": MAGIC's regeneration, 72 more cards in all.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- Regeneration plays: DRUDGE SKELETONS, the TROLLS, UNWORTHY DEAD, CLAY STATUE, RESTLESS DEAD and more. Pay to regenerate a creature and the next time it would be destroyed this turn, it's tapped, taken out of combat and healed instead.
- A creature ready to regenerate shows a green shield in its corner; when it saves itself, the table says so.
- Cards that say it can't be regenerated, like WRATH OF GOD and TERROR, get past the shield.
- Your opponents regenerate their creatures when they're about to be destroyed, in a fight or by your spells.
- MARLOW's deck now has DRUDGE SKELETONS.
- 1542 cards play now, all with their printed art.
