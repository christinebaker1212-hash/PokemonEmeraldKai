Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.62.0 "Here Comes a New Challenger!": the GAME CENTER's 3F has five VS cabinets, with four CAPCOM arcade fighters.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
1.62.0 plays online with friends on 1.61.0 to 1.61.7 too; the VS cabinets need both players on 1.62.0.

## New
- **Five VS cabinets on the GAME CENTER's 3F.** The back-to-back row in the middle of the floor is now five VS
  cabinets with a seat on each side: the west seat plays 1P, the east seat 2P. Each cabinet says which game it is
  when you sit down. Sit and wait for a friend, or play alone.
- **Four real CAPCOM arcade games:** SUPER STREET FIGHTER II X, VAMPIRE SAVIOR, CYBERBOTS and STREET FIGHTER ALPHA 3.
  They run from your own copies of their arcade ROM sets: put the zips in the new arcade folder next to the game (the
  note in it, and VS CABINETS in README.txt, list the files). A cabinet whose set is missing tells you which files it
  needs. START puts in a coin and starts; hold START for 2 seconds to stand up.
- **Play them online.** A friend at the other seat plays you on their own PC, kept smooth the same way as the fights
  you already know. Both of you need the game's set.
- **SPECTATE:** friends who have the set watch your game in their panel; friends who don't see a blank screen that
  says CONNECTING... until you're done.

## Changed
- The fighting game cabinet from MAUVILLE's GAME CORNER moved to the GAME CENTER: it's the fourth VS cabinet, "A weird
  bootleg". MAUVILLE's two machines are slot machines again.
- The two gamers on the GAME CENTER's 3F stand beside the cabinets now, so every seat is free.
