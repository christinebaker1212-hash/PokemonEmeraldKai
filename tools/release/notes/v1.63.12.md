Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.63.12 "Nobody Waiting!": the nurse no longer says someone with a name of jumbled letters is playing.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
1.63.12 plays online with friends on 1.63.7 to 1.63.11 too.

## Fixed
- **POKéMON CENTER nurses:** after healing, a nurse could say "It appears as if" a player whose name was jumbled
  letters "is playing right now". She was asking about the wireless UNION ROOM, which isn't in this game, and read
  nonsense as a name. It happened once per save, most often on a game played in JOHTO. Nobody is ever said to be
  waiting now.
- **POKéMON CENTERS:** each visit to one no longer takes a little memory the game never gave back.
