Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.36.0 "Duel Your Friends!": Yu-Gi-Oh! duels and trades online, the GAME CENTER's Yu-Gi-Oh! counter.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, settings and cards stay as they are.
Playing online needs everyone on 1.36.0.

## New
- Yu-Gi-Oh! with friends: CO-OP > FRIENDS > a friend > CARD DUEL or CARD TRADE, with LEFT / RIGHT picking MOEMON or
  YU-GI-OH!. The CARD CASE button in front of a friend asks for the game whose side the case was on last. Both pick
  a deck and duel, each from their own side of the table; the winner gets a booster and nothing is lost. Trades
  work like POKEMON card trades: each offers a card, both confirm.
- No Yu-Gi-Oh! deck yet? Asked for a Yu-Gi-Oh! duel or trade with only POKEMON cards, you pick a Starter Deck to keep
  first, then play. With Yu-Gi-Oh! cards but no deck ready to duel, you borrow a Starter Deck for that duel.
- The GAME CENTER CARD SHOP's second clerk has finished unpacking: a Yu-Gi-Oh! counter with singles for money (any
  card that can be played, one copy each), the four Starter Decks (one of each), and the same TRADE IN and CARD
  SHOP as the CARD CLUB. Anyone without a Yu-Gi-Oh! deck gets set up first.

## Changed
- The CARD CLUB member now stands behind the POKEMON MART counter, beside the shopkeeper, in OLDALE and MAUVILLE.
  In LILYCOVE's DEPARTMENT STORE both card players are behind the counter.
