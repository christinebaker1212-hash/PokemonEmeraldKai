Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.7.0: the SHARED PC, rebuilt like the PC's boxes.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
Play online only with friends on 1.7.0 too (their game updates itself the same way). If you run the standalone server
on another computer, replace it with the one in this release: it keeps what was already in its SHARED PC (the
Moemon move into the first shared box, the items stay).

## New
- **SHARED PC: MOEMON or ITEMS.** The co-op menu's SHARED PC asks MOEMON, ITEMS or CANCEL.
- **14 shared boxes of 30**, in the PC's own box screen: move, SUMMARY, MARK, RELEASE, TAG groups, box names and
  wallpapers (everyone shares them; a friend's rename or new wallpaper shows up on your screen right away).
  - Friends in the same box show up as a hand in their color with their name just above it, carrying whatever they
    hold. A Moemon somebody is holding can't be taken by anyone else.
  - Along the top: PARTY, MY PC (your own boxes, and back again; a Moemon you're holding comes along) and CLOSE.
  - A Moemon's menu, and the TAG menu, have SEND TO PC / SEND TO SHARED: straight into your own boxes or the shared
    ones, one Moemon or a whole tagged group.
  - Anyone can release a shared Moemon ("Release LUNA's PIKACHU?"); the candy goes to whoever releases it.
- **Shared ITEMS** as a PC item list: the shared items and your own PC's (L/R to switch). DEPOSIT FROM BAG puts items
  in; on an item: WITHDRAW (to your BAG), SEND TO PC / SEND TO SHARED, CANCEL. TAG (X/Square on a controller, F on the
  keyboard) marks several items to move them all at once. Friends' names show on the row they're looking at. Key
  items and HMs stay with you.
