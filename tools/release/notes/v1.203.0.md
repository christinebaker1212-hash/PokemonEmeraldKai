Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.203.0 "Close Ranks!": foes in Double and Triple Battles move together as their numbers shrink.

BATTLES
- In Double and Triple Battles, when a foe faints and nobody takes its place (or is caught), the foes left slide together to the spots that many POKéMON would have from the start: two take the Double Battle's places, one moves to the centre.
- Their HP boxes close the gap at the same time, rising into the empty place, in every UI STYLE.
- Move animations, POKé BALLS and effects follow the POKéMON to their new places, and everyone in a co-op battle sees the same thing.
