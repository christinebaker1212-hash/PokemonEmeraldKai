Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.198.0 "New Scenery for Every Battle!": UNOVA's and SINNOH's battles now play in their own game's backgrounds, and UNOVA catches POKéMON the way Black does.

UNOVA
- Battles in UNOVA take place in Black's own scenery: each place's sky, land and platforms, matching the ground you stand on and the season. Outdoor scenes darken at night as the day turns.
- The battle's opening slide runs smoothly across the scenery.
- Catching follows Black's rules: the odds by ball and by how many POKéMON you have caught, and the rare critical capture, when the ball shakes only once and the POKéMON is caught.
- Dark grass is back to its old tricks: often two wild POKéMON leap out at once for a Double Battle (as long as you have two POKéMON able to fight).

SINNOH
- Battles in SINNOH take place in Platinum's own backgrounds, with its platforms: grass, sand, snow, mud, ice, caves, water and every building, by day, at twilight and at night.

Everywhere
- The new scenery only changes what you see: Double and Triple Battles, co-op battles, joining a friend's battle and every move work exactly as before.
