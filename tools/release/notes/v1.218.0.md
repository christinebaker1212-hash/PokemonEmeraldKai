Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.218.0 "From the Grave!": every YU-GI-OH! effect you're allowed to use can now be used.

YU-GI-OH!
- Cards with an effect you use from the Graveyard can be used again: when one of your cards there can do something, your Graveyard is framed in blue. Open it and the cursor waits on that card; A uses it.
- The same works for cards Banished face-up and in your Extra Deck.
- Monsters that Special Summon themselves from the Deck (the Awakening of the Possessed monsters and others) can be summoned: press A on your Deck and choose them above CONCEDE.
- Effects meant for the start of a phase are no longer skipped for you. The WITCHCRAFTER Spells returning to your hand in the End Phase, Golden Land Traps' Graveyard effects, Call of the Haunted in the End Phase and the like now ask.
- Any effect of a card in your Graveyard, Banished or Extra Deck now asks at a phase change too, since you can't see those cards on the table.
- Your Set cards and Quick-Play Spells in your hand still wait quietly at phase changes; hold L to stop and use one, as before.

Playing online needs everyone on 1.218.0.
