Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.189.5 "Swing Doors!": ORRE's doors open on screen, once, when you walk up to them.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## ORRE
- Doors stay shut until you walk up to them, then open with their sound and stay open. Before, every door in
  ORRE swung open and shut on its own, over and over, the whole time you were in the room.
- PYRITE TOWN: standing behind the bookshelf that hides the stairway, A now presses its button. Before, the
  lookout kid on the other side answered instead, so a save made there had no way out.
