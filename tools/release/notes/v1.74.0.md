Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.74.0 "Friends in Your Pocket!": see who's playing from your phone, join them, and link from anywhere.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are. Update MOEMON
COMPANION too (it offers the update when you open it).

## MOEMON COMPANION: FRIENDS
- **The FRIENDS page:** everyone you've been in a co-op room with. See who's playing right now, where they are, what
  they're up to (exploring, battling, in a card duel) and the POKéMON in their party.
- **JOIN:** tap JOIN beside a friend who's playing, and your game (linked to the phone) heads to their room. A friend
  hosting a game on their own PC shows an invite code instead: JOIN copies it, ready for CO-OP > JOIN A GAME.
- **How much you share:** EVERYTHING (where you are, your room and your party), ONLINE ONLY (just that you're playing),
  or HIDDEN. Choose it at the top of the FRIENDS page. Only your friends can read it.
- **Away from home:** the phone now reaches your game through the co-op server when you're not on the same Wi-Fi, so
  your boxes, party, BAG and the DEVICE page work from anywhere while the game is online.
- **A friend starts playing:** a notification once a day per friend (off unless you turn it on in MENU >
  NOTIFICATIONS).
- The POKéDEX's SPAWNS list has its own button by the search bar.

## Co-op servers
- Play online with friends on 1.74.0 (their game updates itself the same way). If you run your own server, update it
  too: it now keeps friends' status and passes the phone's link through (saved in moemon_mail.dat next to it).
