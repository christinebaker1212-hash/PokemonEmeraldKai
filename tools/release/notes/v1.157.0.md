Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.157.0 "Not A Scratch!": 42 more MAGIC cards that prevent damage or keep things tapped.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- Cards that prevent damage now work: FOG and the other spells that stop all combat damage for a turn, creatures and
  Auras that keep damage off a creature or a player, and spells that stop one creature's combat damage.
- Cards that keep a permanent tapped for a while now work. ICE FLOE and the cards like it stay tapped by themselves
  for as long as they hold something.
- The opponent knows when to play a FOG: during your attack, once blockers are chosen, if the damage would hurt.
- 42 more cards can be played, among them FOG, HOLY DAY, CALLOUS GIANT, ENERGY FIELD, MUZZLE, DIVINE LIGHT, KOR HAVEN,
  RESTRAIN, ICE FLOE and SAMITE ALCHEMIST. Every one has its printed art.
