Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.123.0 "Pay the Price!": MAGIC cards that cost more than mana, 135 more cards in all.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- Cards whose costs ask for more than mana play now: sacrifice a creature, a land or a GOBLIN (GOBLIN BOMBARDMENT, RAZE, ARMS DEALER, NANTUKO HUSK, ATOG...), discard a card of your choice or one at random (MENTAL DISCIPLINE, SONIC SEIZURE, the PARDIC and KELDON ones...), pay life (BOOK OF RASS, GREED, SOULDRINKER...) or return the card to your hand (ATTUNEMENT).
- When a cost asks you to sacrifice or discard, the table asks which: the permanents that can pay light up, or you pick from your hand.
- A card you can't pay for, like a discard with nothing else in your hand, can't be played.
- Your opponents weigh what a cost gives up: they won't throw their best creature away for a little damage.
- MOSSDEEP's regulars use them: RHEA's goblins arm her ARMS DEALER, MARLOW plays SONIC SEIZURE.
- 1470 cards play now, all with their printed art.
