Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.206.1 "Partners in the Party!": a co-op battle's party screen now uses each UI STYLE's own Double Battle layout and shows whose POKéMON are whose.

UI STYLE
- In a co-op Double Battle, the party screen (SWITCH and MOéMON) now uses each style's own Double Battle layout, with your POKéMON in battle and your partner's down the left. FIRERED, RED and CRYSTAL showed their single-battle screen there before.
- FIRERED: your partners' POKéMON are shown in FireRed's gold partner colours, as KAI shows them, in co-op Doubles and Triples.
- RED and CRYSTAL: a thin line now separates each player's POKéMON in co-op Doubles and Triples.
