Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.63.4 "Standing in the Sun!": soft shadows under everyone, and followers that stay in sight.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
1.63.4 plays online with friends on 1.63.0 to 1.63.3 too.

## New
- **Shadows.** A soft shadow now sits under you, every person in every region, your friends, your followers and the
  GARDEN's MOéMON. Turn them off or on in OPTIONS > DISPLAY > SHADOWS.

## Fixed
- **Small followers in tall grass** no longer disappear in it.
- **Big followers' reflections** in ponds and puddles show right under them instead of far below.
