Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.188.0 "Room to Walk!": everywhere ORRE lets you walk in Colosseum, you can walk here.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## ORRE
- Every room's floor was worked out again from Colosseum's own. The strips along walls, counters and furniture, and
  the narrow and slanted ways between them, can be walked now.
- A wall or a railing now stops the step across it, not the whole square beside it.
- In scenes, the picture sits above the message box, so the people talking are in view from head to feet.
- The wall screens in houses and hideouts are dark, switched-off screens. They showed a red or checkered test card.
- AGATE VILLAGE's shrine has soft tree shadows over its grove. They were drawn as grey blocks.
