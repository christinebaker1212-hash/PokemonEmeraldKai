Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.84.0 "Look It Up!": MOEMON COMPANION's POKéDEX grows, a type chart, and the app your way.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are. Update MOEMON
COMPANION too (it offers the update when you open it).

## MOEMON COMPANION: THE POKéDEX
- **Everything about a POKéMON:** each entry now shows its ABILITIES, TYPE MATCHUPS (what hits it four times, twice,
  half or not at all), its EVOLUTION family (tap a stage to go to its entry), the MOVES it learns by level and by TM
  or HM (tap a move for its type, category, power, accuracy and PP), BREEDING (egg groups, gender, steps to hatch) and
  TRAINING (the effort it gives, catch rate, held items, growth).
- **Jump straight there:** the chips at the top of an entry take you to each part.
- **Find them faster:** the list filters by type, sorts by number, name or base stat TOTAL, and remembers your choice.

## MOEMON COMPANION: THE TYPE CHART
- **The whole chart** with every type against every other, or pick **DEFENSE** (one or two types: what's strong and
  weak against them) or **ATTACK** (one type: what it hits hard). Open it from the POKéDEX's CHART button or from any
  entry's TYPE MATCHUPS.

## MOEMON COMPANION: YOUR LOOK
- **MENU > LOOK:** seven color THEMES (SKY, EMERALD, RUBY, SAPPHIRE, LAVENDER, SAND, NIGHT), any of the game's 29
  WINDOW frames, ANIMATIONS on or off, and the page the app OPENS ON (or the one you were on last).
- **Smoother:** pages slide in, the tab bar's plate glides to the tab you pick, and menus open with a little pop.

## MOEMON COMPANION: SMALLER THINGS
- **SUMMARY:** the INFO page says how your POKéMON feels about you, and its POKéDEX ENTRY button opens its page. A
  move on the MOVES page shows whether it's PHYSICAL, SPECIAL or STATUS.
- **POKéGEAR RADIO:** arrows under the dial tune half a step at a time.
