Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.199.2 "Earn It Again!": every region's tools are earned there, and every one is yours in a postgame.

Key items between regions
- Leaving a region, only your CARD CASE, CANDY JAR, BATTLE FLAG and boat tickets come aboard. Every other key item waits in the GLOBAL PC, bikes and rods included, even from a region whose LEAGUE you've beaten.
- Each copy belongs to the region you earned it in: the GOOD ROD from HOENN and the GOOD ROD from KANTO are two. You get a region's own back whenever you return there.
- In a region whose POKéMON LEAGUE you haven't beaten, you use only what you earned there. A tool from somewhere else goes to the GLOBAL PC by itself.
- In a region whose POKéMON LEAGUE you have beaten, every tool you've earned anywhere is in your BAG, one of each. When you leave, each goes back to the GLOBAL PC for its own region.
- Devices from other regions, like the POKéTCH or the POKéGEAR, work in any region whose LEAGUE you've beaten.

Saves that already crossed
- If you reached KANTO, JOHTO, SINNOH or UNOVA carrying another region's bikes, rods or devices, they go to the GLOBAL PC the first time you load. They're back the moment you return home or beat the LEAGUE where you are. Tools you got from that region's own people stay with you.
