Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.33.1 "Fair Shake!": smoother GAME CENTER visits, card duels with friends online, and a fair MoeGatcha.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, settings and cards stay as they are.
1.33.1 plays online with friends on 1.33.0 too.

## Fixed
- GAME CENTER: no more stray picture along the left (or top) of the screen while walking in and around the tower.
- GAME CENTER: arriving on the stairs, you step off them sideways onto the landing, and out of the rooftop door to
  the left, instead of walking down through the rail.
- Card duels with friends online: pressing B while waiting for their answer no longer calls the request off at
  once (they were told you had stopped waiting). It asks "Stop waiting?" first, and NO is already picked.
- Two friends who ask each other for a card duel at the same moment now start the duel, instead of both hearing
  the other is busy.
- SPECTATE: friends' screens beside yours keep playing while you're in a card duel or at the VS cabinet.

## Changed
- MOENAV map: the GAME CENTER's tower icon is gone. Point at RUSTBORO CITY and "GAME CENTER" shows under the city's
  name, the way a route's places do.
- MoeGatcha: the daily and weekly free pulls come once for each new day and week. Setting the PC's clock back and
  forth no longer hands them out again.
- MoeGatcha: every pull is truly random now. Doing exactly the same thing at exactly the same moment no longer
  brings back the same prizes.
