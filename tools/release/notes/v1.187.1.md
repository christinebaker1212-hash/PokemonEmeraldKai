Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.187.1 "Battle Songs!": ORRE's battles play their own music, and HMs wait in the GLOBAL PC.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## ORRE
- Battles in ORRE play Colosseum's own battle songs, each battle with the song it has there.
- When the battle is over, the place's song and its wind come back.

## Travelling between regions
- HMs no longer come along when you travel. Customs puts every HM in the GLOBAL PC, also when you leave a region
  you have beaten.
- Once you beat the ELITE FOUR of the region you are in, your HMs come back from the GLOBAL PC, and an HM you have
  twice becomes one.
