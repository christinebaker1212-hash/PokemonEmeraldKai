Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.95.0 "The Card Shop by the Sea!": MOSSDEEP's old GAME CORNER has a new use. Ask at the counter.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MOSSDEEP CITY
- **The GAME CORNER is a CARD SHOP now.** A small group of regulars meets there to play a card game few people
  have heard of. The clerk at the counter will teach anyone who asks.
- **Pick a starter deck** from five, one for each color, and it's yours to keep.
- **Six regulars** play upstairs and in the play room below, each with a deck of their own. Win and you get a booster
  pack of eight cards. Losing never costs you anything.
- **Your CARD CASE learns a new game** once you have a deck: build decks, look through your cards, read every card's
  rules, and go round all your card games with L and R.
