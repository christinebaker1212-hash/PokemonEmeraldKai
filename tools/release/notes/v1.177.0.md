Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.177.0 "Lights, Camera, ORRE!": ORRE's scenes cut, fade and move as Colosseum's do, and its people stay in the picture.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## ORRE
- **Scenes move their camera.** Story scenes now cut between Colosseum's own camera angles and glide along its camera
  moves, then return smoothly to the field.
- **Scenes fade.** Where a scene fades to black and back, it now does, instead of snapping to the next shot.
- **The news on the TV** at the OUTSKIRT STAND's diner now has its words.
- **People stay in the scene.** People no longer vanish when the camera looks somewhere other than at you: everyone in
  the shot is there. They walk the paths their scenes give them, run where they run, and turn to face what they look
  at.
- Pauses in scenes last as long as Colosseum's.

## FIXES
- Pressing A on certain ORRE people right after arriving in a room no longer closes the game.
