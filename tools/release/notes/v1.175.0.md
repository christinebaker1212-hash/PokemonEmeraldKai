Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.175.0 "The Story of ORRE Begins!": Colosseum's own story now plays in ORRE, from the hideout to the colosseums.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## ORRE
- **Colosseum's story plays.** ORRE's people, scenes, battles and words are Colosseum's own: WILLIE at the OUTSKIRT
  STAND, the hunters at PHENAC CITY's gate, the MAYOR, the STADIUM and on to PYRITE TOWN.
- **The opening.** Your first boat trip to ORRE plays Colosseum's opening: the SNAGEM HIDEOUT, its boss's room, the
  SNAG MACHINE taken and the escape across the desert, ending at the OUTSKIRT STAND with the SNAG MACHINE yours.
  START skips it.
- **RUI travels with you.** Once she's free in PHENAC CITY, RUI walks right behind you, with your POKéMON behind her.
- **Story scenes show Colosseum's own skies,** with its camera; the field view stays as it was.
- **The RELIC STONE** in AGATE VILLAGE purifies a SHADOW POKéMON whose heart is open.
- MT. BATTLE's rest stops let you leave the course and go back to the reception.

## FIXES
- Busy places like PHENAC CITY no longer leave people out: everyone near you is there, and they come back after
  battles.
- Scenes no longer stop halfway when someone's walk is cut short, and they place you where the scene stands you.
