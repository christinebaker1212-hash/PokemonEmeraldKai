Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.0.0: the first release that updates itself.

## Installing
- **Windows:** download `MoemonEmeraldKaiCoop-windows.zip`, right-click it > Properties > tick "Unblock" > OK, unzip it anywhere and start `MoemonEmeraldKaiCoop.exe`.
- **Linux:** download `MoemonEmeraldKaiCoop-linux.zip`, unzip it and start `MoemonEmeraldKaiCoop` (needs SDL2; libcurl for updates).
- **Coming from an earlier test build:** copy your `pokeemerald.sav` (and its `.bak` files, the `.sav.*` files next to it and `moemon_coop.ini`) into the new folder.

This version is downloaded by hand once. From now on the game checks for updates when it starts, installs them by itself and keeps your save and settings (README.txt > UPDATES). `AutoUpdate = 0` in `moemon_coop.ini` turns that off.

## New in this version
- **Auto-updater:** new versions download, are checked and install on launch, then the game restarts. Offline or anything wrong: the game just starts.
- **Triple Battle cards** redrawn from the new pixel art: name and gender, level, the usual HP bar with a small HP label; a status covers the level; the cards stay see-through.
- **Type icons** (OPTIONS > TYPE ICONS) layered beside every battle's boxes: second type behind, first type in front.
- **e-Reader Berries** as their own 12 items (trees, tags, Berry Crush, Blender), and Berries on the MoeGatcha STANDARD banner.
- **MoeGatcha:** Gen 1-3 event Moemon, a daily STANDARD lineup, and a vote to reroll the week's PICKUP into a surprise.
- **Game clock** follows your PC clock (online: the room host's); Berry trees grow on your own time. Fixes a clock that was stuck at day 0.
- **Bag:** START sorts a pocket (TYPE, ID, ABC, OLDEST, NEWEST, MANUAL); new items show a "!" until you look at them. A bigger Bag (stacks up to 999).
- **Shared world:** cut trees, smashed rocks, pushed boulders and Flash light are shared by everyone on the map; sandstorm and hail hit everyone at once.
- **OPTIONS:** CREATURES (Kai's Moemon or the original Pokémon art) and NAME CASE.
- Fixes: black screens in Slateport's shops and the Game Corner slots online, the Dewford trend, Battle Tent tickets, traded Moemon obey, original gender ratios, a stray graphic in menus, window frames flashing in battle.
