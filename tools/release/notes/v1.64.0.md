Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.64.0 "Insert Coin!": PUZZLE BOBBLE and TETRIS THE GRAND MASTER 2 PLUS on the GAME CENTER's 3F, and coins from your COIN CASE.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
1.64.0 plays online with friends on 1.63.7 to 1.63.14 too. An arcade game against a friend at a VS cabinet needs both of
you on 1.64.0.

## New
- **Two arcade cabinets on the GAME CENTER's 3F:** the Astro City cabinets on the top right wall now play PUZZLE BOBBLE
  (left) and TETRIS THE GRAND MASTER 2 PLUS (right), one player each. Like the VS cabinets, they run your own copies of
  the arcade games' ROM sets: put pbobble.zip, and tgm2p.zip with tgm2.zip, in the arcade folder next to the game. A
  cabinet without its set says which files it needs.
- **Coins from your COIN CASE:** at every arcade cabinet SELECT puts in a coin, taking one from your COIN CASE (with
  none left, the screen says NO COINS). START is the game's START, and holding START still stands you up. SELECT can be
  changed on OPTIONS > CONTROLS > FIGHT BUTTONS (COIN).
