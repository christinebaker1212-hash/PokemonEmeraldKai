Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.185.9 "Clean Cut!": using CUT and other field moves can no longer leave you stuck in place.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## All regions
- After using a field move like CUT, with many characters and POKéMON on screen, the ground around you could turn
  solid and you couldn't take a single step. Most often this happened in SINNOH, cutting the trees in front of TEAM
  GALACTIC's building in ETERNA CITY. Field moves now leave the map as it is. If you saved while stuck, load your save
  again and you can walk.
