Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.178.0 "ORRE Takes Shape!": Colosseum's own models for ORRE's props, smoother close-ups, and a story that can't be broken.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## ORRE
- **Colosseum's own models.** Things in ORRE that aren't people, like RUI's sack, the hunters' truck, statues and the
  POKéMON standing around, are now drawn as Colosseum's own models, with a soft outline so they stand out.
- **Smoother close-ups.** When a scene's camera comes close, people are drawn smoothly instead of as big blocks, and
  never more than three times their size.
- **People face the right way** in scenes: when the camera looks from another side, you see the side of them it
  sees.
- **Losing to WILLIE** (or any battle Colosseum treats as the end) no longer breaks the story: you wake at the last
  ORRE POKéMON CENTER you healed at (or the OUTSKIRT STAND), and the scene plays again.
- **PHENAC CITY:** you can't leave before freeing RUI from the sack, and the camera comes back to normal once the scene
  ends, so you can walk to her easily.
- Colosseum's own story barriers now open and close where its scenes say.

## FIXES
- Battles after a scene's camera zoom no longer show the trainer's picture and the HP boxes blown up.
- The place's name banner no longer drags the message box with it when a scene starts.
