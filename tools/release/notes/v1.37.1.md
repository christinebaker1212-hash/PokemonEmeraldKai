Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.37.1 "One Press, One Choice!": the Yu-Gi-Oh! screens no longer take an extra A press, and long names fit their rows.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, settings and cards stay as they are.
Playing online needs everyone on 1.37.1.

## Fixed
- Closing a message with A that opens a Yu-Gi-Oh! screen no longer picks something in it as well. The GAME CENTER
  clerk's counter used to jump straight into SINGLES, and the CARD CLUB duelist's Starter Deck pick asked about
  the first deck right away.
- A name too long for its row in a list ran onto the row below it: "Legend of Blue Eyes White Dragon" garbled
  "Metal Raiders" in the Yu-Gi-Oh! CARD SHOP. Rows now keep to one line, in narrower letters or shortened with
  "..." when they need to, in both card games.
