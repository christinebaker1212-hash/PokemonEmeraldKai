Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.60.2 "A Proper Welcome!": new games in JOHTO and KANTO start the way they should, and a crash in KANTO is gone.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, cards and decks stay as they are.

## New games in JOHTO and KANTO
- A new game started in JOHTO or KANTO no longer plays HOENN's moving truck: you wake up at home.
- PROF. BIRCH no longer says you're moving to LITTLEROOT before you choose where to start, and his farewell sends you
  to PROF. ELM or PROF. OAK instead of his own lab. His question no longer stays on screen behind the next line.
- PROF. OAK gives you 5 POKé BALLS with your POKéDEX, as he should (it was 5 MASTER BALLS).
- Naming a POKéMON you were just given shows its name, not "??????????".
- JESSIE and JAMES only start their tour of the towns once you've beaten the region's POKéMON LEAGUE.

## Fixes
- Battling DARIN in KANTO no longer crashes the game, and TEAM ROCKET's PETREL and ARCHER bring their own teams.
- Every two-tile door mat in JOHTO and KANTO takes you outside from either half.
- RED gives you a LIGHT BALL as a sign of respect (the item named in his words wasn't the one you got).
