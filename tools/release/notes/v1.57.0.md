Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.57.0 "Playing With Purpose!": card opponents that think before they play, and Yu-Gi-Oh! cards that flip and Tribute in style.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, cards and decks stay as they are.

## Yu-Gi-Oh! opponents
- Equip Spells that help go on their own monsters, never on yours; the crippling ones (Paralyzing Potion and the like)
  go on yours. Cards that boost a monster pick their own monsters too.
- One answer to an attack is enough: they no longer spend Magic Cylinder and Mirror Force on the same attack.
- Battle tricks like Waboku wait for a battle, boosts wait for a battle they can win, Life Point gains wait until
  they're needed, and cards that clear the field aren't played when they'd take more of their own.
- FLIP monsters are flipped when their effect has something to do.
- Even at their best they still make the odd human slip, but never one that hands you the game for nothing.

## POKéMON card opponents
- Energy goes where it brings an attack closer (or for the Evolution they're holding) and otherwise stays in the
  hand for a Pokémon still to come, instead of piling onto a Pokémon that already has all it needs.
- Potions only on a hurt Pokémon, Full Heal only on a Pokémon with a condition, POKéMON CENTER only when it's worth
  the Energy it costs.

## Yu-Gi-Oh! duels
- Cards turn over: a Flip Summon, a Set card flipped by an attack, a card turned face-down all spin to show their
  other side.
- Tribute Summons: each Tribute glows white and rises away as light, which gathers on the new monster's zone and
  bursts as it appears.
- The card name and details in the middle line show during the Main Phases and fade away in the others. Summons
  appear in a ring of light, without the tall beam.
