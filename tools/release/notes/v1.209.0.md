Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.209.0 "Town by Town!": every region's towns play TRIPLE TRIAD by their own rules, and battle openings cover the message box in every UI STYLE.

TRIPLE TRIAD
- Every region has its own towns with their own rules and TRADE RULE: eight in JOHTO (NEW BARK to BLACKTHORN), eight in KANTO (PALLET to the INDIGO PLATEAU), eight in SINNOH (TWINLEAF to SUNYSHORE) and eight in UNOVA (NUVEMA to OPELUCID), as HOENN always had.
- RULES OF the region you're in opens on that region's towns, with the town you're in picked. The other regions fold away into one line each: press A on one to see its towns.
- A town's players carry cards that fit how far along it is, and the CARD QUEEN wanders the towns of the region you're in.
- The rules you had for JOHTO, KANTO, SINNOH or UNOVA carry on in each region's first town.
- Playing online needs everyone on 1.209.0.

UI STYLE
- When a battle opens, the opening's picture covers the message box in every UI STYLE, as it does in Kai's own.
