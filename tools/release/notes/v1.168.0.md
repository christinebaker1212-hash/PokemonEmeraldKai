Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.168.0 "Pull Up a Chair!": MOSSDEEP's CARD CLUB comes alive, and POKéMON STADIUM gets its fans.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MOSSDEEP's CARD CLUB
- Going down to the play room works the way the old GAME CORNER did: the clerk lifts the counter, turns to show you
  the way, and you walk through the door behind it.
- The play room has an exit mat at the bottom in place of the old stairs. Step down on it to go back up: you come out
  through the counter, which closes behind you. (It used to drop you at the front door.)
- The play room's players change every half hour. The regulars come and go, visiting guests drop by with decks of
  their own (any deck the game has that plays, structure decks included), and they sit at different tables. Talk to
  anyone at a table to duel. Friends playing together see the same people.
- More people who love MAGIC: two fans upstairs, and two more standing on the play room's carpet (out of the way of
  the tables), with something new to say every half hour.

## POKéMON STADIUM
- Seven fans now fill the STADIUM's lobby on BATTLE ISLAND, each one crazy about their favorite: the PETIT, PIKA, POKé
  and PRIME CUPS, the CHALLENGE CUP, the GYM LEADER CASTLE, and a reporter chasing a story.
