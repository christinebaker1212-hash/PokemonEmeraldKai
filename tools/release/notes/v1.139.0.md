Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.139.0 "Clean Sweep!": MAGIC's sweepers, painlands and more, 58 more cards.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- Cards that move every card of a kind at once now play: EVACUATION and HIBERNATION (all creatures back to the hand), APOCALYPSE, REPLENISH, MORNINGTIDE, FELDON'S CANE, CRYSTAL CHIMES and more.
- The painlands play: BRUSHLAND, ADARKAR WASTES, SULFUROUS SPRINGS, UNDERGROUND RIVER and the rest, and ANCIENT TOMB. They make colorless mana for free and colored mana for 1 damage to you. When they pay a generic cost, they use the free colorless mana.
- Abilities that give a creature landwalk until end of turn play (UNSEEN WALKER, ELVISH PATHCUTTER, CAVE PEOPLE...), and so do ones that stop a creature blocking or attacking this turn (JAMURAAN LION, BOLA WARRIOR, NETTER EN-DAL...).
- 2162 of the 5408 cards in the pool now play.
