Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.44.1 "Cards in Their Place!": Triple Triad hands stay inside their boxes, and the card you're choosing sits on top.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, settings and cards stay as they are.
Playing online works with 1.44.0 and 1.44.1.

## Fixed
- Triple Triad: the opponent's hand on the challenge screen ran past the edge of its box. It fits inside it now.
- Triple Triad: in a game, the card you're choosing in your hand was partly covered by the cards next to it. It's
  drawn on top of the others now.
