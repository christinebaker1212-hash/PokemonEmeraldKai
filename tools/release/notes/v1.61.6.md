Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.61.6 "Rival's Rematch!": BLUE's first battle plays out as it should, KANTO and JOHTO battle to their own music, and legendaries wait their turn.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
1.61.6 plays online with friends on 1.61.0 to 1.61.5 too.

## Fixed
- **BLUE's first battles (OAK's lab and ROUTE 22).** He said his losing line before the battle, his winning line when
  you beat him, and you had to talk to him again before the scene went on. Now there's no line before the battle,
  he says the right one at the end, losing to him doesn't send you back to a POKéMON CENTER, and the scene carries
  on by itself.
- **KANTO and JOHTO battle music.** Their battles played HOENN's themes. Wild POKéMON, trainers, gym leaders, the
  ELITE FOUR and the CHAMPION now battle to FireRed and LeafGreen's themes, trainers who spot you play KANTO's or
  JOHTO's own tune, and LUGIA and HO-OH have JOHTO's.
- **Legendary POKéMON standing on the map** started GROUDON's battle (its screen effect and theme). They now get
  the legendary theme, and MEWTWO its own.
- **ARCEUS on KANTO's routes.** The seventeen ARCEUS of the ARCEUS trials stood there from the start. They now
  appear once ARCEUS has visited you in your bedroom in PALLET TOWN, and the last one once the seventeen are done.
- **Legendary POKéMON in JOHTO and KANTO** stood in their places from the very start. Before a region's POKéMON
  LEAGUE is beaten, only the ones its own games let you meet then are out: RAIKOU, ENTEI and SUICUNE in JOHTO,
  ARTICUNO, ZAPDOS and MOLTRES in KANTO. All the others (and DARKRAI's dreams and MELOETTA's dance) wait until
  you're the region's CHAMPION.

## New
- **More achievements for JOHTO and KANTO**: ANCIENT PUZZLES (all four RUINS OF ALPH puzzles), BUG-CATCHING CHAMP,
  MELOETTA and DARKRAI in both regions, the ARCEUS TRIALS and THE ORIGINAL ONE.
