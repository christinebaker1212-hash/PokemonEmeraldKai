Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.163.0 "Anchors Aweigh!": BATTLE ISLAND joins the ferry's destinations.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## BATTLE ISLAND
- With the BATTLE TICKET, the LILYCOVE and SLATEPORT ferries now list BATTLE ISLAND as one more destination, beside
  SLATEPORT or LILYCOVE, the BATTLE FRONTIER and the islands your tickets open. The clerk no longer asks about
  BATTLE ISLAND before showing the others.
- The boat now sets you down right in front of the island's sailor, wherever the sailor stands.

## Moemon Maker
- Packs with events (including ones started from the game's own scripts) publish again.
