Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.130.0 "Cycle Of Life!": MAGIC's cycling, POKéMON STADIUM's picks and quitting made kinder, followers that hop down ledges.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- Cycling plays now: pay a card's cycling cost from your hand, discard it, and draw a card, at any time you could cast an instant. The cycling lands (LONELY SANDBAR, BARREN MOOR, SECLUDED STEPPE...), the GEMPALM creatures, the Urza's block cyclers and more.
- "When you cycle this card" works too: RENEWED FAITH gains you life, the GEMPALMS change a creature's power, DEATH PULSE and PRIMAL BOOST do their tricks as you cycle them.
- Cycling shows its cost in the card's menu ("CYCLE (DRAW A CARD)"). The other players cycle too: a land once they have plenty, a spell they can't afford for a while.
- 1809 cards play now, all with their printed art.

## POKéMON STADIUM
- The picks screen before a battle goes through all six of your POKéMON with LEFT and RIGHT: no more pressing DOWN for the second row.
- Press R on the picks screen to see the SUMMARY of your six, and come back on the one you looked at.
- Every STADIUM battle is played in SET style, whatever your own BATTLE STYLE is (it comes back after the battle).
- QUIT asks before it ends your cup: press B again to keep playing.

## Followers
- A follower that doesn't fly now hops down a ledge just after you land, instead of gliding down with you. POKéMON that fly or float (FLYING types, LEVITATE) still glide along.

## Fixes
- The SUMMARY's IV and EV rows now change with the POKéMON when you switch between them.
- Move tutors judged the POKéMON from DIAMOND / PEARL and later by the wrong list: they now teach exactly the moves each POKéMON can learn.
