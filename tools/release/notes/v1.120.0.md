Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.120.0 "Lights, Camera, Action!": MODERN battle animations rebuilt from scratch, move by move

- BATTLE ANIMS > MODERN gives every move its own effect, matching what the move is: beams that stream from the user to
  the target, flames and water that pour out, energy balls that arc across the field, punches, kicks, slashes, bites and
  horns that land with their own flash, bolts and rocks falling from the sky, quakes, explosions, waves, sound waves,
  winds, psychic ripples, shadowy mists, powders, healing light, auras, shields, field effects and more.
- Moves that borrow another move's animation (DRAGON PULSE, SHELL SMASH, JET PUNCH and a few hundred more) now look
  like themselves in their own colors, such as BLUE FLARE's blue fire and MOONGEIST BEAM's violet beam.
- Every move that deals damage now lands with an impact, including the 232 moves that had none before.
- MODERN effects never draw over the text box, the menus or the HP boxes, and the impact shake leaves them still.
- The stat up and stat down animation stays exactly as the game draws it.
- Effects show up clearly on the pale battle fields as well as in caves.
- Triple Battles: the Pokémon in the middle no longer vanishes or moves strangely during moves aimed at it or its
  neighbor (on CLASSIC too).
- CLASSIC animations are otherwise unchanged, and a battle with a friend on CLASSIC stays perfectly in step.
