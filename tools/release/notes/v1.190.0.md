Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.190.0 "Whose Dex Is It?": the MOéDEX list shows your own or the group's, switches REGION and NATIONAL with one button, and filters to what you are missing.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## The MOéDEX list
- R switches between OWN and SHARED. SHARED is the group's MOéDEX, as it has always been: everything you and
  your friends have seen and caught. OWN is only what you saw and caught yourself. The SEEN and OWN counts on
  the left follow.
- L switches between REGION and NATIONAL, once you have the National one. No more going through SEARCH for it.
- LT on a controller, F on the keyboard, changes what the list shows: ALL, NOT SEEN, NOT CAUGHT, CAUGHT, and in
  SHARED also FRIENDS': the ones a friend caught and you have not.
- A bar under the picture shows how much of the list you have caught. In SHARED it shows your own part in
  gold and what your friends added in blue.
- Left and right move ten entries at a time, and keep going while held. They moved seven, once a press.
- The list opens where you left it: the same entry, the same view and filter, kept with each save file.

## Good to know
- For a save from before this update, OWN starts as everything in your MOéDEX except the catches a friend is
  credited with. What a friend only showed you could not be told apart from what you saw, so those count as
  seen by you. From now on the game keeps the two apart.
- More is coming to the MOéDEX: more ways to search and sort, and new pages on each entry.
