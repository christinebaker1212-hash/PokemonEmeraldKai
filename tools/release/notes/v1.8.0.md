Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.8.0: Pokémon Stadium colors.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
Play online only with friends on 1.8.0 too (their game updates itself the same way). If you run the standalone server
on another computer, replace it with the one in this release.

## New
- **Pokémon Stadium colors.** A Moemon with a nickname gets its own colors, worked out exactly the way Pokémon
  Stadium does it: its nickname, its OT's name and the Trainer ID pick a hue within what suits its species (Stadium's
  own limits; Hoenn Moemon use the limits of Stadium species of the same type and color).
  - The same nickname always gives the same colors, and your friends see them too.
  - Faces, arms and legs keep their skin tones. With OPTIONS > CREATURES on POKéMON, the whole picture turns, like
    in Stadium.
  - Shows in battle (front and back), on the summary screen, in the PC and the party, on its icon and when it follows
    you.
  - Without a nickname (or named after its species) it keeps its usual colors.
