Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.174.3 "Eight Badges, One at a Time!": the STADIUM's GYM LEADER CASTLE is split into its gyms.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## FIXES
- **The GYM LEADER CASTLE is eight gyms again,** not one run of 37 battles. Choose any gym on the castle's new
  screen: three trainers and then the leader, and beating the leader wins that gym's badge. You can register a new
  team before each gym.
- **The ELITE FOUR opens once all eight badges are won.** LORELEI, BRUNO, AGATHA, LANCE and then your RIVAL come one
  after another with the same team, and beating your RIVAL wins the castle and its prizes.
- If you already won the castle, all eight badges are yours and the ELITE FOUR is open.
