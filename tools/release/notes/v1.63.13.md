Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.63.13 "Whole Again!": smaller followers stay whole, the VS cabinets answer at their seats, and one shadow per jump.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
1.63.13 plays online with friends on 1.63.7 to 1.63.12 too.

## Fixed
- **Smaller followers stay whole:** with SPRITE SCALING on and PMD pictures, a follower or Garden POKéMON lost the
  bottom of its picture whenever it hopped or bobbed: after a menu or a message, when walking up and down, and when
  it jumped.
- **The VS cabinets on the GAME CENTER 3F** answer from the chairs beside them: each seat was one row too high.
- **No wait when a VS cabinet speaks:** its message opens right away. The game is now only read once you choose to
  play, before the screen turns on.
- **One shadow when jumping a ledge:** with SHADOWS on, the jump no longer adds a second, darker shadow.
