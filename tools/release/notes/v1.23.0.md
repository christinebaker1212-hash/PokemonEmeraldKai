Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.23.0 "Your Rules, Your Ripples!": level scaling settings, rare evolved finds, and Garden MOEMON you can watch swim, climb and dig.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
Online, everyone needs 1.23.0: it can't play with 1.22.0 or older.

## New
- LEVEL SCALING has settings. Press A on it in OPTIONS > GAME:
  - WILD: OFF, LOW, MEDIUM or HIGH. MEDIUM, the new default, puts wild MOEMON halfway between their usual level and
    your team's (they were only 3 to 6 below your team before, which is HIGH now).
  - TRAINERS: OFF, BELOW, EVEN or ABOVE your team.
  - BOSS MARGIN: how far above it Gym Leaders, team bosses, your rival, the Elite Four and the Champion are (AUTO
    or +0 to +8).
  - BADGE CAPS: ON or OFF.
  - PAST 8 BADGES: free after the League, always free, or always capped.
- Online, the host decides: HOST A GAME > GAME RULES has all of them, with TEAM PADDING and BATTLE SPEED. Friends
  in the room see the host's choices. Playing alone, your own settings count.
- A wild MOEMON raised past its evolution level stays what the area has, but now and then its evolved form turns up
  instead: a rare find.
- You can see what your MOEMON are up to in the MOEMON GARDEN:
  - WATER types swim out into the pond, only their heads above the water, now and then diving right under.
  - Climbers go up a tree's trunk.
  - Diggers sink half into the soft sand with dirt flying.
  - Helpers' water splashes on the farm's soil, and a plant they helped twinkles.
  - They rustle through the tall grass and leave footprints in the sand, like you do.
  - WATCH tells you who's swimming, up a tree or digging.

## Fixed
- Going RIGHT from the last page of a MOEMON's summary back to the first page no longer shows a jumbled page.
- Going LEFT from the first page of the summary round to the JOURNAL shows the JOURNAL's lined sheet (it showed the
  POKE BALL page's background).
- In the JOURNAL, an entry that wraps onto a second line keeps its stripe color.
