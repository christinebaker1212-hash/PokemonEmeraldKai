Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.142.0 "Pay Up or Else!": 37 more MAGIC cards, the ones that ask for more than mana.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- Cards that say "unless" and ask for something other than mana now play: return a land to your hand, discard a card,
  sacrifice a permanent, pay life or take damage. The table asks what you'd like to give up and lets you pick it.
- 37 more cards can be played, among them CORAL ATOLL, KAROO and the other lands that send a land home, the INVASION
  lairs, BALDUVIAN HORDE, BOG ELEMENTAL, MASTICORE, LAVA BLISTER and BROWBEAT. Every one has its printed art.
- Your opponents weigh these costs too: they won't throw away their last life points or a handful of creatures to
  keep a card.
