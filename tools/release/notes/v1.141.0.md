Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.141.0 "Hang On Tight!": A friend riding on your back now joins your battles.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## Riding piggyback
- When a trainer spots you while a friend rides on your back, your friend jumps into the battle with you as your partner,
  with no questions asked. Two friends on your back make it a Triple Battle where the trainer allows one.
- Wild POKéMON that jump out at you bring your rider into the battle too, on land and while surfing. Out on the water,
  a second wild POKéMON that joins in comes from the water as well.
- Fixed: the friend carrying you could forget you were there a moment after you hopped on.
