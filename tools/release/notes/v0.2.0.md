Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 0.2.0 "Friends Everywhere!": watching battles, teaming up, followers and the MoeGatcha.

Test build of October 19.

## New
- Watch your friends' battles, and walk up to a friend who's been challenged to team up for a Double Battle.
- Co-op wild battles: the closest friend in the grass jumps in.
- Controllers: game pads with automatic layouts and their own button icons.
- Online without a server: the host's game opens the router and gives an invite code. Join by IP address too.
- Follower Moemon (SELECT in the party menu), seen by everyone.
- Emotes, custom phrases, GO TO FRIEND, friends on the map and door labels.
- A shared POKéDEX, a buddy bonus, and the gym badge for your co-op partner.
- The shared PC, trading and daily gifts.
- Daily team goals, streaks, outbreaks, achievements, the weekly race and the MoeGatcha (slot reel, rarities, 10-pulls,
  banners, weekly PICKUPs).
- Gym Leader rematches as a team, and a battle tournament.
- SOCIAL: a chat log, emote combos, sign scribbles, pranks, piggyback rides.
- Online record mixing, Secret Base visits, Trainer Cards, co-op Day Care eggs, shared roaming Moemon.
- BERRY BLENDER, BERRY CRUSH and CONTESTS with friends online.
- LOOK: FireRed / LeafGreen field sprites, ID and back pictures from many games, hand-made colors for every sprite.
- LOOK > CREATE: make up to 3 trainers of your own from a kit of parts.
- Quick emotes and phrases on the pad's L2 / R2, and one round emote picker.
- New game: PROF. BIRCH asks to see you, then LOOK opens. SKIP THE INTRO.
- Save backups (.bak1-.bak3), a server welcome note, map pings, the team photo.
- Handy extras: nature colors in the summary, move power and accuracy on R, registered items on L / R, a REPEL prompt,
  quick moves in the PC, the Move Tutor in CO-OP.

## Fixed
- Playtest fixes for LOOK, spectating, status lag, music, the MoeGatcha and the co-op menus.
