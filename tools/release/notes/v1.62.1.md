Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.62.1 "New Faces in the Grass!": newer POKéMON show up in HOENN's grass as often at GEN 4 as at GEN 9.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
1.62.1 plays online with friends on 1.61.0 to 1.61.7 too; the VS cabinets need both players on 1.62.1.
It also brings everything in 1.62.0: five VS cabinets on the GAME CENTER's 3F with four CAPCOM arcade fighters.

## Changed
- **HOENN's wild POKéMON by GENERATION.** Each route and cave gives its rarer spots to POKéMON from newer
  generations, but until now only the generations turned on got theirs, so at GEN 4 they were rare (on ROUTE 104,
  about 1 in 28 encounters). Now the spots of generations that aren't on go to the ones that are: newer POKéMON make
  up about 1 in 5 encounters at any GENERATION from 4 up. GEN 3 still has HOENN's own POKéMON only.
