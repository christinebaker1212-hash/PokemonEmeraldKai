Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.10.0: over a hundred new characters to wear.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
Play online only with friends on 1.10.0 too (their game updates itself the same way). If you run the standalone server
on another computer, replace it with the one in this release.

## New
- **111 characters from Moemon's other branch** in CO-OP > LOOK: Ethan, Red, Ash, Gloria, Dawn, Cynthia, Steven,
  Iono, Leon, Nemona, Team Rocket, Prof. Oak, Prof. Birch and many more, each with their own walking and running
  sprites, their own Mach Bike and Acro Bike sprites and their own diving sprite. Surfing, fishing, watering, field
  moves and decorating are built for them from Brendan's or May's body with the character's head, like the other
  looks. Their ID pictures and back pictures come with them.
- **Riding instead of biking.** Many characters ride a Pokemon or a vehicle when they get on a bike (Serena on her
  Rhyhorn, Prof. Birch running from a Zigzagoon...). For those, LOOK > BIKE picks RIDE (as drawn) or BIKE (a bicycle).
- **LOOK > GROUP**: the sprites are now sorted into NAMED NPC (people with a name), GENERICS (classes and townsfolk)
  and CUSTOM (your CREATE trainers and sprite sheets). SPRITE steps through the group you're in; each group remembers
  the sprite you last wore in it.
- **More pictures**: 127 new ID PICs (the characters', plus HeartGold/SoulSilver's Archer, Ariana, Bugsy, Karen,
  Will and others) and 107 new BK PICs, at the end of those lists.
- SKIN, HAIR, OUTFIT and ACCENT recolor the new sprites and pictures too.

## Changed
- The characters replace the older looks of the same people (Steven, Red, Leaf, Wally, Wallace, Archie, Maxie, Lucy,
  Prof. Birch, the female Magma Grunt, and FireRed/LeafGreen's Prof. Oak, Giovanni, Blue and Rocket Grunts). If you
  wore one of those, you now wear the new one; nothing else about your look changes.
- The sprite editor (LOOK > EDIT SPRITES) starts from a character's own walking, running, bike and diving sprites.
