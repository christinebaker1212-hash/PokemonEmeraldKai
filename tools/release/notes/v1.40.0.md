Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.40.0 "Shuffle or Boogie!": TRIPLE TRIAD comes to HOENN, with every FINAL FANTASY XIV card and rules that travel from town to town.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
Playing online needs everyone on 1.40.0.

## New
- TRIPLE TRIAD: the CARD CASE's third side. L and R now go round POKeMON, YU-GI-OH! and TRIPLE TRIAD.
  - Five cards a hand and a 3x3 board. Place a card next to one of theirs, and if your number on that side is
    higher, it's yours. The cards are FINAL FANTASY XIV's: all 475, from one star to five.
  - Your first five cards come from a TRIPLE TRIAD seller. You'll find one in the POKeMON CENTERS of PETALBURG,
    SLATEPORT, MAUVILLE, FALLARBOR, LILYCOVE, MOSSDEEP and EVER GRANDE, and one in the GAME CENTER's 2F CARD SHOP,
    with two players beside him who'll always take you on.
  - Press the registered CARD CASE's button in front of someone who plays and a game begins.
- The table: your blue hand on the left, theirs in red on the right. UP / DOWN picks a card, A (or RIGHT) takes
  it to the board, A plays it. R looks at any card you can see up close. B on your hand, START or SELECT opens the
  turn menu (RULES, CONCEDE).
  - The hands are dealt, the rules shown, and a light runs between your names to pick who starts. Cards arc down
    onto the board, taken cards spin over to their new color in a burst of sparks, and SAME, PLUS and COMBO sweep
    across the board when they go off.
- The rules: OPEN, SAME, PLUS, SAME WALL, RANDOM, SUDDEN DEATH and ELEMENTAL, with COMBO after a SAME or PLUS,
  just as in FINAL FANTASY VIII. ELEMENTAL squares carry a card type (PRIMAL, SCION, SOCIETY, GARLEAN): +1 for a
  card of that type, -1 for any other. Rules from farther away turn up too: THREE OPEN, REVERSE, FALLEN ACE,
  ASCENSION, DESCENSION, ORDER, CHAOS and SWAP.
- Rules on the move: HOENN has eight regions, each with its own rules and TRADE RULE (CARD CASE > RULES OF HOENN).
  You carry the rules of the region you last played in. Challenge someone in another region, and one of your rules
  may spread there, or one of theirs you don't carry may be abolished. Spread the rules you like, wear away the
  ones you don't.
- The CARD QUEEN travels between the regions' POKeMON CENTERS. Rules spread and fall away more easily wherever she
  is. Beat her and she'll change the TRADE RULE there or abolish a rule for you. Pay her way and she'll go wherever
  you like, sometimes bringing a rule from a far-off land.
- Losing never costs you a card: only you take cards. Win, and the TRADE RULE says what you take from their hand:
  ONE (your pick), DIFF (as many as you won by), DIRECT (every card of theirs you turned blue) or ALL.
- Every card can be won from someone. Players carry their own region's cards, stronger as you earn badges, and their
  hands change from day to day. Special players bring five-star cards: STEVEN, your rival, WALLY, WALLACE, JUAN,
  WINONA, NORMAN, ROXANNE, TATE & LIZA, MR. STONE, MR. BRINEY, SCOTT, PROF. BIRCH, MOM and the CARD QUEEN.
- Who plays which game: GENTLEMEN, LADIES, PARASOL LADIES, BIRD KEEPERS, DRAGON TAMERS, RANGERS, BREEDERS and
  couples play TRIPLE TRIAD. SAILORS, TRIATHLETES, SCHOOL KIDS, RICH BOYS, BEAUTIES and MOeMON FANS play it as well
  as MOeMON cards; COLLECTORS, COOLTRAINERS, RUIN MANIACS and EXPERTS as well as Yu-Gi-Oh!. Everyone who plays more
  than one game asks which, in their own words. The CARD CLUB, your rival, WALLY and STEVEN play all three.
- Decks: six decks of five different cards, at most two of four stars or more and one of five stars. The CARD
  LIST and the deck builder sort the cards by stars (L / R).
- Sellers: each has a small shelf of its own (one copy of each card per customer), BRONZE, SILVER and GOLD packs
  (three cards of one, two or three stars), and buys your spare copies. MYTHRIL and IMPERIAL packs are never sold.
- A PRACTICE GAME in the CARD CASE against a hand from around wherever you are.
- A TRIPLE TRIAD page of achievements: from FIRST HAND and OPEN RULES to CHAIN REACTION, TRENDSETTER, BY ROYAL
  DECREE and TRIPLE TRIAD MASTER.

## Changed
- The CARD CASE's tabs show all three games; L and R go round them on any side's first screen.
- The CARD CLUB asks which of the three games you'd like to play.
- Some people who used to offer MOeMON card duels now play TRIPLE TRIAD instead (GENTLEMEN, for one).
