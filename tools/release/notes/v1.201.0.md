Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.201.0 "Spin to Win!": a reworked MOEGATCHA with bigger prizes, POINTS and a daily PICKUP, plus Game Boy battles that look the part.

MOEGATCHA
- PULL x10 now takes your MOEGATCHA TICKETS too: your pulls go first and tickets make up the rest. PULL x1 uses a ticket when you are out of pulls.
- Prizes grow with you: the badges of the region you are in (and its LEAGUE) decide how big each bundle is, from ten POKé BALLS and five SUPER POTIONS at the start to ULTRA BALLS and FULL RESTORES later on, with more money too.
- Prizes come in bundles now, never a single POTION, and there are more kinds of them: HEART SCALES, BATTLE SETS of X items, items to hold, type boosters, BIG NUGGETS and COMET SHARDS, and now and then a POKéMON you have not caught yet.
- STANDARD has three more POKéMON from the first three generations every day, from a much longer list.
- Far fewer BERRIES: the everyday ones are gone, and the rare ones come in sets at the higher rarities.
- The POKé BALLS banner is all about BALLS now: no more POKéMON prizes there, but SPECIAL BALLS and LEGEND BALLS sets, MASTER BALLS from SUPER SUPER RARE up, and more ball effects, always one you do not have yet while any are left.
- PICKUP changes every day instead of every week, and there are many more of them: favourites from every generation. Friends online can still vote to reroll the day's PICKUP.
- New: MOEGATCHA POINTS. Every pull gives one, and the pulls you made before this update count too. Spend them at the EXCHANGE on each banner (SELECT shows the next prize): one of today's legends on STANDARD, a PICKUP POKéMON, RARE CANDIES or vitamins on TRAINING, a new ball effect or a MASTER BALL on POKé BALLS.
- MEGA STONES and Z-CRYSTALS only turn up while MEGA EVOLUTION or Z-MOVES are on.

UI STYLE
- New in OPTIONS > DISPLAY for RED and CRYSTAL: MENU POKéMON. ORIGINAL shows the little party POKéMON the Game Boy games drew, with every later POKéMON given the one its family or its look would have had; KAI shows the usual icons.
- RED and CRYSTAL with WHITE BG on: the whole battle background is plain, as on the Game Boy. RED's is white; CRYSTAL's takes the light of the time of day over the whole scene, POKéMON and boxes included: morning, day and night.
- RED's and CRYSTAL's battle boxes no longer bob when it's your turn.
- In every style, the battle's message box now opens with the screen at the start of a battle, as KAI's does, instead of showing before the screen fades in.
