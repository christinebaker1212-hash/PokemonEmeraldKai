Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.83.0 "Call Me Maybe!": JOHTO's trainers give their numbers, call you and want rematches, as in Crystal.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are. 1.83.0 plays online
with friends on 1.82.0 and 1.82.1 too.

## JOHTO's phone
- **Trainers' numbers:** 28 of JOHTO's trainers ask for your number once you've beaten them, with Crystal's own words:
  JOEY on ROUTE 30, WADE on ROUTE 31, LIZ, GINA, ARNIE, ALAN, BETH, VANCE and the rest. Say NO and they ask again
  next time.
- **They call you:** now and then, while you walk in JOHTO or KANTO, one of them rings to talk about their POKéMON, a
  wild one they met, the news, or to ask for a rematch.
- **Rematches:** once a trainer has asked, talk to them on their route for a battle. Their teams grow stronger as your
  journey goes on, as in Crystal.
- **Gifts:** some of them find things for you (BERRIES, evolution stones, vitamins and more) and keep them until you
  come by.
- **The POKéGEAR's phone book** lists every number you have below MOM and PROF. ELM. Call any of them to hear how
  they're doing.
- **OPTIONS > CO-OP > MOENAV CALLS** works for JOHTO's phone too: on BATTLES, only the calls asking for a rematch ring.

## Fixes
- **JOHTO's doors** open as they do in Moemon Quetzal: sliding doors slide apart, swinging ones swing open (they all
  rose up before), and the decorations that aren't doors no longer move.
- **Shadows behind the GAME CENTER's counters** no longer show underneath them.
