Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.118.0 "Star Power!": IV stars and EVs on the summary's STATS page

- On a POKéMON's summary, the STATS page has a new prompt: press A to see each stat's IVs as a rating out of three stars.
  A perfect IV of 31 shows three red stars, so it stands out from an ordinary three-star stat.
- Press A again to see the EVs in each stat, and once more to go back to the stats.
- On the EV view, START clears that POKéMON's EVs (it asks first). Its stats are worked out again right away.
