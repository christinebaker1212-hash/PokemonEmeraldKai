Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.31.0 "Here Comes A New Challenger!": a fighting game cabinet in MAUVILLE's GAME CORNER, against the CPU or a friend.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, settings and cards stay as they are.
Playing online needs everyone on 1.31.0.

## New
- VS CABINET: two slot machines on the right of MAUVILLE's GAME CORNER, in the bottom row nearest the door, are now
  a fighting game cabinet, with the STREET FIGHTER EX PLUS screens and ten fighters (RYU, KEN, CHUN-LI, DHALSIM,
  CAMMY, FEI LONG, DEE JAY, T.HAWK, VEGA and GOUKI). Sit down at either side and pick FIGHT CPU or WAIT FOR 2P.
- FIGHT CPU: pick a fighter and take on the CPU, first to two rounds.
- WAIT FOR 2P: you stay seated with "Fight me!" over your head for everyone in the room to see (B stands up). A
  friend who sits down at the other side is asked "Fight NAME?", and YES starts the fight for you both: pick your
  fighters at the same time, first to two rounds, then you're both back in the GAME CORNER with the result. A
  friend can also sit down while you're fighting the CPU: that fight ends and yours begins.
- Online fights use rollback: your own buttons answer right away, and the game quietly fixes up the moment your
  friend's buttons arrive, so fights stay smooth even with some lag. The buttons go straight between the two of you
  when your routers allow it, and through the room otherwise. A friend who leaves mid-fight ends it as no contest.
- The fight is drawn at twice the game's sharpness and smoothed to fit your window, with the music and sound
  brought to the game's own GBA sound. Nothing of the screens is cut off or squished, and the life bars sit at the
  top of the screen.
- Buttons: arrows or the left stick to move; X, Y and RB (keys U, I, O) and A, B and RT (keys J, K, L) for the six
  attacks; Start (Enter) to start.
