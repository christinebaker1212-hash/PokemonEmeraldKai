Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.87.0 "Sharp Dressed App!": MOEMON COMPANION gets a cleaner, crisper, friendlier look all over.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are. Update MOEMON
COMPANION too (it offers the update when you open it).

## MOEMON COMPANION: A FRESH COAT
- **Crisp small words:** notes, rewards, tab names and the farm's times now use the game's own small font instead of
  shrunken letters, so everything reads sharp.
- **Buttons you can feel:** every button is a raised key that presses down under your finger.
- **A cleaner top bar:** your name, then a light that says LINKED, IN BATTLE or how long ago your game was last
  heard from, a badge with the changes still on their way to your game, and a proper MENU key.
- **Messages that stay out of the way:** the app's notes rise above the tabs, stay a moment and sink away; a tap
  closes one early.
- **The PARTY as cards:** each POKéMON's picture, types, HP, condition and held item, two to a row like the game's
  own party screen, the lead marked.
- **Tidier pages:** every page's section headings share one look, the tabs show their full names where they fit, and
  a SUMMARY's POKéDEX ENTRY sits right under its words.
- **Fixed:** the card games' POKéMON tab said MOéMON when the app showed MOéMON.
