Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.173.0 "Open Up!": the shared PC opens again, smarter STADIUM trainers, IVs on the phone.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are. Update the phone app
too (MENU > UPDATE).
1.173.0 plays online with friends on 1.172.0 too, but the shared PC only opens for players on 1.173.0.

## Fixed
- The shared PC opens again. Once any one of its boxes held all 30 POKéMON, it kept trying to connect and never
  opened for anyone. Everything that was in it is still there.

## New
- POKéMON STADIUM's trainers switch like players: when a POKéMON is in a bad matchup, they send in one that does
  better, and after a faint they send out the best answer to yours. They don't switch twice in a row, give up their
  boosts, or switch out of a fight they're winning.
- The phone app's summary shows IVs as the game does (tap the stats: the stars, three red ones for a perfect IV), then
  the EVs and their total.
- MYSTERY GIFT scans work without a link to the game: the gift waits on the phone and opens the next time the app
  links, counting toward the day you scanned it (up to a week back).
