Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.208.0 "Chatty Chatot!": CHATOT's CHATTER works as it did in Platinum, with your own voice through a microphone.

CHATTER
- A CHATOT that knows CHATTER has CHATTER in its party menu. Press A or B and speak into your microphone: CHATOT learns one second of what it hears, and asks before forgetting a saying it already knows.
- From then on CHATOT's cry is its saying: when it comes out, when it faints, and when it uses CHATTER. It plays a little faster each time, as in Platinum.
- CHATTER's chance to confuse depends on the saying, as in Platinum.
- CHATTER has a new animation: CHATOT sways and sings out its saying.
- Online, your friends hear your CHATOT's saying too, in battles with you and against you. A wild CHATOT, or another trainer's, keeps its own cry.

OPTIONS
- New CONTROLS > MICROPHONE: pick the microphone CHATTER listens to with Left / Right, or press A and speak into the one you want.

- Online, everyone in the room needs this version.
