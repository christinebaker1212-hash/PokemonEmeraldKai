Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.125.0 "Slow and Steady!": MAGIC lands that enter tapped, cards that stop untapping, 64 more cards.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- Lands and artifacts that enter the battlefield tapped play now: COASTAL TOWER, ELFHAME PALACE, the DIAMONDS, ABANDONED OUTPOST and many more. KISMET makes your opponent's things enter tapped.
- Cards that keep permanents from untapping play: MEEKSTONE, BACK TO BASICS, CHOKE, THIRST, GRIM MONOLITH...
- Auras and creatures that grow with what's on the table: BLANCHWOOD ARMOR gets bigger for every FOREST you have, and more like it.
- The WARDS give protection from a color, and creatures that can't be blocked at all play too.
- 1606 cards play now, all with their printed art.
