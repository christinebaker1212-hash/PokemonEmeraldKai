Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.90.0 "Kanto, As You Remember It!": dozens of FireRed's people, words and little scenes come back to KANTO.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## PEOPLE WHO DO THEIR JOBS AGAIN
- **FUCHSIA's MOVE DELETER** makes POKéMON forget moves again.
- **TWO ISLAND's MOVE MANIAC** teaches a forgotten move for two TINYMUSHROOMS or a BIG MUSHROOM.
- **CAPE BRINK:** the old woman teaches FRENZY PLANT, BLAST BURN or HYDRO CANNON to a VENUSAUR, CHARIZARD or
  BLASTOISE that loves you completely.
- **SAFFRON's COPYCAT** teaches MIMIC for a POKé DOLL, and **CELADON DEPT. STORE 3F** has its COUNTER teacher back.
- **LAVENDER's NAME RATER** rates and changes nicknames again.
- **DAISY** gives you a TOWN MAP for grandpa's errand (it shows the map of the region you're in), and after the
  LEAGUE she grooms your POKéMON and tells you how much they like you.
- **CERULEAN's BIKE SHOP** has its million-yen BICYCLE on show and swaps your BIKE VOUCHER, as in FireRed.
- **ROUTE 12's FISHING GURU's brother** and **SIX ISLAND's HERACROSS fan** measure your MAGIKARP and HERACROSS
  for size records again, with a prize for a new record.
- **FIVE ISLAND's WATER LABYRINTH:** the gentleman gives a TOGEPI EGG if your lead POKéMON truly loves you.

## KANTO AND THE SEVII ISLANDS
- **GIOVANNI** leaves his GYM for good after his farewell.
- **ROCK TUNNEL** is pitch dark again: bring FLASH.
- **RUIN VALLEY:** read the braille, then use CUT from your POKéMON's menu in front of the door.
- **TWO ISLAND's shop** grows as the islands change; its last list still has the AURORA and MYSTIC TICKETs.
- **The SEAGALLOP:** "Ahoy, there! Where do you want to sail?" VERMILION is offered once BILL's first trip is over,
  and ISLANDS 4-7 open with the RAINBOW PASS alone.
- **FIVE and SEVEN ISLAND:** the POKéMON JOURNALs are back in the POKéMON CENTERs after the LEAGUE.
- PP UPs and a PP MAX are back where FireRed hid them (they had become NUGGETs). ROCKET HIDEOUT B2F's items are in
  their old spots.
- The S.S. ANNE's resting room is just the kind woman's rest again; SEVEN ISLAND's old woman simply watches battles;
  MELOETTA no longer joins the CHANSEY dance.

## FIRERED'S OWN WORDS
- GYM leaders, their guides and trainers, TM and HM hints, OAK's catching advice, the CYCLING ROAD gates and signs,
  the game designer, BILL's METEORITE, the legendary birds' cries and many more lines are FireRed's again, where
  they had been rewritten to describe things this game doesn't have.
