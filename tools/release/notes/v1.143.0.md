Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.143.0 "Not Yet... Now!": 36 more MAGIC cards whose effects wait for later in the turn.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- Effects that wait for a later moment now play: "draw a card at the beginning of the next turn's upkeep",
  "destroy that creature at end of combat", "at the beginning of the next end step, return it".
- 36 more cards can be played, among them BLESSED WINE, CARRIER PIGEONS and FORESIGHT, COCKATRICE and the basilisks,
  CINDER WALL, WALL OF TEARS, BROOD OF COCKROACHES and LIFELINE. Every one has its printed art.
