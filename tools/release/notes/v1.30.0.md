Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.30.0 "Right On Target!": friends' arrows anywhere in Hoenn, RARE CANDIES by the handful, and a pile of fixes.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, settings and cards stay as they are.
Playing online needs everyone on 1.30.0.

## New
- WHERE IS EVERYONE: the arrows at the edge of the screen point at your friends' exact direction anywhere in Hoenn,
  not just at the middle of the area they're in, and show their name and how many steps away they are. A friend
  inside a building, a cave or a POKéMON CENTER is shown at its door. The arrows are smoother and no longer take
  the place of anything else on screen.
- The Town Map, the POKéNAV map and the FLY map show your own look and each friend's look, outlined in their
  color, instead of Brendan's and May's heads.
- RARE CANDY: with more than one, the game asks how many to use (UP / DOWN by 1, LEFT / RIGHT by 10). The levels
  come one after another, stopping only for new moves, then one window shows how much every stat grew. Any candies
  it can't take (Lv. 100 or the level cap before your next badge) stay in your BAG.
- Using an item from the BAG, the cursor starts on the MOEMON it's for: a status cure on one with that status (the
  one in battle first), a REVIVE on the one that fainted last. Nothing is used until you press A.
- ETHER, MAX ETHER and PP UP show each move's PP left beside it, in battle and out of it.
- Your follower goes back into its POKé BALL when you get on a bike, and comes out again when you get off. Friends
  see it too.

## Fixed
- In co-op Double and Triple Battles, a move a friend's MOEMON learned without forgetting one only showed in their
  summary after the battle. It's there right away now.
- A MOEMON holding an HM put into a PC box kept the HM. It goes back to your BAG, as when you release one.
- After a prize from the CARD COUNTER, the music could stay off for good, and the next door you went through never
  finished opening.
- FURY CUTTER, ROLLOUT and ICE BALL in a CONTEST crashed the game.
- The SAFARI ZONE showed Brendan or May instead of your look.
- A friend's DEOXYS showed in its Normal Forme on everyone else's screen in co-op and PvP battles.
- A copy of the player could appear in the LILYCOVE POKéMON CENTER and spoil the player's colors.
