Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.43.0 "Power Up!": Modern battle animations, the BATTLE SIMULATOR, and every big battle open to friends.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
Playing online needs everyone on 1.43.0.

## New
- OPTIONS > DISPLAY > BATTLE ANIMS: CLASSIC or MODERN. MODERN plays every battle animation with glowing effects,
  trails, shockwaves and bursts in each type's colors when a move lands (embers, splashes, sparks, leaves, rocks...),
  and a little shake for the big hits. Contests use it too. Only your screen changes: friends on either setting stay
  in step, and every animation lasts exactly as long as before. Gentle on the eyes: nothing flashes the whole screen.
- The BATTLE SIMULATOR, at a new counter in each BATTLE TENT (SLATEPORT, VERDANTURF, FALLARBOR). Battle digital copies
  of the players you've met online: their look as a hologram and their six Moemon that have been in the most battles,
  everyone at level 50 (yours come back exactly as they were). Win streaks pay prize money, and new best streaks earn
  items. The opponents battle like the BATTLE FRONTIER's best.
- Join any trainer battle a friend is having, except the ELITE FOUR and the CHAMPION: GYM LEADERS, your rival, TEAM
  AQUA and MAGMA and the rest of the story too. Help a friend beat a GYM LEADER and you get the badge as well.
- Join a battle against two trainers at once: it becomes a Triple Battle against both.
- With TEAM PADDING on, nobody is ever turned away: if a battle has no seat left when you ask to team up, you join it
  as soon as it starts.

## Fixed
- The low HP beep plays three times and stops in every kind of battle, at any battle speed.
- A friend who was spotted by the trainer you're waiting on no longer gives up while you answer "Wait for another?".
- Beating two trainers together now counts them as beaten for everyone in the battle.
