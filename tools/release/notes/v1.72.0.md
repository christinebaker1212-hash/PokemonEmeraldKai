Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.72.0 "Second Screen!": your phone becomes your POKéNAV, POKéTCH or POKéGEAR.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are. Update MOEMON
COMPANION too (it offers the update when you open it).

## MOEMON COMPANION: the DEVICE
- **Your region's device:** the DEVICE page becomes the POKéNAV in HOENN, the POKéTCH in SINNOH and the POKéGEAR in
  JOHTO, once you have it. Switch to any other device you own at the top.
- **The live map:** the town map of the region you're in, with you blinking on it and your friends' places as dots.
  Tap a place to see its trainers (beaten or not), its items (hidden ones stay a secret until you find them), its BERRY
  trees and its wild POKéMON. Press and hold a place to mark it with one of six marks.
- **MATCH CALL:** everyone registered in your POKéNAV, with their picture and where they are. CALL them from the phone
  and they say what they'd say in the game (a POKé BALL shows who wants a rematch), or CHECK their page.
- **CONDITION** and **RIBBONS:** your POKéMON's contest conditions on the POKéNAV's pentagon with their sheen, and
  every RIBBON with the game's words for it.
- **The POKéGEAR:** its CLOCK, the MAP, PHONE calls to MOM and PROF. ELM, and the RADIO: drag the needle and the
  stations' shows come in line by line.
- **Battle mode:** in a battle the phone shows how each of your moves will do against the POKéMON you're facing
  (super effective, not very effective, no effect), and your party's HP, live.
- **HIDE ON PC:** the POKéTCH can stay off the game's screen while your phone shows it.
- A message the phone can't read no longer makes it link again and again.
