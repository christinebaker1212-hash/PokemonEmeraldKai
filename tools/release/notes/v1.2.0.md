Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.2.0: 90 new ball effect pieces to earn, 15 new presets.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts (from 1.0.0 on). Your save and settings stay as they are.
Play online only with friends on 1.2.0 too (their game updates itself the same way). If you run the standalone server on
another computer, replace it with the one in this release.

## New
- **30 new POKé BALL colors** (CRIMSON to BRONZE), for the ball and for its effect's energy.
- **15 new send-outs:** TELEPORT, SOMERSAULT, VORTEX, DASH IN, JELLY DROP, CARD FLIP, HOLOGRAM, STARDUST, SAND SCULPT,
  FLAME PILLAR, GEYSER, LEAF WHIRL, SHADOW STEP, PRISM, BOUNCE IN.
- **15 new entrance particles:** HEARTS, MUSIC NOTES, SNOWFALL, FEATHERS, LEAF GUST, STAR SHOWER, RAINBOW, FIRE RING,
  SPLASH, SAKURA STORM, PAY DAY, BALLOONS, BUTTERFLIES, GEMSTONES, POWER AURA.
- **30 new returns:** TELEPORT OUT, BACKFLIP, VORTEX, DASH OFF, MELT AWAY, CARD FLIP, SWITCH OFF, STARDUST, SAND CRUMBLE,
  FLAME OUT, GEYSER LIFT, LEAF WHIRL, SHADOW SINK, PRISM TWINKLE, BOUNCE BACK, HEART POP, BUBBLE FLOAT,
  LIGHTNING STRIKE, ICE SHATTER, BALLOON RIDE, SUPER JUMP, DIVE, FEATHER SCATTER, BLACK HOLE, GHOST FADE, PIXEL RAIN,
  FIREWORK, NAP TIME, SAKURA SCATTER, SPIRIT ORB. With the AUTO sound, many play a sound of their own.
- Many of them use the game's own battle art: Attract's hearts, Sing's notes, Razor Leaf, Ember, Surf's splash,
  Pay Day's coins, Petal Dance, Focus Energy, Snore's Z and more.
- **Earn them:** half come with achievements (the ACHIEVEMENTS list shows what each one gives; one you already have
  counts right away), half are MOEGATCHA prizes, mostly on the POKE BALLS banner. Pulled one you have already? You get a
  MOEGATCHA TICKET instead.
- **15 new presets** (SWEETHEART, POP STAR, WINTER, WILDFIRE, HIGH TIDE, FOREST SPIRIT, HANAMI, JACKPOT, PARTY TIME,
  SHADOW REALM, CYBERSPACE, GALAXY, DESERT WIND, DAYDREAM, ANGEL): each shows up in PRESET once you have its pieces.
- **MY PRESET 1 to 6** (was 1 to 3).

## Better
- The original effects animate the way they were meant to: confetti flutters, flowers open, fireworks sparkle.
- Smoke looks like smoke on light backgrounds, explosions leave less soot, fireworks burst below the HP boxes.
- A follower's effect is sized for the overworld.
- The editor's preview of an opponent-side Moemon now goes back to the top right, as in battle.

## For everyone online
Your friends see your effects, new pieces included, in co-op battles, link battles, PvP, trades and on followers,
whatever they have earned themselves.
