Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.174.0 "Konnichiwa, STADIUM!": the STADIUM announcer in Japanese, Stadium's own victory song, and prizes you can read.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
1.174.0 plays online with friends on 1.173.0 and 1.173.1 too.

## New
- POKéMON STADIUM's announcer can speak Japanese: OPTIONS > BATTLE > ANNOUNCER is now OFF, ENGLISH or JAPANESE. The
  Japanese voices are the original game's own (POKEMON STADIUM 2 in Japan), calling the same moments as the English
  ones.

## Changed
- "And there goes the battle!" now waits for the "...was defeated!" message and the victory song, then plays.
- STADIUM wins play POKéMON STADIUM's own victory song (and the castle's and MEWTWO's own when you beat them).
- After winning a cup, the prize screen stays up long enough to read: A only moves on once it has been shown for a
  while (a little longer when there are more prizes).
