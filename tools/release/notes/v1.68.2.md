Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.68.2 "Hall of Fame!": SINNOH's POKéMON LEAGUE as in Platinum, and cleaner nights and footsteps.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## SINNOH
- **The POKéMON LEAGUE:** the ELITE FOUR and CYNTHIA say and do what they do in Platinum, from CYNTHIA's room to the
  walk with her and PROF. ROWAN to the HALL OF FAME machine.
- **Rematches:** once CHARON is caught at STARK MOUNTAIN, the ELITE FOUR and CYNTHIA bring their stronger teams.
- **VEILSTONE GAME CORNER:** the slot machines and the people around them speak as in Platinum.
- **Partners:** you can't SURF or ROCK CLIMB while someone walks with you, as in Platinum.

## Everywhere
- **Night colors through encounters:** the time of day's colors no longer drop away while the screen flashes before a
  battle.
- **Follower footsteps:** splashes, ripples in shallow water, hot spring water, sand and short grass now show at your
  following POKéMON's feet instead of beside them.
