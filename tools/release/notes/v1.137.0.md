Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.137.0 "Double Trouble!": MAGIC's spells with two or more targets.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- Spells and abilities with several targets now play: SWELTER, JAGGED LIGHTNING, ASHES TO ASHES, UNDO, ESSENCE FRACTURE, RACK AND RUIN, PEACE AND QUIET, RAIN OF SALT, SYMBIOSIS, HOPE AND GLORY, ARGOTHIAN ELDER and more.
- After choosing the first target, the table asks for the next one. For "up to" spells like CHOKING TETHERS and LEAD ASTRAY, B stops when you have enough.
- Spells with X targets play too: DREGS OF SORROW, DISTORTING WAKE, WORD OF BINDING, MALICIOUS ADVICE. X is never more than the targets there are.
- A spell still works on the targets left if some of them are gone when it resolves.
- 2032 of the 5408 cards in the pool now play.
