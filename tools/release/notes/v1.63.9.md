Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.63.9 "HOENN Restored!": HOENN's buildings, bridges and paths are drawn right again, and every song loops.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
1.63.9 plays online with friends on 1.63.7 and 1.63.8 too.

## Fixed
- **HOENN's maps** show all their buildings, bridges, paths and counters again. Since 1.63.0 some of them were drawn
  as plain grass or black (FALLARBOR's BATTLE TENT, ROUTE 104's bridge, RUSTBORO's paths, a POKé MART's counter...).
- **Music that stopped:** SINNOH's and JOHTO's songs and some of the card games' battle songs now play on and on
  instead of stopping after one time through.
