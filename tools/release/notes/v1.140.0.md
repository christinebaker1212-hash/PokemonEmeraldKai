Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.140.0 "Wake the Land!": MAGIC's lands and artifacts that become creatures.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- Lands and artifacts that become creatures now play: TREETOP VILLAGE, FAERIE CONCLAVE, GHITU ENCAMPMENT, ANIMATE LAND, CHIMERIC STAFF, KARN'S TOUCH, QUIRION DRUID, ANCIENT KAVU and more.
- While it is a creature, the card stands with your creatures on the table with its power and toughness, and can attack and block. At the end of the turn it goes back to being a land or an artifact.
- Opponents wake their lands up to attack too.
- 2190 of the 5408 cards in the pool now play.
