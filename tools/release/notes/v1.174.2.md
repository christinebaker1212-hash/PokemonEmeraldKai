Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.174.2 "Just As They Were!": POKéMON keep their gender when they evolve.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## FIXES
- **A female ESPURR evolves into a female MEOWSTIC:** MEOWSTIC, OINKOLOGNE, BASCULEGION and INDEEDEE were all
  being treated as always male. They are half male and half female again, and every one you already have shows its
  true gender now, with nothing to do on your side.
- **Only female SALANDIT evolve into SALAZZLE,** as in the original games. A male SALANDIT no longer turns into a
  female SALAZZLE at level 40.
- Every POKéMON's chance of being male or female has been checked against the original games, from RED and BLUE to
  SCARLET and VIOLET.
- **The new game's first question fits its box:** "In KANTO or in JOHTO, here in HOENN, or in SINNOH?" no longer
  leaves stray letters behind.
