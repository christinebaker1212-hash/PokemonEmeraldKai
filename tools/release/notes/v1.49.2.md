Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.49.2 "One Hoenn!": online, Triple Triad's rules are the host's for everyone in the room.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, settings and cards stay as they are.
Playing online works with 1.44.0 and later, but card games with friends need everyone on 1.49.2.

## Triple Triad online
- In a room, everyone plays by the host's HOENN: every region's rules, its TRADE RULE and where the CARD QUEEN is
  are the same in every game.
- A rule that spreads or falls away in anyone's game, a CARD QUEEN favor and her journeys change HOENN for the whole
  room at once.
- Your own HOENN waits in your save: playing alone, the rules are yours again. The rules you carry from the last
  region you played in stay your own.
