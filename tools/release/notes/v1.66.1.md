Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.66.1 "Encore!": every custom song made again from the original music: right loops, every instrument.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## Fixed
- **The music of JOHTO, SINNOH and the card games:** all 199 custom songs were made again from the original games'
  music. Nearly a hundred of them started over from their very first note each time they looped, intro and all: they
  now loop where the original songs do. Ninety-five of them had lost some of their instruments; every part now plays.
  Short pieces that play once in the original games (the Azure Flute, the Ending and a few others) now play once too.
- **TRAINER CARD:** the card now moves only when you move it, with the right stick or the mouse, and settles flat when
  you let go.
