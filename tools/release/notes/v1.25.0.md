Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.25.0 "Jump In, Rain or Shine!": join a friend's battle when their trainer spots you, the same weather on every screen, emotes over the right MOEMON, and quicker screen fades.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
Online, everyone needs 1.25.0: it can't play with 1.24.0 or older.

## New
- A trainer who is already battling a friend can spot you too. When they walk up, you choose: "Join their battle, or
  battle alone?" JOIN BATTLE asks your friend to let you in, as JOIN does when you walk up to them (if they say no,
  you battle the trainer yourself). BATTLE ALONE is your own battle with the trainer, as before.
- Fading from one screen to the next is 30% quicker everywhere: the party screen, switching MOEMON, the BAG, menus,
  doors and warps. (The fade at the end of a battle was already quicker; it stays as it is.)

## Fixed
- In co-op battles, friends' screens showed the weather of their own spot on the map, not the battle's: a friend could
  see rain in a battle without any (or the other way round, after walking indoors from a rainy route), and the rain,
  sunlight, sandstorm or hail that a move like RAIN DANCE brings never stayed on their screen. Every screen now shows
  the battle's weather, from its first frame to the last.
- On ROUTE 119 and ROUTE 123, a friend who arrived before the day's weather reached their game could have a different
  sky until they left the route. The weather now changes over as soon as it does.
- When a MOEMON reacted in a co-op battle (a sweat drop, a heart, a sparkle...), everyone saw a bubble over their own
  MOEMON instead. Each bubble now shows over the MOEMON it belongs to, on every screen, and after a win only the one
  that landed the final blow celebrates it.
