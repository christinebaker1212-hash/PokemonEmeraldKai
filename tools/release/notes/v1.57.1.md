Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.57.1 "Clear Banners!": the card text in Yu-Gi-Oh! duels steps aside for every banner.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, cards and decks stay as they are.

## Yu-Gi-Oh! duels
- The card name and details in the middle line fade out the moment a turn or phase banner appears, whenever that
  phase ends, and come back as soon as the banner is gone, in every phase.
