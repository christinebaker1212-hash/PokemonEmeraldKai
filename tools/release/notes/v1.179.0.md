Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.179.0 "ORRE in Focus!": ORRE's people stand where they are, in the right order, and every scene hands the camera back.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## ORRE
- **People in the right order.** Someone farther away is no longer drawn over someone nearer, on the field and in
  every scene, whichever way the camera looks.
- **Nobody where they aren't.** People behind a scene's camera or outside the picture stay out of it: no more of the
  diner's waitress or yourself standing on the TV during the news.
- **PHENAC CITY:** the kidnappers' scene now plays with its own camera work, and the camera is back to normal the
  moment you can walk, so RUI's sack is easy to reach.
- Any scene that ends with its camera still turned away hands it back as soon as you can move.
