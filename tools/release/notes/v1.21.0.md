Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.21.0 "Mystery Dungeon Strut!": followers and Garden Moemon in the PMD style, with all their moves.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
Online, everyone needs 1.21.0: it can't play with 1.20.0 or older.

## New
- Followers and the MOEMON GARDEN's Moemon now use the PMD Collab's sprites, the Pokemon Mystery Dungeon style, for
  all 386.
  - Each one is drawn whole at its real size: big Moemon like ONIX tower over you, and they still stand behind or in
    front of you, other people and tall grass as they should.
  - They turn smoothly through 8 directions instead of 4.
  - Shiny Moemon have their shiny colors, and female ones their own look where there is one. Friends see your
    follower's shiny colors too.
  - A nickname's POKeMON STADIUM colors, grass, water, sand, puddles and reflections all work with them, and so do the
    POKe BALL send-out and recall effects.
- They act out what they're doing:
  - walking (twice as fast when you run or bike), breathing deeply now and then while they wait, and sitting down
    after a while;
  - sleeping when they nap, eating when they forage, munch leaves or get a treat, lying in the sun, crackling in a
    storm, attacking when they spar;
  - nodding when you talk to them, striking a pose when you pet or praise them, a twirl when they level up or greet a
    friend, tripping over a new body after evolving, flinching in a squabble;
  - tugging you along when they want to show you something, and Ghost and Psychic types floating about now and then.
- The artists who drew the sprites are listed in PMD_CREDITS.txt next to the game.
