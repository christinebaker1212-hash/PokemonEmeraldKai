Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.166.0 "Two of a Kind!": breeding works the way it does in POKéMON SCARLET and VIOLET.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## Breeding
- A male NIDORAN (or NIDORINO or NIDOKING) with DITTO now gives NIDORAN EGGS of both kinds, and VOLBEAT with DITTO
  gives VOLBEAT and ILLUMISE EGGS. NIDORAN and VOLBEAT / ILLUMISE EGGS are half one, half the other from any pair.
- Baby POKéMON hatch without an incense: MARILL gives AZURILL, SNORLAX gives MUNCHLAX, CHANSEY gives HAPPINY and so on.
- EGG moves come from either parent, not only the father. Moves from TMs no longer pass down.
- The EGG comes in its mother's POKé BALL (or the parent that isn't DITTO; either one when both parents are the same
  POKéMON), the MASTER BALL and the special balls too, and keeps it when it hatches. The ASTRAL BALL stays with the
  POKéMON caught in it.
- The mother's Ability (or the parent that isn't DITTO's) passes down 4 times in 5.
- A POWER item (POWER WEIGHT, BRACER, BELT, LENS, BAND, ANKLET) passes down its holder's stat, as one of the three
  inherited ones (five with a DESTINY KNOT).
