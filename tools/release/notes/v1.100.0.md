Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.100.0 "Open the Packs!": MAGIC gets booster packs of real sets, triggered abilities, achievements and over 300 more cards.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- **Booster packs of 23 sets**, from Fourth Edition and Ice Age to Onslaught: CARD CASE > BOOSTER PACKS shows the
  ones you hold, and A opens one. Most have 15 cards, 11 commons, 3 uncommons and a rare, all from that set, turned
  over one by one. More sets join as more of their cards can be played.
- **A win puts a booster into your CARD CASE**, and the result screen says which one, with your opponent's words.
- **The CARD SHOP's clerk sells boosters** of any set for 12 CARD POINTS.
- **Triggered abilities**: "when this enters", "when this dies", "at the beginning of your upkeep", "whenever this
  deals damage", "whenever this attacks or blocks" and more. The card lights up and its ability goes on the stack;
  your opponent's show big first. When it's yours to choose you're asked for its target, YES or NO, or whether to pay.
- **1136 cards can be played now**, up from 798: Gravedigger, Nekrataal, Man-o'-War, Flametongue Kavu, Hypnotic
  Specter, Mana Leak, Raise Dead, Zombify, Soul Warden, Braids and many more.
- **Achievements**: a MAGIC tab with thirteen of them, from your first win to winning with all five colors.
- **The cursor and its card's tooltip stay out of the way on your opponent's turn**: they show again once it's your
  move, instead of coming and going between each of their actions.
- Long prompts on the table wrap to as many lines as they need.
