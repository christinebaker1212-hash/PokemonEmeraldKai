Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.199.11 "Castelia Comes Alive!": UNOVA's towns and gyms move as they do in Black.

UNOVA
- CASTELIA's streets are busy: crowds of people walk the lanes, step around you, and now and then someone stops in front of you to chat.
- Doors move: the gates' doors slide open as you walk through, and ROUTE 10's badge gates swing open while the view turns to show them.
- Buildings come to life: windmills, lamps, signs and machines all over UNOVA play their own animations, with the season's own where Black has them.
- Every gym's machinery lights up as in Black: BURGH's switches and plates glow, ELESA's coaster gates open and close, CLAY's lift lamps, BRYCEN's switches, DRAYDEN's buttons and SKYLA's cannons all animate.
- BURGH's gym: the trainers waiting in the honey cocoons come out to battle as in Black.
- SKYLA's cannons: each flight turns the view, follows you through the air and comes back as you land.
- The camera shakes where Black's does: DRAGONSPIRAL TOWER, CHARGESTONE CAVE, the ABYSSAL RUINS and N's CASTLE.
- NUVEMA TOWN's special effect plays by day or by night.
- The GEAR STATION puts you in the right spot when you arrive.
