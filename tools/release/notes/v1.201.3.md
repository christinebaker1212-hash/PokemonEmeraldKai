Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.201.3 "Switcheroo!": RED's and CRYSTAL's POKéMON list swaps and shows held items as the Game Boy games did.

UI STYLE
- SWITCH in RED and CRYSTAL works as it did on the Game Boy: the two POKéMON's lines clear one after the other with the game's own swap sound, then come back in their new places. No more sliding off the screen.
- A POKéMON holding an item shows CRYSTAL's little item picture over the corner of its icon (a letter for MAIL), in RED and CRYSTAL.
