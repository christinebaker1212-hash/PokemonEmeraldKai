Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.70.2 "Full View!": the phone app's MENU and tabs show on newer phones, and it tells you why it closed.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MOEMON COMPANION
- **The whole screen:** on newer Android phones the top bar (with the MENU) and the BOXES, PARTY, BAG, DEX and SPAWNS
  tabs were hidden under the phone's own bars. They show now.
- **If the app closes on its own,** it says why the next time it opens, with COPY to send the whole report.
- **A link problem no longer closes the app:** the link starts over and the app says what happened.
