Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.167.0 "Open for Business!": MAGIC's CARD SHOP counter, structure decks and prizes.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC's CARD SHOP
- MOSSDEEP's CARD SHOP clerk now has a counter like the POKéMON CARD CLUB's, with three tabs (L and R):
  - TRADE IN: sell spare cards for CARD POINTS. Copies past four (and past what your decks use) count as spares:
    sell one, all of one card, or every spare at once.
  - CARD SHOP: structure decks, boosters of every set, a card you pick from the whole list (5, 15 or 50 CARD POINTS
    by rarity), RARE CANDY, PP UP and MOEGATCHA TICKETS.
  - PRIZES: two boosters when you own a quarter of a set, three boosters and 30 CARD POINTS at half, the set's rare
    at three quarters; famous rare cards for your wins, boosters opened, regulars beaten, your first structure deck
    and whole sets; 500 CARD POINTS for owning every card. Saves that already got there can claim theirs.
- Structure decks: real decks of their day, picked by hand. SIXTH STARTER A, INFESTATION, ARMADA and BOMBER are on
  the shelf now; more join them by themselves as more MAGIC cards become playable. A deck's cards go into your
  BINDER and the deck into an empty slot, ready to duel.
- The CARD CASE's MAGIC menu shows your CARD POINTS like the other games, in place of wins and losses.

## MAGIC cards
- 30 more cards play, among them SPIRIT LINK, ESSENCE SLIVER, GRAVEBANE ZOMBIE, MEMORY LAPSE, DARING APPRENTICE,
  SIBILANT SPIRIT and SAGE OWL.
