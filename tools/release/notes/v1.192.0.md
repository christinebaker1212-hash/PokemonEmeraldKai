Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.192.0 "Stadium Style!": POKéMON STADIUM on BATTLE ISLAND in two new looks, and UNOVA fixes.

POKéMON STADIUM
- OPTIONS > BATTLE has a new STADIUM SETTINGS page: STYLE and ANNOUNCER VOICE.
- STYLE: ORIGINAL (as before), POKESTADIUM (the first STADIUM's look) or POKESTADIUM 2 (STADIUM 2's look).
- Both new styles redraw every STADIUM screen: the cups, the classes, the GYM LEADER CASTLE, the cup's menu, SAVED TEAMS, registering a team, VS, THE RULES and the results.
- Both new styles also play the cups the STADIUM 2 way: the cup's ladder of opponents, then VS with both teams, then choosing your three.
- Registering in the new styles: the list three by three, A shows the POKéMON's stats and moves with ENTER Yes / No, and once six are in you can register, reselect some or reselect all.
- After a battle in the new styles: the three that fought on each side with the HP they have left, WIN and LOSE, the fainted crossed out, then the badge board in the GYM LEADER CASTLE or Keep battling? after a loss.
- POKESTADIUM shows all seven cups as pictures floating over the island: LEFT and RIGHT along a row, UP and DOWN between rows.

SINNOH AND UNOVA PEOPLE
- OPTIONS > DISPLAY > SPRITE SCALING, MIXED: many of SINNOH's and UNOVA's people now have their own sprites drawn in FireRed and LeafGreen's style by AveonTrainer: CYNTHIA, Team Galactic, Team Plasma, the gym leaders and ELITE FOUR, CHEREN, BIANCA, PROF. JUNIPER, ALDER, IRIS and many trainer classes.
- Everyone else in MIXED shows a Gen 3 equivalent where there's a true one, and their own sprite made smaller otherwise. The artist's credits are in AVEON_CREDITS.txt.

UNOVA
- Wild POKéMON: after a battle with a trainer, the wild POKéMON you met were that trainer's. They're the route's own again.
- PROF. JUNIPER's, CHEREN's and the BATTLE SUBWAY partners' backs in battle are now the same size and pixel density as yours.
- Your room is left in a mess after BIANCA's battle, not as the battle begins.
- PKMN in trainers' titles (PKMN Trainer BIANCA) shows as letters instead of odd marks.
- The camera puts you where Emerald does on the screen: a little lower than before. Scenes still frame what they show as Black does.
