Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.190.9 "New Faces Aboard!": every first voyage has its own passengers in their own places.

- The ship to each region now carries a different crowd: different people, in different spots. Someone you met in a cabin on the way to KANTO may be up on deck on the way to JOHTO, and the couple, the kid and the others are new faces each time.
- The gangway's sailor and your cabin stay where they were; the lookout stands at a different part of the rail each crossing.
