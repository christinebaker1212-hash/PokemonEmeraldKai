Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.17.1 "Garden Tidy-Up!": GARDEN fixes.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
1.17.0 and 1.17.1 play together online.

## Fixed
- In the MOEMON GARDEN, MOEMON and decorations far away showed up again in the wrong places: in the middle of the
  screen and along the top. Only the ones really on screen show now.
- Jumping down the ledge by the rock plateau sent you back to the escalator. You land below the ledge now.
- Decorations couldn't be placed: PLACE ONE put the decoration in front of you, which was the GARDEN BOARD itself.
  Now you carry it to its spot: it blinks on the tile in front of you as you walk, A puts it down, B puts it back.
