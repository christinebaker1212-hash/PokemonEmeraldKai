Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.52.2 "Full Band!": every part of the new songs plays, and TRIPLE TRIAD gets its battle music.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## Music
- The new songs play in full. Songs with many instruments lost every part past the sixteenth; now every instrument
  plays, so they sound the way they were made.
- TRIPLE TRIAD plays its own battle song in every game, against anyone and with friends, and its own victory song
  when you win.
- The GAME CENTER's music plays on as you go up and down the stairs instead of starting over on every floor.
- Every new song is set to the same volume as the game song it replaces, now checked again for each update.
