Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.121.0 "Welcome to the Stadium!": POKéMON STADIUM opens on BATTLE ISLAND, with its cups, its music and huge prizes.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## POKéMON STADIUM
- Beat any region's ELITE FOUR and a BATTLE TICKET finds you (older saves too): SCOTT in HOENN, a sailor in JOHTO and KANTO, a letter in SINNOH. Show it at any region's port and the boat takes you to BATTLE ISLAND; the island's sailor takes you back to the port you left from.
- The STADIUM's cups as the original had them: the PIKA CUP, the PETIT CUP, the POKé CUP and the PRIME CUP (POKé BALL, GREAT BALL, ULTRA BALL and MASTER BALL classes), the GYM LEADER CASTLE, VS. MEWTWO and ROUND 2, plus the CHALLENGE CUP from STADIUM 2 with its random teams. Every trainer, team, nickname and rental is STADIUM's own.
- Register a team from your party, your PC boxes, the SHARED PC, the GLOBAL PC or the cup's rentals, and look at any of them on the SUMMARY first. Pick three of your six for each battle.
- Each cup has switches: LEVEL CAP, SPECIES, HELD ITEMS and RENTALS ONLY. The harder you set them, the bigger the prizes. RENTALS ONLY turns LEVEL CAP off by itself.
- The SLEEP, FREEZE and SELF-KO clauses work as in the STADIUM.
- SAVED TEAMS: 99 teams per server that anyone can use, copy, save over or erase.
- The STADIUM's own music plays on its screens and in its battles, cup by cup and round by round.

## Prizes
- Each class won for the first time at a new setting gives a prize POKéMON with perfect IVs (more of them for each switch set hard), RARE CANDIES, PP MAXes and a lot of money. Beating MEWTWO gives you MEWTWO, and in ROUND 2, MEW.
- A win with rentals only makes the prize shiny and adds a MASTER BALL. A win with every switch set hard makes it shiny with an ASTRAL BALL on top.
- Three new ribbons for your team: the STADIUM RIBBON, the STADIUM MASTER RIBBON for the hardest classes, and the STADIUM LEGEND RIBBON for a win with every switch set hard.
- A new STADIUM tab of achievements. Wins you already have count.

## Candies from battle facilities
- With the CANDY JAR, every foe POKéMON you faint in a battle facility drops a candy of its type: the BATTLE FRONTIER and the BATTLE TENTS, JOHTO's BATTLE TOWER, SINNOH's BATTLE FRONTIER, the TRAINER HILL and TRAINER TOWER, the BATTLE SIMULATOR and the POKéMON STADIUM. You'll see how many went into the jar after each battle.

## MAGIC
- 116 more cards play: lords and anthems (LORD OF ATLANTIS, GOBLIN KING, ELVISH CHAMPION, CRUSADE, BAD MOON...), cards that give keywords, landwalk or protection to others (ABSOLUTE LAW, CHAOSPHERE...), cards that get stronger while something is true (GIANT TORTOISE while untapped...), and creatures that can't block or can't be blocked by some creatures (BEDLAM, AMROU KITHKIN, AESTHIR GLIDER...), and cards that make spells cost more or less (CHILL, the LEECHES, DARU WARCHIEF, the MEDALLIONS...). 1252 cards play now, all with their printed art.
- The cost shown on a card in your hand is what it costs right now, with those cards on the table.
- On the table, a creature changed by a lord or an anthem shows its power and toughness as they are now.
- MOSSDEEP's regulars play them: PIP's elves have their ELVISH CHAMPION, RHEA her GOBLIN KING, MARLOW a BAD MOON, TESS her LORD OF ATLANTIS and NOLAN's knights march under a CRUSADE.
