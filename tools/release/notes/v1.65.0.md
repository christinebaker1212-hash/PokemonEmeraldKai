Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.65.0 "Shine On!": a TRAINER CARD for every region, a BADGE CASE to polish, and your friends' cards.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
Playing online needs everyone on 1.65.0.

## New
- **A TRAINER CARD for every region:** L / R on your card goes between the regions you've been to. HOENN's card is the
  same as ever; KANTO's is FireRed's, with KANTO's badges and your team; JOHTO's is HeartGold's and SINNOH's is
  Platinum's, with your picture, your details, and on the back that region's badges and your team. Each card's color
  shows its stars: that region's CHAMPION, all eight badges, all eight shining, every badge of every region, and one
  more for beating every CHAMPION.
- **The BADGE CASE:** SELECT on your card opens a case with every badge of all four regions, L / R for each region's
  tray. Each badge remembers the day you won it, who led your team, and the friends who battled beside you. Badges you
  already had say they were won before records began.
- **Polish your badges:** as in Platinum, badges lose a little shine each day. Press A on a badge to hold it up close
  and rub it with LEFT / RIGHT, or drag the mouse over it, until it sparkles. Each badge chimes its own note when you
  pick it. OPTIONS > GAME > BADGE FADING sets it to PLATINUM, SLOW or OFF.
- **MY CARD:** START on your card. Keep a team on your card or show your party as it is, pick a title from your
  achievements, and turn the title on or off (also OPTIONS > GAME > CARD TITLE).
- **Friends' cards:** looking at a friend's TRAINER CARD (CO-OP > FRIENDS) now shows every region: L / R for their other
  cards, SELECT for their BADGE CASE. The back of a friend's card shows what the two of you have done: battles won
  together, your record against each other, trades, and the day you first met.
- **FRIENDS' CARDS:** every friend's card you look at is kept in MY CARD > FRIENDS' CARDS, to look at any time.

## Fixed
- **Missed HMs:** if friends moved the story on while you were away and you missed HM04 STRENGTH, HM02 FLY, HM08 DIVE
  or HM07 WATERFALL for good, the one who would have given it now finds you and hands it over. Nobody comes if you
  already have it.
