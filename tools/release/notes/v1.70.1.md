Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.70.1 "Steady Hands!": the phone app no longer closes after each move or gift.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MOEMON COMPANION
- **No more closing:** moving a POKéMON, giving or taking an item, opening a map's SPAWNS or linking again no longer
  closes the app. Everything you did before still happened: nothing was lost.
- The app offers this update itself when it opens (or MENU > UPDATE).
- If the app ever does close on its own, it says why the next time it opens.
