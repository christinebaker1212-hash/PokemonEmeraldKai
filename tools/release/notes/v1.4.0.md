Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.4.0 "Eyes on Your Friends!": watch your friends' screens while you play.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
Play online only with friends on 1.4.0 too (their game updates itself the same way). If you run the standalone server
on another computer, replace it with the one in this release.

## New
- **FRIENDS' SCREENS (CO-OP > SPECTATE).** Turn it on to see up to 3 friends' screens live in a panel under your own,
  all the time: exploring, in menus, in battle. Each card shows the friend's name and where they are, with a red
  IN BATTLE tag while they battle. Pick a SIZE from 1X to 4X; your own screen shrinks to make room so everything stays
  visible, and the cards use the biggest size that fits your window or monitor. The window grows to fit the panel and
  goes back to its own size when you turn it off. The panel keeps going while you watch someone's battle up close.
