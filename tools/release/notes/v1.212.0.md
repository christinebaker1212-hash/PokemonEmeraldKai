Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.212.0 "All Together Now!": moves that hit several POKéMON land on all of them at once, INTIMIDATE for everyone, and your own POKéDEX's second ball.

BATTLES
- A move that hits several POKéMON (SURF, EARTHQUAKE, EXPLOSION, DISCHARGE...) now lands on all of them at once: every target flashes together, every HP bar goes down together, then each one's lines ("It's super effective on Foe MAGNEMITE!") and added effects, and all the POKéMON it knocks out faint together before anyone switches in.
- INTIMIDATE lowers every foe's ATTACK at once, with one animation and one line ("GYARADOS's INTIMIDATE cuts the foes' ATTACK!"). COTTON DOWN ("Everyone's SPEED was lowered!") and SUPERSWEET SYRUP work the same way.

CO-OP
- Catching a POKéMON a friend on the server already caught now puts it in your own POKéDEX too, with "...'s data was added to my MOéDEX." and its page.
- On a server, a POKéMON you've caught for your own POKéDEX as well as the shared one shows a second ball just behind the first: in the POKéDEX list and on a wild POKéMON's box in battle, in every UI STYLE.
- New in OPTIONS > CO-OP: MY DEX BALL, to show or hide that second ball.

YU-GI-OH!
- A Tribute Summon now lets you choose the zone, starting on the zone of the monster you Tributed.
- The turn and phase banners no longer overlap, and the words under them no longer show through.
- A chance to respond now offers CHECK between your cards and PASS: look at any card on the field, then CANCEL to come back and answer.

Playing online needs everyone on 1.212.0.
