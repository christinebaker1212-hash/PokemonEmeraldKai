Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.164.0 "Crowd Pleaser!": POKéMON STADIUM's ribbons, prizes, opponents and screens polished.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## POKéMON STADIUM
- Ribbons are given on every win, so a POKéMON that joins your team later earns them by winning again.
- The STADIUM LEGEND RIBBON now goes to your own POKéMON for winning with LEVEL CAP off, SPECIES on and HELD ITEMS off.
  It used to need RENTALS ONLY too, which meant no POKéMON of yours could ever wear it.
- The shiny prize POKéMON and the ASTRAL BALL are now saved for the MASTER BALL classes of the POKé CUP, PRIME CUP
  and CHALLENGE CUP.
- Opponents no longer bring the same three POKéMON every time. They still favor the ones that match up well against
  your team, more so in the higher classes, but you can't count on it.
- The STADIUM's screens now fade in and out: from the lobby, between screens, into battles and back.
