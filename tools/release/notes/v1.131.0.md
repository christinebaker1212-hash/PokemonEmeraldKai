Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.131.0 "Fresh Pulls!": a MoeGatcha that keeps surprising you, and wild odds that keep rare POKéMON rare.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MoeGatcha
- Shiny POKéMON now only come at SUPER SUPER RARE and up. SUPER RARE has its own event POKéMON instead, and its chance went from 5% to 3%.
- STANDARD's daily lineup is much bigger: four event POKéMON, one from Generations 1-3 out of a long list, and one from every later generation. Its legends: four of the week's real events, two from every Generation 1-3 legend in turn, and one from every later generation, real events included (the ALAMOS DARKRAI, OAK'S LETTER SHAYMIN, the AZURE FLUTE ARCEUS, the LIBERTY VICTINI, the DADA ZARUDE, the MOCHI PECHARUNT and more). Every one comes up once before any comes back.
- PICKUP has 26 featured lineups (57 at GENERATION 9): the REGIS, KYOGRE / GROUDON / RAYQUAZA, HOENN's starters, the Eeveelutions, each generation's trios and pseudo-legends, and more. Its RARE prizes are today's event POKéMON.
- Fewer repeats: a prize you won lately is rerolled, a POKéMON your POKéDEX has caught comes up half as often, and random POKéMON prizes pick one you haven't caught when they can.
- TRAINING: PERFECT POKéMON, with the best IVs in every stat (legendary ones at ULTRA RARE, shiny at EX), plus SOOTHE BELL, EVERSTONE and DESTINY KNOT. Better odds of SUPER SUPER RARE and up.
- POKé BALLS: POKéMON that come in a special ball (DREAM, MOON, LOVE, GENESIS... BALL), legendary ones at ULTRA RARE, shiny at EX. Better odds of SUPER SUPER RARE and up.

## Wild POKéMON
- A lead POKéMON with STATIC or MAGNET PULL no longer makes rare Electric or Steel POKéMON show up all the time: it now doubles their chance instead. With a PIKACHU in front, VIRIDIAN FOREST's rare ones went from one in four to about one in thirteen.
