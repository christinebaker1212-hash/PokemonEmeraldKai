Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.6.0 "Sweet Rewards!": new items, PC tags and the CANDY JAR.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
Play online only with friends on 1.6.0 too (their game updates itself the same way). If you run the standalone server
on another computer, replace it with the one in this release.

## New
- **Items from later games**, from the MoeGatcha STANDARD banner:
  - QUICK POWDER: held by DITTO, doubles its Speed (not while it's transformed).
  - EVIOLITE: held by a Moemon that can still evolve, DEFENSE and SP. DEF x1.5.
  - Seven treats (OLD GATEAU, CASTELIACONE, LUMIOSEGALETE, SHALOUR SABLE, BIG MALASADA, PEWTERCRUNCHY,
    RAGECANDYBAR): heal all status problems and confusion, from the BAG or in battle.
- **PC TAG mode.** In the PC's MOVE POKeMON, TAG (X/Square on a controller, F on the keyboard) marks the Moemon under
  the cursor. Tag as many as you like in one box or in your party, then press A: MOVE picks them all up together
  (A puts them down as arranged, or in the empty slots from the cursor on; R or PARTY POKeMON puts them in your
  party), RELEASE lets them all go at once. B drops the tags.
- **The CANDY JAR.** Every Moemon you release leaves a candy of its type. After your first release, PROF. BIRCH walks
  over with the CANDY JAR (a KEY ITEM). Open it from the BAG or a registered button: the lid pops off and your candies
  pour in, bouncing and rolling into a pile (the more you have, the smaller they get). SELECT shakes the jar. A:
  CONVERT turns every ten of one kind into a RARE CANDY; they fly out of the jar and the RARE CANDY goes to your BAG.

## Fixed
- Picking up a group of Moemon with SELECT in the PC no longer turns the screen black.
