Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.105.0 "Back to Crystal!": FLORIA by the odd tree, the NATIONAL PARK's people and the RUINS OF ALPH's hidden walls, as in Crystal.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## JOHTO
- **FLORIA is back by the odd tree on ROUTE 36.** Talk to her, visit her sister at GOLDENROD's FLOWER SHOP, and the
  SQUIRTBOTTLE is yours once you have the PLAINBADGE, just as in Crystal.
- **The NATIONAL PARK's people are Crystal's again:** the PERSIAN by the QUICK CLAW teacher, the mother reading her
  daughter's MAIL, the teacher out for a walk and the boy printing his POKéDEX. The PARK's items are a PARLYZ HEAL and
  TM28 DIG.
- ROUTE 35's OFFICER DIRK only battles at night. By day he just checks on your POKéMON.
- **The RUINS OF ALPH's hidden walls open the way they did in Crystal.** Each wall's pattern spells a word in UNOWN.
  Read it, do what it says, and the wall opens the next time you come in.
- The RUINS scientist notices what you do in the chambers, and knows when you've caught every UNOWN.
- ELM's lab has its window again, ELM's house has his PC, and his assistant follows the story. PROF. ELM's calls
  follow the story too, the EGG from his assistant included.
- New signs in VIOLET CITY (the city, SPROUT TOWER and EARL'S ACADEMY) and on ROUTE 33.
- ROUTE 36's lass, the RUINS gates' officers and the GOLDENROD gate's fisher say their Crystal lines.
