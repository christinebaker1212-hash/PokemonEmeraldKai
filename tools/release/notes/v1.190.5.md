Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.190.5 "Part of the Screen!": the MOéDEX's FIND menu, its new buttons and the MORE pages are now built into the MOéDEX's own screen.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## The MOéDEX
- FIND (LT, or F on the keyboard) lives on the same layer as the START menu and slides up and down on it,
  exactly as that menu does. The list's numbers and buttons pass behind it.
- The L, R and LT buttons on the list are the same kind of thing as START and SELECT beside them.
- MORE on an entry (R) is a page of the entry's own screen, and appears with it. The entry's picture steps
  aside while a page is open.
- Nothing about how any of it works has changed, only how it is put on the screen: it fades, slides and
  sits in line with everything around it.
