Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.189.4 "Open Doors!": ORRE's doors open, and the rooms behind them can be reached.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## ORRE
- ORRE's doors work as doors. Until now a closed door inside a building was a wall for good, so whole parts of
  ORRE could never be walked into: the back of PYRITE's police station, rooms of the labs, the hotels, the
  subway, PYRITE's cave and more. Now a door opens when you walk up to it, as it should.
- Doors that the story keeps locked stay locked until the story unlocks them.
- PYRITE TOWN: the bookshelf that hides a stairway opens with its button. Before, coming up those stairs from
  the room below left you standing behind the bookshelf with no way on.
- The doors don't swing open on screen yet: for now you walk through them as they stand.
