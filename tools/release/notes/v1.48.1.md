Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.48.1 "Fair Deal!": card duels shuffle anew every time, Yu-Gi-Oh! zones pick right, and Triple Triad plays fair.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, settings and cards stay as they are.
Playing online works with 1.44.0 and later, but card games with friends need everyone on 1.48.0.

## Fixed
- Pokémon card, Yu-Gi-Oh! and Triple Triad duels no longer deal the same opening hands again after you load your
  save: every duel is shuffled anew.
- Yu-Gi-Oh!: Setting a Spell or Trap goes straight from SET to picking its zone; the extra "which Spell & Trap Zone?"
  question is gone.
- Yu-Gi-Oh!: when a Summon asks where the monster goes (after Tributes, a Fusion, a Ritual, a Special Summon), the
  Monster Zones light up, not the Spell & Trap Zones; and zones on the other side light up there.

## Triple Triad
- The computer no longer knows your face-down cards. It plays against what it has seen, and guesses the rest.
- It slips like a person now: a move nearly as good as the best is often taken, a real blunder hardly ever. The
  better the player, the smaller the slips.
- RANDOM deals both sides fairly: your five and theirs come out about as strong, so the CARD QUEEN can be beaten
  under RANDOM too.
- With CARD SCALING on, players bring a hand that fits the deck you pick: a touch weaker than yours for most people,
  a real test from the special players and the CARD QUEEN. Early players whose cards can't keep up play more carefully
  instead.
- The challenge screen shows how the game looks for the deck under the cursor: from VERY EASY to FEARSOME, with how
  their cards compare and how well they play. It learns how you play as you go.
