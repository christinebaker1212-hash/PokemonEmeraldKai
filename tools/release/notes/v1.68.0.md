Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.68.0 "Apricorn Season!": JOHTO's fruit trees and KURT's BALLS as in Crystal.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## JOHTO
- **The POKéGEAR:** MOM hands it over as you set out, as in Crystal, and it's on START in JOHTO: the CLOCK, the
  TOWN MAP, the PHONE (call MOM about your savings, or PROF. ELM) and the RADIO, with PROF. OAK's POKéMON TALK and the
  POKé FLUTE. Once JOHTO's ELITE FOUR is beaten it comes along to the other regions as a key item.
- **APRICORNS:** the APRICORN trees of AZALEA TOWN, ROUTE 37 and ROUTE 42 bear one APRICORN each a day, as in
  Crystal: RED, BLU, YLW, GRN, WHT, BLK and PNK APRICORNS.
- **KURT:** bring him APRICORNS and he makes them into BALLS by the next day: LEVEL, LURE, MOON, FRIEND, FAST,
  HEAVY and LOVE BALLS.
- **MY ROOM:** your room in NEW BARK TOWN has a PC again, and its DECORATION puts your dolls and cushions in the
  room, separate from your room in LITTLEROOT.
- **Doors:** JOHTO's doors open and close as you go in and out.
- **BERRY patches:** JOHTO's other fruit trees are BERRY patches you can plant in, starting with the BERRIES Crystal's
  trees bore there. Some of them used to share their BERRIES with the GARDEN's farm; each has its own now.

## SINNOH
- **CYCLING ROAD:** you can no longer step off the side of its lanes and fall through to the valley below.
