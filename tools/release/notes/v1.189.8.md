Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.189.8 "Feet on the Ground!": people and followers stand on the ground in ORRE's scenes, RUI shows up and gets her name, and SINNOH's clerks stand behind their counters.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## ORRE
- In scenes filmed from low down, people and followers were sunk into the ground: legs cut off, a small
  follower up to its middle. Everyone now stands on the spot they are on, from any camera, and stays there
  while the camera moves.
- PHENAC CITY: once RUI is out of the sack you can see her. Before, she was invisible until she joined you.
- PHENAC CITY: you now give her a name, as in the original ("Her name is..."). Leave it as it is and she is
  RUI. The name you give is used wherever she is named.
- Scenes hold you in place until they are over. In some, a second part of the scene went on while you could
  already walk away.

## SINNOH
- People stand where Platinum has them: three lines higher than before. A nurse or a clerk behind a counter
  no longer looks like they are standing in the middle of it.
- A new game in SINNOH: you start facing the TV, as the show ends. Before, you had your back to it.

## ORRE and SINNOH
- The same three lines apply to everyone on the 3D maps, you and your followers too, so nothing is out of
  step with anything else.
