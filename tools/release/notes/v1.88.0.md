Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.88.0 "Back to Basics!": KANTO and JOHTO lose their worst bugs and play more like FireRed and Crystal.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## KANTO AND JOHTO: THE BIG FIXES
- **Gym wins were damaging saves:** beating a JOHTO or KANTO gym leader wrote to the wrong place in memory. They now
  mark the gym's trainers as beaten, as they should.
- **CELIO and the SAPPHIRE:** handing over the SAPPHIRE undid your KANTO LEAGUE win, closing the ferries to ISLANDS
  4-7 and MT. EMBER again. Not any more, and saves it already happened to get their win back.
- **JOHTO's roaming beasts:** after the BURNED TOWER, RAIKOU and ENTEI now roam JOHTO's routes as in Crystal (until
  now a LATIOS was set loose in HOENN instead). If you passed the BURNED TOWER already, they're out there now.
- **SEAFOAM ISLANDS and VICTORY ROAD:** STRENGTH boulders fall through holes again, so SEAFOAM's currents can be
  stopped and ARTICUNO reached. The current at the bottom now drops you on dry land.
- **BIRTH ISLAND (KANTO):** touching the triangle no longer freezes the game, and the puzzle keeps its own progress
  (separate from HOENN's island).
- **NEW BARK TOWN:** the first visit no longer replants your GARDEN farm and JOHTO's BERRY patches.
- **CHARCOAL KILN:** the apprentice gives his CHARCOAL again once FARFETCH'D is home, and FARFETCH'D waits in the kiln.
- **The way to the LEAGUE:** NEW BARK's crossing to ROUTE 27 and the VICTORY ROAD gate only ask for your eight
  badges, as in Crystal.
- **GOLDENROD:** WHITNEY waits in her gym from the start. RADIO TOWER 1F has Crystal's RADIO CARD quiz again, and the
  RADIO CARD is what puts the radio on your POKéGEAR (if you passed WHITNEY's old quiz, you have it already).
- **Gone:** the PRIMAL CHAMBER under the CINNABAR mansion, MAXIE and ARCHIE at TIN TOWER and the WHIRL ISLANDS, ICE
  PATH's SWINUB, and the FLOWER SHOP's strange gifts and BERRY stalls.
- The legendaries' "flew away" lines name the right POKéMON (DEOXYS said VOLBEAT).

## MORE OF FIRERED AND CRYSTAL, LESS OF THE ODD ADDITIONS
- **Gone for good:** JESSIE & JAMES's daily visits (their prize was an empty POKéMON), ARCEUS in the PALLET bedroom,
  the REGIs crowding every TANOBY chamber, the GALARIAN birds of the SEVII ISLANDS, the RUINS OF ALPH's drilling
  DUDUNSPARCE and its tunnel, MT. SILVER's COSMOG bag and DEOXYS rock, CERULEAN CAVE's empty extra room.
- **VERMILION:** once the S.S. ANNE has sailed, she's gone: the pier says "The ship set sail.", and the harbor's people
  and signs say what FireRed's do.
- **CELADON GAME CORNER:** its twelve hidden COIN piles can be found again (a COIN CASE needed).
- **SILPH CO.:** the receptionist no longer opens an empty shop.
- **PALLET TOWN:** once you've opened the menu, the girl waits by ROUTE 1 with her TRAINER TIPS, as in FireRed.
- A gift POKéMON sent to the PC names the BOX it really went to.
