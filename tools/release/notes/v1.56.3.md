Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.56.3 "Every Phase Counts!": every Yu-Gi-Oh! phase gets its banner again, and the end of a duel opens straight to its words.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, cards and decks stay as they are.

## Yu-Gi-Oh!
- Every phase shows its banner again, even when nothing happens in it.
- The card name and details in the middle line fade out while a banner plays and come back as soon as it's gone.

## POKéMON and Yu-Gi-Oh! card duels
- At the end of a duel, YOU WIN! or YOU LOSE... shows on its own for a second, then the message box opens with the
  quote and prizes already in it.
