Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.61.7 "Gold and Silver Sounds!": JOHTO now plays HeartGold and SoulSilver's music, battles included.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
1.61.7 plays online with friends on 1.61.0 to 1.61.6 too.

## New
- **JOHTO's music from HeartGold and SoulSilver.** Its towns, routes, caves, towers, the POKéMON CENTER and MART,
  gyms, the RUINS OF ALPH, the NATIONAL PARK, the ROCKET HIDEOUT, VICTORY ROAD, the POKéMON LEAGUE, the trainers who
  spot you, and HO-OH's and LUGIA's battles all play HeartGold and SoulSilver's versions.
- **JOHTO's own battle music.** Its battles played KANTO's themes. Wild POKéMON, trainers, gym leaders and the ELITE
  FOUR, the CHAMPION, your rival and Team Rocket now each battle to JOHTO's theme, with JOHTO's victory songs after.
  RAIKOU, ENTEI and SUICUNE each have their own battle theme.
- KANTO keeps FireRed and LeafGreen's music.
