Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.69.4 "Lucky Streak!": the GAME CORNER's TM64, SNOWPOINT's trendy sayings, POKéDEX forms.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## SINNOH
- **The GAME CORNER's prize for luck:** hit ten BONUS rounds (three 7s) in one sitting at VEILSTONE's slots and the
  clerk hands you TM64, as in Platinum.
- **SNOWPOINT CITY:** the man who loves trendy sayings teaches you a new one each day for your EASY CHAT.
- **POKéDEX FORMS:** after the scientist in the ROUTE 218 gate upgrades your POKéDEX, press SELECT on UNOWN's page to
  look through every letter you've seen (LEFT and RIGHT turn the pages).
