Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.102.0 "Turn the Key!": the WORKS KEY opens the VALLEY WINDWORKS, TRAINER HILL's floors look right and the POKéTCH CALENDAR marks days.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## SINNOH
- **The WORKS KEY opens the VALLEY WINDWORKS.** Walking up to the locked door used to put you in the doorway, where
  the key couldn't be used. Now you stop in front of it, and A asks to use the key, as in Platinum. This works on
  every save that has the key.
- **The POKéTCH CALENDAR marks days.** Touch a day to ring it, touch it again to take the ring off. Each month keeps
  its own marks.

## HOENN
- **TRAINER HILL's floors** are drawn from their own maps: no more scrambled tiles or trainers in the wrong places.
