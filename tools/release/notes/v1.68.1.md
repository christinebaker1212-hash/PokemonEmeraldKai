Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.68.1 "Right Where They Belong!": SINNOH's story people show up, walk with you and leave as in Platinum.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## SINNOH
- **Walking partners leave:** BARRY no longer follows you forever after the walk to LAKE VERITY. Anyone who walks with
  you stops when their part of the story is done, and LAKE VERITY's own BARRY takes over there instead of a second one.
- **DAWN and LUCAS look like themselves:** your friend from next door wears their own Platinum look everywhere instead
  of MAY's or someone else's, and your own colors no longer break when they're near.
- **The story's people are there:** CYRUS, BARRY and the STARLY at LAKE VERITY, PROF. ROWAN on ROUTE 201, the VILLA's
  visitors and others who used to be missing now come and go as the story goes.
- **Reflections:** your reflection shows only on the water, once you're right next to it, as in Platinum.

## Looks
- **DAWN and LUCAS:** with the DAWN or LUCAS look you surf, use field moves and fish in Platinum's own sprites and
  colors.

## JOHTO
- **The gym leaders out and about:** once JOHTO's ELITE FOUR is beaten, MORTY, WHITNEY, PRYCE and JASMINE can be found
  around JOHTO.
