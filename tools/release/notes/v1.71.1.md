Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.71.1 "Use It!": use items on your POKéMON from the phone, with the effect they have in the game.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MOEMON COMPANION
- **USE:** items work on your party from the phone exactly as the BAG's USE does in the game: POTIONS and other
  medicine, REVIVES, ETHERS and ELIXIRS (pick the move), PP UP, vitamins, BERRIES that lower EVs, SACRED ASH, RARE
  CANDY (with the moves it learns), TMs and HMs (pick the move to forget when it knows four), and evolution stones.
- **Evolutions play in the game:** an evolution stone or a RARE CANDY's level-up evolution starts the game's own
  evolution scene right away. A move it wants to learn with four already known is taught in the game, as ever.
- Use it from a party POKéMON's SUMMARY (USE) or from the BAG (pick an item, then USE). The game's own rules apply:
  RARE CANDIES stop at the next BADGE's level, and nothing is used in a battle.
