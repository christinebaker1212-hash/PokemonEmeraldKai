Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.189.0 "ORRE in a New Light!": Colosseum's lighting and smoother pictures in ORRE.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## ORRE
- Every place in ORRE is lit by its own lights from Colosseum. Furniture, machines and other fittings are shaded
  by where the light comes from, and no longer flat.
- Floors and walls far from the view no longer sparkle with stray dots, and the close-ups in scenes are smooth
  where they were blocky.
- The railing beside the diner's ramp at the OUTSKIRT STAND closes the step across it, as railings do.
