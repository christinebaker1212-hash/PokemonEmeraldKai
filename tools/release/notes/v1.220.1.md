Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.220.1 "Kids These Days!": YU-GI-OH! duelists misplay again, children most of all.

YU-GI-OH!
- Duelists make mistakes again, more or less often by how good they are: a beginner slips often, a skilled duelist now and then, the very best never.
- Children slip far more than grown-ups, and a child still learning the game can be a sight: proudly paying for an effect that does nothing, wiping out their own field, or even aiming their removal at their own monster.
- Grown-ups slip less, and the stronger a duelist is, the fewer mistakes they make.

Playing online needs everyone on 1.220.1.
