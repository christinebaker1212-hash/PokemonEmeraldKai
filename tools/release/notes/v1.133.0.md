Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.133.0 "Threshold!": 41 more MAGIC cards play, from Threshold creatures to picky attackers.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- Threshold: cards that grow stronger once seven or more cards are in your graveyard (ANURID BARKRIPPER, FRIGHTCRAWLER, BATTLEWISE AVEN, CHILDHOOD HORROR and more) now play.
- Creatures that can't attack unless the defending player controls something (HAMMERHEAD SHARK and the other Island-hunting sea creatures, BRANDED BRAWLERS and more) now play.
- Abilities that only work on your turn, or only on your opponent's, now play.
- Auras like MANIACAL RAGE and CAGEMAIL, which pump a creature and stop it from blocking or attacking, now play.
- 1850 of the 5408 cards in the pool now play, and they show up in boosters, the CARD SHOP and opponents' decks.
