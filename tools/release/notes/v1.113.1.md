Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.113.1 "Box Hop!": the PC's box grid shows your POKéMON again, and switching boxes is quicker.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## PC boxes
- After picking a box from the box grid (L2 / R2 held), the POKéMON in it show again when the grid zooms back in.
- Picking a box from the grid opens it at once, then zooms in, with no slide behind the grid.
- Switching boxes with L2 / R2 slides 30% faster.
