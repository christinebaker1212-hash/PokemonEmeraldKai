Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.149.0 "Once Per Turn!": 41 more MAGIC cards with limits on when they work.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- Abilities that work only so many times each turn, only when you could cast a sorcery, only on your turn or only at
  certain points of the turn now play by those rules. Spells like PANIC can only be cast when the card says.
- Pressing A on a card you can't use right now tells you why, like "Only twice each turn."
- 41 more cards can be played, among them PIT IMP, ROOTWALLA, FIRE DRAKE, RECURRING NIGHTMARE, QUIRION RANGER,
  WIREWOOD SYMBIOTE, VOLRATH'S GARDENS and PANIC. Every one has its printed art.
