Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.69.0 "Tower Tycoon!": SINNOH's BATTLE TOWER as in Platinum, from the lobby to PALMER.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## SINNOH: the BATTLE TOWER
- **Platinum's tower:** the counters, the elevator, the corridor and the battle rooms say and do what they do in
  Platinum. Seven of Platinum's own TOWER trainers a room, with their own teams and words, harder with every room you
  clear.
- **Single, double and multi battles:** pick three, four or two POKéMON of any level; in the rooms they battle at level
  50, as in Platinum. The banned POKéMON are Platinum's.
- **The BATTLE SALON:** take on multi battles beside CHERYL, MIRA, RILEY, MARLEY or BUCK once you have met them on
  your journey. Each tells you about the team they bring this time.
- **PALMER:** the TOWER TYCOON waits as your 21st and 49th opponent in single battles, with his silver and gold
  prints and ribbons for beating him.
- **REST:** save and quit between battles, and carry on from the lobby the next time you play.
- **BATTLE POINTS and records:** Platinum's BP for every room cleared, and your best and latest streaks kept.
- **The STATS JUDGE:** the man in the lobby judges your POKéMON's potential.

## Fixes
- **SINNOH's BATTLE HALL, CASTLE and ARCADE:** battles there no longer pick your POKéMON's moves for you or end after
  three turns.
