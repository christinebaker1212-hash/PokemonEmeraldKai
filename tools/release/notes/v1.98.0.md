Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.98.0 "See Every Move!": MAGIC duels show each move as it happens, and your hand's tray takes your deck's colors.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- **Every move plays out on the table, one at a time**: cards fly from the hand and the library to their places,
  lands and creatures turn as they tap, attackers lunge forward, blockers light up, damage flies to its target with
  the number it deals, and creatures break apart as they go to the graveyard. Life totals light up as they change.
- **Your opponent's turn is easy to follow**: you see them draw, play their lands and cast their spells (each one big
  first, then on its way to the stack), and the table waits for each move to finish before the next.
- **Costs at a glance**: the card under the cursor shows its cost on its top edge, and a card on the table that can
  be used shows what using it costs.
- A button pressed just as a move finishes playing is no longer lost.

## MAGIC AND POKEMON CARD DUELS
- **The hand's tray takes your deck's main color**: for MAGIC by its cards' colors and its lands, for POKEMON cards by
  its Pokemon's types and its Energy.
- **TRAY COLOR** in a deck's menu picks another: any of the game's colors, or any POKe BALL color you have unlocked.
  Each deck keeps its own.
