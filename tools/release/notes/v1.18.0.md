Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.18.0: the new CANDY JAR, and decorating the GARDEN fixed.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
1.17.x and 1.18.0 play together online.

## New
- The CANDY JAR has a new look, drawn from the design you sent: the jar in its own framed panel, a list of every
  kind with its candy and count, diamonds drifting by in the background.
  - The jar's lid pops off and the candies pour in; with the lid off, the jar has a proper glass rim.
  - A on a kind with ten or more makes a RARE CANDY from it (YES / NO first); ALL makes every one it can.
  - Move the cursor and that kind's candies hop in the jar. SELECT shakes it (the lid now shakes with the glass).

## Fixed
- Putting a decoration down in the MOEMON GARDEN left you unable to move. Decorating the Garden now works just like
  decorating a SECRET BASE: DECORATE > DOLL or CUSHION, move it with the + Control Pad, "Place it here?" YES.
  PUT AWAY: move the cursor onto one and press A. A placed doll shows as in use, so it can't be placed twice.
