Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.3.0 "Ride, Surf and Fish!": every look can bike, surf and fish, and HMs without the old rules.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
Play online only with friends on 1.3.0 too (their game updates itself the same way). If you run the standalone server
on another computer, replace it with the one in this release.

## New
- **Every look has action sprites.** NPC sprites, RS BRENDAN / RS MAY and your CREATE trainers used to turn into
  Brendan or May on a bike or surfing, and just stood still while fishing, watering, using a field move or decorating.
  Now they wear Brendan's or May's body for the action, in the look's own clothes colors, with the look's own head.
  CREATE trainers keep their own Mach Bike. Pokemon looks (Mew, Wingull...) ride and surf as themselves. Friends see
  it too.
- **No HM restrictions.** HM moves can be forgotten like any move: a TM can replace one, so can a new move at a level
  up or the MOVE DELETER. You can release a Moemon that knows SURF or DIVE.
- **Hold an HM to use it.** Give an HM to a Moemon (BAG > TMs & HMs > GIVE) and it can use that move outside battle as
  if it knew it: CUT a tree, SURF, STRENGTH... (you still need the badge). Held HMs do nothing in battle. A held HM
  never leaves you: releasing, trading or storing that Moemon in the shared PC puts the HM back in your BAG.

## Fixed
- An NPC look on a bike, surfing or fishing could show up as scrambled black and white blocks.
- The surf blob took the colors of an NPC look (a pink blob for the Hiker). It's always the blob's own colors now.
- Your reflection in water showed Brendan's colors with an NPC or CREATE look.
- A BRENDAN look on a girl's save (or MAY on a boy's) became the other one on a bike or surfing.
- Mixing records online could be dropped when a friend left right after sending theirs while a message was on your
  screen. Once every record has arrived, the mix always goes ahead.
