Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.61.5 "Every Color, Every Card!": the POKéMON pictures stop freezing battles, nicknamed POKéMON of every generation get their own colors, and Yu-Gi-Oh! tokens get a picture.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
1.61.5 plays online with friends on 1.61.0 to 1.61.4 too.

## Fixed
- **OPTIONS > CREATURES: POKéMON could freeze a battle.** With the original POKéMON pictures, a battle against
  HAKAMO-O froze the game, and many POKéMON from later generations moved oddly or took on another one's colors.
  Every POKéMON's picture now shows and moves as it should, and the ones that showed stray colors have their own.
- **A rare crash in battle** with a POKéMON from GEN 4 or later in your party: depending on its nature, its
  send-out animation could pick one that doesn't exist and close the game. It now plays as it should.
- **Yu-Gi-Oh! tokens** showed an empty picture and called themselves TRAP CARDs on the card screen. They show as the
  monsters they are, with their Attribute, Type, Level, ATK and DEF.

## New
- **Stadium colors for every generation.** A nicknamed POKéMON from GEN 4 to 9 gets its own color shift, as the
  older ones always have.
- **SEALED POKéMON are easy to spot.** A POKéMON past the GENERATION setting has a gray icon in the party and the PC,
  says SEALED in the party, and its summary shows SEALED in red.
- **A picture for every Yu-Gi-Oh! token**: a coin in its Attribute's color.
