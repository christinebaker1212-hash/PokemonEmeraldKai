Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.34.0 "Shuffle Up!": card duel tournaments, Prize formats and records, and a fairer CARD SHOP.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, settings and cards stay as they are.
Playing online needs everyone on 1.34.0.

## New
- CO-OP > TOURNAMENT: choose BATTLES or CARD DUELS. In a card duel tournament everyone duels everyone once with
  the deck they have in use, nothing is wagered, and leaving a duel loses that match. The champion and second
  place get their prizes as in a battle tournament.
- Card players have their own ways to play: most go for 6 or 3 Prize cards, some for 4 or 2. The challenge tells
  you how many.
- The challenge shows your record against that card player (wins and losses, a concession counts as a loss) and
  a WON TODAY ribbon once you've beaten them today.
- POKEMON cards show their weakness, resistance and retreat cost on the bottom row, in the same dots as the attack
  costs (weakness left, resistance middle, a dot per Energy of retreat cost right), so you can read them on the
  table without looking up close.
- The GAME CENTER CARD SHOP's owner runs a full CARD COUNTER too: TRADE IN, CARD SHOP and PRIZES, just like the
  CARD CLUB.

## Changed
- GAME CENTER CARD SHOP: one of each structure deck (SOLD OUT once bought) and one copy of each single (marked
  SOLD in its corner).
- The card counts in the binder and the deck builder sit on the picture now, so they don't hide the bottom row.

## Fixed
- Conceding an online card duel ends it at once, like offline. Before, it told you to wait for your turn and
  nothing more could be done, sometimes on both games.
