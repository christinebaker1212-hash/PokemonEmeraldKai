Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.150.0 "Heads or Tails!": 18 more MAGIC cards that flip coins or mill libraries.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- Coin flips: the card flips a coin and does one thing if you win and another if you lose. The table tells you how
  the coin landed.
- Cards that put the top cards of a library into the graveyard now work too.
- 18 more cards can be played, among them MILLSTONE, MENTAL NOTE, ALTAR OF DEMENTIA, RAY OF ERASURE, TOLARIAN SERPENT,
  BOTTLE OF SULEIMAN, GOBLIN BOMB, CHAOTIC GOO and WINTER SKY. Every one has its printed art.
