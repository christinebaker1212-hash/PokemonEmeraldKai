Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.219.0 "Spin Cycle!": KANTO's spin tiles and elevators work, and CO-OP features work in every region.

KANTO
- The spinning arrow tiles in the ROCKET HIDEOUT work: step on one and you spin its way until a STOP tile, another arrow or a wall. VIRIDIAN GYM and FIVE ISLAND's ROCKET WAREHOUSE have them too.
- The ROCKET HIDEOUT's elevator works with the LIFT KEY, and every KANTO elevator now opens its floor list on the floor you're on, with B to cancel.

CO-OP
- DAILY: the day's mass outbreak happens on the routes of the region you're in (KANTO's and JOHTO's too), and its goals count that region's badges. The SECRET BASE goal only comes up in HOENN.
- MOVE TUTOR: outside HOENN, where HEART SCALES are hard to find, a BIG MUSHROOM pays as well.
- FRIENDS > VISIT SECRET BASE works from any region and brings you back where you were.
- The FRIEND FINDER points at friends in KANTO and JOHTO properly.
- KANTO's and JOHTO's gym leaders' TMs come with a MOEGATCHA TICKET too.

OPTIONS
- New: BATTLE > RARE CANDY CAP. Off by default: RARE CANDIES raise any level. On, they stop at the next gym leader's ace, counting the badges of the region you're in.

YU-GI-OH!
- Synchro, Xyz, Link and Pendulum Summons are named as such in a card's menu, and their materials are asked for by name ("Link Materials"...).
- A trigger effect's question says where the card is ("...from the field?") and no longer runs over the YES / NO choices.

Playing online needs everyone on 1.219.0.
