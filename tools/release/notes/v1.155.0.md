Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.155.0 "Guardian Angels!": 33 more MAGIC cards, Auras that watch over their creature.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- Auras that act when their creature attacks, deals damage or dies now work: ELEPHANT GUIDE leaves a 3/3 Elephant
  behind.
- Creatures like SENGIR VAMPIRE grow when a creature they hurt this turn dies.
- 33 more cards can be played, among them ELEPHANT GUIDE, SENGIR VAMPIRE, BARON SENGIR, VENOMOUS FANGS, SQUEE'S
  EMBRACE, CURIOSITY and REWARDS OF DIVERSITY. Every one has its printed art.
