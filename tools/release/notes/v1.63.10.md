Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.63.10 "Sinnoh in Motion!": SINNOH's water and windmills move, and its warps, stairs and escalators take you the right way.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
1.63.10 plays online with friends on 1.63.7 to 1.63.9 too.

## New
- **SINNOH's water moves:** the sea, the shores, lakes and marshes ripple as in Platinum, and lamps and signs light up
  in turn.
- **SINNOH's windmills and machines turn.**
- **Stairs and escalators in SINNOH** carry you up or down instead of just taking you to the next floor.

## Fixed
- **SINNOH's doors, gates and cave mouths:** you keep walking the way you were going after one, so a cave leads you in
  instead of turning you back, and side doors no longer turn you south.
- **Shadows:** everyone's shadow is the same size, whatever LOOK they wear; followers' shadows no longer move while
  they walk; shadows no longer flicker on SINNOH's slopes.
