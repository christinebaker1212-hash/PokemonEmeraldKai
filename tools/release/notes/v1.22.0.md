Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.22.0 "Sow, Grow, Share!": a BERRY farm in the MOEMON GARDEN.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
Online, everyone needs 1.22.0: it can't play with 1.21.0 or older.

## New
- A BERRY farm in the MOEMON GARDEN: two rows of soft, loamy soil just below the landing.
  - 4 plots are open at first, and every badge opens 2 more (20 with all eight).
  - Plant, water with the WAILMER PAIL and pick just like the BERRY patches on the routes: Emerald's growing times
    and yields, on your own PC's clock, so they keep growing while you're away.
- Your MOEMON help on the farm, each in its own way:
  - GRASS types help a plant grow a little faster.
  - WATER types and the kindest MOEMON water dry soil.
  - A hungry or mischievous one may sneak a bite of BERRIES left ripe for too long.
  - WATCH on the GARDEN BOARD tells you who's doing what.
- Online, the Garden's farm is the host's: everyone plants, waters and picks there together. Whoever reaches a plot
  first gets to plant it or pick its BERRIES, so nothing is ever picked twice. Your own farm waits for you, still
  growing, until you play alone or host.
