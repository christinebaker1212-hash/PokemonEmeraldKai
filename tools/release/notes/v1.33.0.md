Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.33.0 "Round Two, Fight!": the VS cabinet keeps score, plays its music at the right speed and fills the whole screen.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, settings and cards stay as they are.
Playing online needs everyone on 1.33.0.

## New
- Wins and losses: every VS cabinet fight you win or lose is counted, and the back of your TRAINER CARD shows your
  VS FIGHTS record. Five new achievements: INSERT COIN and ARCADE MODE for beating the CPU, A NEW CHALLENGER, WORLD
  WARRIOR and GRAND MASTER for beating friends.
- FIGHT BUTTONS: OPTIONS > CONTROLS > FIGHT BUTTONS lets you pick the key and the controller button for each punch
  and kick. Press A on one, then the key or button you want (Esc or Back keeps it as it was). DEFAULTS puts the
  arcade layout back.
- The VS screen names the stage you're about to fight on, and every stage has a proper name now (SUZAKU CASTLE AT
  NIGHT - RYU, LAS VEGAS - BALROG...).
- The picture turns on and off like an old arcade screen: a bright line opens into the fight, and closes it at the
  end.
- Online: if your friend's game goes quiet, "Waiting for NAME... 10" counts down over the fight (it carries on if
  they come back), and a small signal icon in the corner warns you when the connection is struggling.
- Online with different fight files: fighters or stages that aren't exactly the same on both PCs are greyed out
  and never picked at random. If the fighting game itself is different, you're told so and no fight starts.
- Online, both players fight by the rules of the cabinet that was challenged (rounds, life, time, stages).
- If the two games ever drift apart during an online fight, they quietly get back in step with the left player's
  game and carry on.

## Fixed
- The fight music played far too fast. Every song now plays at its own speed.
- The character select, VS and results screens no longer have black bars at the sides: their backgrounds carry on
  to the edges of the screen.
