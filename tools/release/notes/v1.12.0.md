Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.12.0 "Picture in Picture!": friends' screens anywhere you like.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
You can play online with friends on 1.11.0 or later.

## New
- **CO-OP > SPECTATE, one row per friend.** Every friend online has a row; A opens their page:
  - SHOW: show this friend's screen or not.
  - PLACE: BELOW, ABOVE, LEFT or RIGHT of your screen, or OWN WINDOW: a window of its own you can move anywhere,
    another monitor too. Closing that window stops showing that friend only; the others stay.
  - SIZE: 1X to 4X, for each friend separately.
- Each friend's settings are remembered by their name. NEW FRIENDS sets the size for friends you haven't set up yet.
- Your own screen still shrinks to make room, and the window grows to fit, so everything stays in view.
