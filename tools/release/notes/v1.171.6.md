The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## Changed
- EGGS hatch in half the steps again: a hatching cycle every 32 steps (the original game took 256). FLAME BODY and
  MAGMA ARMOR still halve it once more. EGGS on the companion app's walk count the same way.
- The DAY CARE MAN checks for an EGG every 32 steps (it was 64): the same odds each time, twice as often.
