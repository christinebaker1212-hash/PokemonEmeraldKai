Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.103.0 "Scan Everything!": MYSTERY GIFTS from any bar code, and choosing POKéMON from the boxes in the Companion app.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are. Update the
Companion app from its MENU > UPDATE.

## MYSTERY GIFT
- **Scan any QR code, or the bar code on something from a store,** with the Companion app (ME > MYSTERY GIFT) and a
  gift arrives in your BAG: twelve a day. It could be a POKé DOLL... or a MASTER BALL.
- The same code gives the same gift all day, and a new one tomorrow. A code you've already scanned today won't give
  a second gift.

## The Companion app
- **Choosing a POKéMON for a walk, for training or for a trade opens your boxes**, just like the BOXES page: swipe
  through every BOX, and the ones that can't go are dimmed and say why. Before, it was a list of your party and one
  box.
- The POKéDEX's logo is whole again, and the tabs on a POKéMON's entry fit the screen.
- Card names under a just-opened pack are whole, and NEW! no longer sits on the row above.
- The BAG's tab frame, the walk's step count and other small things look right.
