Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.35.0 "It's Time to Duel!": Yu-Gi-Oh! card duels, Starter Decks, boosters and duelists across HOENN.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, settings and cards stay as they are.
Playing online needs everyone on 1.35.0.

## New
- Yu-Gi-Oh! The CARD CASE has a second side: L and R switch between POKEMON and YU-GI-OH!. Duels play by
  today's rules (8000 Life Points, the first player doesn't draw or attack on their first turn) on today's table,
  with the cards of the four Starter Decks, Legend of Blue Eyes White Dragon and Metal Raiders.
- The CARD CLUB has a Yu-Gi-Oh! duelist. They hand you a CARD CASE if you don't have one, let you pick YUGI's,
  KAIBA's, JOEY's or PEGASUS's Starter Deck, offer a teaching duel, and later run a counter: TRADE IN spare cards
  for CARD POINTS (the same points as the POKEMON cards) and a CARD SHOP for boosters or any card you pick.
- Who plays what: PSYCHICS, HEX MANIACS, BLACK BELTS, COOLTRAINERS, TEAM AQUA and MAGMA grunts and lots of young
  folks duel at Yu-Gi-Oh!; kids, HIKERS, SWIMMERS and older folks stay with POKEMON cards. The CARD CLUB, your
  rival, WALLY and STEVEN ask which game you'd like.
- The ELITE FOUR, MAXIE, ARCHIE and the FRONTIER BRAINS duel at Yu-Gi-Oh! with decks of their own, and hand over
  their signature card the first time you beat them. Beating an ELITE FOUR member still gives their old POKEMON
  promo card once.
- Every win gives a booster pack, and once a day each opponent lets you pick a card of their deck. Boosters have
  9 cards and a Rare or better in each, with Super, Ultra and Secret Rares as often as in the real packs. Rarities
  shine on the cards as they were printed.
- Decks of 40 to 60 cards and up to 15 Fusion Monsters, with Stairway to the Destined Duel's Forbidden and
  Limited list. PRACTICE DUEL in the CARD CASE plays a Starter Deck for fun.
- CARD ART and CARD BACKS work for Yu-Gi-Oh! cards too: GAME BOY shows Stairway to the Destined Duel's pictures,
  and CARD BACKS: PRINTED the real card back.
- A YU-GI-OH! achievements tab.

## Changed
- The CARD CLUB moved from the POKEMON CENTERS of OLDALE, MAUVILLE and LILYCOVE to the POKEMON MARTS of OLDALE and
  MAUVILLE and LILYCOVE's DEPARTMENT STORE.

## Not yet
- Yu-Gi-Oh! with friends online, and the cards of the sets after Metal Raiders. They're coming next.
