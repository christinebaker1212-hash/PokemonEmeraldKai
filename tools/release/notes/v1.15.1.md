Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.15.1 "Steady Steps!": crash fixes for co-op battles, smoother followers, the same moves on every screen.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
To play online together, everyone needs 1.15.1.

## Fixed
- **Crashes in co-op battles.** Switching Moemon or using an item like REVIVE in a battle with a friend could crash
  the game when the party screen opened. Fixed.
- **A+B+START+SELECT** (the GBA's soft reset) crashed the game. It now closes the game cleanly, like CTRL+R.
- The GARDEN BOARD reads MOéMON GARDEN again (the é was missing).
- **Your Moemon no longer snaps back to you.** When you walk on while it's off doing its own thing, it runs after
  you (around fences and trees, faster when you run or ride) and falls in behind you.
- **No more bubbles at every door.** Your Moemon's arrival bubble now shows only when you come into a different area,
  and a "?" only the first time you're somewhere new.
- **WATCH QUIETLY** is no longer a shortcut through the JOURNAL. Watching a Moemon teaches you something you can see
  for yourself (how it acts, what it loves doing), at most once every half hour of play for each Moemon.
- **The same moves on every screen.** A friend's Moemon walking about on its own, and their Moemon in the GARDEN,
  now take exactly the same steps and face exactly the same ways on your screen as on theirs.
- **DEOXYS** looks right in the PC boxes and on the party screen.
