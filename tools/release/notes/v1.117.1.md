Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.117.1 "Stone Cold!": every evolution stone and item works from the BAG.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## Fixes
- The DUSK STONE, SHINY STONE, DAWN STONE and ICE STONE evolve the POKéMON they should: LAMPENT into CHANDELURE, MISDREAVUS into MISMAGIUS, and the rest. Before, they said "It won't have any effect."
- The same goes for the other evolution items: PROTECTOR, ELECTIRIZER, MAGMARIZER, DUBIOUS DISC, REAPER CLOTH, the LINKING CORD, the apples, sweets, pots, teacups, scrolls, armors, METAL ALLOY, WHIPPED DREAM and SACHET.
