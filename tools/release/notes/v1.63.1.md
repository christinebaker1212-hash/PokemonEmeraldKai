Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.63.1 "A Fresh Start in SINNOH!": a NEW GAME can begin in SINNOH.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
1.63.1 plays online with friends on 1.63.0 too.

## New
- **Start a NEW GAME in SINNOH.** PROF. BIRCH asks where your journey begins: KANTO, JOHTO, HOENN or SINNOH.
  In SINNOH you wake up in your room in TWINLEAF TOWN with no MOéMON. On ROUTE 201, a briefcase left behind holds
  three POKé BALLS: pick TURTWIG, CHIMCHAR or PIPLUP. PROF. ROWAN in SANDGEM TOWN then gives you the POKéDEX and
  five POKé BALLS.
- Wild MOéMON leave you alone until you have one, and BARRY and the story's battles wait for you to get one too.

## Changed
- PROF. BIRCH's region list reads KANTO, JOHTO, HOENN, SINNOH, with the cursor on HOENN.
