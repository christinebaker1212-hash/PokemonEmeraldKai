Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.115.0 "The Other Side!": the DISTORTION WORLD, as Platinum has it, from the first floor to GIRATINA.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## The DISTORTION WORLD
- After SPEAR PILLAR, the DISTORTION WORLD is Platinum's own: its floating floors, the walls and ceilings you walk on as the world turns around you, and the camera turning with it.
- Ride its elevators between floors, with CYNTHIA riding along where she should.
- SURF across its lakes and ride the waterfalls up onto the ceiling and back down.
- Push STRENGTH boulders through the holes to the floor below and into the lake trio's pits to bring out the last stepping stones.
- Meet MESPRIT, AZELF and UXIE, CYNTHIA and CYRUS along the way, all in Platinum's own words.
- On the lowest floor, battle CYRUS, then follow CYNTHIA into GIRATINA's room: its stepping stones appear one by one, GIRATINA comes down out of the dark, and the portal it leaves takes you home to SENDOFF SPRING.
- The floating rocks, platforms and portals of every floor are there, fading in and out as Platinum shows them.

## Fixes
- HP bars fill once and stop, everywhere: a trainer's POTION, LEFTOVERS, healing moves and more could make a bar fill, drop to zero and fill again, over and over (most often in Triple Battles while another POKéMON's status changed).
- In SINNOH, a legendary POKéMON you've battled is gone as soon as the battle ends, instead of standing there until you leave and come back (UXIE, AZELF and MESPRIT in their caves, GIRATINA, and the bollards in FANTINA's gym).
