Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.204.5 "Who's On The Field?": the party in Double and Triple Battles shows who is in battle, in every UI STYLE.

UI STYLE
- Double and Triple Battles: the party screen in RED, CRYSTAL, FIRERED, PLATINUM and BLACK now sets the POKéMON in battle apart from the rest, as KAI does, each in the style's own way:
  - RED / CRYSTAL: those in battle in a framed box on the left, drawn as the status screens draw them, the rest on the right.
  - FIRERED: FireRed's own Double Battle party, the two in battle in the large slots on the left; in a Triple Battle, three large slots on the left.
  - PLATINUM / BLACK: the two in battle along the top under a line in a Double Battle; the three in battle down the left side in a Triple Battle.
- The cursor moves as each screen is laid out: left and right between the two sides, up and down within one.

BATTLES
- Triple Battles on your own (as in UNOVA) now open the Triple Battle party screen, with all three POKéMON in battle on the left, instead of the Double Battle's.
