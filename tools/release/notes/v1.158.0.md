Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.158.0 "Circle Of Protection!": 26 more MAGIC cards that stop the damage of a source you choose.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- Cards that name "a source of your choice" now work: you pick a creature or a spell on the table, and its next
  damage to you is prevented. The table asks which one when there's more than one.
- REVERSE DAMAGE and CHO-ARRIM ALCHEMIST turn that damage into life instead.
- The opponent uses its circles when a creature or spell of the right color is about to hurt it.
- 26 more cards can be played, among them every CIRCLE OF PROTECTION and RUNE OF PROTECTION, PENTAGRAM OF THE AGES,
  RIGHTEOUS AURA and SANCTUM GUARDIAN. Every one has its printed art.
