Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.85.0 "Ball's in Your Court!": MOEMON COMPANION gets the POKé BALL page, MOEGATCHA pulls and a page all about you.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are. Update MOEMON
COMPANION too (it offers the update when you open it).

## MOEMON COMPANION: THE POKé BALL PAGE
- **A new BALL tab on every SUMMARY:** change a POKéMON's ball, its COLOR and DECOR, and its whole send-out and
  return: PRESET, SEND-OUT, PARTICLES, ENERGY, RETURN and SOUND, just like the game's own ball effect editor.
- **See it before you pick it:** a stage plays each send-out, particle and return as the game plays them, and the
  ball in the corner shows its color and decoration.
- **Every piece to look at:** pieces you haven't earned yet show where they come from (an achievement, or a MOEGATCHA
  prize), so you know what to chase. Only the ones you have can be kept.
- **MY PRESETS and the party:** use or save MY PRESET 1-6, or give a mix to your whole party at once. Moving a POKéMON
  into another kind of ball uses one from your BAG, as in the game.

## MOEMON COMPANION: THE ME TAB
- **Your TRAINER CARD:** name, ID, money, POKéDEX, play time and every region's BADGES.
- **ACHIEVEMENTS:** all of them, by kind, with what's done, what's left, and the rewards waiting.
- **MOEGATCHA on your phone:** pick a banner and pull, with your free pulls, TICKETS or pulls. The prizes are the same
  ones the machine in the game gives, pity and all, and they go straight into your game.
- **The CANDY JAR:** every kind's candies, and CONVERT turns each ten of a kind into a RARE CANDY.
- **Your GARDEN's BERRY farm:** each plot as it grows, how long it has left, and which are ready to pick.

## MOEMON COMPANION: SMALLER THINGS
- **RENAME:** give any POKéMON a new name from its SUMMARY.
- **Fixed:** the BAG's money didn't show.
