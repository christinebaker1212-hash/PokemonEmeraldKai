Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.63.14 "True Colors!": handheld screen colors and grids, a friendlier OPTIONS, and FAVORITES in your own order.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
1.63.14 plays online with friends on 1.63.7 to 1.63.13 too. A MUGEN fight at the VS cabinet needs both of you on 1.63.14.

## New
- **COLOR (OPTIONS > DISPLAY):** the game's colors as a handheld's screen showed them: DEFAULT, GBA CORRECTED (the
  first GBA's unlit screen), GBA SP, GBA SP 101 (the backlit one) or NDS.
- **Screen grids:** FILTER now has each handheld's own grid (GBA, GBA SP, SP 101, NDS). MATCH COLOR, the first
  choice, puts the grid of your COLOR on screen; any grid also goes with any COLOR.
- **Better SCANLINES and LCD GRID:** scanlines are soft rows like a real TV's, with a red, green and blue grille up
  close; the LCD grid shows each pixel's red, green and blue stripes.
- **OPTIONS in five tabs:** GAME, BATTLE, DISPLAY, CONTROLS and CO-OP. Every setting is still there: battle settings
  (LEVEL SCALING, GENERATION, the battle gimmicks, BATTLE ANIMS) have their own tab, and DISPLAY has the window and
  how the world looks (FOLLOWERS, SPRITE SCALING, SHADOWS). The card game settings are on GAME.
- **FAVORITES in your own order:** the BAG's FAVORITES keeps the order you marked them in, SELECT moves one, and
  START sorts it like the other pockets (MANUAL brings your own order back).
- **SPRITE SCALING in 5% steps:** A turns it on and off in one press; LEFT and RIGHT set how close small followers and
  GARDEN Moemon come to their true size, from 5% to 100%.

## Changed
- **The GAME CENTER's games follow SCALING:** PIXEL PERFECT and SHARP FIT show each game's own picture with black
  beside it; STRETCH fills the whole screen with it.
- **MUGEN fights look as their makers drew them:** the 4:3 picture, the pack's own camera and life bars, no longer
  widened to fit the screen.
- **Followers stand like PMD pictures in every style:** the original sprites put their feet on your line and stand
  centered behind you, and every Moemon's shadow is the same size as yours.
