Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.27.0 "Let's Duel!": an optional Pokemon Trading Card Game with the original 228 cards, against card players all over HOENN or your friends online.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
Online, everyone needs 1.27.0: it can't play with 1.26.4 or older.

## New
- Card duels: face a card player and press the CO-OP button (C key, Y/Triangle on a controller) to ask for a duel.
  Talking to them with A works as always. Card players are in OLDALE, PETALBURG, RUSTBORO, DEWFORD, MAUVILLE,
  VERDANTURF, FALLARBOR, FORTREE, LILYCOVE, MOSSDEEP and PACIFIDLOG.
- The first time, they offer a CARD CASE and a choice of three starter decks: EMBER FRIENDS, TIDAL FRIENDS and GARDEN
  FRIENDS. Say NO and nothing changes; nobody asks you unprompted.
- The duel table: the opponent sits on top, you at the bottom, the Active Pokemon face each other on a POKé BALL
  circle, with Benches, PRIZES, decks and DISCARD piles around them. Every card slides to where it goes: draws,
  plays, Energy, evolutions, Knock Outs and Prizes.
- Attacks hit with flashes, sounds and a big damage number; the HP plate counts down, damage counters drop on the
  card, and WEAKNESS x2 / RESISTANCE -30 show up when they count. Coins spin for every flip, Asleep and Paralyzed
  Pokemon turn sideways and Confused ones upside down, and YOUR TURN sweeps across the table.
- Move over the table itself with the D-Pad: A on a card shows what you can do with it, a card you play floats over
  the spaces it can go, START looks at any face-up card up close, and SELECT opens the classic duel menu. Hints show
  your own controller's buttons.
- Every Trainer card asks for its choices on the table or in a card picker: POTION, COMPUTER SEARCH, ENERGY REMOVAL,
  POKé BALL, POKéDEX and all the others.
- CARD CASE: a collection binder with a page per type, decks built the classic way (LEFT/RIGHT take a card out or put
  it in, START checks the deck), eight deck slots, practice duels, and booster packs that tear open and turn over one
  card at a time, rare cards shining.
- Every win gives a booster pack. Losing costs nothing.
- Online card duels: both open CARD CASE > ONLINE CARDS in the same room, one invites, the other accepts.
- Your cards are saved with your game.
