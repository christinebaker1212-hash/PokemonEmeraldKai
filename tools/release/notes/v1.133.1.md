Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.133.1 "Fresh Start!": STADIUM teams can be cleared again.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## POKéMON STADIUM
- On the REGISTER A TEAM screen, press START to move onto your team: A takes the POKéMON under the cursor off, whichever list it came from, even if it has changed since.
- Press START again on your team to clear all six at once (it asks first). Your POKéMON stay where they are.
- Before, a POKéMON that had gained EXP or moved boxes after being registered couldn't be taken off the team.
