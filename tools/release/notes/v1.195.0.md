Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.195.0 "To N's Castle!": UNOVA can be played through to the credits, with the TOWN MAP and FLY, SKYLA's cannons and the POKéMON LEAGUE.

UNOVA
- The TOWN MAP shows UNOVA, and FLY takes you to every town you've visited, landing in front of its POKéMON CENTER.
- SKYLA's gym: walk into a cannon and it fires you to the next platform.
- DRAGONSPIRAL TOWER's spiral slopes can be climbed all the way up.
- RELIC CASTLE: the quicksand pulls you down to the floor below, so the way to the bottom opens.
- DRAYDEN's gym and other places with exits that lead back the way you came now take you to the right spot.
- The POKéMON LEAGUE: its plaza can be walked, the ELITE FOUR's rooms work, and the way to the CHAMPION opens once all four are beaten.
- N's CASTLE: every hall can be walked, up to the throne room, its battles and the ending.
- Trainers who hand over an item after a battle give the right one.
