Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.202.0 "Ninety New Tricks!": 90 new POKé BALL effects to earn, an editor that shows them all at a glance, and a batch of battle fixes.

POKé BALL EFFECTS
- 90 new pieces to earn from KANTO, JOHTO and SINNOH achievements: 35 entrances, 35 returns and 20 particle sets, drawn with art from the move animations of Emerald, FireRed, Platinum and Black.
- The editor shows every piece of a kind in one list: what you have, what is still locked, your favourites and what is new. Colours pick from a grid of all 41, presets from a list with your own six marked.
- SOMERSAULT, CARD FLIP and the jumps move more smoothly, and returns always head off to the left (except those that go up or down).
- Every effect is sized to read well in Single, Double and Triple battles.

BATTLES
- In co-op Double and Triple battles, catching one wild POKéMON while another is still out no longer stops the battle music after the catch jingle.
- The caught mark on wild POKéMON shows in co-op Double and Triple battles, in every UI STYLE.
- After choosing to switch POKéMON, the YES / NO box goes away at once instead of staying on screen until the switch.
- The level up's stat box closes cleanly in every UI STYLE.

UI STYLE
- Status conditions show on the battle boxes in every style, the way each game shows them: RED and CRYSTAL write PSN, PAR, SLP, FRZ or BRN where the level goes; FIRERED, PLATINUM and BLACK show their own status badges.
- RED and CRYSTAL with WHITE BG on: the battle boxes and all their words have a thin white border, so they stay readable over the POKéMON.
- RED and CRYSTAL windows lay out long text the same way as short text: a long line goes on to the next line and the window grows to fit.
- RED and CRYSTAL's MENU POKéMON stand perfectly still as they animate.
