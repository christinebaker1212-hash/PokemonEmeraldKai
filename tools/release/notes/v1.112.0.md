Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.112.0 "Shuffle Up!": card players bring far more decks, the PC boxes zoom out to show every box, and a handful of handy touches.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## Card games
- **POKéMON card players** no longer reach for the same couple of decks. Each one brings their own pick from every deck that suits your strength: the first Game Boy game's decks and Pokemon Card GB2's, mixed (KANTO's players lean to the first game's, JOHTO's to GB2's), and decks you've just faced are set aside for a while.
- **Gym Leaders and other special trainers** have GB2 versions of their decks too, built around their own POKéMON.
- **YU-GI-OH! duelists** pick from far more decks as well. World Championship 2006's decks come up three times as often, and skilled duelists, and a third of the named ones, can bring a deck from goatformat.com's guides to the classic Goat Format.
- The **BOOSTER PACKS** page of the POKéMON side opens on the first pack you have to open, as YU-GI-OH! and MAGIC already did, and every booster page moves on to another kind when you've opened the last of one.

## The PC
- **L2 / R2** (Q / E on the keyboard) go to the box before or after. **Hold one** and the box shrinks into a grid of every box, right where the box sits, each with its own wallpaper and POKéMON. Move to a box, press A or L2 / R2 again, and the PC goes there and zooms back in. B goes back to the box you came from.

## Other touches
- **OPTIONS > GAME > MUTE IN BACKGROUND**: the game goes quiet while another window is in front.
- **Healing** fills the HP bar faster: POTIONS, FULL RESTORE, REVIVE and healing moves move as quickly as your BATTLE SPEED (30% faster at +30%).
- **EGGS** hatch in half the steps. FLAME BODY and MAGMA ARMOR still speed them up on top of that.
- **The credits** can be skipped: press START, then START again to confirm.
