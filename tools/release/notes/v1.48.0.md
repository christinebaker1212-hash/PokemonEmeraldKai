Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.48.0 "Every Card, Every Effect!": Yu-Gi-Oh! duels now run on EDOPro's duel engine, decks come from codes, and duelists bring real decks from the old games.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, settings and cards stay as they are.
Playing online works with 1.44.0 and later, but card games with friends need everyone on 1.48.0.

## New
- Yu-Gi-Oh! duels are played by EDOPro's duel engine: every card does exactly what its text says, with today's
  rules for chains, timing and the Damage Step. Every card in the game can now go in a deck.
- Cards you can activate in more than one way show each choice in its own words, and the engine's questions (which
  cards to Tribute, Levels that must add up for a Ritual, a card name to declare, the order of cards on your Deck)
  are asked on the table.
- FROM CODE: an empty deck slot offers BUILD A DECK, FROM RECIPE and FROM CODE. Copy a YDKE deck code and it shows
  up on its own; your cards make the deck, the ones you don't have yet show gray, and the deck is ready to duel once
  you have them all. A code with cards the game doesn't have is turned down.
- DECK CODE in a deck's menu copies its code to share it.
- On Windows: FROM .YDK FILE and EXPORT .YDK open and save EDOPro / YGOPro deck files.
- Forbidden & Limited lists: the DECKS screen's LIST row picks the one your decks follow, the game's own or any of
  EDOPro's (the current TCG and OCG lists, GOAT and more).
- Most duelists you meet now bring a deck once played by an opponent in the Game Boy Advance Yu-Gi-Oh! games
  (Stairway to the Destined Duel, The Eternal Duelist Soul, World Championship 2004, Reshef of Destruction, The Sacred
  Cards), weaker ones early on and stronger ones as you earn badges.

## Changed
- Your opponent's cards on the Yu-Gi-Oh! table now face them, upside down to you, as across a real table.
- Yu-Gi-Oh! opponents play from what they can see on the table; they never peek at your hand or face-down cards.
