Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.56.2 "A Moment to Breathe!": Yu-Gi-Oh! phase banners only when something happens, and the end of a card duel takes its time.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, cards and decks stay as they are.

## Yu-Gi-Oh!
- A phase's banner only appears when something can happen in it: a card drawn, a card played, an attack, or a
  chance for you to chain. Phases that pass with nothing to do go by quietly.
- While a turn or phase banner plays, the card name and details in the middle line fade out, and fade back in once
  it's your move again.

## POKéMON and Yu-Gi-Oh! card duels
- The end of a duel takes a breath: YOU WIN! or YOU LOSE... shows on its own for a second, then the message box
  opens, and a second later the words and prizes appear in it.
