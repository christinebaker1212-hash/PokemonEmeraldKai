Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.63.6 "Fresh from the Pack!": NEW on the cards you just got, and booster packs front and center.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
1.63.6 plays online with friends on 1.63.0 to 1.63.5 too.

## New
- **NEW cards in the BINDER.** In all three card games, a card you got since you last looked at it in the BINDER
  shows NEW, whether it came from a pack, a prize, a trade or a duel. Point at it and the tag goes.
- **Booster packs front and center.** In the POKéMON and Yu-Gi-Oh! BOOSTER PACKS, the pack you're choosing sits in the
  middle of the screen, with the others gliding by on both sides.
