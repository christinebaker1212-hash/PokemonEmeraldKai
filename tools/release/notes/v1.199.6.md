Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.199.6 "All Aboard the ROYAL UNOVA!": the pleasure boat sails, Triple Battles come to UNOVA, and more of Black's town life.

UNOVA
- The ROYAL UNOVA sails from CASTELIA's pier. Each day's passengers are dealt to the cabins as in Black: more trainers aboard on weekends, a few other guests and the rest of the travellers. A chime marks each part of the trip, talking to passengers passes the time, and after the fourth chime the boat comes back to port.
- Trainers who ask for a Triple Battle now get one: three of your POKéMON against three of theirs. Rotation Battle trainers fight a Double Battle instead.
- CASTELIA's buildings have working elevators: choose a floor from the panel and ride there.
- Catch every POKéMON in UNOVA and the GAME FREAK office awards you a diploma, now naming the UNOVA POKéDEX (or the National one).
- At the GEAR STATION, the girl who collects battle phrases opens the EASY CHAT on your own introduction, win and loss phrases.
- NACRENE's gym shelves can be checked where they've moved to, and the man who checks your TMs, the HIDDEN POWER reader and the ZORUA girl's POKé BALL check all work as in Black.
- Rooms you enter through a door inside the same building now set up their people as Black does.

Key items
- Key items you get in UNOVA that other regions also give (the TOWN MAP, the VS. RECORDER, the PAL PAD, the GRACIDEA, the BICYCLE) are UNOVA's own copies and follow UNOVA's region code.

Diplomas
- Diplomas in every region name the region you're in, instead of always saying HOENN.
