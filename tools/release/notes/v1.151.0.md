Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.151.0 "Show And Tell!": 39 more MAGIC cards that reveal, remember and check.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- Cards can reveal cards from your hand, and the table names each one as it's shown.
- Effects can now look back at the cards an earlier part of the spell moved, sacrificed or revealed, and parts of a
  spell that only happen "if" now check first.
- Spells whose target belongs to a later part of the card (like SCENT OF IVY) ask for it as you cast them.
- 39 more cards can be played, among them SCENT OF IVY, IVY SEER, SOUL REND, RUPTURE, STITCH TOGETHER, FORGET,
  URZA'S BAUBLE, SCROLL RACK and VOLCANIC ERUPTION. Every one has its printed art.
