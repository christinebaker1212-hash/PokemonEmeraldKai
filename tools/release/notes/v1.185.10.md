Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.185.10 "Out of the Ring!": no more getting walled in at VEILSTONE's gym, and JOHTO's phone and SINNOH's story keep apart.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## SINNOH
- VEILSTONE CITY's gym: if you saved after pushing the punching bags, loading the save put every bag back where it
  started, which could wall you in beside MAYLENE with no way out. The bags and tire stacks now stay where you left
  them when you save in the gym, as in Platinum. If you saved in the gym with an older version, the bags are already
  pushed out of the way when you load, so you can walk out.
- SNOWPOINT CITY's gym keeps its broken snowballs when you save there too.

## JOHTO and SINNOH
- JOHTO's phone and parts of SINNOH's story were kept in the same place in your save. Registering numbers on JOHTO's
  POKéGEAR could make SINNOH skip story events (like BARRY's in TWINLEAF TOWN, the VALLEY WINDWORKS or LAKE VERITY),
  and playing SINNOH could scramble your JOHTO phone contacts. They're now kept apart; your save is sorted out by
  itself the first time you load it.
- The first boat to KANTO no longer blanks two letters of your JOHTO rival's name.
