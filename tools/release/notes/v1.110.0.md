Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.110.0 "Jump For Joy!": TWO ISLAND's JOYFUL GAME CORNER plays its games again, and JOHTO's last climb waits for the strongest.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## TWO ISLAND's JOYFUL GAME CORNER
- The games are back, as in FireRed. Talk to the attendant to play **POKéMON JUMP** or **DODRIO BERRY PICKING** with friends online. Everyone plays with the first POKéMON in their party that the game allows.
- The man beside him explains each game's rules.

## JOHTO
- **MT. SILVER** opens once you hold all 16 BADGES, as in Crystal. RED waits at the top with his team from Crystal.
- **LAKE OF RAGE**: LANCE asks for your help himself, as in Crystal, and it no longer rains there. The trainers around the lake come out once the hideout in MAHOGANY is dealt with.
- **ROUTE 42**: SUICUNE shows up there after you've seen it at CIANWOOD.
- **BLACKTHORN**: the GYM stays shut until the RADIO TOWER is freed, and the DRAGON'S DEN until you've beaten CLAIR, with Crystal's words.

## KANTO
- The ELITE FOUR and the CHAMPION come back with stronger teams, as in FireRed, once the network machine on ONE ISLAND is fixed.
