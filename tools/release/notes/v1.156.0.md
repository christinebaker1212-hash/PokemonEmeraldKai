Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.156.0 "Just For Now!": 25 more MAGIC cards that change the rules for a while.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- Spells and abilities that change the rules for a while now work: a creature that can't be blocked this turn,
  creatures that can't block, a player who can't cast creatures, a creature that must attack or can't regenerate.
- 25 more cards can be played, among them FALTER, INFILTRATE, CEASE-FIRE, ABEYANCE, HURR JACKAL, DWARVEN WARRIORS,
  BOILING BLOOD and INSTIGATOR. Every one has its printed art.
