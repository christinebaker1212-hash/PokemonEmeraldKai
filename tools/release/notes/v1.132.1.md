Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.132.1 "Clean Sound!": the arcade cabinets sound clean again, and the game keeps up in the background.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## Arcade cabinets
- The GAME CENTER's arcade cabinets (the CAPCOM fighters, PUZZLE BOBBLE and TETRIS THE GRAND MASTER 2 PLUS) no longer crackle or stutter. Their sound now plays as one steady stream at the board's own speed instead of being squeezed or stretched every frame.

## In the background
- On Windows the game asks not to be slowed down when its window isn't in front, so it keeps its full speed while you look at something else.
- While the window is minimized the game no longer draws pictures nobody can see, and waits calmly between frames instead of running flat out.
