Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.28.1 "Picture Perfect!": the real printed cards arrive, in three looks, with the real card back.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, settings and cards stay as they are.
1.28.1 plays online with friends on 1.28.0 too.

## New
- The printed cards come with the game now: 209 of the 228 cards, every one the game shares with the real card game.
- OPTIONS > DISPLAY > CARD ART has three looks. PRINTED ART (the new default): each card's printed picture in the
  Game Boy card frame, with a wider picture on Trainer cards, as printed. GAME BOY: the original Game Boy pictures in
  the same frame. FULL CARD: the whole printed card.
- OPTIONS > DISPLAY > CARD BACKS: the real printed card back (the new default) or the pixel-art one.
- The big picture shows the printed card or its picture filling the screen.
- The 19 cards that were only ever in the Game Boy game (MEW's MYSTERY ATTACK, IMAKUNI? and the others) keep
  their Game Boy picture in every look.

## Fixed
- CARD ART showed the Game Boy pictures whatever it was set to.
- A card trade could finish for only one of the two friends: the other was told their friend had left, and their
  card never arrived. Both sides now get their card.
