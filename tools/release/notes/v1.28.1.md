Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.28.1 "Picture Perfect!": the printed card pictures arrive, with three ways to show cards.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, settings and cards stay as they are.
Online, 1.28.1 plays with 1.28.0.

## New
- OPTIONS > DISPLAY > CARD ART has three choices:
  - PRINTED ART: the picture from the real printed card inside the classic card frame (the new default).
  - PRINTED CARD: the whole printed card.
  - GAME BOY: the Game Boy pictures inside the classic card frame.

## Fixed
- Cards always showed the Game Boy pictures: the printed card pictures never came with the game. They now download by
  themselves, once (about 28 MB), right after this update installs. ESC skips them for now; the game asks again at the
  next start. Until then, CARD ART says they haven't downloaded yet.
