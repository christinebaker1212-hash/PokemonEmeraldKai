Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.185.16 "On Thin Ice!": SNOWPOINT CITY's gym can no longer trap you sliding back and forth.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## SINNOH
- SNOWPOINT GYM: sliding up a slope you couldn't climb sent you back down, and on some stretches of level ice you then
  slid up the other slope and back again forever, with no way to move. Now you slide back to the foot of the slope
  and stop there, as on Platinum's ice, and every part of the gym can still be reached.
