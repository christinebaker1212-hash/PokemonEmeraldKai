Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.5.0: jump into a friend's battle.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
Play online only with friends on 1.5.0 too (their game updates itself the same way). If you run the standalone server
on another computer, replace it with the one in this release.

## New
- **JOIN a friend's battle.** Walk up to a friend who is IN BATTLE and press A: "Would you like to spectate, bet, or
  join?" Pick JOIN and, at the start of their next turn, they're asked "<your name> wants to join your battle! Is
  this okay?". If they say yes, your trainer slides in ("<your name> has come to help!") and sends out your first
  Moemon; if they say no, you're told so (and they aren't asked again in that battle). B while you wait: never mind.
  - Works for wild Moemon and ordinary trainers (not gym leaders, rivals, TEAM AQUA / MAGMA or other story battles).
  - A battle alone becomes a Double Battle, or a Triple Battle if two friends join at once; a Double Battle with a
    friend becomes a Triple Battle. In a Double Battle you started alone, your second Moemon goes back to its Ball
    and your friend takes its spot.
  - Everyone brings their Moemon in party order, the one out now first: three each in a Double Battle, two each in a
    Triple. The rest wait in your party just as they are, and any that leveled up still evolve (and learn their
    moves) after a win.
  - The trainer gets a spot for each new player and sends out its next Moemon there if it has one (with TEAM PADDING,
    at most one extra Moemon).
  - Talking to a friend's partner works too: you're sent on to whoever started the battle.

## Fixed
- A Triple Battle that started as a Double Battle now shows the Triple Battle cards.
- Trainers made in LOOK > CREATE stand lower in battle, at Brendan's and May's height, and their whole throw shows:
  the hand no longer disappears while they throw a POKé BALL.
