Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.206.0 "Every Card in Play!": all 1,227 regional cards can now be played, and a new CARD GEN setting picks which sets your card games use.

POKéMON CARDS
- The last ten cards of the regional sets can now be played, so every one of the 1,227 is in the game:
  - Koga's Ninja Trick and Charity: after the attack, decide whether to switch or to take 10 less damage.
  - Vermilion City Gym: flip its coin before the attack's damage.
  - Sabrina's ESP: keep your coins or flip them all again.
  - Misty's Psyduck: copy one of the Defending POKéMON's attacks.
  - Narrow Gym: each player picks which POKéMON leave a Bench of more than four.
  - Brock's Ninetales: Shapeshift takes the shape of an Evolution card from your hand.
  - Sabrina's Psychic Control: use a Trainer from your opponent's discard pile as your own.
  - Chaos Gym: on tails, your opponent may use the Trainer you played.
  - Lt. Surge's Secret Plan: put any card from your hand face down onto your Bench. It plays as a Basic POKéMON until it's turned over, and if it isn't one it's discarded.
- The sets' theme decks now hold Vermilion City Gym and Sabrina's Psychic Control, as the real decks did.

CARD GEN
- New settings: OPTIONS > GAME > POKéMON CARD GEN and MAGIC GEN. Set ALL, or choose the newest set your card games may use: from BASE SET through the nine regional sets for POKéMON, and by set release for MAGIC.
- Card players only bring decks made of allowed sets. A deck of yours that holds a newer card is turned away at the challenge, with that card and its set named.
- Online, the host sets both in HOST A GAME > GAME RULES.
- Online card duels and games need everyone on this version.
