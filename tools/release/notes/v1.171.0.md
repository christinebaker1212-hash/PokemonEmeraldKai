Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.171.0 "Westward Ho!": a first look at ORRE, a far-off desert region reached by boat after the ELITE FOUR.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
1.171.0 plays online with friends on 1.170.0 and 1.170.1 too.

## ORRE (a work in progress)
- Beat any region's ELITE FOUR and an ORRE TICKET finds you, right after the BATTLE TICKET (saves past that point get it
  too): SCOTT in HOENN, a sailor in JOHTO and KANTO, a letter in SINNOH.
- LILYCOVE's and SLATEPORT's ferries list ORRE among their destinations, and every other region's port offers it. The
  boat lands at the OUTSKIRT STAND, where a sailor waits to take you back to the port you came from.
- ORRE's world is its own download. The game fetches it by itself when it starts, in the background while you play;
  if it can't, it tries again next time. If you reach the boat before it's done, the clerk shows how far along it is.
- ORRE is still being built: its towns, buildings and world map are there, but its people and story aren't yet.

## Ferries
- LILYCOVE's destination list scrolls when you hold more tickets than the window can show, and SLATEPORT's list now
  shows exactly the trips your tickets open.
