Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.41.0 "Friends at the Table!": TRIPLE TRIAD with your friends: games and card trades online.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
Playing online needs everyone on 1.41.0.

## New
- TRIPLE TRIAD with friends: CO-OP > FRIENDS > a friend > CARD DUEL or CARD TRADE, with TRIAD picked (LEFT / RIGHT
  now go round MOEMON, YU-GI-OH! and TRIAD). Or press the CARD CASE's button in front of a friend while your case is
  on its TRIPLE TRIAD side.
  - A game: the one who asks brings the rules of the region they're in, and each of you picks a hand. Under RANDOM,
    each gets five cards dealt from their own collection. The table shows every SAME, PLUS and COMBO on both screens.
  - Games between friends never trade cards and don't spread rules, but a win counts toward OPEN RULES, SHUFFLE OR
    BOOGIE and TABLE TALK.
  - A trade: offer any card you hold beyond the hand in use (L / R pick the stars), see what they offer, and press
    START when you're both happy.
