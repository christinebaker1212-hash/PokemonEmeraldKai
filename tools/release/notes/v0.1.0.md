Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 0.1.0 "First Build!": Moemon Emerald Kai on PC, with friends.

The very first test build (October 18), passed around by hand.

## New
- Moemon Emerald Kai as a game for Windows and Linux, with online co-op for up to 4 players and PvP battles.
- An online lobby: host a server with a name and an optional password, or pick a friend's from the list.
- GO ONLINE from START > CO-OP, in the middle of a game.
- Wear any trainer or NPC sprite, with your own skin and outfit colors, or a sprite pack for every animation.
- EDIT SPRITES in the CO-OP menu, and a sprite editor to make your own packs.
- DISPLAY: real fullscreen, scaling modes and screen filters.
