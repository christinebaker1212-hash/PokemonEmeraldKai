Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.172.0 "Pocket Nursery!": an EGG INCUBATOR in the phone app, and the app keeps up with your game.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are. Update the phone app
too (MENU > UPDATE): the incubator needs both.
1.172.0 plays online with friends on 1.171.6 too.

## New
- EGG INCUBATOR in the phone app (WALK): up to twelve EGGS can ride in your phone at once, and your real steps hatch
  them, a cycle every 32 steps, each counting from when it went in. Add them from your party or boxes with + ADD EGG.
- Bringing them home: tap the EGGS you want (or bring them all), then choose a box with room for every one of them.
  The ones that are ready hatch on the way home; the rest come back as EGGS, closer to hatching than before.
- Your phone lets you know when an EGG in the incubator is ready to hatch.

## Changed
- The phone app's POKéDEX: SEEN now shows only the POKéMON you've seen and not caught yet, and CAUGHT (once OWNED)
  shows the ones you've caught.

## Fixed
- The phone app comes with updates again: every update since 1.131.1 went out without it, so phones stayed on
  1.131.0's app. This one brings everything the app gained since.
- The phone app's WALK page now keeps up with your game: training you start or stop, a walk and its numbers show
  right away, without closing and opening the app.
- Training's time estimates on the phone no longer fall behind after you do something in the app.
- Typing in the phone app works: renaming, the POKéDEX search and every other text box take what you type, letter by
  letter, with any phone keyboard.
- The phone app's POKéMON card table no longer closes when a Trainer card sits in the Active spot.
