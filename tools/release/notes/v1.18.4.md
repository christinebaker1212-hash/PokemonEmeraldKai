Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.18.4 "Weather the Storm!": weather you can see the whole battle.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
1.17.x and 1.18.x play together online.

## Changed
- Battle weather stays on screen from start to end: the rain keeps falling and the field turns a little darker, hail
  keeps coming down, the sandstorm blows across the field, and in strong sunlight the Moemon look brighter. All of it
  uses the game's own weather art.
- So the battle no longer repeats "Rain continues to fall.", "The sunlight is strong." and the like with a weather
  animation every turn. You still see when weather starts and stops, and who the sandstorm or hail hurts.
