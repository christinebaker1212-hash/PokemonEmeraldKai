Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.165.0 "And There Goes the Battle!": POKéMON STADIUM's announcer calls the STADIUM's battles.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## The announcer
- POKéMON STADIUM's own announcer now calls the battles on BATTLE ISLAND: "Oh! It's PIKACHU!" when a POKéMON comes
  out, the moves as they're used, super effective hits, critical hits, misses, statuses, faints, the last POKéMON in
  reserve and the win. On the VS screen he introduces the GYM LEADER CASTLE's trainers, leaders, ELITE FOUR and
  champion, and he cheers a stage cleared and the champion decided.
- Battles here move much faster than in POKéMON STADIUM, so the announcer only says a line when it can finish before
  the battle moves on. He learns how fast your battle goes, including your text speed and how quickly you press A
  through the messages, and picks a shorter line or keeps quiet when there isn't time. A line is never cut off by
  another.
- OPTIONS > BATTLE > ANNOUNCER turns him on and off (on to start with).
- The voices come with the game in its stadium folder. If they're missing, putting your own POKéMON STADIUM (USA) ROM
  in that folder as "Pokemon Stadium (USA).z64" works too.
