Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.148.0 "Top Picks!": 21 more MAGIC cards that look at the top of your library.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- Spells that look at the top cards of a library now work. The cards fan out on the table, you take the ones you want
  and the rest go where the card says: to the bottom, to the graveyard or back on top.
- 21 more cards can be played, among them IMPULSE, ANCESTRAL MEMORIES, SLEIGHT OF HAND, PROPHETIC BOLT, GOBLIN
  RINGLEADER, WORLDLY COUNSEL, CALL OF THE WILD, DIABOLIC VISION and SEALED FATE. Every one has its printed art.
