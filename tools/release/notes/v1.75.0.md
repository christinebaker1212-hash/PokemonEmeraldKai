Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.75.0 "Trades by Mail!": trade POKéMON with friends from your phone, even when you're not playing at the same time.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are. Update MOEMON
COMPANION too (it offers the update when you open it).

## MOEMON COMPANION: TRADES BY MAIL
- **Offer a trade:** on FRIENDS, tap TRADE beside a friend, pick one of your POKéMON, and choose what you'd like for it:
  anything they pick, or one of the POKéMON in their party. Your POKéMON stays in your game, marked ON OFFER (the PC
  shows it, and it can't be released, sent on a walk or put in training while it's on offer).
- **Answer an offer:** a friend's offers show at the top of FRIENDS (and a notification tells you one came). ACCEPT
  and pick which of yours goes, or DECLINE. You can CALL OFF your own offer until your friend answers.
- **The swap:** your game and your friend's game send their POKéMON the next time each is online on the same co-op
  server. You don't need to play at the same time. Neither POKéMON can go missing or be copied: if your game closes
  at the wrong moment, it picks up where it was.
- **The trade plays in your game:** the classic trade scene, with your friend's name, and any trade evolution.
- Offers run out after 7 days.
- Friends you met before this update: play together in a room once more, then you can trade by mail.

## Co-op servers
- Play online with friends on 1.75.0 (their game updates itself the same way). If you run your own server, update it
  too: it now keeps the POKéMON of trades in progress safe (saved in moemon_escrow.dat next to it: keep that file).
