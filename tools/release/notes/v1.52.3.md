Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.52.3 "Savor the Win!": the victory song lasts while you take your card, and no button presses slip in during card animations.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## Card games
- After you win a POKéMON card duel, a Yu-Gi-Oh! duel or a game of TRIPLE TRIAD, the victory song keeps playing while
  you choose which card to take, and the place's own music comes back once you're done.
- Yu-Gi-Oh! duels now play the victory song when you win.
- While the table is showing what happened (an attack, a card being drawn, a coin flip, a Pokémon or monster being
  Knocked Out or destroyed, a Prize card being taken, a Tribute Summon, a card being captured...), button presses are
  ignored. Nothing can be chosen in the middle of an animation, and no press waits to go through after it ends.
- TRIPLE TRIAD's deal and its spin for who goes first always play out.
