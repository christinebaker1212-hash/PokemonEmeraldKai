Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.207.0 "Worth the Fight!": a new EXP CURVE setting, SCALED, makes trainer and wild battles rewarding again with LEVEL SCALING on.

LEVEL SCALING
- New EXP CURVE setting: SCALED (OPTIONS > GAME > LEVEL SCALING > EXP CURVE; online, the host's GAME RULES). A beaten POKéMON gives the EXP it would give at 5 levels below the level LEVEL SCALING set it to, with no cut for being past the area. Trainer battles feel worth fighting again, and wild battles give good EXP.
- SCALED is now the default. If your EXP CURVE was on GENTLE, the old default, it moves to SCALED. OFF, GENTLE and STRONG are still there if you prefer them.
- Online, everyone in the room needs this version.
