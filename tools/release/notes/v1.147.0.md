Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.147.0 "Back on Top!": 30 more MAGIC cards, the tutors among them.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- Cards can now go on top of a library, on the bottom, or a few cards down. The tutors find their card, shuffle the
  rest and leave the find right on top for your next draw.
- 30 more cards can be played, among them ENLIGHTENED TUTOR, MYSTICAL TUTOR, VAMPIRIC TUTOR, WORLDLY TUTOR, REPEL,
  TIME EBB, SUBMERGE, AURA EXTRACTION, PLOW UNDER and REINFORCEMENTS. Every one has its printed art.
