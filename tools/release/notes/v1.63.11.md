Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.63.11 "Side by Side!": followers stand on the ground beside you, steady shadows, and each save file keeps its own LOOK.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
1.63.11 plays online with friends on 1.63.7 to 1.63.10 too.

## Fixed
- **Followers stand beside you:** a follower's feet and shadow line up with yours instead of floating a little
  higher, and its body stays right behind you whichever way it faces (it leaned to one side facing left, right or
  away).
- **Followers walk with both feet:** the walk no longer starts over at every step, so the whole stride shows when
  walking left and right.
- **Smaller followers are small from the start:** with SPRITE SCALING on, a follower comes out of its ball at its own
  size (it popped out big and shrank), and its reflection in water is the same size too.
- **Shadows no longer flicker:** for a frame or two a shadow could turn solid, and its owner see-through, while the
  screen changed.
- **Each save file keeps its own LOOK:** going back to another save file no longer brings the LOOK, CREATE look or
  sprite sheet from the file you just played.
- **KANTO's gifts name the right item and pocket:** OAK's PARCEL, the rods, fossils, gym TMs and other gifts said
  "????????" in place of the item, and the message named the wrong BAG pocket (POKé BALLS in MEDICINE). JOHTO's
  messages name the right pocket too.
- **CELADON's rooftop girls** take the drink they asked for and give their gift.
- **The starter table:** in KANTO and JOHTO you can't step back from the table and walk off before choosing.
- **CHERRYGROVE:** a TEAM ROCKET grunt and his GROWLITHE no longer stand by the POKéMON CENTER on a new game (they
  belong to a story far later, and were in AZALEA, ROUTE 35 and GOLDENROD too).
- **Y / Triangle is only the CO-OP button:** it no longer also works as SELECT in menus, messages and battles, so
  opening the CO-OP menu can't use your registered item. SELECT is Back / Share / - on a controller.
