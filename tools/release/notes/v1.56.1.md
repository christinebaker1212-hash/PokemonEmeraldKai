Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.56.1 "Phase by Phase!": every Yu-Gi-Oh! phase announced, direct attacks that hit the player, and win screens that fit their words.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, cards and decks stay as they are.

## Yu-Gi-Oh!
- Every phase is announced as it begins: DRAW PHASE, STANDBY PHASE, MAIN PHASE 1, BATTLE PHASE, MAIN PHASE 2 and END
  PHASE sweep across the table, after YOUR TURN or OPPONENT'S TURN, just like the turn banners in the POKéMON card
  game. A card you can use in two phases in a row (Mystical Space Typhoon in the Draw and Standby Phases) now asks
  once in each, and you can see which.
- A direct attack now goes for the player: the monster dashes to the middle of the far edge of its target's side of
  the table. The blow sounds the moment it slams home, and the damage shows right after.
- The end of a duel looks like the POKéMON card game's: YOU WIN! or YOU LOSE... on a band with who you played,
  then how the duel ended and what it brought.

## All three card games
- The message box at the end of a duel grows to fit everything in it, so a card player's parting words are never
  cut off.
