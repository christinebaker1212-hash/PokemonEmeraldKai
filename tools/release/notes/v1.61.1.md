Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.61.1 "Johto Jamboree!": JOHTO and KANTO battle again at GEN 3, the Bug-Catching Contest, the RUINS OF ALPH puzzles and each gimmick on the host's rules.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
1.61.1 plays online with friends on 1.61.0 too.

## Fixed
- **POKéMON couldn't battle in JOHTO and KANTO.** At GEN 3, almost every POKéMON was SEALED there, as if its
  ability came from a later generation, so a trainer battle could end in a whiteout after the first POKéMON fainted.
  JOHTO and KANTO now seal only what GEN 3 seals anywhere else. (At GEN 3 their wild POKéMON and trainers' teams
  still stay the ones of their own games.)
- **The RUINS OF ALPH's stone panels froze the game.** Each chamber's panel is now its sliding puzzle: rebuild the
  picture of KABUTO, OMANYTE, AERODACTYL or HO-OH to open the way below.
- **A crash on some warps in JOHTO and KANTO**, among them the way into the NATIONAL PARK's contest.
- **The credits after KANTO's or JOHTO's HALL OF FAME** crashed for a trainer who hadn't caught any POKéMON.
  After them, CONTINUE now starts in the region's first town, and they no longer count as HOENN's game cleared.
- **Beating RED on MT. SILVER** now rolls the credits and saves, as the original does.

## New
- **The Bug-Catching Contest** in JOHTO's NATIONAL PARK: bring your first POKéMON and 30 SAFARI BALLS, and catch
  bug POKéMON for eight minutes (up to five). Every POKéMON you catch is yours to keep, and the judges score your
  best one by its level, strength, rarity and shininess. Prizes: a SUN STONE, an EVERSTONE, a SITRUS BERRY or an
  ORAN BERRY. Your other POKéMON and your own SAFARI BALLS wait at the gate and come back afterwards.
- **HOST A GAME > GAME RULES** has MEGA EVOLUTION, Z-MOVES, DYNAMAX and TERA on rows of their own (AUTO, ON or
  OFF), like OPTIONS > GAME. The list scrolls.
