Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.32.0 "Game On!": the GAME CENTER opens in RUSTBORO CITY, and that last holo is worth chasing.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, settings and cards stay as they are.
Playing online needs everyone on 1.32.0.

## New
- GAME CENTER: a brand-new tower in RUSTBORO CITY's north-east corner, right by the road east. Its tower is marked
  on the Town Map, the MOENAV map and the FLY map.
- 1F RECEPTION: admission is 500, good for every floor until you leave. The stairs and the elevator are behind the
  reception.
- 2F CARD SHOP: the best card selection in HOENN. Buy any card of the four sets with money (rares too), or one of
  three structure decks, the 1999 theme decks OVERGROWTH, ZAP! and BRUSHFIRE, all 60 cards, ready to play in an
  empty deck slot. No CARD CASE yet? The owner hands you one. The players at the table duel you whenever you talk
  to them. A second seller is still unpacking his supplies...
- 3F GAMES: consoles, TVs and arcade cabinets, and a COIN counter (50 COINS for 1000).
- ROOFTOP: the SKY LOUNGE, with a view over the city and drinks from the vending machines.

## Changed
- Booster packs: the rare in a pack is a HOLO one pack in three. Holos only come from packs and trades, so
  finishing a set takes real effort now. After 20 packs of a set without a holo you didn't have, the next pack
  brings one, so the hunt never goes on forever.
- Opening a pack marks the cards you didn't have as NEW, holos shine with a HOLO! foil, and the pack that finishes a
  set says so with a fanfare.
- CARD POINTS: spare cards are worth 1 / 2 / 6 by rarity (a holo 20), a pack at the CARD COUNTER costs 40, and the
  counters sell commons and uncommons (rares are at the GAME CENTER).
- A win's card from your opponent's deck can't be a holo.
