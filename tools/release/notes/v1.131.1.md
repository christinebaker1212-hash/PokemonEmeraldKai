Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.131.1 "Smooth Sailing!": no more hitches when the game saves its records, a smoother MoeGatcha, a crash fix and SINNOH fixes.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## No more hitching
- The game keeps many records next to your save (the BAG, the PC boxes, the Garden, the cards, the POKéDEX, the TRAINER CARD, the PHONE LINK and more). Writing them used to pause the game for a moment, many times in a walk or a battle. They are now written in the background, so the game never waits on them.
- Your co-op records are written once after a change instead of once for every small change.

## Fixes
- OPTIONS > RETURN TO MAIN MENU no longer crashes the game on Windows when people are walking around nearby.

## MoeGatcha
- Changing banners and pulling no longer stutter.
- No POKéMON with perfect IVs come from the MoeGatcha any more. TRAINING gives RARE CANDIES, vitamins and its held items.

## Eggs
- Eggs hatch in half the time again (on top of the last change).

## SINNOH
- Battles against TEAM GALACTIC, its commanders and its boss, the Frontier Brains, and the legendary POKéMON (the lake trio, DIALGA and PALKIA, GIRATINA, the REGIS, ARCEUS and the rest) play their own themes from Platinum.
- Cave mouths you walk into from the side, like ROUTE 207's way into MT. CORONET, take you inside again.
- Signposts and mailboxes show up in towns and on routes, where Platinum has them.
