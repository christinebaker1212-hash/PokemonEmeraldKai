Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.199.10 "Change of Forme!": POKéMON forms in every region, ROTOM's appliances, and SINNOH's BATTLE FRONTIER records.

POKéMON forms
- POKéMON with more than one form now keep their form outside battle in every region, with its own stats, types, Ability, pictures and party icon: ROTOM's appliances, DEOXYS's Formes, SHAYMIN's Sky Forme, GIRATINA's Origin Forme, BURMY's and WORMADAM's cloaks, SHELLOS's and GASTRODON's East Sea, ARCEUS's PLATEs, DEERLING's and SAWSBUCK's seasons and BASCULIN's stripes.
- DEOXYS depends on where you meet it: Speed Forme in HOENN, Attack Forme in KANTO, Normal Forme in JOHTO, SINNOH and UNOVA. Touch a meteorite in SINNOH or UNOVA to change its Forme.
- Use the GRACIDEA from the BAG on SHAYMIN during the day for its Sky Forme. It changes back at night.
- BURMY takes the cloak of the place it last battled in.
- GIRATINA takes its Origin Forme while holding the GRISEOUS ORB, and in the DISTORTION WORLD.
- ARCEUS changes type with the PLATE it holds.
- Trainers in UNOVA bring their POKéMON in the forms Black gives them.

SINNOH
- ROTOM's room in ETERNA's Galactic building is open, no SECRET KEY needed. Bring a ROTOM to the microwave, washing machine, fridge, fan or lawn mower to change its form and its special move.
- Each BATTLE FRONTIER lobby has its records board: check your SINGLE, DOUBLE and MULTI records, your CASTLE POINTS, and each POKéMON's BATTLE HALL record.
- The BATTLE HALL keeps every POKéMON's best streak. Its record keeper adds them up and gives BATTLE POINTS at each milestone. SERENA or WINSTON cheers you on, and a special visitor may drop by after a challenge.
- The CONTEST HALL's practice desk is open again for VISUAL, DANCE and ACTING practice.

UNOVA
- The BATTLE SUBWAY's route map can be looked at, as in Black.
