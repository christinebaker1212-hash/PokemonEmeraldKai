Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.185.6 "Victory Road!": JOHTO's VICTORY ROAD can be crossed again, so the POKéMON LEAGUE is in reach.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## JOHTO
- On VICTORY ROAD's second floor, a boulder in the narrow passage to the ladder up could only be pushed into a corner,
  where it stuck and blocked the way for good, so the POKéMON LEAGUE couldn't be reached. The boulder now sits beside
  the passage: push it aside with STRENGTH and the way up is open. If it's stuck in your game, leave the floor and
  come back.
