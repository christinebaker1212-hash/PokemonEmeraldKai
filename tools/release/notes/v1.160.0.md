Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.160.0 "Tap Into It!": 37 more MAGIC cards that react to taps, lands and upkeeps.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- Cards that react when something becomes tapped now work: CITY OF BRASS hurts you when you tap it, PSYCHIC VENOM and
  BLIGHT punish the enchanted land, SEIZURES the enchanted creature.
- Cards that react when a land is played now work: CITY OF TRAITORS, BURGEONING, HORN OF GREED.
- Auras that hit the enchanted card's controller in that player's upkeep now work: CURSED LAND, WARP ARTIFACT,
  LINGERING DEATH, FEEDBACK.
- "That player may" now asks the right player: with FECUNDITY, a creature's controller decides whether to draw.
- LAND TAX and other cards that check a count before they act now work.
- 37 more cards can be played. Every one has its printed art.
