Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.114.1 "Steady Bars!": healing in battle fills the HP bar once, at every BATTLE SPEED.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## Battles
- LEFTOVERS and other healing after a sandstorm or hail no longer make the HP bar fill, empty and fill again over and over at the quicker BATTLE SPEED settings: the bar fills once and the battle goes on.
