Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.19.0: level scaling.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
Online, everyone needs 1.19.0: it can't play with 1.18.x.

## New
- LEVEL SCALING (OPTIONS > GAME, on at first): wild Moemon and trainers keep up with your team, so Double and Triple
  Battles don't leave the whole game behind.
  - Your team's level is the average of your three highest-level Moemon; in a co-op battle, of everyone's teams.
  - Trainers' strongest Moemon are at your team's level. Gym Leaders, Team Aqua / Magma bosses, your rival, the Elite
    Four and the Champion are 2 to 5 levels above it. Wild Moemon are 3 to 6 below.
  - Nothing is ever weaker than it normally is, and nothing goes above a cap that rises with your badges (18 with
    none, 65 with all eight). After the League there's no cap.
  - A raised Moemon evolves if it would have by that level, and knows the moves it would know at it.
  - EXP is the usual for the level they are.
  - Online, the host's setting counts for everyone.

## Fixed
- Summary screen: a JOURNAL with only one page showed the INFO prompt at the top, and pressing A there mixed
  move text into the page. Now the top shows A MORE only when there's another page, and A does nothing on a one-page
  journal.
- Summary screen: LEFT on the first page goes to the last one, and RIGHT on the last goes back to the first.
- Reflections in water: your follower's and your friends' reflections now have the same pale, watery look as your
  own, instead of their full colors.
