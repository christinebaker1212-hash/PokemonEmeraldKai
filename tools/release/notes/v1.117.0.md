Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.117.0 "Spear of Time!": SINNOH's story plays through SPEAR PILLAR as Platinum has it.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## SINNOH's story
- At the top of SPEAR PILLAR, team up with BARRY against MARS and JUPITER, then watch CYRUS call DIALGA and PALKIA, in Platinum's own words. The pillar tears open and the way into the DISTORTION WORLD follows, as in Platinum.
- In GALACTIC HQ, the big button now frees MESPRIT, AZELF and UXIE.
- RILEY's scenes on IRON ISLAND play as in Platinum.
- People who have nothing to say in Platinum no longer say lines that were written for this game, and more signs and people say Platinum's own words.
- ETERNA CITY's herb shop sells its herbs.

## Fixes
- Talking to CYRUS at SPEAR PILLAR no longer skips straight into the DISTORTION WORLD.
- Fixed a rare crash when the game starts.
