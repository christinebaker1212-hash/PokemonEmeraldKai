Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.70.3 "True Colors!": the phone app shows POKéMON in their own colors and waits out battles.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MOEMON COMPANION
- **True colors:** every POKéMON's picture showed its shiny colors. Now each one looks as it does in the game: shiny
  ones shiny, nicknamed ones in their own hue, the rest in their usual colors.
- **Battles:** while you're in a battle, the app doesn't move POKéMON or give or take items. The top bar says IN
  BATTLE until it's over.
- **Co-op:** in a battle with friends, the app's PARTY shows only your own POKéMON, never your friends'.
