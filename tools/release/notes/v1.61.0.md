Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.61.0 "Nine Generations!": every POKéMON up to the ninth generation, MEGA EVOLUTION, Z-MOVES, DYNAMAX and TERA, and a GENERATION setting to bring them in.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save stays as it is.

## New POKéMON
- The POKéMON of the fifth to ninth generations, from VICTINI to PECHARUNT, as MOéMON: their art, cries, POKéDEX
  entries, moves, evolutions and followers.
- Their moves, abilities and held items work in battle: terrains, WONDER ROOM and MAGIC ROOM, AURORA VEIL, STICKY WEB,
  moves that raise or lower several stats at once, PRANKSTER, MOXIE, PROTEAN, the SURGES, PROTOSYNTHESIS and QUARK
  DRIVE, DISGUISE, the RUIN abilities, ASSAULT VEST, ROCKY HELMET, WEAKNESS POLICY, BOOSTER ENERGY and many more.
- POKéMON that change form in battle do: AEGISLASH's STANCE CHANGE, WISHIWASHI's SCHOOLING, MINIOR's SHIELDS DOWN,
  PALAFIN's ZERO TO HERO, TERAPAGOS's TERA SHIFT, CRAMORANT's GULP MISSILE and MORPEKO's HUNGER SWITCH.
- A wild GOLISOPOD or WIMPOD hit below half its HP runs away, and yours switch out.
- The FAIRY type.

## GENERATION
- OPTIONS > GAME > GENERATION (GEN 3 to GEN 9) decides which generations' POKéMON, moves, abilities and types the game
  shows. Everyone starts at GEN 3.
- IMPORTANT: if your team has fourth-generation POKéMON, or POKéMON that learned fourth-generation moves, they can't
  battle at GEN 3. Set OPTIONS > GAME > GENERATION to GEN 4 (or higher) to battle with them again. Nothing is lost,
  and if none of your team can battle, your first POKéMON still does.
- Each step up brings more POKéMON into the wild and into trainers' teams in HOENN, JOHTO and KANTO: a few rarer
  wild POKéMON in each place and some members of trainers' teams turn into newer ones, up to your generation. GYM
  LEADERS, the ELITE FOUR and the rivals keep their aces.
- POKéMON only learn moves of your generation or earlier, and those of a later generation evolve into newer ones
  only once it's raised.
- FAIRY comes in at GEN 6; below it, POKéMON and moves have their older types.
- Lowering GENERATION never takes anything away, but what comes from a later generation rests until you raise it
  again, all the way down to GEN 3:
  - POKéMON of a later generation, or that know a later move or have a later ability, can't battle, like an EGG.
  - Items they hold from a later generation go back to your BAG.
  - Items of a later generation stay in your BAG but can't be used or given.
- Online, the host's GENERATION counts: hosts set it on HOST A GAME > GAME RULES.

## MEGA EVOLUTION, Z-MOVES, DYNAMAX and TERA
- In battle, press START in the move list to pick one before you choose the move. Each trainer can use one of
  them once a battle.
- MEGA EVOLUTION: a POKéMON holding its MEGA STONE MEGA EVOLVES into its MOéMON MEGA form, with its own types,
  ability and stats, and stays that way for the rest of the battle. KYOGRE and GROUDON return to their primal forms
  while you carry the BLUE ORB or RED ORB, and RAYQUAZA MEGA EVOLVES when it knows DRAGON ASCENT.
- Z-MOVES: a POKéMON holding a Z-CRYSTAL turns a move of the crystal's type into a Z-MOVE of far greater power.
- DYNAMAX: for three turns its moves become MAX MOVES, stronger and with their own effects (weather, terrain or
  stat changes), it takes half the damage, can't flinch, and its status moves become MAX GUARD.
- TERA: the POKéMON becomes its first type, and moves of that type hit harder.
- OPTIONS > GAME has each of them: AUTO (on from the generation that brought it: MEGA at GEN 6, Z-MOVES at GEN 7,
  DYNAMAX at GEN 8, TERA at GEN 9), ON or OFF. Online, the host's settings count (HOST A GAME > GAME RULES >
  GIMMICKS).
- LILYCOVE's, GOLDENROD's and CELADON's department stores sell the MEGA STONES and Z-CRYSTALS while their gimmick
  is on.
- Trainers use theirs too, when they're down to their last POKéMON.

## Items
- The department stores sell newer battle items (ASSAULT VEST, ROCKY HELMET, WEAKNESS POLICY, the terrain seeds...)
  and evolution items, up to your generation.
- PICKUP can find newer items too.

## Hall of Fame
- POKéMON from the fourth generation on now show as themselves in the HALL OF FAME.
