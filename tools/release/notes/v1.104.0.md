Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.104.0 "Cards in Motion!": the Companion app's card tables now move like the game's.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are. Update the
Companion app from its MENU > UPDATE.

## The Companion app's card games
- **The POKéMON card table moves:** cards fly from the deck to your hand and from your hand to the table, an attacker
  lunges, the card it hits shakes red, a weakness hit flashes, a Knocked Out card fades and its Prize card flies over,
  and the coin spins. A banner says whose turn it is.
- **The YU-GI-OH! table moves too:** summoned and Set cards fly to their zones, flipped cards turn, activated cards
  glow, destroyed cards fade on their way to the Graveyard, and big hits flash the screen.
- **TRIPLE TRIAD:** a played card flies from the hand (or from your finger, if you dragged it) to its square, lands with
  a glint, and captured cards lift off the table as they turn.
- Your opponent's moves play out one by one, so you can see what they did.
