Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.73.0 "Out for a Walk!": take a POKéMON with you on your phone, and train others while you're away.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are. Update MOEMON
COMPANION too (it offers the update when you open it).

## MOEMON COMPANION: the WALK
- **Send a POKéMON on a walk:** on the new WALK page, pick a POKéMON from your party or box. It goes with your phone
  (the game saves right away), and your real steps count for it, up to 10,000 a day.
- **What a walk brings:** EXP for every step (up to your next BADGE's level), Friendship along the way, and points:
  **FIND** an item from the place it set off from, or **MEET** a wild POKéMON that lives there and try to catch it
  (three a day). Time your throw when the ring is small!
- **Coming home:** tap HOME while linked. It comes back with everything: new levels and moves, the items in your BAG,
  the POKéMON it caught in your PC, and a line in its JOURNAL. An EGG's steps count too, and it hatches in the game.
- If you ever forget the phone in the game, a walking POKéMON comes home just as it left.

## TRAINING and the GARDEN
- **Training:** POKéMON in your boxes can train while you're away: EVs in one stat, levels up to your BADGE's cap, or
  rest for Friendship. Collect it from the phone. Taking one into your party ends its training, and the PC shows
  TRAINING for the ones that are.
- **The Garden in your pocket:** pet your party's POKéMON or share a BERRY from the phone. It happens in the game the
  next time you link, just as it would in the GARDEN. Read each one's JOURNAL too.
- **Your day:** the first time you open the app each day it shows your walk, your BERRIES ready, EGGS, today's goals
  and free MoeGatcha pulls.
- **Notifications** (a few a day at most, never at night, each kind can be turned off in MENU), and a **home-screen
  widget** with your walker and today's steps.
