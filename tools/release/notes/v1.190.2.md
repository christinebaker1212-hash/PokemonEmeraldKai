Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.190.2 "Tell Me More!": every MOéDEX entry has three new pages: its stats, its evolution line, and where to find it.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## The MOéDEX: MORE
On an entry, R opens MORE. R goes on to the next page, L goes back, B closes it.
- STATS, for one you have caught: its types, Abilities and egg groups, and its six base stats as numbers and
  bars with their total. For one only seen it asks you to catch it first.
- EVOLUTION: the whole family and how each one evolves: the level, the stone or item, a trade, friendship by
  day or night, a move it must know. Ones you have not seen yet are shown as ?????.
- WHERE: the places in the region you are in where it appears in the wild, and how: in grass, on water, with
  a rod, or from smashed rocks.

## Good to know
- WHERE lists wild encounters. Gifts, trades and one-of-a-kind encounters are not listed.
- The pages follow OWN and SHARED like the list: in OWN, a family member only a friend has seen stays ?????.
