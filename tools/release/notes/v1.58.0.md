Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.58.0 "Every Card Counts!": 17 new POKéMON card prizes, so every card in the game can now be collected.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, cards and decks stay as they are.

## POKéMON cards
- Seventeen promo cards that no one handed out are now prizes under PRIZES at the CARD CLUB counters and the GAME
  CENTER 2F CARD SHOP, one for each card milestone:
  - winning 1, 10, 25, 50 and 100 card duels;
  - opening 10, 50 and 100 booster packs;
  - beating 5, 10 and 15 special trainers' decks, and all four ELITE FOUR decks;
  - the CLEAN SWEEP and PURE ENERGY achievements;
  - completing 1, 4 and all 11 booster sets.
- Everything you've already done counts: open PRIZES and claim the ones you've earned.
- With these, every POKéMON card can be collected, and the CARD MASTER achievement now asks for every card.
