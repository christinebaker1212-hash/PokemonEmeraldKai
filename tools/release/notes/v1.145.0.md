Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.145.0 "Mine Now!": 17 more MAGIC cards that take control of permanents.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- Taking control now plays: until the end of the turn (untapped and ready to attack, like THREATEN), for as long as
  the card that took it stays, or for good. The permanent moves to its new side of the table and goes back when the
  effect ends.
- 17 more cards can be played, among them THREATEN, ALADDIN, HELM OF POSSESSION, SLEEPER AGENT, RUBINIA SOULSINGER,
  JINXED IDOL and CUSTODY BATTLE. Every one has its printed art.
- Your opponents steal your best creature when they can, and borrow one to attack with before their combat.
