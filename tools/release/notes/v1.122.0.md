Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.122.0 "Enchanted, I'm Sure!": MAGIC's Auras come to the table, 83 more cards in all.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- Auras play: cast one onto a creature and it stays on it. HOLY STRENGTH, UNHOLY STRENGTH, FLIGHT, FEAR, FIREBREATHING, RANCOR, PACIFISM, WEB, the SCARABS, SERRA'S EMBRACE and more. An Aura goes to the graveyard when its creature leaves the table, and RANCOR comes back to your hand.
- On the table, a creature wearing an Aura has a gold mark in its corner. Put the cursor on an Aura and its creature lights up in gold, and the other way round; the Aura's name says which creature it's on.
- Your opponents know which Auras help and which hurt: a PACIFISM goes on your best creature, a HOLY STRENGTH on theirs.
- MOSSDEEP's regulars use them: PIP and RHEA play RANCOR, NOLAN plays HOLY STRENGTH and PACIFISM.
- 1335 cards play now, all with their printed art.
