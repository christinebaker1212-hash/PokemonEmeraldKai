Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.208.1 "Keeping Up Appearances!": message boxes keep their UI STYLE as battles open, RED can have day and night, and TRIPLE TRIAD shows the rules of the region you're in.

UI STYLE
- When a trainer spots you, the message box stays in your UI STYLE while the battle opens, instead of switching back to Kai's own box.

RED
- New in OPTIONS > DISPLAY > GB OPTIONS: DAY / NIGHT. Turn it on to bring back the time of day in RED. Dawn and dusk darken the lightest shade, and night darkens every shade by one, the way the Game Boy darkened its screens. Off by default, as Red had no time of day.

TRIPLE TRIAD
- The CARD CASE shows the rules of the region you're in: KANTO RULES in KANTO, RULES OF JOHTO in JOHTO, and so on, instead of always HOENN's.
- The rules list scrolls, so JOHTO, KANTO and SINNOH are no longer hidden at the bottom of the screen. It opens on the region you're in.
- UNOVA has its own TRIPLE TRIAD rules now: OPEN, SAME and RANDOM, and the DIFF trade rule.
- Playing online needs everyone on 1.208.1.
