Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.185.0 "Bridge Builder!": a BREEDING calculator in the phone app, and MISTY EXPLOSION goes off like EXPLOSION.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are. Update the phone app
too (MENU > UPDATE): the calculator needs both.

## BREEDING in the phone app
- **BREED** in the POKéDEX opens it. A POKéDEX entry's BREED starts with that kind as the target, and a POKéMON's
  summary starts from that POKéMON.
- **FROM:** one of your own POKéMON (its gender and IVs, best IVs first) or any kind. **TO:** the kind that should end
  up with the IVs.
- **The route:** the fewest eggs from one to the other, even across egg groups: each egg's father and mother, what
  hatches, and which son carries the IVs on. Every mother that would do is shown, the ones you've caught in color and
  first. Two more routes follow when there are.
- It follows the game's own rules: a female starts by laying the first egg herself, DITTO passes its IVs straight into
  any egg, NIDORAN and VOLBEAT / ILLUMISE eggs can be either kind, and it says so when a kind can't be reached (TAUROS
  or MAGNEMITE only breed with DITTO, MEWTWO never hatches).
- **EGG MOVES:** the target's egg moves are listed; pick up to four and the plan says which fathers know each one and
  in what order to breed them onto a mother of the target before the last egg.
- Tips on DESTINY KNOT, POWER items and EVERSTONE.

## Battles
- **MISTY EXPLOSION** now makes its user faint, as it should, and a POKéMON with DAMP stops it. It still hits both
  foes and your partner, and it's still stronger on MISTY TERRAIN.
- Trainers think twice before using MISTY EXPLOSION, the same way they do with EXPLOSION.
