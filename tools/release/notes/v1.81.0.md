Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.81.0 "Sound Check!": MOEMON COMPANION plays the game's own sounds and music.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are. Update MOEMON
COMPANION too (it offers the update when you open it).

## MOEMON COMPANION: SOUND
- **The game's sounds on your phone:** pages, tabs and menus answer with the game's own sounds, a POKéMON's SUMMARY
  and POKéDEX entry play its cry (tap its picture to hear it again), and a MATCH CALL rings like the POKéNAV.
- **Card duels with music:** TRIPLE TRIAD, the POKéMON card game and YU-GI-OH! play the same songs as their tables in
  the game, with every card, attack, coin toss and Knock Out sounding as it does there, and the victory song when you
  win. Opening a pack sounds like the game's too.
- **Your call:** MENU > SOUND switches SOUND EFFECTS and MUSIC on or off. The music waits while another app is playing
  yours, and stops when you leave the app.
