Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.70.4 "Field Notes!": the phone app's SUMMARY, POKéDEX entries and item words, in a fresh look.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MOEMON COMPANION
- **SUMMARY:** every POKéMON's three pages, as in the game. INFO: its Trainer and ID No., its item and what it does,
  its nature and where and at what level it was met. SKILLS: its stats with its nature's marks, EXP. POINTS and how
  far to the next level, its ability and what it does. MOVES: each move's type, PP, power, accuracy and description.
  Open it from a box's card or the PARTY; swipe for the next one.
- **POKéDEX entries:** a big picture, its category, height and weight, types, the entries from RUBY, SAPPHIRE,
  EMERALD and FIRERED, and its base stats. Filters for ALL, SEEN, CAUGHT and MISSING, sorted by number or name.
- **BAG:** tap an item to read what it does; GIVE it from there.
- **BOXES:** all 40 boxes on their own wallpapers, how full each one is.
- **SPAWNS:** places grouped by region, with how their POKéMON are found and how likely each one is.
- **A fresh look:** banners on every page, the game's type icons, condition marks in the PARTY.
