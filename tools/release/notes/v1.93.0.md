Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.93.0 "Goldenrod's Tutor Returns!": a move tutor in GOLDENROD, and tutors that take yes for an answer.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## JOHTO AS IT WAS
- **GOLDENROD CITY:** once you've beaten the LEAGUE, a man by the GAME CORNER teaches FLAMETHROWER, THUNDERBOLT or
  ICE BEAM for 4,000 coins on Wednesdays and Saturdays, to any POKéMON that can learn the TM. Bring your COIN CASE.
- **GOLDENROD's NAME RATER** rates and renames your POKéMON again.
- **GOLDENROD GAME CORNER:** the TM counter is back: THUNDER, BLIZZARD and FIRE BLAST for 5,500 coins each.
- **CIANWOOD CITY:** the POKé SEER is home again. Show her a POKéMON and she tells you where you met it and at
  what level, or who you traded it from, and how much it has grown since.
- **GOLDENROD CITY:** the city sign is back, a woman tells you about the RADIO TOWER's offer for your POKéGEAR, and the
  old man who can't find his way says what he said in Crystal.

## FIXED
- **Move tutors in JOHTO and KANTO** no longer act as if you'd said no when your POKéMON had to forget a move to learn
  the new one.
