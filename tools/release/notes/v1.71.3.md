Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.71.3 "Full Hands!": a big hand of POKéMON cards no longer closes the game.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## CARD DUELS
- **Big hands:** in a POKéMON card duel, holding 22 or more cards closed the game the moment the cursor moved. A hand
  can now hold every card of a deck, and the cursor walks it all.
