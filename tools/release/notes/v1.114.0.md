Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.114.0 "Brains and Brawn!": SINNOH's Frontier Brains step out, and the places after the LEAGUE open up.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are. Friends need this version too to play together.

## SINNOH's BATTLE FRONTIER
- **The Frontier Brains**: keep a streak going and they come out to battle you. The CASTLE VALET DARACH and the ARCADE STAR DAHLIA on your 21st and 49th battles, the HALL MATRON ARGENTA on your 50th and 170th, each with their own POKéMON and their own words.
- Beat a Brain to win their Silver Print, then their Gold Print.
- The Brains battle to the Frontier Brain theme.

## After the HALL OF FAME
- **The BATTLEGROUND** in the SURVIVAL AREA is open: each day three gym leaders and one of SINNOH's partner trainers wait there for a rematch with their strongest teams.
- The FIGHT AREA's sailor sails you to SNOWPOINT CITY.
- At the FIGHT AREA, BARRY teams up with you against VOLKNER and FLINT, as in Platinum.
- REGIGIGAS stirs in SNOWPOINT's TEMPLE as it should.
