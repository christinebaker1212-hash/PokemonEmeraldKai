Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.171.5 "Your Own Nest!": every DAY CARE is yours alone, and EGGS turn up four times as often.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
1.171.5 plays online with friends on 1.171.4 too.

## Changed
- The DAY CARE MAN checks for an EGG every 64 steps instead of every 256: the same odds each time, four times as
  often. Every DAY CARE works this way, in HOENN, KANTO and JOHTO.
- Each player's DAY CARE is their own. A MOEMON left alone at your DAY CARE no longer makes an EGG with one left
  alone at a friend's, and friends' DAY CARE MOEMON never meet yours. An EGG already waiting from a friend's MOEMON
  is still the DAY CARE MAN's to hand over.
