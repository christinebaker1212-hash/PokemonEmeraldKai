Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.70.0 "Pocket Link!": MOEMON COMPANION, the phone app: your boxes, party, BAG, POKéDEX and spawns on Android.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MOEMON COMPANION (Android)
- **A phone app for your save:** MoemonCompanion.apk on the release page. It shows your 40 PC boxes on their own
  wallpapers, your party, the BAG, the POKéDEX and where POKéMON live, in the game's own font, windows and pictures.
- **Pairing:** OPTIONS > CO-OP > PHONE LINK shows a code. Scan it in the app, or type the 6 letters under it. The
  phone and the PC need the same Wi-Fi. After that the app links by itself whenever the game runs with that save,
  and opens on the last thing it saw when the game is away.
- **BOXES:** drag a POKéMON to move it, swipe for the next box, tap the box's name for all 40. The party trades places
  with the PC only where a PC could be reached: towns, routes and buildings, not caves or the Elite Four.
- **Items:** GIVE and TAKE held items from the boxes, the party or the BAG.
- **SPAWNS:** every map's POKéMON by GRASS, SURF, ROCK SMASH and each ROD, with levels and chances. Rare ones stay a
  shadow and a hint until you've met them there.
- **Safe by design:** the game makes every change itself, only when it's free on the map. A change made while it's
  busy or away waits with a dashed outline and is done on the next link; one that no longer makes sense is listed
  under MENU > COULDN'T DO. No POKéMON is ever lost or copied.
- **Updates:** the app updates itself from the game's releases (MENU > UPDATE). Android asks once to allow installing.
- SELECT on the PHONE LINK screen forgets every phone paired with the save.
