Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.82.1 "Out of Sight!": no more shadow peeking out from behind trees.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are. 1.82.1 plays online
with friends on 1.82.0 and 1.81.0 too.

## Fixes
- **Shadows behind trees:** standing behind a tree whose top is drawn over you (and behind anything else drawn over
  your feet), your shadow showed as a dark line under the leaves. Now there's no shadow while you're hidden there. The
  same goes for your POKéMON and the ones in the GARDEN.
