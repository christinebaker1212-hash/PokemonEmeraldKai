Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.197.0 "The Gyms Come Alive!": UNOVA's gym machinery is all there to see, and its DAY CARE and move teachers are open.

UNOVA
- Every Gym's machinery is now on screen and moves as in Black: BURGH's honey doors and plates, ELESA's coasters and their rails, CLAY's lifts and elevator, SKYLA's cannons, BRYCEN's switch pieces, DRAYDEN's dragons and levers, the curtains in STRIATON's Gym and the shelf in NACRENE's.
- ELESA's Gym: the coasters run their loops and stop at the platforms. Stand on a platform facing the rails and hop in when your coaster arrives; hold DOWN at a stop to get off.
- SKYLA's Gym: the cannon swings round, you climb in, and you fly across the Gym before you land.
- CLAY's Gym: the lifts and the elevator carry you up and down instead of jumping, and you now stand on the walkways rather than far below them.
- BURGH's Gym remembers the switches you've found when you leave and come back.
- ROUTE 3's DAY CARE takes your POKéMON and finds EGGS, using the DAY CARE you know from HOENN.
- MISTRALTON's Move Deleter and Move Reminder, and the move tutors in DRIFTVEIL and on ROUTE 13, teach and forget moves with the usual screens. EGGS hatch as you walk in UNOVA too.
