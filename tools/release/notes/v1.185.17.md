Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.185.17 "Security Clearance!": GALACTIC HQ's locked doors and bollards are back, and places that set things up as you arrive now do.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## SINNOH
- GALACTIC HQ: its locked security doors and the bollards on 4F were missing, so the warp panel by the front door led
  straight to the laboratory, past CYRUS, SATURN and the whole hideout. They stand where Platinum has them again: the
  way in is through the warehouse, with the STORAGE KEY and the GALACTIC KEY. If you saved inside the hideout or its
  warehouse, its doors are already open for you.
- The bollards in HEARTHOME CITY, the FIGHT AREA, FULLMOON ISLAND and NEWMOON ISLAND, and the two in FANTINA's room,
  stand where Platinum has them too.
- What some places set up the moment you arrive was skipped: the counterpart's look on ROUTE 201 and at LAKE VERITY,
  BARRY's and others' places in CANALAVE and PASTORIA, and the arrival marks of GALACTIC HQ, the lake caverns, the
  LEAGUE and more. They now happen as in Platinum.
- DRIFLOON floats by the VALLEY WINDWORKS on Fridays once you've freed it from TEAM GALACTIC, as in Platinum, and the
  days of the week SINNOH's people talk about follow the game's calendar (the room host's when playing together)
  instead of your computer's own clock.
