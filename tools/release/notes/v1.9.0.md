Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.9.0: two trainers at once, and teaming up on the same trainer.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
Play online only with friends on 1.9.0 too (their game updates itself the same way). If you run the standalone server
on another computer, replace it with the one in this release.

## New
- **Two trainers at once, together.** When two trainers spot you at the same time, the team-up steps now happen
  too (they used to be skipped): after the second trainer's speech you're asked about waiting for a partner, or a
  friend who walked up can join. With a friend it's the two of you against both trainers, each trainer sending out
  their own Moemon on their side of the field; both speak at the end and both pay the prize. Alone it's the usual
  battle against both.
- **Grouped on one trainer.** While you wait for a partner, the trainer you're waiting on can still spot a friend
  who steps into its line of sight: it walks up to them as usual and they go straight into your battle
  ("You team up with <you>!"). If two of you end up waiting on the same trainer, whoever started waiting first leads
  and the other joins them automatically.
