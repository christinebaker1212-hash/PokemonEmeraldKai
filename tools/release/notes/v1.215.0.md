Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.215.0 "Undercover!": STREAMER MODE hides every name on screen, trade evolutions work in CO-OP, and a far-behind MOéMON catches up.

STREAMER MODE
- STREAMER MODE now hides the names drawn over the field too: the name tags over your friends, their names over the doors of the buildings they're in, pings, the FRIEND FINDER's arrows, the SPECTATE panel and its pop-out windows' titles, the Triple Battle cards and friends' cursors in the PC.
- The Original Trainer of a traded MOéMON is shown under a stand-in name as well.

EVOLUTIONS
- A MOéMON traded in CO-OP while holding its evolution item (SPRITZEE with a SACHET, ONIX with a METAL COAT, SCYTHER, SEADRA, PORYGON, CLAMPERL and the rest) now evolves properly. Before, it could turn into an empty "??????????" with no HP.
- The held item is used up only once the evolution has happened, also when a LINKING CORD does the trading.
- No evolution can ever turn a MOéMON into nothing again: every evolution in the game was checked.

EXP
- With the EXP CURVE on SCALED, a MOéMON far below the level of where you are gets boosted EXP again (up to double), so a newcomer to a strong team catches up quickly. Nobody's EXP is cut for being ahead.

Playing online needs everyone on 1.215.0.
