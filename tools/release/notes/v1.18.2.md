Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.18.2 "Triple Lineup!": a new party screen for Triple Battles.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
1.17.x and 1.18.x play together online.

## Changed
- Co-op Triple Battles have their own party screen: the three Moemon out in battle in panels down the left (yours
  first, then your allies'), and the ones waiting on the bench on the right, in the same order. Your allies' boxes are
  gold, and the Moemon out in battle have an orange edge.
