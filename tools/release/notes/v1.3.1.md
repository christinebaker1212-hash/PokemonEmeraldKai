Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.3.1: a fix for Triple Battles against trainers.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## Fixed
- **A BAD EGG could come out for the trainer in a 3-player battle.** When two or three of a trainer's Moemon fainted in
  the same turn (VOLTORB's EXPLOSION or SELF-DESTRUCT, a SURF or EARTHQUAKE) and they had fewer left than had fainted, the last one to choose
  sent out a broken Moemon that wasn't in the party at all (seen against WATTSON), and the battle could get stuck.
  Now only as many come out as the trainer has left; the empty spot stays empty.
- ROAR and WHIRLWIND in a 3-player battle could bring in a Moemon that was already out, or one of another player's
  Moemon. They now bring in one of the target's own that isn't out yet (or fail if there's none).
- A trainer choosing to switch Moemon in a 3-player battle looked at the wrong half of their party: it could pick one
  that was already out (or, with nothing left, a broken one). Trainers now only switch to one that's free.
