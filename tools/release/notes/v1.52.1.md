Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.52.1 "Turn It Up (or Down)!": the new songs play as loud as the game's own music.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## Music
- Every new song now plays at the same volume as the game song it replaces, so the music no longer jumps louder or
  quieter when a new song comes on.
- TRIPLE TRIAD's battle and victory songs were much too quiet. They are now as loud as the regular battle music.
- The GAME CENTER's music on 1F, 2F and 3F was too quiet. It now matches the rest of the game.
- The YU-GI-OH! and card duel songs, and the battle song in the CABLE CLUB, were a little quieter than the rest. They now match.
