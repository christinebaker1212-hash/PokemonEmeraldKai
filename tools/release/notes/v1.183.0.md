Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.183.0 "Pack Light!": each region keeps its own KEY ITEMS, and SINNOH rides the MTN. BIKE.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## KEY ITEMS between regions
- **A new region, a fresh start:** a region's own KEY ITEMS stay behind when you sail away and are waiting when you come
  back. That means its bike, its device (MOéNAV, POKéGEAR, POKéTCH...), its cases, its rods, COIN CASE, ITEMFINDER and
  S.S. TICKET, and its story items (tickets, fossils, keys, letters...).
- **Beat a region's POKéMON LEAGUE** and its bike, devices, cases and other tools go anywhere with you from then on.
  Story items always stay home.
- **One of each:** tools every region has (rods, the COIN CASE, the ITEMFINDER, the S.S. TICKET...) become a single
  item once they're free, and any copies waiting in other regions or the GLOBAL PC join the one in your BAG.
- **HMs** wait in the GLOBAL PC until the LEAGUE of the region they came from is beaten, then come back to your BAG.
- **Always with you:** the CARD CASE, the CANDY JAR, the BATTLE FLAG and the ORRE and BATTLE ISLAND tickets.
- Customs tells you what stays behind.
- Saves from before sort their BAG once: tools from regions you've beaten travel, other regions' items go home.

## SINNOH
- **The MTN. BIKE** is SINNOH's own bike, from the CYCLE SHOP in ETERNA CITY. KANTO and JOHTO keep the BICYCLE.
  A save that started in SINNOH has its bike turned into the MTN. BIKE.
