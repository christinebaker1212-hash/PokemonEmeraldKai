Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.161.0 "Who, Me?": 19 more MAGIC cards that react when they're targeted.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- Cards that react when a spell or ability targets them now work: ANGELIC PROTECTOR toughens up before your burn spell
  lands, the CEPHALIDS mill cards, SKULKING GHOST and its kin give up, COWARDICE sends any targeted creature home.
- CLOUD COVER and DARU SPIRITUALIST protect your side when the opponent takes aim.
- 19 more cards can be played, among them CURSED MONSTROSITY, SPINAL GRAFT, TETHERED SKIRGE and SLEEPING POTION.
  Every one has its printed art.
