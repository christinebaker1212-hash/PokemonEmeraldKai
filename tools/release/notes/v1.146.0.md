Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.146.0 "Count On It!": 44 more MAGIC cards with counters of every kind.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- Counters of every kind now play: charge, fuse, arrowhead, ice and the rest, plus +2/+2 and other counters that change
  a creature's size. Cards can enter with counters, add them, remove them to pay for an ability, or count them.
- A permanent's counters show as an amber number in its corner, and the table says what was added or removed.
- 44 more cards can be played, among them the SPIKES (SPIKE FEEDER, SPIKE SOLDIER...), TRISKELION, SERRATED ARROWS,
  LOTUS BLOSSOM, ICEBERG, TIME BOMB and BOUNTY HUNTER. Every one has its printed art.
