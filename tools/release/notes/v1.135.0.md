Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.135.0 "Slivers Unite!": MAGIC's cards that give abilities to others, and 66 more cards.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- Cards that give other permanents an ability now play: the Slivers (ACIDIC, ARMOR, BARBED, CLOT, CRYPT SLIVER...), ARCANE TEACHINGS, BARBED FIELD, CITANUL HIEROPHANTS (your creatures tap for green mana) and more. The permanent shows the ability on its own menu, with its own name.
- Cards that give others a trigger play too: AURA FLUX and ENERGY FLUX ("sacrifice this unless you pay 2"), CEPHALID SAGE, CENTAUR CHIEFTAIN and the other Threshold creatures that gain one.
- Creatures that attack each combat if able (BLOODROCK CYCLOPS, GOBLIN BRIGAND) join the attack by themselves. The table picks them for you and won't let you take them out.
- DENSE FOLIAGE keeps creatures from being targeted by spells. Cards that stop spells being cast or lands being played work as well.
- Auras and creatures that count cards in your hand, in graveyards or half your lands now play: EMPYRIAL ARMOR, MARO, ASPECT OF WOLF, MORTIVORE, COGNIVORE, KAVU SCOUT and more.
- 1953 of the 5408 cards in the pool now play.
