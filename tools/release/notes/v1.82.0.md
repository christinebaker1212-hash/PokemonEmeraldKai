Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.82.0 "Fixer Upper!": a long list of fixes for SINNOH, JOHTO, KANTO and the GAME CENTER.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are. 1.82.0 plays online
with friends on 1.81.0 too.

## SAVE FILES
- **Twelve save files** instead of three. LEFT and RIGHT on SAVE FILE load that file right away, so the file screen
  shows its player, time and region.
- SINNOH saves name SINNOH as their region.
- MOEMON MAKER's test files now count as CHAMPION where they should: the LEAGUE beaten, the S.S. TICKET in the BAG,
  and the boats to every region.

## SINNOH
- A new game starts in front of the TV, as if you were watching it.
- PROF. ROWAN's lab plays its first visit: LUCAS or DAWN takes you inside and you get the POKéDEX. Then MOM gives you
  what you need, and LUCAS or DAWN lets you leave town.
- BARRY no longer stands on ROUTE 201 after the LAKE VERITY scene.
- Stairs take you up or down only when you walk into them, not when you step beside them.
- People stand on the same line as you (they were a pixel higher).

## JOHTO AND KANTO
- The first rival battle in every region is at Lv5, whatever the level scaling.
- No NATIONAL DEX and POKé BALLS the moment you choose your starter: OAK's and ELM's POKéDEX is the NATIONAL DEX.
- The BICYCLE can be ridden.
- PCs light up properly and keep working after you use them.
- GOLDENROD DEPT. STORE's rooftop shows up (it was all dark). The LAKE OF RAGE, SILVER CAVE's summit, TIN TOWER's roof
  and two KANTO places that change after an event show the right map as well.
- Fossil POKéMON aren't met in the wild until you've beaten the region's LEAGUE.
- JOHTO's BERRY trees show their BERRIES only when they're ripe, can't be planted, and grow back on their own after
  you pick them.
- JOHTO's item balls are round POKé BALLs again (some were squares).
- ETHAN's ball throw plays in the right order.
- MOM hands over the RUNNING SHOES in JOHTO with the same words as KANTO's, the ROUTE 30 BERRY man says Crystal's
  lines, and SAGE LI gives you the HM after your battle, not when the ELDER talks to your rival.
- The MAGNET TRAIN runs between GOLDENROD and SAFFRON once you've beaten JOHTO's or KANTO's LEAGUE and hold the
  S.S. TICKET, with Crystal's own ride on the way.
- SEAFOAM ISLANDS' bottom floors calm down once both boulders stop the currents, and DUNSPARCE TUNNEL opens up once
  it's been dug out.
- SPROUT TOWER's middle pillar sways as it does in Crystal.
- The GAME CENTER above the GAME CORNERS charges admission like HOENN's, its regulars look like the region's own,
  and it no longer sends you to MAUVILLE for a COIN CASE.

## EVERYWHERE
- The CARD CLUB people name the region you're in, and don't give you a second CARD CASE.
- HOENN's GAME CENTER darkens and brightens with the time of day like the rest of RUSTBORO.
- Rain and hail in battle fall behind the HP boxes, and half of it passes behind the POKéMON.
- The newer POKéMON cards' printed pictures no longer show yellow bits of the card's border.
