Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.185.20 "Even Volume!": every song plays equally loud, and MAGIC duels have their own music.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## Music
- Every new song in the game now plays at the same loudness as the rest, without losing its quiet and loud parts.
  Songs that were much louder or quieter than the others are evened out.

## SINNOH
- SENDOFF SPRING: coming back from the DISTORTION WORLD, CYNTHIA's talk there plays again, so PROF. ROWAN hears the
  news and the way to SUNYSHORE CITY opens. If you already left the spring, go back to it once.

## MAGIC
- Duels at the MOSSDEEP CARD CLUB have two songs of their own; each duel plays one of them.
