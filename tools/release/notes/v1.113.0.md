Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.113.0 "Dear Diary!": SINNOH's JOURNAL, Super Contests with friends on Platinum's own stages, a TV that knows everything you do, and two more Gym puzzles.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are. Friends need this version too to play together.

## SINNOH
- **The JOURNAL**: your counterpart's gift from Platinum is back. Open it from the BAG and leaf through a page for each day you played in SINNOH: places you arrived at, trainers you beat, things you bought, field moves you used, and battles with your friends, written just as Platinum writes them.
- **HEARTHOME's and VEILSTONE's Gyms** work as in Platinum, puzzles and all.
- **Super Contests with friends**: at the CONTEST HALL's link counter, one of you chooses BECOME LEADER and the others JOIN GROUP. Everyone in the group enters the same Contest: you see each other's dress-ups, dance steps and moves, and the computer fills the empty seats.
- **Super Contests take place on Platinum's own stages**: the VISUAL stage and its audience, the DANCE floor, the ACTING stage and the results' board.

## SINNOH's TV
- **BERRY LOOKOUT** reports on the soft soil patches around SINNOH and how far their BERRIES have grown.
- **POKéMON PHOTO RATING** rates the photo you took at JUBILIFE TV.
- The TV now covers the BATTLE TOWER (your best streaks, and interviews after a run), the BATTLE HALL, the BATTLE POINTS you earn, new ribbons, big wins at the GAME CORNER's slots and POFFINS you make at the POFFIN HOUSE.
- **Your friends are on TV**: when you mix records, their catches, records and interviews come with them and can air on your TV, under their names.
- **JUBILIFE TV's 3F** survey is open again: give your two-word opinion of the TV.
