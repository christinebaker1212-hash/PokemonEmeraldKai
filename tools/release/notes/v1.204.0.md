Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.204.0 "Game Boy Ways!": GB OPTIONS for the RED and CRYSTAL styles, with their own POKé BALLS, battle openings, colours and move animations, and INSTANT TEXT you can turn off.

GB OPTIONS
- OPTIONS > DISPLAY > GB OPTIONS (with UI STYLE RED or CRYSTAL): RED's and CRYSTAL's own ways, each style keeping its own settings. Every one is a switch (BALL THROWS, TRANSITIONS and MOVES can go back to KAI's own), and each player keeps their own, online too. WHITE BG and MENU POKéMON live here now.
- BALL THROWS: every POKé BALL is thrown as Red or Crystal throws it, with their sounds and each ball's own colours.
- TRANSITIONS: wild and trainer battles open with Red's or Crystal's own swirls, wipes and flashes, taking just as long as before.
- COLORS: the battle and the field drawn in the Game Boy's colours. RED: the original green screen (DMG), POCKET, LIGHT, the Game Boy Color's own colours for Red, or GBA. CRYSTAL: true Game Boy Color colours (four to a palette, eight palettes at a time), or GBA.
- MOVES: every move animated and heard as Red or Crystal show it, over the battle, taking just as long as before. Moves newer than those games use their closest match.
- BATTLE SCENERY: battles in RED and CRYSTAL no longer open with grass, water or cave strips sliding across, as on the Game Boy. Turn it on to have them back.
- CAUGHT MARK (RED): a POKé BALL by a wild POKéMON you have already caught, drawn with Red's own ball. Off by default, as Red had none.

TEXT
- OPTIONS > GAME > INSTANT TEXT: words you have read before appear at once (ON), or type out at your TEXT SPEED like the first time (OFF).

OPTIONS
- The titles of option pages read correctly in the RED and CRYSTAL styles.
