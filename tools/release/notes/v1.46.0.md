Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.46.0 "Every Card Counts!": every attack on every POKéMON card now works, and the cursor jumps to your Active Pokémon when it's time to attack.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, settings and cards stay as they are.
Playing online works with 1.44.0 and later, but POKéMON card duels with friends need everyone on 1.46.0.

## New
- The last 20 POKéMON card attacks work now, as they did on the Game Boy: Clefairy's and Clefable's Metronome,
  Mew's Mystery Attack and Devolution Beam, Ditto's Morph, Porygon's Conversion 1 and 2, Pidgeot's Gale and
  Hurricane, Spearow's and Pidgeotto's Mirror Move, Poliwhirl's and Slowpoke's Amnesia, Slowpoke's Scavenge,
  Gastly's Destiny Bond, Hypno's Prophecy, Moltres's Wildfire, Ninetales's Mix-Up, Magnemite's Magnetic Storm and
  Marowak's Wail.
- Attacks that need a choice ask for it: Metronome lists the Defending Pokémon's attacks to copy, Amnesia the attack
  to block, Wildfire how many Fire Energy to discard, Conversion the new type, Prophecy whose deck to look at and the
  order to put the cards back in, Devolution Beam whose Pokémon to devolve, and Scavenge which Trainer to take back.
- Your opponent's decks use these attacks too.

## Changed
- POKéMON card duels: once nothing in your hand can be played (no Basic Pokémon with room on the Bench, no
  evolution, no Trainer with something to do, and your Energy for the turn attached), the cursor moves to your
  Active Pokémon, ready to attack.
- Duels against the game's opponents are rated again with every attack working, so the match shown before a duel
  stays accurate.
