Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.16.0: the MOéMON GARDEN moves upstairs, into every POKéMON CENTER.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
To play online together, everyone needs 1.16.0.

## Changed
- **The GARDEN is every POKéMON CENTER's second floor.** Ride a CENTER's escalator up and you're in the Garden: a
  meadow inside the CENTER where your party lives. The escalator right where you arrive takes you back down to the
  CENTER you came from. Friends who come up in other CENTERs are there with you.
- **START > GARDEN.** Once you've found the Garden, the START menu (and your PC's MOéMON GARDEN row) takes you there
  from outdoors or a CENTER, and the way down leads to the last POKéMON CENTER you visited. It isn't available in
  caves, dungeons, the SAFARI ZONE, BATTLE FRONTIER challenges or in the middle of a story moment: a message says why.
- **One open Garden.** The tree line down the middle is gone: the whole meadow is always open, alone or with friends.
- The CABLE CLUB upstairs is gone (co-op does all of that from the CO-OP menu now).

## Fixed
- The Garden could trap you: the hedge to the bigger meadow closed when your friends left while you were on the
  other side, and the way between LITTLEROOT and the Garden drew tiles out of place the longer you walked around.
  LITTLEROOT and the Garden aren't joined any more, and nothing in the Garden can close you in.
- If you saved in the Garden on an older version, you'll find yourself by the escalator; decorations whose spot is
  gone go back to your collection.
