Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.11.0: EXP for catching, BATTLE FLAG phrases, and a fixed shared PC cursor.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
Play online only with friends on 1.11.0 too (their game updates itself the same way). If you run the standalone server
on another computer, replace it with the one in this release.

## New
- **Catch EXP.** Catching a Moemon now gives EXP too, to everyone in the battle (and when you play alone). The more
  of its HP you knocked off before the catch, the more you get: almost a whole faint's worth at 1 HP, a tenth of it
  at full HP. Level-ups from a catch evolve after the battle, as after a win.
- **BATTLE FLAG phrases.** Using the flag now asks "Set phrases, or plant?". PLANT stands guard as before. PHRASES
  lets you make up, from the game's word list, your CHALLENGE (said when you walk up to someone), what you say if
  you WIN and what you say if you LOSE. Both players see the challenge before the battle, and afterwards the winner
  sees the loser's LOSE phrase and the loser sees the winner's WIN phrase. (They're the same phrases the BATTLE
  TOWER uses.)

## Changed
- BATTLE FLAG battles: the winner still gets a tenth of the loser's money, but the loser no longer pays it. They
  keep all their money.

## Fixed
- The shared PC's hand cursor was drawn behind the PARTY / MY PC / CLOSE buttons, turned upside down on them, and
  stayed upside down after MY PC / SHARED PC until you opened a summary. It now points down onto the buttons, over
  them, and is always the right way up.
