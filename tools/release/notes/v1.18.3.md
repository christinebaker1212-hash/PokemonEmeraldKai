Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.18.3 "Every Step Counts!": followers walk the ground like you.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
1.17.x and 1.18.x play together online.

## Changed
- Your follower walks the ground the way you do: tall and long grass shakes around it and covers its feet, it leaves
  footprints in sand, splashes through puddles and shallow water, sinks into hot springs, ripples the water, and shows
  in ponds and on ice. Friends see the same around their followers.
