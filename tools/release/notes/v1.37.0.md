Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.37.0 "Same Hands, Two Games!": the card duel tables share their buttons, the Yu-Gi-Oh! screens match.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, settings and cards stay as they are.
Playing online needs everyone on 1.37.0.

## Changed
- Both card duel tables now use the same buttons: A acts on the card under the cursor, R looks at it up close, and
  B (after leaving the hand), START or SELECT opens the turn menu with END TURN chosen, so B then A ends your turn.
  START or SELECT also finishes picking several cards.
- The POKEMON card table's old six-button menu is gone. Its turn menu is END TURN (READY while setting up) and
  CONCEDE, in the same place as the Yu-Gi-Oh! table's. ATTACK, RETREAT and PKMN POWER are on your Pokemon (A on
  them), as before. The CONCEDE button on the mat moved into the menu.
- END TURN no longer asks "End your turn?"; it still reminds you when you could have attached an Energy card.
- Holding A or B no longer hurries the POKEMON table's animations (the DUEL SPEED option still does).
- Yu-Gi-Oh! boosters reveal their cards on their own, like the POKEMON packs: A shows the next one now, B shows them
  all.
- The Yu-Gi-Oh! counters (the CARD CLUB's and the GAME CENTER's) look and work like the POKEMON ones. TRADE IN on
  both: A trades one copy, SELECT every spare copy of that card, and ALL SPARES at the end of the list trades all
  of them.
- The Yu-Gi-Oh! deck screens use the POKEMON screens' sounds, messages and words: copying a deck asks first, the
  builder's type tabs work on both sides, LEFT from the deck list lands on that card.
- START or R also closes a card's close-up, START looks at a Starter Deck's leading card, and a copied deck is
  named after the one it copies.
