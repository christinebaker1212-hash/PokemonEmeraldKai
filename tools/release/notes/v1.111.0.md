Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.111.0 "Old Friends, Old Fossils!": JOHTO's BATTLE TOWER and KANTO's TRAINER TOWER open their doors, every trainer brings the original team, and CINNABAR's fossil doctor is back.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## JOHTO
- **MAHOGANY's TEAM ROCKET hideout** plays out as in Crystal. On the second floor, LANCE's DRAGONITE breaks in, LANCE takes on the grunt and you battle the EXECUTIVE yourself, with your whole party. The executive on the floor below guards the office in his own uniform. Everyone in the hideout says Crystal's words.
- **CIANWOOD's PHOTO STUDIO** is open again beside the GYM. The photographer takes a free photo of your team for your TRAINER CARD. The cave that stood in its place is gone, and so are the roads beyond it.
- **ICE PATH** no longer has a girl stuck on the ice.
- **The RUINS OF ALPH RESEARCH CENTER** studies the RUINS again instead of reviving fossils, as in Crystal.
- **Marts** sell each town's own stock again. MAHOGANY's shop is run by TEAM ROCKET until the hideout falls, and the old woman opens it again once the RADIO TOWER is freed.
- ELM's aide in VIOLET only needs room in your party for the EGG, and asks again if you said no.
- **The Bug-Catching Contest** follows Crystal's rules: it's held on TUESDAY, THURSDAY and SATURDAY, once a day, with 20 SAFARI BALLS and 20 minutes. You keep one catch (a second asks whether to switch), five of the ten contestants come each day and wander the park, and the judging, prizes and words are Crystal's. If your BAG is full, the officer keeps your prize for the rest of the day.
- **BUENA's PASSWORD** is on the air again. Listen to her show on the POKéGEAR after 6 PM for the day's password, then tell it to BUENA at the RADIO TOWER for a point on your BLUE CARD. The receptionist beside her trades points for prizes.
- **The legends of ECRUTEAK** follow Crystal's story again. The KIMONO GIRLS dance at their theater, where the old man gives you HM03 SURF once you've beaten all five. The RADIO TOWER's director gives you the CLEAR BELL, the WISE TRIO test you before the TIN TOWER, and SUICUNE waits inside, after leading you around CIANWOOD, ROUTE 42 and ROUTE 36. After the LEAGUE, the sage at the TIN TOWER gives the RAINBOW WING to a trainer with RAIKOU, ENTEI and SUICUNE, and HO-OH waits on the roof. LUGIA waits in the WHIRL ISLANDS for the trainer with the SILVER WING, from an old man in KANTO's PEWTER CITY.
- **The BATTLE TOWER** stands north of ROUTE 40 again, as in Crystal. Bring three different POKéMON, pick a level from L:10 up (to L:100 once you've beaten the LEAGUE) and battle seven trainers in a row in the BATTLE ROOM. Win all seven for a prize of vitamins. You can save and stop between battles, and pick up where you left off.
- **Trades**: KYLE trades his ONIX ROCKY, MIKE his MACHOP MUSCLE, TIM his VOLTORB VOLTY and EMY her DODRIO DORIS, each with Crystal's words.

## JOHTO and KANTO
- **Every trainer** brings the original games' team again: FireRed's in KANTO and Crystal's in JOHTO, from the first YOUNGSTER to the ELITE FOUR. (With GENERATION set to 5 or more, trainers keep their modern teams.)
- **Legendary POKéMON** are the original games' again: the ones from later generations no longer wait around JOHTO and KANTO after the LEAGUE (MOEGATCHA has them), and neither do JIRACHI, MANAPHY or the SUICUNE by the LAKE OF RAGE.

## KANTO
- **PROF. OAK** rates your POKéDEX as in FireRed again, with his comments for every ten POKéMON you've caught in KANTO. His AIDES count KANTO's POKéDEX too.
- **CINNABAR's fossil doctor** works as in FireRed: show him a HELIX FOSSIL, DOME FOSSIL or OLD AMBER, take a walk outside the lab, and come back for your POKéMON.
- **Marts** sell each town's own stock, as in FireRed.
- **The VS SEEKER** is back: the woman in VERMILION's POKéMON CENTER gives it to you. Walk 100 steps to charge it, then use it near trainers you've beaten: the ones who want a rematch show "!!", and battle you again with stronger teams as your journey goes on, just like in FireRed.
- **The TRAINER TOWER** on SEVEN ISLAND is open, as in FireRed: pick SINGLE, DOUBLE, KNOCKOUT or MIXED at the counter and race the clock up eight floors of TRAINERS to the OWNER on the roof. He has a prize for each challenge, and your best times go up on the Time Board in the lobby.
- **The SAFARI ZONE** is FireRed's SAFARI GAME again: pay 500 at the gate for 30 SAFARI BALLS and 600 steps, with FireRed's signs and rest-house visitors.
- **Trades** are FireRed's again: MIMIEN, ZYNX (with her letter), MS. NIDO, CH'DING, NINA, MARC, ESPHERE, TANGENY and SEELOR.
