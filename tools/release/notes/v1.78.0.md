Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.78.0 "Pocket Card Club!": the POKéMON card game on your phone, with your own cards and decks.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are. Update MOEMON
COMPANION too (it offers the update when you open it).

## MOEMON COMPANION: CARDS
- **POKéMON CARDS on the phone:** CARDS now has two games. Tap POKéMON CARDS at the top to play the game's own
  POKéMON card game: the same rules, every card's attacks and Pokémon Powers, and the same computer players, laid out
  upright for your phone with the real printed cards.
- **Playing:** tap any card to see it up close with what it can do now (and why not, if it can't). When a card or an
  attack needs a choice, the spaces you can pick glow on the table, or a card picker or a list opens. Attacks, coin
  flips, damage, Knock Outs and Prize cards are called out as they happen. Your opponent's cards face them, across the
  table, as in the game.
- **FREE DUEL:** take on a card player with a deck about as strong as yours, as in the game. Win and a booster goes to
  your game: three a day. Every duel, won or lost, teaches the game how well you play.
- **VS FRIENDS:** challenge a friend to a POKéMON card duel a turn at a time, with your own deck. A notification says
  when it's your turn.
- **BOOSTERS, DECKS and the BINDER:** open your game's boosters on the phone (ten cards turning over, NEW! on the ones
  you didn't have), build and rename your eight decks with the game's deck rules, choose the one you duel with, and
  browse your BINDER set by set.
- You need your first deck from the CARD CLUB in the game before you can play.

## The POKéMON card game in the game
- While setting up, your first Basic POKéMON always goes in the Active spot.
- An Evolution card now only offers the POKéMON that can evolve this turn.
- SCOOP UP is no longer suggested for your only POKéMON in play.
