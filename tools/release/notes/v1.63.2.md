Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.63.2 "Pick One from the Table!": choose your first MOéMON from the lab's table in KANTO and JOHTO, and the way out of your house in SINNOH is clear.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
1.63.2 plays online with friends on 1.63.0 and 1.63.1 too.

## New
- **The lab's table.** When PROF. OAK or PROF. ELM tells you to pick a MOéMON, you walk up to the table and see its
  three POKé BALLS up close: move the hand, watch the ball under it wiggle, and see each MOéMON before you choose.
  KANTO offers BULBASAUR, SQUIRTLE and CHARMANDER; JOHTO offers CYNDAQUIL, TOTODILE and CHIKORITA.
- **BLUE as in FireRed.** BLUE takes the MOéMON strong against yours, and his team in every battle, from the lab to
  the CHAMPION's room, is the one he has in FireRed.

## Fixed
- **SINNOH:** BARRY's mom no longer stands in your house's doorway. The first time you head out, she drops by, says
  hello and leaves, as in Platinum.
- **SINNOH:** CRASHER WAKE no longer blocks PASTORIA's and VEILSTONE's gym doors, the sailor in CANALAVE no longer
  blocks his own front door, and the policemen at STARK MOUNTAIN no longer block the way out.
