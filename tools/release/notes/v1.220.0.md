Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.220.0 "Under the Weather!": the sky changes every hour in every region, and YU-GI-OH! duelists play their cards smarter.

WEATHER
- Every hour the weather changes on the routes and towns of HOENN, KANTO, JOHTO, SINNOH and UNOVA: clear, rain, a thunderstorm now and then, or bright sun.
- Rain and sun come in fronts that drift across the region: walk to the next route and the rain may still be falling, cross the region and the sky may be another.
- Bright sun only shines by day: at night the sky is simply clear. In battle it's sunshine, and the rain is rain.
- Cold places (JOHTO's north, MT. SILVER, SINNOH's snowy mountain routes, all of UNOVA in winter) get hail instead of rain, and the hail pelts in battle too.
- Very rarely, a sandstorm blows over UNOVA's deserts on a day it otherwise wouldn't.
- Places with weather of their own keep it: ROUTE 119's rain, the desert's sandstorm, SINNOH's snow and fog, UNOVA's seasons, and every story event's weather.
- Everyone in a room sees the same sky at the same hour, and so does a game played alone.
- POKéMON that like the weather come out of a route's rare spots more often while it lasts. The companion app's POKéDEX says which: "Likes the sun.", "Likes the rain.", "Likes the hail.", "Likes sandstorms."

FOG AND HAZE
- Fog and haze cover everyone alike: the people around are in the fog as much as you.
- With a UI STYLE, the fog no longer stops short around the START menu and message boxes.

YU-GI-OH!
- Duelists read what their cards do before playing them: they never pay a cost for an effect that does nothing, as a negated monster's, or wipe a field that takes more of their own cards than yours.
- Draws and searches come first; removal waits for something worth removing. The better the duelist, the sharper their reckoning.

Playing online needs everyone on 1.220.0.
