Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.210.0 "From Fusion to Link!": YU-GI-OH! reaches today, with Synchro, Xyz, Pendulum and Link cards and the table to play them.

YU-GI-OH!
- Every English set to today is in the game: over 14,000 cards, from LEGEND OF BLUE EYES WHITE DRAGON to this year's sets, with Synchro, Xyz, Pendulum and Link Monsters, Tuners and the newer Types.
- Boosters by era, by region: the sets up to 2006 are sold everywhere as before; the later Fusion-era sets in HOENN, the Synchro era's in JOHTO and KANTO, the Xyz era's in SINNOH, and the Pendulum and Link eras' in UNOVA.
- New in OPTIONS > GAME: YU-GI-OH! GEN. Choose the cards decks may use by summon type: FUSION, SYNCHRO, XYZ, PENDULUM, or ALL. Online, the host's choice is used (HOST A GAME > GAME RULES). A deck with a card from a later era is turned away before a duel, naming the card.
- At ALL, the table has the two Extra Monster Zones in the middle, as today's game does. Pendulum Scales show on the outer Spell & Trap Zones, an Xyz Monster shows how many materials it has, and Link Monsters show their Link Arrows.
- Cards show their Rank, Link Rating and Pendulum Scale. The BINDER's FUSION tab is now EXTRA, and SEARCH knows the newer Types.

MAGIC
- More cards play: creatures with Shadow, Flanking, Rampage and Fading, 57 cards in all.

Playing online needs everyone on 1.210.0.
