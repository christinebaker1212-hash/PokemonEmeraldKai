Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.194.0 "Gyms of Unova!": BURGH's, ELESA's and CLAY's gyms work as in Black, CASTELIA's streets open from every side, and more.

UNOVA
- BURGH's gym: the floor switches open the honey doors, and you walk through the open ones to reach BURGH.
- ELESA's gym: walk onto a dock facing the rails and the coaster takes you to the next stop. The floor switches change a lane's track, and two lanes open once their trainers are beaten.
- CLAY's gym: the mine's platforms stand at three heights. Pull a lever to send its lift up or down, riding it if you stand on it, and work your way down to CLAY.
- CASTELIA: the entrances to the streets around the ring road now work whichever way you walk onto them, and you no longer bounce back out of the street you just entered.
- CASTELIA's ring road: buildings no longer hide you as you walk behind them.
- The Cold Storage's container can only be entered through its door, so its story plays in the right order.
- Doors can no longer be walked into from the side.
- After a game update, continuing a save no longer brings back the old map around you.

EVERYWHERE
- Up to 128 people can stand on a map at once (it was 16), so busy places show everyone.

CO-OP
- The daily outbreak notice says HOENN when you see it from another region.
