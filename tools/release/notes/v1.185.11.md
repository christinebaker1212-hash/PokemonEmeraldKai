Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.185.11 "Under the Bridge!": the way to CELESTIC TOWN through the fog of ROUTE 210 is open.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## SINNOH
- ROUTE 210: on the way to CELESTIC TOWN, the lower wooden bridge passes under a narrow bike bridge. The game treated
  the spot under the bike bridge as if you were riding on top of it, so you could only go north or south there and
  couldn't cross west toward CELESTIC TOWN. You can now ride or walk straight under it, and get off your BICYCLE
  there too.
