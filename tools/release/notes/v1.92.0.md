Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.92.0 "Hidden Treasures of Johto!": JOHTO's hidden items are back, and the week's siblings keep their own days again.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## HIDDEN ITEMS
- **JOHTO has its hidden items again**: 57 of them, where Crystal hid them, from AZALEA's FULL HEAL to the WHIRL
  ISLANDS' RARE CANDY. The ITEMFINDER finds them too.
- **Item balls** across JOHTO hold Crystal's items again, and ROUTE 30 and ROUTE 26 have the balls they were missing.

## THE DAYS OF THE WEEK
- **MONICA, TUSCANY, WESLEY, ARTHUR, FRIEDA, SANTOS and SUNNY** are only out on their own day of the week. Meet them
  and they give you their gift. No more guessing the day.
- **UNION CAVE:** on Fridays a LAPRAS waits deep in the cave.
- **GOLDENROD DEPT. STORE 5F:** on Sundays a lady checks how much your lead POKéMON likes you, and gives TM27
  RETURN or TM21 FRUSTRATION.

## JOHTO AS IT WAS
- **ROUTE 29:** the DUDE shows you how to catch a POKéMON, with his own throw.
- **CHERRYGROVE:** losing to your rival no longer sends you back to a POKéMON CENTER, and the guide gives you the MAP
  CARD with a fanfare.
- **PROF. ELM** calls you on the POKéGEAR in his own words.
- **NEW BARK and your home:** MOM, the neighbors and the fisherman say what they said in Crystal, and the stove, sink,
  fridge and TV can be checked.
- **EARL's POKéMON ACADEMY** teaches Crystal's lessons again, with the notebook and the blackboard.
- **SLOWPOKE WELL:** the man with the KING'S ROCK is back, and SLOWPOKE rest in the WELL, in AZALEA and at KURT's
  until the ROCKETS are gone.
- **ROUTE 34:** KATE gives you SOFT SAND.
- POKéMON in houses (PIDGEY in VIOLET, FEAROW on ROUTE 28) cry when you talk to them, ELM's lab has its signs and
  TRAVEL TIPS, and many more of JOHTO's lines are Crystal's again.

## FIXED
- Leftover scenes and calls that didn't belong in JOHTO or KANTO were taken out.
