Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.47.0 "Set the Stage!": Yu-Gi-Oh! duels get backgrounds of their own, Field Spells paint the table, and the Battle Phase can end your turn.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, settings and cards stay as they are.
Playing online works with 1.44.0 and later, but card games with friends need everyone on 1.47.0.

## New
- Yu-Gi-Oh! duels have a background that suits your opponent: night and mist against DARK decks, a golden glow
  against LIGHT ones, embers for FIRE, the deep for WATER, stone for EARTH, sky for WIND, and an arena's grid against
  friends.
- A Field Spell in play turns the whole table into its own picture, made from the card's artwork, for as long as it
  stays on the field. Every Field Spell does it, Yami, Umi, Sogen and the rest alike.

## Changed
- From the Battle Phase you can now END TURN right away, without going through Main Phase 2. MAIN PHASE 2 is still
  the one highlighted first.

## Fixed
- Yu-Gi-Oh! menus no longer break long choices onto a second line, like TRIBUTE SUMMON: each menu is as wide as its
  longest choice and stays on screen.
