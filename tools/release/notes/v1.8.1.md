Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.8.1: a clean screen when friends join your battle.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
1.8.1 plays online with friends on 1.8.0 too.

## Fixed
- **Garbled screen when a friend joined your battle.** In a Double Battle you started alone, saying YES to a friend
  calls your second Moemon back first. The screen was then meant to fade to black while it is rebuilt for everyone,
  but the fade-in left over from that recall stopped the fade to black from starting. The rebuild showed in plain
  view: a white flash, the background alone, empty boxes and stray pieces, then a sudden cut to black. It now
  waits for that fade to finish and rebuilds behind black, as it always did in single battles. The same fix covers
  a friend who was already battling with you when a third player joins.
- **"Waiting for <friend>…" started over** when the friend's Moemon arrived. It now prints once.
