Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.199.13 "Bridges, Lamps and Grand Entrances!": UNOVA's traffic, the LEAGUE's lamps and descent, the ELITE FOUR's entrances and more of Black's scenes.

UNOVA
- SKYARROW BRIDGE is busy again: cars drive up and down its ramps both ways and a ship sails beneath, and TUBELINE BRIDGE's trains rumble past under your feet.
- The bridges sound as in Black: each car, ship and train is heard as it comes near and fades as it goes, from the side it passes on, and SKYARROW's music grows as the view rises over the span.
- ROUTE 4's trucks and cars pass along their lanes, and ROUTE 10's rope bridge sways under you.
- The POKéMON LEAGUE: a lamp lights in the hall for each of the ELITE FOUR you beat, and with all four lit the statue sinks to take you down to the CHAMPION.
- Each of the ELITE FOUR's rooms has its own entrance scene again, the view sweeping round as you are carried up to the challenge.
- The special POKéMON's moments play out: send out the right POKéMON in CASTELIA and the ball flies, and a certain lost boy disappears in a puff of smoke.
- The LIBERTY ship is moored at LIBERTY GARDEN's pier, its wake rippling.
- The time effect around NUVEMA TOWN now also plays on ROUTE 1.
- UNITY TOWER and the stations' greeting boards answer as they should when no one from abroad has visited.
