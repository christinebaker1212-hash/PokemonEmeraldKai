Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.199.12 "Lights, News and Weather!": UNOVA's weather, its gates' news boards, CHARGESTONE's stones and more of Black's touches.

UNOVA
- Weather comes and goes as in Black: rain, snow, sandstorms and mist fall on each place on the days Black has them, and TORNADUS and THUNDURUS bring their storms wherever they roam.
- Every gate has its electric news board again: the date, the weather in the places nearby, the day's sayings and the week's notice scroll across in glowing amber, with the story's news when there is some.
- After the HALL OF FAME, a mass outbreak of a rare POKéMON appears on a different route each day, and the gates' boards tell you where.
- CHARGESTONE CAVE's floating stones are back: walk into one and it slides off to the electric panel it meets.
- DRAYDEN's gym: the walkways and the dragons' necks are walked as in Black, so the dragons' poses set the way through.
- N's CASTLE: the LIGHT STONE shines in the throne room, and N's ball flies to the dais as it should.
- The PEP QUIZ in ICIRRUS takes your answers: type the word when asked.
- Fades that should flash white now do, and message boxes always close when a scene clears them.
