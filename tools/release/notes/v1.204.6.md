Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.204.6 "Your Pick!": every POKéMON card that chooses cards now lets you choose, and a Game Boy catch lets its fanfare finish.

GB OPTIONS
- BALL THROWS: after a catch with a ball thrown the RED or CRYSTAL way, or a GENESIS or EPOCH BALL, the "caught a POKéMON" music now waits until the catch fanfare has finished, instead of playing over it.
- The caught POKé BALL now fades away with the battle screen, and no longer shows on the POKéDEX page, the nickname screen or the fade-out after the battle.

POKéMON CARDS
- Every card that picks something now lets you pick it, on the duel table and in the phone app: ENERGY SPIKE (which basic Energy, and which of your POKéMON it goes on), GIGASHOCK (which 3 Benched POKéMON), CALL FOR FAMILY and SPROUT, ENERGY CONVERSION and ABSORPTION, HYPER BEAM, WHIRLPOOL and MICROWAVE (which Energy), FIRE SPIN, FIREWORKS and THIRD EYE (which of your Energy), BURNING FIRE (which Fire Energy, from any of your POKéMON), MAGNETIC LINES (which Energy moves), RAPID EVOLUTION (which evolution).
- Rocket's Sneak Attack shows your opponent's hand and lets you choose the Trainer; Challenge! lets you choose which Basic POKéMON go on your Bench.
- POKéMON Powers that work as they come into play now ask too: Dark Golbat's SNEAK ATTACK and Gengar's POWER OF DARKNESS (which of their POKéMON), Dark Slowbro's REEL IN and Dark Dragonite's SUMMON MINIONS (which cards).
- Online card duels need both players on this version.
