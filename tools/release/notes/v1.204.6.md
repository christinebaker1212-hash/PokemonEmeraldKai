Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.204.6 "Fanfare First!": a Game Boy catch lets its fanfare finish before the caught music, and its ball leaves with the battle.

GB OPTIONS
- BALL THROWS: after a catch with a ball thrown the RED or CRYSTAL way, or a GENESIS or EPOCH BALL, the "caught a POKéMON" music now waits until the catch fanfare has finished, instead of playing over it.
- The caught POKé BALL now fades away with the battle screen, and no longer shows on the POKéDEX page, the nickname screen or the fade-out after the battle.
