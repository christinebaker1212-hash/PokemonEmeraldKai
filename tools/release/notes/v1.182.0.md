Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.182.0 "Every Region Rewards!": KANTO, JOHTO and SINNOH get their own achievements, and ORRE's stadium is open.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## Achievements
- **Every region counts on its own:** BATTLE, CATCHING, TOGETHER and EXTRAS now come in a set for each region.
  Battles, catches, steps, co-op wins, MOEGATCHA pulls and the rest made in KANTO count toward KANTO's set, and so on,
  so a new region always has achievements left to earn. Everything you earned before stays HOENN's.
- **KANTO, JOHTO and SINNOH at HOENN's level:** each region's tab has dozens of new goals from its own story and
  places: the S.S. ANNE, SILPH CO., the SEAFOAM ISLANDS and the SEVII ISLANDS in KANTO; SPROUT TOWER, the RADIO TOWER,
  KURT's balls and MT. SILVER in JOHTO; TEAM GALACTIC, the Underground, SUPER CONTESTS and the VILLA in SINNOH. There
  are also KANTO's and JOHTO's complete POKéDEX, and steps, POKéMON CENTERS, shopping and more counted in each region.
- **The menu:** L and R go through JOURNEY, the region's tab and its BATTLE, CATCHING, TOGETHER and EXTRAS. SELECT
  shows another region's.
- MELOETTA's and DARKRAI's achievements left KANTO's and JOHTO's lists: those POKéMON aren't met there any more.
  If you already earned one, it stays yours.

## KANTO and JOHTO
- Healing at a POKéMON CENTER now counts as a POKéMON CENTER visit. It used to count as a ledge jump.

## ORRE
- **PHENAC STADIUM can be reached:** the way up to it, and a handful of other spots across ORRE that only joined at a
  corner, are now open to walk through.
