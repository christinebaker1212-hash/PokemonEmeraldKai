Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.15.0 "A Life of Their Own!": the MOéMON GARDEN. Every Moemon has a life of its own.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are (the Garden keeps its
own notes in a new file next to your save).
To play online together, everyone needs 1.15.0.

## New
- **A life of its own.** Every Moemon has a temperament (bold or timid, warm or aloof, lively, curious, social,
  proud), needs, feelings and memories of what it lived through with you. Nothing is ever lost while you're away: a
  Moemon never suffers or sulks because you didn't play. Big moments together (a badge, a hungry Moemon fed, comfort
  when it was scared) can raise its friendship a little, once.
- **JOURNAL.** A new page in each Moemon's summary, after the POKé BALL page: what you've noticed about it so far, in
  plain sentences. A = next page.
- **Your follower does its own thing.** Stand still and it looks around, sniffs at things, naps when it's tired,
  splashes in water, basks, sits by you or invites you to play. Once in a while a curious one finds something hidden.
  Walk on and it hurries back. Round pink bubbles over a Moemon are its own feelings.
- **A and hold A.** A in front of your follower does the fitting thing (comfort it, play, praise it, or just talk).
  Hold A for a wheel: PET, PLAY, FEED, TALK / PRAISE, COMFORT, GROOM, SIT WITH / WATCH, KEEPSAKE; let go on your
  choice. Each Moemon likes some things more than others, and some would rather not be petted.
- **In battle** a Moemon remembers what stands out: hanging on at the last moment, the knockout that won it, a
  comeback, a friend who stepped in. Pink bubbles on its sprite show how it feels, and very rarely, after the biggest
  moments, a pink line in the message box tells you what it's feeling.
- **With friends** Moemon get to know each other and you: followers meet when you stand together, friends can pet or
  feed yours, and a Moemon's life story travels with it in a trade or through the shared PC.
- **The GARDEN.** Walk west out of LITTLEROOT into PROF. BIRCH's research meadow. Your whole party lives there
  freely: napping, playing tag, splashing at the pond, keeping you company. After your first visit, your PC has a
  MOéMON GARDEN row that takes you there, and the GARDEN BOARD by the pond takes you back. The Garden grows as your
  journey goes on. On the board: DECORATE (dolls and cushions from your SECRET BASE collection) and WATCH (sit and
  watch; A tells you what's going on, B stands up). With friends there, a hedge opens to a bigger meadow, and
  everyone's Moemon and decorations are there together.
- **Over time.** A Moemon may take up something it loves in the Garden (climbing the trees, splashing in the pond,
  singing, collecting pretty stones, digging tunnels, showing the younger ones), gets better at it, and the JOURNAL
  celebrates the big steps. Once in a long while, when the right Moemon, the time of day and the weather line up,
  something rare happens; the JOURNAL keeps the ones you saw. A Moemon hatched from an EGG knows its parents and may
  take after them a little. After it evolves, it takes a while to get used to its new body, and its friends come to
  see.
