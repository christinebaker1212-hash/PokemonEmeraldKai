Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.39.0 "Let's Dance!": a DANCE DANCE REVOLUTION EXTREME machine on the GAME CENTER's 3F, with all 240 songs, alone or with a friend.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
Playing online needs everyone on 1.39.0.

## New
- DANCE machine: the GAME CENTER's 3F in RUSTBORO CITY has a new look, with a DANCE DANCE REVOLUTION EXTREME
  machine at the top left. Step up in front of it and press A: the left side is the 1P pad, the right side 2P.
  - Every one of EXTREME's 240 songs, with EXTREME's screens, arrows and announcer, made for the GBA screen and
    drawn like the rest of the game (your window size, filters and SPECTATE all work as usual).
  - The first time you step up, the machine offers to download its songs (about 400 MB, just once: they stay in
    the stepmania folder next to the game).
  - A credit is four songs, the fourth the FINAL STAGE. Fail a song and the credit ends after its results.
  - SELECT STYLE, SELECT DIFFICULTY (BEGINNER, LIGHT, STANDARD, HEAVY, CHALLENGE) and SELECT MUSIC: turn the
    wheel with LEFT and RIGHT, change your difficulty with UP and DOWN, change the sort (SERIES, ABC, BPM) with R.
    Each song plays its preview while you look at it.
  - OPTIONS: SPEED (x0.5 to x8), BOOST, HIDDEN, SUDDEN, STEALTH, DARK, REVERSE, MIRROR, LEFT, RIGHT, SHUFFLE,
    LITTLE, FREEZE ARROW, arrow colors (VIVID, NOTE, FLAT, SOLO), JUDGMENT and TIMING. Your options are kept.
  - Judged like the EXTREME arcade: PERFECT, GREAT, GOOD, BOO, MISS, freeze arrows O.K. / N.G., EXTREME's scoring,
    grades from AAA to E and its life bar. JUDGMENT MARVELOUS shows MARVELOUS steps too.
  - Steps from the D-Pad (or left stick) and the face buttons, both as arrows: X LEFT, A DOWN, Y UP, B RIGHT.
    On a keyboard: the arrows, WASD or IJKL. Every step is timed by the moment you pressed it, not the frame.
  - RESULTS shows how early or late your steps were on average. If your PC's sound comes out late, set TIMING
    to match.
  - Hold START for a moment and a half to give up a song.
- Dance with a friend: pick WAIT FOR 2P and "Let's dance!" appears over you. A friend who steps up to the other
  pad is asked "Dance with NAME?", and you're both in VERSUS: 1P picks the songs, each of you picks your own
  difficulty and options, and you see each other's arrows side by side. A slow connection never makes your own
  arrows stutter.
- Everyone in the GAME CENTER sees dancers up on the pads, turning with their steps.
- Records: each song's best scores are kept by the server for everyone on it, whatever room they're in.
  SELECT MUSIC shows the best, and RESULTS says NEW RECORD. Scores you make offline are sent the next time you
  play at the machine online.
- A DANCE page of achievements: from FIRST STEPS to MARVELOUS full combos on 10-footers, MAX 300, PARANOiA
  survivor MAX, every boss, four full combos in one credit, VERSUS wins and all 240 songs.
- Your own songs: any StepMania song folder dropped into stepmania\Songs\<series>\ shows up on the wheel.

## Changed
- GAME CENTER 3F: you can now walk right up to the arcade cabinets and across the top of the consoles, and the stair rails, the consoles, the
  COIN counter's edge and the plants are drawn in front of you as you pass behind them.
