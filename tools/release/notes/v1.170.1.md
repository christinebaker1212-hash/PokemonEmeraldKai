Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.170.1 "Quick Cut!": switching card games is instant again, and PATCH NOTES go all the way back.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
1.170.1 plays online with friends on 1.170.0 too.

- CARD CASE: L and R between POKeMON, YU-GI-OH!, MAGIC and TRIPLE TRIAD switch straight to the next game, with no fade.
  Opening and closing the case, and moving between its pages, shops and tables, still fade as before.

## Fixed
- PATCH NOTES: the list now goes all the way back to the very first build, 0.1. The oldest versions had stopped
  showing once the list grew past 128 lines.
