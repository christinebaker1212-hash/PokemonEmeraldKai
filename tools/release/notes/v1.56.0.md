Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.56.0 "Shadow of Infinity!": Yu-Gi-Oh! grows to World Championship 2006, with over 800 new cards, nine new boosters, eleven new decks to buy and two more Game Boy Advance games' duelists.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, cards and decks stay as they are.

## Yu-Gi-Oh!
- 802 new cards, from Invasion of Chaos to Shadow of Infinity, plus every card of World Championship 2006 for the
  Game Boy Advance: Chaos monsters, Spirits and Unions, the Monarchs, Cyber Dragon, the Elemental HEROes, the
  Ancient Gears, the Dark World and many more. 2,311 cards in all, every one played by the real rules, each with
  its printed card art.
- Nine new booster packs: Invasion of Chaos, Ancient Sanctuary, Soul of the Duelist, Rise of Destiny, Flaming
  Eternity, The Lost Millennium, Cybernetic Revolution, Elemental Energy and Shadow of Infinity. Duelists hand them
  out as prizes, and the CARD CLUB counter sells them for CARD POINTS.
- The GAME CENTER's Yu-Gi-Oh! clerk now has 15 decks on the shelf, card for card as they were sold:
  - Starter Decks: Yugi, Kaiba, Joey and Pegasus, Kaiba Evolution, Yugi Evolution and Starter Deck 2006.
  - Structure Decks, a little more: Dragon's Roar, Zombie Madness, Blaze of Destruction, Fury from the Deep,
    Warrior's Triumph, Spellcaster's Judgment, Invincible Fortress and Lord of the Storm.
- DECK RECIPES has your own Starter Deck and every deck you buy from the clerk, and once you beat one of the special
  duelists (the ELITE FOUR, the FRONTIER BRAINS, MAXIE, ARCHIE, STEVEN, WALLY and your rival), their deck too: copy
  it into a slot and play it yourself.
- Duelists all over HOENN bring decks from World Championship 2006 and GX Duel Academy as well as the older games,
  and the themed decks they build use the new cards, so every corner of the region plays differently.
- The newer cards follow World Championship 2006's Forbidden & Limited list. Cards you already had keep the limits
  they had, so every deck you've built still works.

## POKéMON card game
- DECK RECIPES shows a GAME CENTER structure deck only once you've bought it (decks you already bought are there).
- Holo cards now shimmer in the BINDER and up close too, not only when a pack is opened.

## All three card games
- Rare cards shine in a new way: a soft band of light glides across the picture now and then, with a few small
  twinkles, and leaves the art's own colors alone. Holo POKéMON cards glitter like the printed starlight holos;
  Yu-Gi-Oh!'s Super Rares shine white, Ultra Rares gold (with a gold name and edge) and Secret Rares through every
  color; Rares get a glint along the name; TRIPLE TRIAD's four- and five-star cards shine too. The shine covers the
  whole picture in every CARD ART setting.
- A rare card you pull from a pack glows softly around its edges instead of sitting on a colored box.
- At the GAME CENTER's 2F counter, the TRIPLE TRIAD seller now stands between the Yu-Gi-Oh! and POKéMON card clerks,
  and you can talk to the POKéMON card clerk from the counter's side as well as from the front.
