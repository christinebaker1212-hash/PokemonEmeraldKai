Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.185.2 "Follow the Arrows!": the ROCKET HIDEOUT can be finished again, with its arrow floors walkable and GIOVANNI's door opening.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## KANTO
- The arrow tiles on the floors of the ROCKET HIDEOUT, SILPH CO. and the VIRIDIAN GYM had turned into walls: on the
  HIDEOUT's B3F a player could end up boxed in with no way out, and the stairs down to GIOVANNI couldn't be reached.
  They're floor again.
- The door to GIOVANNI's room on the HIDEOUT's B4F stayed shut after beating the two guards in front of it. It opens
  now (if you beat them already, it's open when you go back).
- The same mix-up kept other places from noticing a beaten trainer (a way that opens, what someone says or does after
  the battle). They notice now: the HIDEOUT's B1F, the CINNABAR GYM, MT. MOON, CERULEAN CITY, MT. EMBER, FIVE ISLAND's
  ROCKET WAREHOUSE and LOST CAVE, and the CHAMPION's room.
