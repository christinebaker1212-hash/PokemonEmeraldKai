Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.185.4 "Mind the Gap!": the holes in JOHTO's floors can be fallen through again, so JASMINE can be found at the top of the OLIVINE LIGHTHOUSE.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## JOHTO
- The holes in the floors of the OLIVINE LIGHTHOUSE acted like walls, and the way up to JASMINE and her sick AMPHY
  goes through them: the top floor couldn't be reached. You fall through them now, as in POKéMON Crystal.
- The same goes for the other holes in JOHTO: the pits in ECRUTEAK CITY's GYM (a fall takes you back to the entrance),
  the ICE PATH, the RUINS OF ALPH and VICTORY ROAD.
