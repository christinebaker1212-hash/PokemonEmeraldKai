Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.186.0 "All Aboard!": your first trip to a new region is now a real voyage, with a ship to explore and a welcome waiting on the pier.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## The first voyage to a region
- The first time you sail to a region, you travel aboard a ship. Walk the corridor, the cabins and the open deck, and
  talk with ten passengers who are all bound for the same place. There are no battles aboard.
- What they say depends on where the ship is going. One shows you a POKéMON that lives there, and one gives you a
  treat from the region to keep.
- Talk to half of the passengers and the ship announces that land is in sight. The lookout on deck will lend you his
  spyglass to see the coast.
- When you are ready, rest on the bed in your cabin and the ship makes port. You can talk to everyone first.
- With friends, everyone aboard counts each other's talks.

## Arriving
- A sailor meets you on the pier, welcomes you to the region, and takes you to the town where your journey starts,
  right to the door of the house where you will stay.
- The POKéMON that sailed with you goes ahead to the region's professor for a checkup. You begin the region as its
  story expects, and the professor returns your partner when you receive your first POKéMON there.
- HOENN can now be a first voyage too, for a save that began in another region.

## Your host family
- Only the MOM of the region you started in is your MOM. In every other region she is your HOST MOM, and she
  welcomes you the first time you meet. In HOENN, the PETALBURG GYM LEADER is your HOST DAD.

## Keepsakes
- The POKéMON that sailed with you remembers the crossing in its JOURNAL.
- Your TRAINER CARD gets a stamp for each region you first reached by sea, on the BADGES page.
