Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.134.0 "Free Spells!": MAGIC's pitch spells, cast without mana by paying another cost.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- Spells that say "you may ... rather than pay this spell's mana cost" now play: CRASH and DOWNHILL CHARGE (sacrifice a Mountain), GUSH and DAZE (return Islands), CAVE-IN, BOUNTY OF THE HUNT and the other Mercadian pitch spells (exile a card of their color from your hand), ABOLISH (discard a Plains), DARK TRIUMPH, ANGELIC FAVOR and more.
- When both ways are possible, the card's menu shows CAST and the other way in the card's own words (CAST: SACRIFICE A MOUNTAIN). The table then asks which card pays.
- Abilities that tap your untapped creatures, exile cards from your hand or return your permanents as part of their cost now play too.
- Opponents use these spells as well, and prefer paying mana when they can.
- 1887 of the 5408 cards in the pool now play.
