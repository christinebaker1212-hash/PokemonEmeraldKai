Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.136.0 "Two Thousand Strong!": MAGIC passes 2000 playable cards, with Control Magic, Arrest and the Avatars.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- CONTROL MAGIC, PERSUASION, CONFISCATE, STEAL ARTIFACT and the other "you control enchanted ..." Auras play. The stolen card goes back to its owner when the Aura leaves.
- ARREST, NULL ROD, CURSED TOTEM, STUPEFYING TOUCH and STABILIZER stop abilities from being used.
- ANGER, WONDER, VALOR, BRAWN and FILTH work from your graveyard.
- PSYCHATOG, BEARSCAPE, CABAL SURGEON and other cards that exile cards from your graveyard as a cost play. You pick the cards from your graveyard.
- The Avatars cost 6 less when their condition is met: AVATAR OF WOE, AVATAR OF FURY, AVATAR OF MIGHT, AVATAR OF WILL.
- The Legates and MASSACRE can be cast for free when the opponent has the right land. Their menu shows CAST: FOR FREE.
- 2000 of the 5408 cards in the pool now play.
