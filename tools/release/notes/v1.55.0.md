Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.55.0 "Here Comes Team GR!": the second Game Boy card game's 214 new cards, boosters and decks, 11 more theme decks, and card players with more to say.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, cards and decks stay as they are.

## POKéMON card game
- 214 new cards from Pokémon Card GB2: Dark Pokémon and Team Rocket, GR's Mewtwo and the other Team GR cards, Cool
  Porygon and Hungry Snorlax, Lugia, the Rocket's Trainers and four special Energy (Potion, Full Heal, Rainbow,
  Recycle). Every attack, Pokémon Power and Trainer works, played the way the Game Boy game plays them. Where that
  differs from what was printed on the card, the card's text says what it does in this game.
- Each one shows its real card art when PRINTED or HYBRID is on: the English printing when there was one, otherwise
  the Japanese card.
- Seven new booster packs: BEGINNING POKéMON, LEGENDARY POWER, ISLAND OF FOSSIL, PSYCHIC BATTLE, SKY-FLYING POKéMON,
  WE ARE TEAM ROCKET and TEAM ROCKET'S AMBITION. Each has its own set prizes and promo card, and the BOOSTERS page
  scrolls through all 11.
- Card players all over HOENN now play the second game's decks as well as the first's, so there's much more to
  face, and about half of them hand over one of the new boosters when you win. The CARD CLUB's CARD SHOP sells all
  11 boosters for CARD POINTS.
- The GAME CENTER's CARD SHOP has 11 more structure decks, each the English theme deck card for card:
  - Blackout (1999 Base Set), at the same price as Overgrowth, Zap! and Brushfire.
  - A little more for the later sets: Water Blast and Power Reserve (Jungle), Lock Down and BodyGuard (Fossil),
    Grass Chopper, Hot Water, Lightning Bug and Psych Out (Base Set 2), and Devastation and Trouble (Team Rocket).
- DECK RECIPES now has every special trainer's deck once you've beaten them: copy it into a slot and play it
  yourself.
- New attacks ask what they need: which of their Pokémon to aim at, how many Energy to discard, or which card to
  take from the deck.

## All three card games
- Card players have plenty more to say. Before a game they greet you by how it's gone between you: meeting you for
  the first time, chasing a rematch after losing to you, or defending their lead. When the game ends they have a word
  for the winner too, and every GYM LEADER, the ELITE FOUR, the FRONTIER BRAINS, STEVEN, WALLY, your rival and the
  CARD QUEEN speak as themselves. Always friendly, always up for another round.
- Your record against each card player is kept for Yu-Gi-Oh! and TRIPLE TRIAD too, not only the POKéMON card game.
