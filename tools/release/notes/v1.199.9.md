Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.199.9 "Rustling in the Grass!": UNOVA's grass shakes, its caves puff dust and its waters ripple, and NIMBASA's stadiums fill with trainers.

UNOVA
- Once you have UNOVA's first badge, walking around UNOVA's routes and caves sometimes stirs something up nearby: rustling grass, a cloud of dust in a cave, rippling water, or the shadow of a bird circling over a bridge. Step onto it to meet POKéMON you won't find anywhere else there. Dust clouds and birds' shadows can also leave behind a gem, a stone or a wing.
- Fishing works in UNOVA, with each place's own POKéMON. Cast into rippling water for a sure bite from its rarer catches.
- NIMBASA's BIG STADIUM and SMALL COURT have their trainers and crowds. Who you'll meet depends on how far your story has come.
- People around UNOVA greet you differently depending on the time, your journey's records and your POKéMON, as they do in Black.
- The man who asks how many people you've traded with now counts the trainers your POKéMON came from, in your party and your boxes.
- When a team isn't allowed to enter a battle, you're told which of your POKéMON aren't allowed.
