Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 0.3.0 "Strength in Numbers!": Triple Battles, co-op Double Battles, rescue and faster battles.

Test build of October 20.

## New
- 114 more hairstyles for CREATE.
- Lend field moves from a friend close by.
- Rescue instead of blacking out, priced by your team size and missing HP.
- Controller button icons in the game's own scenes.
- Difficulty: a steeper curve alone, a real challenge for two.
- Scaled MoeGatcha economy, new daily goals, 70 achievements.
- Quiet autosave on the map, and a crash report (crash_log.txt).
- Faster battles: quicker starts, shorter message pauses, faster text, quicker send-outs, a low HP beep that stops.
- Co-op trainers: offer to wait for a partner; trainers get new Moemon instead of copies when teamed up.
- Healing items on yourself or your partner in co-op battles, and you can see what your partner is doing.
- Battle emotes and quick phrases.
- Summary screen: SELECT renames, and a POKé BALL page to move a Moemon into another ball or recolor it.
- Special POKé BALLS from the MoeGatcha, and 68 emblems to decorate a POKé BALL.
- Co-op Double Battles: the team picks together, each owner gets their own EXP, the team can run, catches survive a
  disconnect, and a target picker for POKé BALLS.
- Version mismatch: the game says who needs to update.
- Triple Battles: three players against three, in the wild and against trainers, with a card for every Moemon.

## Fixed
- Co-op battles no longer hang over the internet, even across the world.
- A crash on a co-op partner's screen in Windows.
- Co-op catches turning into BAD EGGS.
- A crash when a Moemon forgets a move to learn one in battle.
- Co-op palettes no longer starve tall grass and followers; piggyback rides no longer leave players out of place.
