Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.49.1 "Combo Breaker!": Triple Triad's PLUS and COMBO no longer sweep the board, and the CARD QUEEN is beatable.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, settings and cards stay as they are.
Playing online works with 1.44.0 and later, but card games with friends need everyone on 1.49.1.

## Triple Triad
- COMBO works as in Final Fantasy VIII: only the cards SAME or PLUS took go on to take their neighbors, and then the
  cards those took. Cards the played card takes the plain way no longer start a COMBO, so one PLUS can't flip most of
  the board any more.
- The computer doesn't always see a SAME or PLUS on the board, just like a person: experts spot most of them,
  beginners few.
- Where SAME or PLUS is played, players bring a gentler hand, and the special players and the CARD QUEEN ease off a
  little. A special player whose hand would still be far stronger than yours leads with a four-star card instead of
  their five-star one.
