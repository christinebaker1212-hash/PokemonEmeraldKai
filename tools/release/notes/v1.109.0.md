Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.109.0 "Raise Them Right!": the DAY-CAREs of KANTO and JOHTO open again, and SAFFRON's TRAINER FAN CLUB cheers for you.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are. This update also brings everything from 1.108.0 (CYCLING ROAD's slope, VIRIDIAN's catching lesson and the two department stores).

## The DAY-CAREs
- **ROUTE 5's DAY-CARE** raises one POKéMON for you, as in FireRed. It grows as you walk and comes back for a small fee, and it's apart from your other DAY CARE.
- **FOUR ISLAND's DAY CARE** works again: the lady looks after your POKéMON and the old man outside hands you any EGG they find.
- **JOHTO's DAY-CARE on ROUTE 34** works as in Crystal: the DAY-CARE MAN and the DAY-CARE LADY each raise one POKéMON, and you can see both playing in the yard outside. When there's an EGG, the MAN waits for you by the door. He still gives you the ODD EGG the first time you meet.
- Fixed: a DAY CARE could say you didn't have enough money when you did.

## SAFFRON's POKéMON TRAINER FAN CLUB
- The FAN CLUB is back as FireRed had it. Beat the KANTO LEAGUE and you'll have your first three fans. Win battles against friends to gain more, and lose them to see fans drift away. The members chat about the friends you've battled most.
- The POKéMON JOURNAL on the club's shelf has a feature on BRUNO once you've entered the HALL OF FAME.

## JOHTO
- The GYM LEADERS stay in their GYMS after the LEAGUE, as in Crystal. No more promises of rematches at a FIGHTING DOJO that never had them.
- The ELITE FOUR, LANCE, the HALL OF FAME and CLAIR say the same words every time you take on the LEAGUE, as in Crystal.
