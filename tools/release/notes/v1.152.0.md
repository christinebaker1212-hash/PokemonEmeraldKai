Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.152.0 "Come Back Home!": 15 more MAGIC cards that send your own cards back.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- Cards that make you return one of your own creatures or lands to your hand, or bring a card back from your
  graveyard, now ask which one with "Return which?".
- 15 more cards can be played, among them SHRIEKING DRAKE, SILVER DRAKE, HARVEST WURM, MARSH CROCODILE, STEEL LEAF
  PALADIN, FALSE MEMORIES and TERRAIN GENERATOR. Every one has its printed art.
