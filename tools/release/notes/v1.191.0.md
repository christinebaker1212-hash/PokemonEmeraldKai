Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.191.0 "Welcome To UNOVA!": Pokemon Black's UNOVA as a fifth region, from NUVEMA TOWN to N's Castle and beyond, and SINNOH's post-game finished.

UNOVA
- UNOVA, the region of Pokemon Black, is here: start a new game there, or take the boat to CASTELIA from any port.
- Its towns, routes, caves and bridges in 3D with Black's own camera, its people and trainers with Black's own sprites, words and scenes, and Black's whole soundtrack.
- The whole story: the gift box in your room, PROF. JUNIPER, the eight gyms, Team Plasma, the DRAGONSPIRAL TOWER, the ELITE FOUR and N's Castle, then the credits.
- After the credits: ALDER, the HALL OF FAME, BLACK CITY and its residents and stalls, the BATTLE SUBWAY with its lines and BP, the BP exchanges, LIBERTY GARDEN and VICTINI, CYNTHIA's villa in UNDELLA, the Seven Sages, LANDORUS, KYUREM, the Swords of Justice and VOLCARONA.
- White's own POKéMON live in UNOVA too: PETILIL, SOLOSIS and RUFFLET and their evolutions, THUNDURUS roaming beside TORNADUS, and ZEKROM waiting at the top of DRAGONSPIRAL TOWER after the credits.
- UNOVA's wild POKéMON change with the seasons, as in Black.
- Black's new items: the Xtransceiver, the gems, the wings, the treasures, the new fossils, PRISM SCALE and more, and Black's TMs whose moves had no TM before (TM140 to TM164).
- STRIATON's gym: its doorways open only once you step on the right switch.

SINNOH
- The FIGHT AREA: the way to ROUTE 225 is blocked until you have the NATIONAL DEX, as in Platinum.
- The FIGHT AREA, SURVIVAL AREA, RESORT AREA and ROUTES 224 to 227: their people say Platinum's own lines.
- STARK MOUNTAIN: BUCK follows you through the mountain, and trainers who spot you together battle you both, BUCK beside you. After any battle won with a partner, your team is healed, as in Platinum.
- STARK MOUNTAIN: once you've met BUCK at the BATTLEGROUND, HEATRAN waits in the deepest room, and nothing stands in the way.
- Platinum's daily events start over each day: the swarm, ROUTE 221's daily level, MR. BACKLOT, DEOXYS's meteorites and the game director.
- The BATTLE FRONTIER's exchange counters speak Platinum's own words.
