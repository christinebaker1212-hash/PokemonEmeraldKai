Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.169.0 "Ball After Ball!": AUTO THROW keeps throwing your POKé BALLS for you.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## AUTO THROW
- In a wild battle, hold L (the button that throws your last kind of Ball) and the game asks "Would you like to turn
  on Auto Throw?". Say YES, and every turn throws that Ball by itself.
- "AUTO THROW ON" shows at the bottom left while it's on. It keeps going until the Moemon is caught or you run out of
  that Ball, and then the menu is yours again.
- Press L at any time to stop it.
- A quick tap of L still throws one Ball, as before.
- In co-op battles it works the same: each of your turns throws your last Ball (at the Moemon in front of you when
  there are two).
