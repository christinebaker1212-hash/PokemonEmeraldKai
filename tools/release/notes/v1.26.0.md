Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.26.0 "Spin and Toss!": HEARTGOLD / SOULSILVER POKé BALLS that spin through the air.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
1.26.0 plays online with friends on 1.25.0 to 1.25.3 too.

## New
- The POKé BALLS in battle are the ones from HEARTGOLD and SOULSILVER (ripped by Sunday-Doneko): every kind, from the
  POKé BALL to the MASTER BALL. A thrown ball spins all the way to its target, a ball sent out spins on its way down,
  and it pops open the HGSS way. Your MOEMON's ball shows the same way on its summary.
- The MoeGatcha special balls (LOVE, FRIEND, FAST, LEVEL, LURE, HEAVY, MOON, HEAL, DUSK and QUICK) now have their own
  art in battle instead of a recolored POKé BALL. The DREAM BALL keeps its colors; the GENESIS and EPOCH BALLS keep
  their own look.
- Ball colors and decorations work on the new POKé BALL too, and a decoration stays in sight while the ball spins.
