Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.190.1 "Find and Sort!": SELECT in the MOéDEX opens a new panel: jump to an entry by typing, sort by stats, and narrow the list by generation, type, Ability, move or egg group.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## The MOéDEX: FIND AND SORT
SELECT on the list opens a panel over it. Up and down pick a row, left and right change it, B closes it and
the list follows.
- JUMP TO: press A and type a name, or just its first letters, or a number, then Enter: the list goes there.
  With a controller, left and right pick a first letter and A goes to it.
- SORT BY: besides number, A to Z, weight and height, the list can go by HP, ATTACK, DEFENSE, SP. ATK,
  SP. DEF, SPEED, the TOTAL of them, and FIRST CAUGHT: your own catches in the order you made them.
- SHOW: the same filter as LT / F on the list.
- GENERATION: one generation's entries only, 1 KANTO to 9 PALDEA.
- TYPE and AND TYPE: one type, or two together.
- ABILITY, MOVE and EGG GROUP: those that have the Ability, can learn the move, or are in the group. L and R
  skip to the next first letter.
- NAME AND COLOR SEARCH opens the search the MOéDEX always had. CLEAR ALL puts everything back.

## Good to know
- ABILITY, MOVE, EGG GROUP and the stat orders only list what you have caught (in OWN) or the group has
  (in SHARED), and only offer Abilities and moves those have. Nothing about the others is given away.
- FIRST CAUGHT starts counting with this update. Catches from before come first, by number.
- When nothing fits, the list stays as it was and says NOTHING FOUND.
