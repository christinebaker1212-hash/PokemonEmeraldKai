Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.60.1 "Saves Safe and Sound!": saves from every earlier version load again, and OAK's POKéDEX no longer crashes the game.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, cards and decks stay as they are.

## Saves
- Windows saves made before 1.60.0, or played on 1.60.0, load again with everything in place: badges, money, your
  party, where you stood and the people around you. On 1.60.0 these showed 0 badges on CONTINUE or crashed soon after.
- Nothing needs to be done: the game updates an older save the first time it loads it, and it keeps working on both
  Windows and Linux.

## Fixes
- Getting the National POKéDEX from PROF. OAK no longer crashes the game.
- The badge checks at ROUTE 22's gate and on ROUTE 23 name the right badge instead of crashing.
- Walking into SEVEN ISLAND's TRAINER TOWER no longer freezes the game. The tower is closed for now: its receptionist
  says so, and you can walk back out.
