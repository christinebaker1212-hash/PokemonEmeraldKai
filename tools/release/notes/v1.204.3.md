Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.204.3 "Gotcha Jingle!": Game Boy catches play their own fanfare, and CRYSTAL's battles sit on Crystal's own background.

GB OPTIONS
- BALL THROWS: catching a POKéMON with a ball thrown the RED or CRYSTAL way now plays Red's or Crystal's own catch fanfare with the "Gotcha!" words, as the GENESIS and EPOCH BALLS do, for everyone in the battle.
- CRYSTAL: with COLORS on, the battle background is Crystal's own, lit by the time of day as in Crystal, whatever WHITE BG is set to. RED's is Red's own white the same way.
