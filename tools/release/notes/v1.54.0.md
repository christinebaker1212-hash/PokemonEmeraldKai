Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.54.0 "Find That Card!": SEARCH & SORT in every card game's BINDER and deck builder.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## Card games
- New: SEARCH & SORT. In the BINDER and the deck builder of the POKéMON card game, Yu-Gi-Oh! and TRIPLE TRIAD, press
  L2 / LT / ZL (F on the keyboard). R2 still looks at a card up close, and L / R still turn the tabs.
  - SEARCH finds cards by any part of their name, in any case: type it on the keyboard or with the keys on screen,
    and the list follows every letter.
  - SORT BY and ORDER: number, name, rarity or copies owned, highest or lowest first, plus each game's own numbers:
    HP and Retreat Cost; Level, ATK and DEF; stars, each side and the total of a TRIPLE TRIAD card (A counts as 10).
  - OWNED ONLY, and each game's filters. POKéMON: POKéMON / TRAINER / ENERGY, type, stage, HP, Weakness, Resistance,
    Retreat Cost. Yu-Gi-Oh!: Monster / Spell / Trap, Monster Type, Attribute, Level, ATK and DEF ranges, and the
    Forbidden & Limited list's status. TRIPLE TRIAD: stars (or every star at once), the least each side may have, and
    type.
  - They all work together. START empties a row and CLEAR ALL empties everything; the menu counts the cards left, and
    a badge under the list shows what's narrowing it. When nothing matches, the list says how to get your cards back.
  - It only changes what the list shows and in which order: never your decks or your cards. The cursor stays on its
    card, and each game remembers its own settings.
- Cards you haven't met yet in TRIPLE TRIAD keep their secrets: no search or filter gives them away.
- The end of a lost duel, a friend leaving and a lost connection no longer add that nothing was lost.
