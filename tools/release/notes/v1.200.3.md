Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.200.3 "Every Pocket in Place!": a big round of fixes for the UI STYLES, from the BAG to the summary to the CO-OP menu.

UI STYLE
- The BAG's picture changes with the pocket in every style, and it's the bag your trainer carries: a girl gets KRIS's pink PACK in CRYSTAL, the girl's BAG in FIRERED and PLATINUM, and HILDA's tote in BLACK.
- BLACK's BAG shows its whole picture now, with the pocket arrows beside it and the pocket marks under it.
- PLATINUM's BAG shows five items at a time like KAI's: START SORT moved up beside the bag.
- FIRERED's item icons sit in the middle of their box.
- Item, move and Ability descriptions too long for a style's box turn their pages by themselves instead of running out of the box.
- CRYSTAL shows "BATTLE ITEMS" on two lines under the PACK, and RED and CRYSTAL write "TMs & HMs" with a real &.
- The CO-OP menu keeps its icons and layout in every style, in the style's window frame. Any window whose words wouldn't fit the style's letters does the same, so no text runs out of a window.
- Button names like LT and RT show in every style's messages.
- START opens in the style's own look from its very first frame, with no flash of another menu.
- The summary: PLATINUM's type icons have their full black outline and both types fit on the INFO page; RED and CRYSTAL show a second type; APPEAL and JAM sit cleanly in PLATINUM and FIRERED; FIRERED's stats, HP and NEXT LV. sit in the middle of their boxes; CANCEL on the move list shows no stray words.
- The summary's JOURNAL can be read to its end in every style: A turns its pages.
- BLACK's summary: the page dots stay in one place, and the POKé BALL page lines up with the other pages.
- BLACK's battle: the cursor is a pink outline around the chosen button, and POKéMON no longer show over the menu in Double and Triple Battles.
- Coming back from the BAG in battle no longer leaves a POKéMON invisible.
- OPTIONS > DISPLAY > FRAME is locked while a UI STYLE is on (the style draws its own windows).
