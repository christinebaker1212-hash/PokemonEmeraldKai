Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.61.4 "Better Together!": JOHTO's and KANTO's gyms and trainers work with friends, and a friend's REVIVE brings your POKéMON back.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
1.61.4 plays online with friends on 1.61.0 to 1.61.3 too.

## Fixed
- **A REVIVE from a friend** in a Double or Triple Battle, after all of your POKéMON fainted, now lets you send the
  revived one back out. Before, the game could skip asking you, say it was "already in battle", or show it on the
  host's screen only.
- **Friends couldn't join battles with JOHTO's and KANTO's trainers.** Gym leaders, route trainers and the rest can
  be joined and teamed up with as in HOENN, and a friend who helps beat a gym leader gets the BADGE too.
- **BROCK, MORTY and a few other leaders** could crash a co-op battle with their long speech at the end.
- **TEAM PADDING** sent out POKéMON from later generations than the room's GENERATION in Double and Triple Battles.
  The extra POKéMON now come from your GENERATION.

## New
- **GIOVANNI's past.** After JOHTO's League, dress as one of TEAM ROCKET (the GIOVANNI, ROCKET GRUNT, JESSIE or
  JAMES LOOK), lead with CELEBI and find GIOVANNI at TOHJO FALLS: CELEBI takes you both into the past, where you
  fight beside GIOVANNI and then beside SILVER, to the story's end.
- **The MoeGatcha follows your GENERATION.** At GEN 3 the banners stay as they were; each later GENERATION adds its
  own legend and event POKéMON to STANDARD's daily picks, two PICKUP weeks (its legends, its first partners), new held
  and evolution items, MEGA STONES (GEN 6) and Z-CRYSTALS (GEN 7), and POWER items on TRAINING. Prizes from a later
  GENERATION than yours no longer come up.
