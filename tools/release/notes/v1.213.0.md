Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.213.0 "Pocket Pals!": the MOéMON VMU PET for the Dreamcast's VMU and VM2, and PC > VM2 to bring its MOéMON home or send it EGGS.

THE MOéMON VMU PET
- New in the VMU PET folder: the MOéMON VMU PET, KANTO EDITION. Raise one MOéMON from an EGG on a Dreamcast VMU or a VM2: a day's care (four meals, three trainings, cleaning up) takes a few minutes whenever you have them, and about a week of good care grows it to level 50.
- Every EGG looks like the MOéMON inside it, and the VMU's file icon shows the EGG, then the MOéMON's portrait.
- Tap your MOéMON for SPARKS, hire helpers that earn while the VMU sleeps, and spend them on TAP UPs, RARE CANDY, IV UPs, a shiny, or MASTER and ASTRAL BALLS to bring home.
- BATTLE wild MOéMON on the VMU, and battle a friend's VMU.
- On a VM2, LINK swaps MOéMON with a VMU joined on top, battles it, and sends it each new version of the pet.
- The VMU PET folder's README.txt says how to put it on a VMU or VM2.

PC > VM2
- With a VM2 plugged into your PC in GUI-mode, a PC in a POKéMON CENTER has VM2. Its box holds what the pet has: take its EGG or its grown MOéMON, or put an EGG of KANTO's MOéMON there for the pet to raise.
- A grown MOéMON comes at its level with its IVs and nature, shiny if you bought it, its good care as friendship and EVs, and the MASTER and ASTRAL BALLS you bought on the pet go to your BAG.
- Closing the box writes the VM2 and lets it go: "It's safe to unplug it."
