Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.86.0 "Moé or Poké?": MOEMON COMPANION follows your CREATURES choice, or picks its own.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are. Update MOEMON
COMPANION too (it offers the update when you open it).

## MOEMON COMPANION: MOéMON OR POKéMON
- **As your game shows them:** with OPTIONS > CREATURES on POKéMON in your game, the app shows the original POKéMON
  pictures and says POKéMON, POKéDEX and so on; on MOéMON, it shows Kai's MOéMON and says MOéMON, MOéDEX and MOéNAV,
  everywhere, the home screen widget too.
- **Or your own way:** MENU > LOOK > CREATURES picks AS THE GAME, MOéMON or POKéMON for the app alone.
- **Shiny and colored POKéMON:** shiny ones and nicknamed ones with their own colors keep them in either look.
