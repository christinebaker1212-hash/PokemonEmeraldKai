Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.94.0 "Catch a Falling Star!": the ASTRAL BALL, a MoeGatcha prize that never misses and makes every catch shiny.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## THE ASTRAL BALL
- **A new POKé BALL in the MoeGatcha:** the ASTRAL BALL, as rare as the MASTER BALL, from the BALL banner's top
  prizes.
- **It never misses and never breaks:** throw it at any wild POKéMON, at any health, and it's caught on the spot.
- **Everything it catches is shiny:** the POKéMON keeps its nature, gender and everything else you can see (even
  UNOWN's letter and how WURMPLE will evolve), it just shines in its rare colors.
- **It shimmers:** a rainbow sheen ripples across the ball, light and shadow drift over it, and silver sparkles
  twinkle around it in the BAG, in flight and while it shakes.
- **Then it's yours:** when it clicks shut, the colors fade to the ball's own grey and every sparkle is drawn into
  it. From then on it's a plain grey ball, in the summary and when the POKéMON comes out.
- Only a catch puts a POKéMON in an ASTRAL BALL, so it isn't offered when you move a POKéMON to another ball.
