Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.138.0 "Seek and Find!": MAGIC's library searches, with 72 more cards.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## MAGIC
- Cards that search your library now play: DIABOLIC TUTOR, ELADAMRI'S CALL, NATURAL ORDER, RAMPANT GROWTH, UNTAMED WILDS, LAY OF THE LAND, GAEA'S BOUNTY, HARROW, CROP ROTATION, the fetch lands (POLLUTED DELTA, BLOODSTAINED MIRE, FLOOD PLAIN, BAD RIVER...) and the Mercenaries that find another Mercenary.
- The table fans out the cards you can find, one of each, with the chosen one large in the middle. LEFT and RIGHT move along, A takes it. Your library is shuffled after.
- Cards that put a card from your hand onto the battlefield, like ELVISH PIPER, play too.
- Opponents search their libraries as well, and take what they need most.
- 2104 of the 5408 cards in the pool now play.
