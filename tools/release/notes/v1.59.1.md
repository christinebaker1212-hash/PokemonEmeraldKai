Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.59.1 "No More Spare Energy!": POKéMON card packs bring one more common instead of a Basic Energy.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, cards and decks stay as they are.

## POKéMON cards
- Booster packs no longer hold a Basic Energy card: Basic Energy is free and endless when you build a deck. The slot
  is now one more common card, so every pack has 6 commons, 3 uncommons and a rare.
- The rare is now the last card you turn over.
