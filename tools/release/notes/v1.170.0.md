Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.170.0 "Smooth Shuffle!": card screens fade like the rest of the game, and MAGIC deals its hands.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## All the card games
- Every card screen now fades out and back in, the way the game's own menus do: opening and closing the CARD CASE, and
  moving between its pages, the shops, the challenge screens and the tables, in all four card games.

## MAGIC
- The challenge screen shows your opponent's full trainer picture, their deck's colors, and a BEATEN ribbon once you've
  beaten a regular, like the POKéMON and YU-GI-OH! screens.
- Duels open with both hands dealt one card at a time instead of appearing all at once.
- Structure decks are sold for money on the clerk's new DECKS tab, one of each, like the GAME CENTER's POKéMON theme
  decks. They're no longer on the CARD POINTS list.

## TRIPLE TRIAD
- The CARD QUEEN's challenge screen shows her full trainer picture.
