Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.49.0 "A Fair Fight!": card players in all three card games play like people, at their own skill, and every challenge shows how the match looks.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, settings and cards stay as they are.
Playing online works with 1.44.0 and later, but card games with friends need everyone on 1.48.2.

## Card players who play like people
- In POKéMON cards, Yu-Gi-Oh! and Triple Triad, the computer never looks at your hidden cards: your hand, your
  face-down cards and your deck stay a secret, just like at a real table.
- It makes the kind of mistakes people make: a move almost as good as the best one now and then, a Trap held a
  moment too long, an Evolution or a Trainer kept for later, the second-best target. Real blunders are rare.
- Every card player has a skill: BEGINNER, CASUAL, SKILLED or EXPERT. Townsfolk early on play casually, more skilled
  ones show up as you earn badges, and Gym Leaders, special players and the CARD QUEEN play their best. A player whose
  cards can't keep up with yours this early plays more carefully instead.

## Matches that fit you
- With CARD SCALING on, card players bring a deck that fits the one you pick, in all three games: most come out a
  touch weaker than you, so a good game usually goes your way, while the special players are a real test.
- The challenge screen shows how the match looks with the deck under the cursor: a word from VERY EASY to FEARSOME
  and six pips, and under it, taking turns, how their cards compare with yours and how they play. Move the cursor to
  another deck and it changes right away.
- Each game learns how you do as you play, and fits the next match to that.
- Every card in all three games has been rated by thousands of computer games and by how it stood in the
  tournaments of its day, so the game knows how strong a deck really is. The numbers stay hidden.
