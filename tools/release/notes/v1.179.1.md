Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.179.1 "Box Hop, Polished!": the PC's box grid in your MOéMON's own colors, a cleaner zoom, and moving a tagged group no longer hides the menus.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## PC boxes
- **The box grid in true colors.** The small boxes in the L2 / R2 grid now show each MOéMON as the box does:
  nicknamed ones in their own colors, SEALED ones faded gray.
- **A cleaner zoom back in.** Picking a box from the grid no longer flashes the top row's MOéMON at full size above
  the box before it has grown back.
- **Tagged groups go both ways.** While carrying a tagged group, UP past the top now reaches PARTY POKéMON / CLOSE
  BOX, just as DOWN past the bottom does, and UP or DOWN from there brings the group back.
- **Menus stay visible.** After putting a tagged group into the party with PARTY POKéMON, the PC's menus and
  messages no longer go invisible.
