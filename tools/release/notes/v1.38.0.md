Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.38.0 "Name Your Deck!": rename decks, pick their ACE CARD, rebuild them from recipes, and choose where your Yu-Gi-Oh! cards go.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save, settings and cards stay as they are.
Playing online needs everyone on 1.38.0.

## New
- Decks, in both card games: A on a deck opens its menu: EDIT, RENAME, ACE CARD, DUEL WITH IT, COPY, DELETE.
  - RENAME: type a new name on the keyboard on screen (the D-pad and A; B deletes, SELECT switches ABC / abc,
    START keeps it) or on your real keyboard.
  - ACE CARD: pick the card that stands for the deck in the CARD CASE and the deck list.
- DECK RECIPES (in DECKS, and on an empty slot): the starter decks, the GAME CENTER's structure decks and the
  Yu-Gi-Oh! Starter Decks, built again card for card from the cards you have. Each recipe shows how many of its
  cards you own and which ones are missing.
- Yu-Gi-Oh!: when you Summon, Set or activate a card from your hand, you choose the zone it goes to, the way a
  POKEMON goes to the Bench spot you pick.
- Yu-Gi-Oh!: new animations. An attacker winds up, dashes at its target and comes back; the hit lands with a
  flash and a shake of the table; targets lock on with closing brackets; destroyed cards break apart; Summons
  rise in a ring of light; the attack you're aiming shows its line.

## Changed
- Yu-Gi-Oh! turn menu: the cursor starts on BATTLE PHASE when one of your monsters can attack, on MAIN PHASE 2
  after your attacks when there's a card you can still play, and on END TURN otherwise.
- Yu-Gi-Oh!: a Spell's menu lists ACTIVATE before SET; choosing an attack target starts on the best one (a
  direct attack, or a monster you can destroy), choosing a Tribute on your weakest monster.
- POKEMON cards, setting up: after you put a Basic Pokemon down, the cursor goes to the next Basic Pokemon in your
  hand, or to READY when there's none left. During your turn, after playing a card from your hand the cursor goes
  back to your hand, and after attaching the Energy your Active Pokemon needed to attack, to that Pokemon.
- Co-op Double and Triple Battles show the place's own battle background (a cave, the grass, the water...)
  instead of a building.
- Team Aqua and Team Magma grunts who just spot you can be joined by friends, as a Double or Triple Battle.
- With TEAM PADDING on, a third player can always join a battle against one trainer: the trainer gets enough
  Moemon for a Triple Battle.
- Watering BERRIES is 50% quicker.
- The GAME CENTER in RUSTBORO now lines up with the paths: its doors sit exactly on their spots. The sign in front
  of it is gone, a tree closes the gap at its right side, and the cut-off tree at its top right corner is whole
  again.

## Fixed
- Text that ran off the card screens: long names in the deck lists end in "." when they're cut, a long prompt on
  the POKEMON table takes two lines, three-line messages fit their window, and RARE! / HOLO! no longer hide under
  the bottom bar.
