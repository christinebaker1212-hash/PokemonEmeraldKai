Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.63.0 "Welcome to SINNOH!": a fourth region, SINNOH from POKéMON Platinum, drawn in 3D, with its story, gyms, LEAGUE and BATTLE FRONTIER, and the PÓKETCH for every region.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.
1.63.0 plays online only with friends on 1.63.0: everyone in a room needs this update.

## SINNOH
- **A whole new region.** Clear a region and take the S.S. TICKET to the harbor: SINNOH is on the boat's list now.
  The first boat lands at CANALAVE, and a sailor takes you to TWINLEAF TOWN, where a room is waiting for you.
- **Drawn in 3D.** SINNOH's towns, routes, caves and buildings are Platinum's own 3D scenes, lit for the hour of the
  day, with you and every person standing in them. Its 127 songs play, with night versions for towns and routes.
- **Every place and person.** All 499 places, with words written for this game for every person and sign, its
  MOéMON CENTERS and MARTS, its item balls and hidden items, 470 trainers, boats to IRON ISLAND, the moon islands and
  the BATTLE ZONE, the TOWN MAP and FLY, elevators, CUT trees, ROCK SMASH rocks and STRENGTH boulders.
  SINNOH's HMs need SINNOH's badges, as in Platinum.
- **The story.** BARRY, PROF. ROWAN, CYNTHIA, LOOKER and TEAM GALACTIC: from JUBILIFE and the VALLEY WINDWORKS
  to the three lakes, the GALACTIC HQ, SPEAR PILLAR and the DISTORTION WORLD, people come and go as the story moves on.
- **Eight gyms, the LEAGUE, the HALL OF FAME.** The gyms work as they do in Platinum: PASTORIA's rising water,
  SUNYSHORE's turning gears, CANALAVE's lifts, ETERNA's flower clock, VEILSTONE's punching bags, SNOWPOINT's icy
  slopes and snowballs, HEARTHOME's dark rooms and their clues. Friends who join a gym battle get the badge too.
- **Legendaries** where Platinum keeps them: the lake trio, GIRATINA, HEATRAN, REGIGIGAS and the REGIS, ROTOM,
  CRESSELIA, DARKRAI, SHAYMIN, ARCEUS, and DIALGA and PALKIA atop SPEAR PILLAR after the LEAGUE.
- **Wild MOéMON** by morning, day and night, daily swarms, HONEY trees, the GREAT MARSH's SAFARI GAME with its daily
  MOéMON, the TROPHY GARDEN, Friday DRIFLOON, the HALLOWED TOWER's SPIRITOMB, and the POKé RADAR with chains and
  shiny patches. Fossils come back to life at OREBURGH's MINING MUSEUM.
- **The BATTLE FRONTIER.** The BATTLE TOWER, FACTORY, HALL (one MOéMON against every type), CASTLE (spend CASTLE
  POINTS on rest and berries) and ARCADE (a board picks an event before each battle), with BP exchange booths.
- **VEILSTONE's GAME CORNER** slots and PRIZE EXCHANGE, and the card games in SINNOH with its leaders, ELITE FOUR
  and CYNTHIA as card players.
- With GENERATION above 4, SINNOH's wild MOéMON and trainers' teams take in the newer generations, as HOENN's do.

## The PÓKETCH
- **The PÓKETCH, in every region.** PÓKETCH Co.'s president in JUBILIFE gives it. All of Platinum's apps, with their
  own looks: the clock, the pedometer, the party, the dowsing machine, the BERRY SEARCHER, the TRAINER COUNTER and more.
- Show it with the right stick, small, large or hidden, with a cursor to tap its buttons.

## Also new
- **OPTIONS > DISPLAY > SINNOH PEOPLE:** GEN 3 (the game's own look), MIXED or GEN 4 (Platinum's).
- **OPTIONS > DISPLAY > SPRITE SCALING:** the GARDEN's and followers' MOéMON pictures sized by their POKéDEX height.
- **The POKéDEX's AREA page** shows every region's wild tables as the game uses them: the newer generations, JOHTO,
  KANTO and HEADBUTT trees.
- **Starter MOéMON no longer appear in the wild.** They come from the daily Outbreak (one day in four) and the
  MoeGatcha.
- **TM51 to TM92** as new TMs.
- **JOHTO plays HeartGold and SoulSilver's music**, with battle and victory songs of its own.
- New achievements for SINNOH, and two for all four regions.
