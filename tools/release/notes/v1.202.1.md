Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.202.1 "Fair Shares!": EXP that stays fair for everyone, sliding party switches in every style, and card game fixes.

EXP
- The EXP CURVE no longer counts areas you can only reach past your badges (like water you can SURF early). A team could get many times more EXP than it should, most of all whoever held SURF early.
- Full, unsplit EXP with LEVEL SCALING only comes once this region's LEAGUE is beaten.
- MoeGatcha prize POKéMON no longer get the traded POKéMON EXP bonus.
- A POKéMON traded by mail from another region starts at Lv5 in its new region, like every other POKéMON.
- Field moves in KANTO and JOHTO ask for the right badge (SURF no longer asked for FLASH's badge).

UI STYLE
- FIRERED, PLATINUM and BLACK: switching two POKéMON in the party slides their boxes out and back in, as in KAI.
- Messages on the party screen (like "HP was restored") show over the party, never behind it, in every style.

CARD GAMES
- Trainers that search your deck (MOON STONE, THE BOSS'S WAY, POKé BALL, BILL and others) can't be played with an empty deck, instead of failing after you pick them.
- POKéMON BREEDER only offers Basic POKéMON that could evolve this turn.
