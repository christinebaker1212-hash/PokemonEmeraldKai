Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.60.0 "Three Regions!": JOHTO and KANTO join HOENN, from NEW BARK TOWN to PALLET TOWN and the SEVII ISLANDS.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save stays as it is: JOHTO and KANTO open up once your
HOENN journey reaches its POKéMON LEAGUE, or you can start a new game in either of them.

## JOHTO and KANTO
- Two whole new regions with their towns, routes, caves, gyms, Elite Fours, stories, trainers, wild POKéMON, music
  and the SEVII ISLANDS.
- A new game can start in any region: PROF. BIRCH asks where your journey begins. JOHTO starts in NEW BARK TOWN
  and KANTO in PALLET TOWN, where PROF. ELM or PROF. OAK lets you pick your first POKéMON from a long list of
  starters of the first two generations.
- Each region keeps its own badges, progress and PC boxes.

## Traveling between regions
- Beat a region's POKéMON LEAGUE and carry the S.S. TICKET to sail on: SLATEPORT and LILYCOVE in HOENN
  ("Other regions" at the ferry desk), OLIVINE's port in JOHTO and VERMILION in KANTO.
- Only one POKéMON boards with you: the others wait in the new GLOBAL PC.
- Your first trip to a new region takes you on to its first town and professor; later trips land at the port.
- Your POKéMON have a level in each region: one that's new to a region starts there at Lv. 5 and keeps its moves,
  and it's back at its old level when you return.
- After JOHTO's League, GOLDENROD's MAGNET TRAIN runs to SAFFRON CITY. Champions of both JOHTO and KANTO can
  walk through ROUTE 22's gate between their Victory Roads.
- JOHTO and KANTO have the POKéMON of their own days, the first two generations: no later POKéMON in the wild, in
  trainers' teams or among the starters there, and a POKéMON won't evolve into a later generation's. HOENN keeps
  the first three generations.

## PCs
- The GLOBAL PC is in every PC's menu after your first League. It works in any region whose League you've beaten.

## Maps
- The TOWN MAP, the POKéNAV and FLY show JOHTO and KANTO (L and R turn to the SEVII ISLANDS).
- A POKéMON caught in JOHTO names its JOHTO place on its summary.

## Card games
- JOHTO's and KANTO's people play POKéMON cards, YU-GI-OH! and TRIPLE TRIAD too.
- CARD CLUB staff and TRIPLE TRIAD sellers are in GOLDENROD, ECRUTEAK, OLIVINE, CELADON, SAFFRON and CERULEAN's
  POKéMON CENTERS, and a YU-GI-OH! duelist is in both department stores.
- TRIPLE TRIAD has JOHTO and KANTO rules of their own, and the CARD QUEEN travels there too.

## Co-op
- A room is in its host's region. If you're in another region you'll be told where the room is.
