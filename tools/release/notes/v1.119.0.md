Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.119.0 "Training Montage!": more EXP, stronger vitamins and a richer TRAINING MOEGATCHA

- LUCKY EGG: now doubles the EXP its holder earns (it added half before).
- EXP SHARE: the POKéMON that fought keep all their EXP, and every POKéMON holding an EXP SHARE gets the same amount
  as the one that fought. Hold as many as you like; they don't split it. If the one that fought holds a LUCKY EGG,
  the EXP SHARE holders get the doubled amount too.
- After you beat an Elite Four, with LEVEL SCALING on, every POKéMON that fought gets the full EXP instead of a share.
- Catching a POKéMON now gives all the EXP that knocking it out would, however much HP it had left.
- Vitamins (HP UP, PROTEIN, IRON, CALCIUM, ZINC, CARBOS) give 126 EVs each, up to 252 in a stat.
- The TRAINING MOEGATCHA: every vitamin and RARE CANDY prize comes with 3 to 5 extra, and they come up more often.
- Fixed a crash that a MOEGATCHA pull could cause.
