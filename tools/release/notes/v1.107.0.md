Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.107.0 "Say Cheese!": the GAME CORNER's photo printer and FOUR ISLAND's STICKER MAN put your team and your stories on your TRAINER CARD.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## Your TRAINER CARD
- **CELADON GAME CORNER's MOéMON PRINTER works again.** For a little money it prints your party onto your TRAINER CARD's TEAM page, in NORMAL, BLACK, PINK or SEPIA.
- **FOUR ISLAND's STICKER MAN is back.** Brag about your HALL OF FAME entries, the EGGS you've hatched or your link VICTORIES, and he puts a sticker on your card. Bigger stories earn fancier stickers. Friends see your stickers when they look at your card.

## KANTO
- ROUTE 21's WADE only catches MAGIKARP again, as in FireRed.
- VERMILION's empty lot is back, with the man building on it and his MACHOP stomping the land flat.
- ROUTE 4 no longer has a door into an empty cave.
- The POKéMON CENTERs' upstairs attendants talk about the game's own link rooms.

## JOHTO
- After the 7th badge, PROF. ELM calls about the strange radio broadcasts, as in Crystal.
- INDIGO PLATEAU: the man with the ABRA leaves after you enter the HALL OF FAME, and the guards and the "Congrats" man who never belonged there are gone.
- Statues can only be read in the RUINS OF ALPH, as in Crystal, and ICE PATH lost its warning sign.
