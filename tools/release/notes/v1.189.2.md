Moemon Emerald Kai Co-op 1.189.2 "Stand and Listen!": ORRE's scenes hold you in place, and the PRE GYM battles.

The game installs this update by itself when it starts. Your save and settings stay as they are.

## ORRE
- In scenes that start by themselves as you walk into a place, you now stand still until the scene is over, and
  the scene keeps its own view. Before, in some of them you could walk away while people were still talking to
  you: the three from TEAM SNAGEM who stop you in PHENAC CITY, for one.
- PHENAC's PRE GYM: after JUSTY explains the challenge, his trainers step up and battle you one after another, as
  they should. Before, the first one's words never appeared and no battle began.
- People call them POKé BALLS in every line. Three lines said "MOé BALLS".
